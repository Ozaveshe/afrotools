2016 Lexus ES350 asking prices in Lagos State

In a sample of 20 Foreign Used asking prices reviewed 2026-09-27, the median was NGN 27,500,000. The middle half ran from NGN 25,900,000 to NGN 28,500,000.

Method: First 20 visible results reviewed; all were labelled Foreign Used and described as 2016 ES350. Interpolated quartiles of the asking prices, rounded to the nearest NGN 100,000. An Autochek Lagos sample is reported separately.

Limits: Several Jiji offers have very similar seller copy, so one dealer may be overrepresented. Trim options, mileage, promoted placement, and unverified seller claims can shift the band. These are asks, not completed sales.
