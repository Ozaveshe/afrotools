Buying or importing a car in Tanzania

Tanzania pages split customs costs from first-registration and licensing costs so buyers can see where the final on-road total comes from.

Hidden costs to budget for: Age-based excise, registration tax, licence fees, Dar port costs, and inland transport are the common surprises.

Age and compliance risk: Used passenger vehicles around the 8-10 and over-10-year age bands should be inspected carefully for excise sensitivity.
