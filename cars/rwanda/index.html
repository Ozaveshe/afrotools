Hidden costs to budget for: FX movement, inspection, shipping documents, inland delivery, storage, registration, and dealer margins can move the final on-road number.

Age and compliance risk: This market is in directory-estimate mode until a full customs rule pack is added, so use the local currency range for planning and verify duties with a licensed clearing agent before paying.
