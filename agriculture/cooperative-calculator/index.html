Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cooperative surplus dividend?

This calculator subtracts your entered expenses from revenue and allocates the remaining surplus using your chosen percentages. It models a distribution; it does not determine an approved payment or a legal entitlement.

What is a patronage refund?

In this calculator, the patronage pool is shared in proportion to each member’s entered production. A member supplying 20% of the total receives 20% of that pool. Use a consistent unit and the allocation basis approved for your cooperative.

What is the statutory reserve fund?

The reserve percentage here is an editable scenario input. The default 25% is an example, not a verified legal requirement. Check the current rules, bylaws and approved accounts applicable to your cooperative before using an allocation.

How is a SACCO dividend calculated?

The calculator shows a surplus-based share distribution and, when a rate is entered, a declared-rate estimate on share capital. These are alternative estimates and must not be added together. Confirm the eligible share basis and approved distribution with your SACCO.

Why join a cooperative instead of selling independently?

Use the comparison to test your own produce payment against selling the same quantity independently. It requires confirmation that total cooperative expenses already include member produce payments. Results exclude personal costs and taxes and do not establish that joining a cooperative will increase earnings.

About This Calculator

Model surplus allocations for an agricultural cooperative, SACCO or multi-purpose cooperative using your own financial figures. All calculations run locally in this page.

Three Distribution Methods

Choose a distribution by share capital, by production delivered, or a hybrid split. The calculator checks that allocations total 100% and that your contribution does not exceed the relevant cooperative total.

Assumptions and Approval

Planning estimates only. The prefilled percentages are examples, including the 25% reserve; they are not verified statutory rates. Confirm current applicable rules, bylaws, approved accounts and member payment arrangements. No current market prices or legal rates are supplied.
