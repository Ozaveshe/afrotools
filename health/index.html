Before you use a tool

Know what this health directory can and cannot do

The hub helps you find an informational tool. It is not a medical service, and the presence of a tool does not mean its result is suitable for diagnosis, treatment or an emergency decision.

Medical-information boundary

Use results to prepare questions and organise information. A qualified clinician, pharmacist or relevant public-health service must confirm medical meaning, medication decisions, test results and next steps. Contact local emergency services for urgent symptoms.

Privacy boundary

This directory does not ask for health data. On an individual tool, review its privacy and export notice before entering information. Account, email or sharing actions may use a network; avoid names and other identifiers unless they are essential.

Source and freshness boundary

Health thresholds, schedules, prices and public programmes can change by country and date. Check the method and source shown on the individual tool. If a source or checked date is absent, treat the output only as unverified planning help.
