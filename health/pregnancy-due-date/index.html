Dates must not delay care

Contact a maternity service urgently for bleeding, severe or persistent abdominal pain, a severe headache or vision change, fluid leakage, breathing difficulty, or a change in your baby's usual movements. Use local emergency services if the situation is life-threatening.

Before you calculate

What this plan can and cannot do

  • It can map a working due date to general routine contact weeks.
  • It cannot confirm pregnancy, gestational age, fetal development, labour timing, or whether a symptom is safe.
  • Your provider may use ultrasound findings and your history to set a different due date or visit schedule.
  • High-risk, multiple, uncertain-date, or complicated pregnancies may need a different plan.

Questions this planner answers carefully

Why prefer a maternity-team due date?

A date based only on the last period assumes a known, regular cycle. A maternity team can consider ultrasound dating and clinical history. Use their documented date as the working date when available.

Is the 37- to 42-week range a prediction of labour?

No. It shows the calendar span corresponding to 37 through 42 weeks from the calculated LMP basis. It does not predict when labour will start or whether waiting is safe.

Does every pregnancy use these eight dates?

No. The WHO schedule is a general routine model. Local protocols, availability, symptoms, scan results, multiple pregnancy and health history can change timing and frequency.
