How stable is this BMI measurement?

This route focuses on measurement quality: compare repeat height and weight readings and see the actual BMI interval produced by the readings you entered. It does not invent a universal device tolerance.

BMI is not a diagnosis. It does not directly measure body fat, fitness, nutrition or health. It may be less informative for pregnancy, some disabilities, oedema, high muscularity and some body compositions. Children and teenagers require age- and sex-specific growth assessment.

Why this is separate from the standard calculator

The standard tool at /tools/bmi-calculator/ supports quick metric and imperial conversion with a conventional adult screening band. This health worksheet instead shows whether repeat measurements change the displayed number before anyone interprets it.
