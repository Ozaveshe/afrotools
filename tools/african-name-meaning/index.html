Frequently Asked Questions

How many records are in this dataset?

There are 308 records across 20 listed language labels.

Are the meanings and origins verified?

No. The assigned app directory has no per-entry source ledger. Meaning, country, community, gender and pronunciation are shown as unknown or unverified.

Can I use this page to assign ethnic ownership?

No. A listed language label is not proof of country, community or exclusive cultural ownership. Use community-backed sources and local speakers.

Does searching or exporting send or save a name?

No. Search state is kept only in page memory, local TXT and print/PDF exports are ungated, and raw names or results are not placed in browser persistence, URLs, analytics, accounts, logs or email.
