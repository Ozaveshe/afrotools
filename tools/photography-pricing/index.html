Nigerian Wedding Photography Note
An experienced Nigerian wedding photographer charges ₦200,000–₦2,000,000+ per wedding depending on packages. Always charge a non-refundable booking deposit (minimum 50%) to secure dates. Delivery timeline should be clearly stated in contracts — aim for 2–4 weeks.
