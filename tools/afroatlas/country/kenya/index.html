Read the numbers in context

Total GDP describes economic scale. GDP per person is average output per resident, not a salary or a cost-of-living measure. Both use current US dollars; exchange rates and national accounts revisions can change them.

Real GDP growth measures annual output change after price changes. Electricity access and internet use describe national coverage, not service reliability, speed or affordability in a particular city. Life expectancy is an average at birth.

Exports and imports include goods and services. Their totals should not be mixed with an undated goods-only product breakdown.

Questions about Kenya

What is the latest GDP figure for Kenya?

The World Bank WDI observation is $135,941,278,878.8 for 2025, in current US dollars. It measures total economic output, not household income or purchasing power.

What are Kenya's population and GDP per person?

Population is 57,532,493 (2025). GDP per person is $2,363 (2025) in current US dollars. It is an average output measure, not a salary.

What are Kenya's main natural resources?

The undated AfroAtlas reference list includes Arable Land, Coffee, Fisheries. These entries do not establish current production amounts or rankings; check primary resource statistics.

Which exports appear in the Kenya profile?

The undated product reference list includes Tea, Cut Flowers, Coffee, Vegetables, Petroleum Products. Goods-only trade sources can differ from the WDI totals, which include goods and services.

How can I compare Kenya fairly with another country?

Use the latest shared observation year for each indicator. AfroAtlas compares matched years by default, shows missing observations as N/A, and offers a latest-observation view that labels differences in years.
