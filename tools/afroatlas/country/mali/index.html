Read the numbers in context

Total GDP describes economic scale. GDP per person is average output per resident, not a salary or a cost-of-living measure. Both use current US dollars; exchange rates and national accounts revisions can change them.

Real GDP growth measures annual output change after price changes. Electricity access and internet use describe national coverage, not service reliability, speed or affordability in a particular city. Life expectancy is an average at birth.

Exports and imports include goods and services. Their totals should not be mixed with an undated goods-only product breakdown.

Questions about Mali

What is the latest GDP figure for Mali?

The World Bank WDI observation is $30,069,148,580.8 for 2025, in current US dollars. It measures total economic output, not household income or purchasing power.

What are Mali's population and GDP per person?

Population is 25,198,821 (2025). GDP per person is $1,193 (2025) in current US dollars. It is an average output measure, not a salary.

What are Mali's main natural resources?

The undated AfroAtlas reference list includes Gold, Arable Land. These entries do not establish current production amounts or rankings; check primary resource statistics.

Which exports appear in the Mali profile?

The undated product reference list includes Gold, Raw Cotton, Live Animals, Sesame Seeds. Goods-only trade sources can differ from the WDI totals, which include goods and services.

How can I compare Mali fairly with another country?

Use the latest shared observation year for each indicator. AfroAtlas compares matched years by default, shows missing observations as N/A, and offers a latest-observation view that labels differences in years.
