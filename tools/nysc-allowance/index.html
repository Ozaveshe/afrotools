FAQ

NYSC allowance budget questions

Why is the federal figure editable?

The federal source cited above says ₦77,000 took effect from March 2025, but policy and individual payment records can change. Your verified current amount is the correct calculator input.

Why are there no state presets?

State support can depend on posting, role, employer and effective date. A generic state dropdown can silently turn an old report into a false entitlement claim.

Should unpaid arrears be entered?

Only include them when you deliberately want an expected-income scenario. Do not treat expected arrears as cash already available.

Does this determine eligibility or payment timing?

No. It performs arithmetic only and cannot inspect NYSC, state, PPA, payroll or bank records.
