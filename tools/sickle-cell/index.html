    Trait and disease are different

    Read the letters carefully

    AS
    Sickle cell trait: one haemoglobin A allele and one S allele. Trait does not become sickle cell disease.
    SS
    HbSS (sickle cell anaemia), a form of sickle cell disease.
    AC
    Haemoglobin C trait: one A allele and one C allele.
    SC
    HbSC, a form of sickle cell disease involving S and C alleles.
    CC
    HbCC disease, which is not sickle cell disease; clinical interpretation still belongs with a professional.

    Questions for a clinician or counsellor

    Use the result to prepare—not decide

    • Were both results confirmed by the right laboratory method?

    • What testing and counselling options are locally available?
    • What newborn screening and confirmatory follow-up are offered locally?

    Newborn screening

    A screen is not the end of the process

    Screening programmes and timing vary by country. A screen can identify a baby who may have a haemoglobin condition, but positive or unclear screens need timely confirmatory testing and clinical follow-up under the local programme.

    Method and limits

    How the Punnett model works

    Each selected result contains two letters. The model crosses each letter from the first result with each letter from the second result, producing four equally weighted cells. Matching cells are grouped, so each unique genotype receives 25%, 50%, or 100% in this simplified Mendelian model.

    The order of the two selections does not change the distribution. The model deliberately does not estimate clinical severity, symptoms, treatment, life expectancy, population burden, testing prices, or the outcome of an individual pregnancy.

    Does “AA” mean a person has no health condition?

    No. In this page it means only that the simplified haemoglobin selection is AA. It does not assess any other gene, blood disorder, symptom, or health condition.

    What if a report uses different notation?

    Do not translate or guess the result yourself. Ask the laboratory, clinician, or genetic counsellor to explain it. This page supports only AA, AS, AC, SS, SC, and CC.

    Can this tool recommend a reproductive option?

    No. It supplies neutral inheritance education only. A qualified clinician or genetic counsellor can explain testing options, limitations, timing, access, and personal implications without the calculator making the decision.
