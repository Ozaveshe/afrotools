How the score works

Age, source-score sex, prior gestational diabetes, close family history, diagnosed high blood pressure, activity and a weight category are added. The metric version maps BMI 25–29.9 to 1 point, 30–39.9 to 2 points and 40+ to 3 points, with the source score's optional lower 23 BMI threshold for Asian American adults. Symptoms, current pregnancy and a previous abnormal glucose result override low-score reassurance. A total of 5 or more is a prompt for confirmatory blood testing—not a diagnosis.
