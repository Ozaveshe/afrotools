How to calculate water tariff in Addis Ababa

The Addis mode models the fifth-year schedule in AAWSA Regulation 149/2023: 8 July 2026 to 7 July 2027 (article 5, PDF page 8). This is a dated schedule estimate. Later adjustments and current provider implementation are not confirmed. Check your bill before using these rates.

Domestic water is charged progressively: each portion of monthly usage uses its own tier. Non-domestic water uses the rate of the reached tier for the whole volume (article 4). A minimum of 3 m³ is billable (Schedule 5, item 11). A commercial activity attached to a dwelling may change its classification; confirm your class with AAWSA.

Add the other charges shown on your bill separately: this mode does not automatically calculate sewerage, meter rent, waste collection, tax or arrears.

Ethiopia Water Bill Calculator — What You Need to Know

Water tariffs depend on the provider, customer class and effective period. The former national default and automatic 8% charge have been withdrawn because they were not supported by an applicable verified schedule.

In flat-rate mode, the calculation is usage × your flat rate + your other charges. Daily figures assume 30 days. Keep your dated bill or quotation with the result. Only the Addis schedule mode calculates tiers.

Planning estimate: Confirm your selected basis, customer class and other charges with your water provider. Neither mode confirms the amount currently payable on your account.
