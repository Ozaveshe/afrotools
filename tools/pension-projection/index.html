Pension Fund Projection FAQ

How do I use the Pension Fund Projection?

Enter your amounts, rates and dates in the fields above. The Pension Fund Projection then gives you a clear estimate with the assumptions shown you can use straight away.

How accurate is the Pension Fund Projection?

It is as accurate as the values you enter and shows the assumptions behind the result. Always confirm current balances, contribution rules, fees and scheme terms before relying on it. It is a planning estimate, not financial advice.

Is the Pension Fund Projection free, and does it cover my country?

Yes — it is free, works without signup, accepts any three-letter currency code, and keeps the calculation in your browser.
