    What changes across the five admission systems

    These summaries identify the route owner and verification boundary. They are not complete entry requirements.

    Nigeria

    JAMB’s IBASS provides programme and subject-combination checks; CAPS handles the central admissions process. Institutions retain programme-specific selection requirements.

    Open official IBASS eligibility checker

    Kenya

    KUCCPS publishes programme minimums and carries out placement for covered routes. Placement remains competitive and depends on choices, performance and available capacity.

    Open official KUCCPS programmes

    South Africa

    The NSC sets minimum study-level requirements, while each university and faculty publishes its own programme rules, APS method, deadlines and any NBT requirement.

    Read official NSC guidance

    Ghana

    Admissions requirements and aggregate rules are institution- and programme-specific. Check the institution’s current admissions page and confirm institutional or programme recognition with GTEC.

    Check GTEC recognition notices

    Zimbabwe

    Universities publish their own general and programme-specific entry requirements. Confirm that the institution and programme are registered or accredited through ZIMCHE.

    Check ZIMCHE institutions

    How to use this planner responsibly

    Minimum requirement versus admission

    A minimum normally means you may be considered or may submit an application. It does not guarantee placement or admission. Programme subject rules, ranking, competition, capacity, quotas, selection tests and the admission category may still apply.

    Why AfroTools calculators are supporting tools

    WAEC, JAMB, KCSE and APS calculators can help reproduce arithmetic from marks you enter. The official owner or institution remains the authority for subject combinations, programme rules, cutoffs, placement and admission decisions.

    Source and freshness policy

    The external links on this page point to government, placement, regulator or institution-owned pages and were checked on 26 July 2026. Requirements and cycles change, so re-open the official source when you apply.

    University admission route questions

    Does this tool predict university admission?

    No. It builds a verification route. Meeting a minimum does not establish admission because competition, capacity, subject requirements and institutional selection differ.

    Why is there no Ghana or Zimbabwe score verdict?

    A generic points table cannot safely represent every university, faculty, programme or current cycle. This page now links to institutional and regulator sources instead.

    What should I export?

    Export the country checklist, then add the programme name, source URL, checked date, required subjects, current cutoff or selection rule, deadline, documents and applicant-portal receipt outside AfroTools.
