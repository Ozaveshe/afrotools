When does the national minimum apply?

The national minimum is ₦70,000 per month for covered employment under the 2024 amendment signed on 29 July. Section 4 of the 2019 Act excludes establishments employing fewer than 25 people and specifies other exceptions. Check who employs the worker, especially when an agency arranges the placement. The cited law gives no separate national salary for a housekeeper, nanny or other domestic worker.
