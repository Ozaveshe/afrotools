Which payment applies?

Severance under section 35. Select this for redundancy, retrenchment, an employer’s economic, technical, structural or operational requirements, or unfair dismissal. Section 35 also addresses employer insolvency or death. Ordinary fixed-term expiry, completion of a specified task, probation and fair misconduct dismissal are excluded. Check the exceptions for re-employment offers, partnership dissolution and succession under sections 35(5) and (7).

Enter the current monthly wage under section 35(2), including eligible housing and transport benefits. Work expenses and employer pension contributions are generally excluded. The first five completed years earn two weeks each, years six to ten earn three each, and later years earn four each. Service below one completed year earns no weeks under this schedule.

Gratuity under section 35A. On retirement, termination or death, a confirmed employer exemption from pension provision and at least three continuous months with the employer in a given year are required. Use the final month’s salary and completed months. The statutory rate is 5% for each month. Confirm the exemption with your employer. Salary alone does not establish it.

Estimate one payment at a time. Pension balances, contractual gratuity, notice pay, compensation and transitional entitlements accrued before the 2011 change need separate assessment.
