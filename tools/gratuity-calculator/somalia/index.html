When the redundancy formula applies

Article 107(1)(s) provides one month's final salary per year worked when a covered employee loses their job through redundancy. Article 107(2) excludes redundancy caused by employer bankruptcy or insolvency. For resignation, retirement or another reason for leaving, use the agreement option only if your contract or collective agreement specifies a gratuity rate.

Article 3 covers employers and employees, including public employees whose service conditions are not governed by a separate law. It excludes members of the armed forces, police, custodial and security services, apart from civilian hires. Check which law governs your appointment.

This estimate covers the selected payment only. Under Article 107, unused leave and salary in lieu of the one-month notice may be due separately. Check your notice, leave balance and applicable collective agreement before agreeing a final settlement.
