Use a qualification in another country

Choose the country where you will use your certificate, diploma or degree. Then choose why you need it recognised. The organisation receiving your application can tell you which report it accepts.

Where will you use your qualification?

Ghana

University admission or employment

Ask the admissions office or employer whether they need a Ghanaian equivalence report. The Ghana Tertiary Education Commission assesses completed qualifications. Prepare your certificate, official academic records and a certified English translation for documents in another language.

Check the GTEC requirements and start an evaluation

Professional registration

Ask the regulator for your profession whether it accepts a GTEC report and which registration documents it requires. GTEC supplies the academic evaluation; the regulator decides registration.

Kenya

Equation of a foreign school certificate

For a school certificate, the Kenya National Examinations Council handles equation requests through QMIS. Check the documents and translation rules for your examination system before applying.

Read KNEC’s equation guidelines (PDF)

Recognition of a foreign university award

If a university or employer requests recognition or equation of a foreign university qualification, check whether your award falls within the Commission for University Education’s scope. Its guidance lists accepted awards and how to certify certificates and transcripts.

Read the CUE recognition and equation requirements

Qualification level or authenticity

If the recipient asks for the qualification’s level on the Kenyan framework, choose alignment. If it asks for confirmation that an award was genuinely issued, choose validation.

Open the KNQA alignment and validation portal

Nigeria

A foreign qualification for study or work

On the Federal Ministry of Education’s portal, select Foreign qualification for other use in Nigeria. If an official transcript is required, arrange for your awarding institution to send it directly to the assessment service.

A foreign bachelor’s qualification for NYSC

Select Foreign qualification for NYSC for mobilisation, exemption or exclusion. Check the service’s transcript and evidence-of-study instructions before applying.

A Nigerian certificate for use abroad

Select Use of Nigerian certificate abroad for authentication. Ask the recipient abroad whether it also requires its own equivalence assessment.

Choose your service on the Federal Ministry of Education portal

South Africa

Compare a foreign award with the South African framework

SAQA evaluates foreign qualifications against the South African National Qualifications Framework. Check the service’s scope and application requirements before ordering the evaluation.

Read SAQA’s foreign qualification evaluation instructions

Admission, employment or a professional licence

Ask the university about its entry requirements, the employer about its evidence requirements or the professional body about registration. Each applies its own criteria when considering the SAQA evaluation.

Canada

Express Entry immigration

Use Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada’s list of designated organisations and professional bodies. Select an assessment for immigration purposes and check whether your occupation requires a specific assessor.

Find an IRCC-designated assessor

Professional licensing or university admission

For a regulated occupation, identify the regulator in the province or territory where you intend to work. For study, ask your chosen institution which transcripts or assessment report its admissions office accepts.

Find the Canadian occupational regulator for your destination

Before ordering an assessment

Tell the recipient the award title, awarding institution, country of award and intended use. Ask for the name of the accepted service, who must send the transcript, and whether translations or certified copies are required. Use this same request if your destination is outside the list above.
