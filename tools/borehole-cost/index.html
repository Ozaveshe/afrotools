Typical Costs

Nigeria: ₦800K - ₦3M+ for domestic. Lagos/SW cheaper than North (harder rock).

Kenya: KSh 300K - 1.5M. Nairobi area typically 80-120m deep.

South Africa: R30K - R150K. Requires NWRA licence for large boreholes.

Ghana: GH₵15K - GH₵80K. Shallow in coastal areas, deeper inland.

Tanzania: TSh 50M - 200M+. Hard basement rock common in central/southern regions. EWURA regulates water utilities.

Uganda: USh 40M - 150M. Ministry of Water issues permits. Kampala area has good shallow aquifers.

Before You Drill

  • Get a hydrogeological survey first
  • Check water table depth in your area
  • Obtain required permits/licences
  • Use a licensed/registered driller
  • Get water quality tested after drilling
  • Budget for annual maintenance

Borehole Drilling in Africa

Access to clean water remains one of Africa's biggest challenges, with an estimated 400 million people lacking access to basic drinking water services. Boreholes (water wells) are the primary solution for groundwater access across the continent, serving homes, farms, communities, and industries. Understanding the costs involved helps individuals and organisations budget accurately for this essential infrastructure.

Depth, geology, access, casing, test pumping, treatment, permits, power and contractor availability can all change the final cost. The calculator preserves an undated legacy allowance only so the calculation remains reproducible; it does not represent a current market benchmark.

A hydrogeological survey and current written scope are essential. A licensed driller and the relevant water or environmental authority should confirm siting, construction, yield testing, water quality and permit requirements before work begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

How should I choose a borehole depth?

Use a site-specific hydrogeological survey and a licensed driller. This calculator does not predict groundwater depth or yield.

Are the embedded costs current?

No. They are an undated legacy snapshot with no maintained source ledger. Obtain current written quotes and confirm permits, testing and scope.
