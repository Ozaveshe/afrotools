Medicine safety boundary

Do not use price alone to change a medicine, brand, strength, form, pack, dose or schedule. Products that sound similar may not be interchangeable. Confirm the exact prescription and any change with a licensed pharmacist or prescriber. Seek urgent local help for a severe reaction, overdose or emergency.
