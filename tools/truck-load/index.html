Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculate a legal truck load limit?
No. Capacity is a value you enter. Confirm the vehicle plate and registration plus current axle, gross-weight and route restrictions with the responsible authority before dispatch.
What does cost per tonne-km mean?
It is the total trip cost you entered divided by the load in tonnes and trip distance in kilometres. The calculator does not supply a fare, tariff or market benchmark.
When should I compare consolidation?
Compare scenarios when utilization is below your target, but also check delivery timing, cargo compatibility, safety and current legal restrictions. The calculator does not recommend a dispatch decision.
