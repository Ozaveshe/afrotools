What every audit covers

Titles & metadata

Title and description length, charset, language attribute, and favicon — the basics most pages still get wrong.

Crawling & indexing

noindex directives, canonical URLs, robots.txt, XML sitemap, hreflang, HTTPS, and mobile viewport.

Content & headings

H1 discipline, heading hierarchy, thin-content detection, image alt coverage, and lazy loading.

Links

Internal vs external link balance, empty anchor text, and insecure http:// resources on https pages.

Social sharing

Open Graph and Twitter card declarations for social previews. An HTML check does not predict shares, AI citations or search visibility.

Structured data & speed

JSON-LD validity and types, HTML weight, compression, redirect chains, and server response time.

Frequently asked questions

What does the free SEO audit check?

Audits check fetched HTML across seven categories: metadata, indexing directives, headings, links, social tags, JSON-LD syntax and speed signals. robots.txt and sitemap reachability do not prove crawl permission or Google index status.

Are the results real or estimated?

The auditor uses fetched HTML and response headers. Length and performance thresholds are heuristics for review. The checklist score is not a Google ranking score, traffic estimate or authority metric.

How many free audits do I get?

The free page offers three audits per device per day. Pro adds device projects, search-export comparisons, fix tracking and printable reports. Service rate limits apply.

Does llms.txt improve my audit score?

No. llms.txt is not part of the audit score. Google states it does not affect visibility in Google Search. This tool does not predict AI citations.

Does this work for sites outside Africa?

Yes — the audit works for any public website. The recommendations are written with African businesses in mind: WhatsApp share previews, low-bandwidth performance, and local search visibility.
