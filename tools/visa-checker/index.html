Verify the route before you travel.

This planner does not issue a visa verdict. It turns your passport and destination choice into a short official-source checklist so you can confirm the current rule, purpose, transit conditions, permitted stay, fee, and application route yourself.

Build a route brief

No passport number, name, travel date, payment detail, or application reference is requested or stored. The planner makes no network request.

Verification brief

No live verdict: official confirmation required
  • Confirm the rule for this passport nationality, travel purpose, and planned length of stay.
  • Check transit, onward-ticket, passport-validity, vaccination, and proof-of-funds conditions.
  • Confirm whether an embassy, official eVisa portal, or arrival process applies.
  • Confirm the current fee and processing time before making a non-refundable booking.
