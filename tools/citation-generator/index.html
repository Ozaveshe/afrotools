Submission check

What this worksheet can and cannot decide

  • It formats four common source types from the exact details you enter.
  • It does not verify authorship, dates, title case, journal abbreviations, legal authorities or source credibility.
  • Harvard is not one universal standard; the Harvard option is a generic author-date template.
  • Chicago here means author-date, not notes and bibliography.
  • Missing metadata can change a reference. Compare every entry with the original source.

Citation generator questions

Why are only four source types included?

They are the common patterns this worksheet can support transparently. Interviews, social posts, audiovisual works, legal cases, legislation and specialist technical styles have rules that need more context than the old generic templates captured. Use the official manual for those sources.

Why does Harvard have a warning?

“Harvard” describes a family of author-date systems rather than one centrally governed manual. Universities and publishers can differ on punctuation, access dates and other details.

Does saving send my research to AfroTools?

No. Save locally writes only the bibliography entries to this browser’s local storage. Copy, TXT download and print also run locally.
