Quick Quiz

How to use this Yoruba starter phrasebook

Search the embedded English–Yoruba draft by English wording, Yoruba spelling, or category. The page works locally for these 175 records; text outside the inventory can be sent to the separate machine-translation service only after explicit page-session consent.

Orthography is part of the meaning

Yoruba uses tone marks and dot-below letters. This draft preserves those marks where supplied but does not claim complete tone marking, dialect coverage, or qualified review. Do not remove diacritics from important wording merely because a keyboard makes them inconvenient.

Usage varies

Nigerian, Beninese, Togolese, community, and diaspora wording can differ. Treat medical, legal, safety, financial, public, or ceremonial phrases as prompts for qualified human review, not approved translations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much Yoruba does this phrasebook contain?

It contains 175 embedded starter records from an unreviewed app-local draft. It is not an exhaustive dictionary or a qualified translation resource.

Are tone marks and spellings complete?

No. The page preserves tone marks and dot-below letters where supplied, but tone marking is incomplete and important wording requires qualified Yoruba review.

Is the listen button a pronunciation authority?

No. It uses your browser’s speech system, which may not provide a Yoruba voice and may fall back to another voice. Verify pronunciation with a qualified Yoruba speaker.
