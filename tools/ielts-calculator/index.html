Academic vs General

  • Same sections: Listening and Speaking use the same test in Academic and General Training.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the IELTS overall band score calculated?

The overall band is the average of Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, rounded to the nearest half or whole band. This page uses that standard rounding logic.

Are the raw Listening and Reading conversions official?

No. AfroTools uses reference tables to estimate Listening and Reading bands from raw answers out of 40. They are useful for planning, but they do not replace official test scoring.

Should I choose Academic or General Training?

IELTS Academic is designed around higher-education language use; General Training is designed around everyday, workplace, and migration contexts. Only the receiving organisation can confirm the test type it accepts.

Can this page help with scholarships too?

Use the result as one input when researching a scholarship. It does not determine funding readiness or eligibility; confirm the accepted test and score rules on the provider page.

Why this is more than an IELTS score calculator

The overall IELTS band is the average of Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, reported to the nearest whole or half band. Raw Listening and Reading estimates are less exact because the marks needed for a band may vary slightly by test version.

Academic and General Training use the same Listening and Speaking tests, while Reading and Writing differ. This page keeps separate Reading estimates and never treats a target comparison as an admission, scholarship, work, professional-registration, or immigration decision.
