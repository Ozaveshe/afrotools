Halal Business Compliance Checker — Africa FAQ

Does a high score mean my business is halal-certified?

No. The score only organises your answers. Certification requires an assessment by a qualified certifier operating in your market and product scope.

Which information should I verify?

Verify ingredients, supplier evidence, handling and cleaning controls, finance contracts, staff procedures, certifier scope, fees and renewal terms directly.

Does AfroTools send or store this checklist?

The calculation runs in your browser. Do not enter confidential recipes, contracts or personal data; keep sensitive evidence in your own controlled records.
