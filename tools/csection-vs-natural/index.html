Clinical safety comes before price

Both vaginal and caesarean birth can have benefits and risks. The balance depends on the pregnant person's circumstances, preferences, pregnancy, baby, facility capability and how labour unfolds. A maternity team must assess those factors. Do not delay urgent maternity care to complete this worksheet or compare costs.

Respectful language

Use vaginal birth, not “natural”

Calling one route “natural” can imply that surgery, assisted birth or a changing plan is a failure. This route uses vaginal birth and caesarean birth as neutral descriptions.

      Keep the discussion balanced

      Which mode does this tool say is safer?

      Neither. Precise individual risks cannot be calculated from a generic web form. The maternity team should explain how each option applies to the actual circumstances.

      Can cost decide the mode of birth?

      No. Cost and coverage matter for preparation and access, but must not rank a medically needed procedure below a cheaper route or recommend surgery because of a price.

      Does a previous caesarean determine the next birth?

      Not from this page. Previous surgery details, current pregnancy, preferences and facility capability require individualized assessment.
