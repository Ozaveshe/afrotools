Dots, tone, and vowel harmony

Igbo distinguishes eight written vowels: a, e, i, ị, o, ọ, u, ụ. Dot-below letters are not decorative alternatives. Tone can also change meaning, but ordinary text does not always show every tone mark. The phrasebook preserves reviewed spellings while stating that its tone marking and pronunciation cues remain incomplete.

Dialect and community boundary

Wording may differ across Standard Igbo and regional or community varieties. This app does not promise that one phrase is preferred everywhere. Confirm important, medical, legal, public, ceremonial, or relationship-sensitive wording with a qualified speaker from the relevant community.
