Food variety and safety worksheet

Pregnancy food variety planner

Select the broad food groups you expect to have available. The planner turns them into a discussion card, not a calorie target, meal prescription or judgement of whether a pregnancy diet is adequate.

No calorie calculationNo supplement doseNo stored diet record

Individual needs belong with maternity and dietetic care

Do not use this worksheet to manage diabetes, high blood pressure, anaemia, severe vomiting, kidney or liver disease, food allergy, an eating disorder, undernutrition, multiple pregnancy or another complication. A maternity clinician or qualified dietitian should individualize food, weight and supplement advice.

Read first

What the card can do

Reflect variety
Lists the broad groups you selected without awarding a nutrition score.
Prompt questions
Turns unselected groups and unchecked safety steps into neutral questions for care.
Respect access
Does not assume a specific African diet, income, facility, market or food supply.

What it cannot do

It cannot calculate energy, protein, iron, folate or weight-gain needs; diagnose a deficiency; treat nausea; or establish that any meal is safe for an individual.

        Use the worksheet safely

        Why is there no calorie or protein target?

        Body size, stage of pregnancy, activity, symptoms, food access and health conditions change individual needs. A generic web calculation could create false precision and is not an antenatal nutrition assessment.

        Does an unselected group mean my diet is deficient?

        No. One checkbox list cannot measure quantity, absorption, preparation, substitutions, fortified foods or a longer eating pattern. It only prepares questions.

        Can this manage a pregnancy complication?

        No. Complications and significant symptoms need individualized maternity and dietetic care. The tool does not prescribe around them.
