Fluid intake log

Add drinks consumed during one day. The tool totals millilitres and does not invent a personal target.

No clinical prescription. Fluid needs and restrictions can change with kidney or heart conditions, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medicines, fever, vomiting, diarrhoea, heat and activity. Follow advice from your own qualified care team. Seek urgent help for confusion, fainting, inability to keep fluids down, severe weakness, breathing difficulty or very little urine.

What this tool does

It adds the quantities you enter. If—and only if—you enter a target already provided by your clinician, it shows the arithmetic difference. It does not estimate a need from body weight, prescribe extra water, or tell you to ignore thirst, symptoms or fluid restrictions.

Official source, reviewed 26 July 2026: CDC: About Water and Healthier Drinks (5 March 2026), which notes that intake needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy, activity and breastfeeding and that food and other beverages contribute.

Private and local: entries are not stored, sent, placed in URLs or included in analytics.
