FAQ

Common Questions

What image formats are supported?
PNG, JPEG, and WebP inputs up to 10MB. Each resized output is encoded as PNG.
How many sizes can I generate at once?
Up to 12 sizes covering Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, Pinterest, and WhatsApp. Download all as a single ZIP.
What is the focal point feature?
Click the important part of the image. Crop-only output centres its crop around that manual point, helping preserve a face, product, or text area.
Does it compress my images?
The tool decodes the source in your browser and creates new PNG files at the listed pixel dimensions. Inspect the exported crop before publishing.
Is my image uploaded to a server?
No. Image decoding, Canvas resizing, PNG encoding, and ZIP packaging happen in your browser. The workspace does not send the file or pixels to AfroTools or AI.
What are the background fill modes?
Blur Fill, Solid Color, Gradient, Edge Extend, or Crop Only. Each handles aspect ratio mismatches differently — choose what looks best.
