Method

What this payoff estimate does

Each month, the worksheet applies one-twelfth of the annual rate to the outstanding balance, applies the entered payment first to interest and then principal, and caps the last payment to the amount actually due. This is a reducing-balance planning convention; verify the posting method on your statement. It stops after 600 months rather than pretending an impractically long path is precise.

FAQ

Kenya HELB repayment questions

How much should my employer deduct?

Use the amount in HELB's billing schedule or employer portal. HELB does not publish a universal salary percentage on its current employer FAQ.

Does this include default penalties?

No. Applied penalties and exceptions depend on the account. Enter the current balance from your statement and contact HELB about arrears.

Is 4% guaranteed for my loan?

No. It is a published undergraduate/TVET reference rate. Confirm the rate in your agreement or statement and edit the field when it differs.

Can this prove compliance or clearance?

No. HELB issues compliance certificates to eligible users and a clearance certificate after it confirms full repayment.
