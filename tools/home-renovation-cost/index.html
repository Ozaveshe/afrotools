Home Renovation Cost Calculator

Estimate renovation costs from your own quantities and contractor prices. Include fixed costs and contingency, then plan an itemized budget.

Use a dated supplier or contractor quote in one currency. This is a planning estimate; prices are not supplied by AfroTools. Boundary: no live rate, price, valuation, legal rule, title status, eligibility, approval or official integration is supplied. Empty inputs stay empty.

Estimate one renovation work item

Use matching units: square metres with a price per square metre, or a count with a price per item. Enter 0 for fixed costs or contingency when none applies.

This workflow sends no form values over the network and writes nothing to browser storage.

How renovation cost is calculated

Total = (quantity × unit cost + fixed costs) × (1 + contingency ÷ 100). The contingency applies to the item cost and fixed costs together.

For an illustrative painting job, 40 m² × 50 currency units per m² gives 2,000. Add 200 for separately quoted preparation or transport, then 10% contingency: (2,000 + 200) × 1.10 = 2,420.

These numbers demonstrate the arithmetic; they are not market prices or a recommended contingency. Replace them with measurements, scope and quotes for your project.

Build a complete renovation budget

  • Measure each room or work area and record the unit used in the quote.
  • Separate materials and labour when quoted separately; do not add labour again when the unit price already includes it.
  • Check removal, surface preparation, delivery, access, waste disposal and making good.
  • Confirm whether tax is included. Add any separately quoted fees or tax once; this worksheet does not calculate a statutory tax rate.
  • Agree what is excluded and choose a contingency for the uncertainty in your scope.

For painting, flooring, plumbing and other items with different units or prices, use an itemized budget instead of combining their quantities.

Check your quotes before committing

Rate data: none is bundled. Use your own measured quantities and dated quotes.

Confidence: high for the displayed arithmetic or checklist count; low for any legal, official, valuation, market, eligibility or approval conclusion because the tool does not make one.

Record the supplier, quote date, currency, unit, validity period and included work. Reconfirm prices when the scope or start date changes.

The result covers only what you enter. Have the contractor check site conditions and scope; obtain qualified advice for structural work and local approvals where needed.
