02 / Let’s cook

One step at a time

Keep the rhythm calm, watch the texture, and adjust seasoning at the end.

Back to Malawi
3 steps Not recorded prep Not recorded cook (check method) Not recorded total easy
1
Step 1
Combine the flour, sugar, yeast and salt. Work in warm water a little at a time until a soft dough forms. Knead until the dough releases from your hands.
2
Step 2
Cover and let rise in a warm spot for 30–40 minutes. Briefly knead again, divide into small balls and rest covered for another 10–15 minutes.
3
Step 3
Fry a test piece in hot oil, turning until golden on both sides. Open it to check the centre is cooked; adjust the heat or piece size before frying the rest in small batches. Drain before serving.
