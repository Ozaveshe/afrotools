5 steps 20 min prep 90 min cook 110 min total medium
1
Tenderize
Simmer raw octopus in boiling water until tender, approximately 30–40 minutes; extend as needed. Drain, cool enough to handle, then cut into pieces.
2
Aromatics
Heat oil over medium heat. Soften and lightly brown onion. Add garlic, ginger and optional curry leaves; stir 2–3 minutes.
3
Spices
Stir in curry powder, turmeric, cinnamon and optional paprika. Add chilli; cook one minute.
4
Curry
Add octopus, then coconut milk and measured water. Simmer gently 20–25 minutes.
5
Finish
Season with salt, pepper and lime. Simmer five minutes. Serve hot; this adaptation omits the source's coriander garnish.
