02 / Let’s cook

One step at a time

Keep the rhythm calm, watch the texture, and adjust seasoning at the end.

Back to Seychelles
6 steps 20 min prep About 90 min cook (check method) Not recorded total medium
1
Preboil the raw octopus
Start with cleaned raw octopus for this adaptation. Boil until tender; the source gives an approximate 30–40 minutes. Check tenderness from about 30 minutes and continue as needed, then cut into bite-size pieces. If using already boiled octopus, do not automatically repeat the raw preboil.
Check octopus tenderness; continue as needed 30:00
2
Cook the aromatics
Heat the oil with the cinnamon sticks. Add onion, garlic, ginger and the optional curry leaves; cook until fragrant.
3
Add spices
Stir in curry powder, turmeric, optional paprika and chilli, coating the aromatics.
4
Simmer with coconut milk
Add the octopus, coconut milk and the selected half cup of water. Simmer approximately 20–25 minutes as described by the source, checking the texture and continuing as needed.
Check the curry; continue as needed 20:00
5
Finish seasoning
Season with salt, black pepper and lime. The source calls for a further approximately 5-minute simmer after seasoning. This adaptation omits the cilantro garnish.
Check final seasoning and texture 05:00
6
Serve the attributed adaptation
Serve the curry. Four servings and the 20-minute preparation/90-minute cooking figures are the author’s estimates, not guaranteed completion or a separately established total duration.
