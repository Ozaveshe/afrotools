Rwandan Ikivuguto

Rwanda

Rwandan ikivuguto is a fermented milk drink. Find out what to ask for at a milk bar and how much ready-to-drink ikivuguto to buy for your table.

Ordering at a Kigali milk bar

Ask for ikivuguto for fermented milk. Some Kigali milk bars also sell bread or mandazi, a fried dough snack, alongside the drink.

Serving packaged ikivuguto

Choose ready-to-drink ikivuguto made with pasteurized milk. Check the use-by date and follow the pack’s instructions for storage and use after opening. Pour into clean cups just before serving. Contains milk.

Multiply your chosen pour by the number of guests. Four 200 mL cups use 800 mL and six use 1.2 litres. Adjust the pour to suit your cups.

  • Rwanda Standards Board identifies ikivuguto as fermented milk.
  • The Rwanda Basic Education Board lesson on traditional foods includes ikivuguto among drinks made from milk.
  • Inka Tours’ local guide to Kigali milk bars describes the drinks and snacks served there.

Preparation method under source review

This guide uses finished, packaged ikivuguto. A source-reviewed method for fermenting it at home is still pending.

Contains dairy: this is a dairy-milk drink; it is not dairy-free.
