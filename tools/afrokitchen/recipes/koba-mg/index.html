5 steps 15 min prep 25 min cook 40 min total medium
1
Mix
Combine mashed banana, vanilla seeds, peanuts, sugar and rice flour.
2
Prepare leaves
Wash leaves, cut into squares, soften briefly in hot water, then drain.
3
Wrap
Put about 3 tablespoons of mixture in each leaf. Fold closed and secure with food-safe kitchen twine.
4
Steam
Steam covered for about 20–25 minutes, until set. Keep water below the basket and replenish as needed; larger parcels may take longer.
Check texture; approximate steaming time 20:00
5
Unwrap
Cool enough to handle safely, remove twine and leaves, and serve warm.

Small steamed parcels; not a large, long-cooked roll.

Chef watch-outs
  • Adding too much liquid after the rice goes in.
  • Stirring too often once the grains should be steaming.
  • Rushing the base before the raw edge has cooked out.
How you know it is ready
  • The grains should be tender but still distinct, with steam carrying the seasoning upward.
  • The aroma should smell rounded rather than raw or sharp.
  • Oil, sauce, broth, or steam should look settled and deliberate.
