02 / Let’s cook

One step at a time

Keep the rhythm calm, watch the texture, and adjust seasoning at the end.

Back to Zambia
6 steps Cook 20–25 minCool 35–40 min; check 40°CFermentation varies with warmth (see method) medium
1
Make the maize slurry
Bring 900 ml water to a boil. Separately whisk the maize meal with 200 ml water; stir this paste into the pot.
2
Cook the porridge
Turn the heat down to medium. Cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring now and then.
Check the cooking stage at 20 minutes 20:00
3
Cool and check the temperature
Switch off the heat. Stir occasionally for 35–40 minutes and use a thermometer to check that the porridge has cooled to 40°C.
Check cooling temperature; target 40°C 35:00
4
Sweeten and cover
Dissolve 50 g sugar in the remaining 150 ml water. Stir into the porridge, transfer to a clean food container and use a vented lid.
5
Allow fermentation
Leave the covered container in a cupboard. The authors observed 96 hours in cold weather and described about 24 hours in warm weather; conditions change fermentation time.
6
Serve chilled
Chill the fermented drink before serving.

Regional South African method; Zambia-specific household variations are not documented here.
