02 / Let’s cook

One step at a time

5 steps Overnight fermentation1–2 min per sheet medium
1
Mix the batter
Whisk 250 g sorghum flour with 500 ml water. Add more water gradually, up to 750 ml total, for a thin pouring batter. Stir in 3–4 tbsp plain yoghurt.
2
Ferment overnight
Cover the bowl and leave overnight in a cool, dry place. Do not taste raw batter or keep batter as starter for another batch.
3
Heat the saaj
Heat a saaj or flat griddle and wipe with a little oil.
4
Spread a thin sheet
Pour about half a cup of batter onto the hot surface. Quickly spread it very thin with a crepe spreader.
5
Cook and stack
Cook on one side for about 1–2 minutes, until the sheet is set and lifts off. Remove, stack and repeat with the remaining batter. Serve with stew.
