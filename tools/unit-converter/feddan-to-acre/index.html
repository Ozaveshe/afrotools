How to convert feddans into acres

Multiply the number of feddans by the square metres in one feddan, then divide by 4,046.8564224, the square metres in one international acre.

Acres = feddans × square metres per feddan ÷ 4,046.8564224.

For example, 10 feddans on the 4,200 m² basis cover 42,000 m²: 42,000 ÷ 4,046.8564224 ≈ 10.378426 acres. To convert back, multiply acres by 4,046.8564224 and divide by your feddan basis.

Which feddan definition should I use?

The default here follows the 4,200 m² reference in FAO’s published Egypt national report. It is a stated calculation assumption, not a determination of the area or legal boundaries of a particular property. Use the measurement stated in your survey or land document when it differs.

The full unit converter also includes a 4,200.83 m² Egyptian preset. Enter 4200.83 above if that is the basis specified in your document; do not mix results calculated on different bases.

Is a feddan the same as an acre?

No. On the default basis, one feddan is about 3.78% larger than one international acre. Neither unit describes the shape of a plot: the same area can have many different lengths and widths.

Sources and calculation notes
