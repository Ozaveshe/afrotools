Frequently Asked Questions

How often does AfroTools update its regulatory data?
This page reads a public feed when connected. An empty or unavailable feed does not prove no changes have occurred. Check each original source and effective date; the page does not establish complete country coverage.
What types of regulatory changes are tracked?
The initial editorial list contains one historical South African minimum-wage event. Filters support other change categories, but available filters do not establish records or coverage.
What happens after I report a change?
A server receipt confirms your report was received for private review. A reviewer must verify the official source; receipt does not confirm the regulation, publish the report or update a calculator. No review deadline or points award is promised.
How is notification delivery handled?
This page lists selected historical events from official sources. Subscription controls are a preview; this feed does not send notifications.
Which related tools can I open from a record?
Record links point to related tools for reference. A feed record does not update or certify a calculator; check each tool's own source and effective-date information.
