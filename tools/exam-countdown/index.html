How it works — methodology & sources

Source cards checked 26 July 2026. Here is how calendar-day counting, local storage, and official-source verification work.

Methodology & assumptions
The engine validates a date in YYYY-MM-DD form, converts the target and the current local date into calendar-day values, and compares them. This avoids daylight-saving and clock-time drift in a date-only planning tool. Today is shown as Today; past dates remain visible so you can replace them. Planning stages are simple workflow prompts, not predictions of readiness. Entries remain in the current session unless you explicitly select Remember on this device, which stores them only in this browser.
Limitations & what to verify
This is a planning worksheet, not an official timetable or registration service. A published timetable opening can differ from a candidate's first oral, practical, or written paper. The UNEB card is specifically a registration deadline. Before acting, confirm the candidate's exact paper date, time, venue, subject entry, permitted materials, and registration status with the linked authority or school.
Sources & references
Dated cards link to the official UNEB notice, ECZ pages or PDF, NECTA final CSEE PDF, and South African DBE NSC PDF. WAEC Ghana, KNEC, and ZIMSEC cards require you to choose the date that applies from the relevant official timetable. The expired ECZ GCE 2026 window is not offered as a future countdown. Frequently Asked Questions

What do the source cards count down to?
Every card states its exact meaning: an examination-window opening, a published-timetable opening, a registration deadline, or a date you must choose from the official timetable. It does not imply that one national date is every candidate's first paper.
Does the planner save automatically?
No. Entries stay in the current browser session unless you select Remember on this device. That option uses local browser storage only; entries are not sent to AfroTools and can be cleared from the page.
Why are there no ticking seconds?
Official timetable dates are date-only planning anchors. Calendar-day counting is calmer and less misleading than implying a paper begins at midnight. Confirm the exact start time separately.
What is included when I share?
Sharing sends only the AfroTools route and a generic description of the planner. It does not include the countdown name, date, country, or remaining days.
Should I rely on a source card without checking it?
No. Open the linked official authority or PDF and confirm the candidate's paper date, time, venue, subject entry, permitted materials, and registration status before acting.
