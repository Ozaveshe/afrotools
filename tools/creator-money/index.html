What the planner calculates

The deterministic engine uses only the amounts, hours and percentages you enter. It does not connect to a bank or infer national tax rules.

  • Monthly profit and margin.
  • Effective profit per hour worked.
  • Tax reserve based on your own assumption.
  • Creator pay, reinvestment and remaining cash.
  • Structured JSON and TXT exports.
