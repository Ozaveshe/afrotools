Independent checks before any decision

Verify the professional’s licence and any facility accreditation directly with the issuing regulator; a logo or broker claim is not proof. Obtain a written treatment plan, exclusions, complication policy, aftercare plan, records-transfer process and refund terms. Ask your own qualified clinician whether and when travel may be appropriate. Arrange emergency and follow-up care at home.

This calculator does not confirm accreditation, quality, travel fitness, visa eligibility, insurance coverage or clinical outcomes. All details stay on this page and are not stored or transmitted.
