    Honest preparation boundaries
    • Ask the recruiter about format, accessibility, attire or required materials when unclear.
    • Use official employer sources and verify dated claims.
    • Do not disclose former clients, protected data or confidential metrics.
    • Separate your actions from the team’s work.
    • Do not treat a checklist count as a hiring probability.
    STAR structure
    Situation: only the context needed.
    Task: your responsibility or objective.
    Action: what you personally did and why.
    Result: an honest outcome, evidence and lesson.
    
  
    

  
    Method and limitations
    User-driven, not stereotype-driven
    The tool does not select advice from country, gender, age, company type or seniority. Employer instructions, accessibility needs, role requirements and the user’s own evidence are the relevant inputs.
    No generic question or hiring prediction
    Interviewers may change format, questions and criteria. The question list contains only prompts the user enters. Completing the pack cannot predict shortlisting, an offer or interview performance.
    Privacy and evidence safety
    Entries stay in the current browser page. Exports happen only when requested. Redact personal identifiers, client names, internal financial data, security information and confidential documents before entering or exporting content.
    
  
    Interview preparation questions
    What should I wear?
    Follow written employer instructions. If none are provided and attire affects your participation, ask the recruiter. Choose something safe, accessible, comfortable and appropriate for the stated setting rather than relying on country stereotypes.
    How many STAR stories should I prepare?
    There is no universal number. Build enough distinct, truthful examples to cover the requirements you identified, and avoid forcing one story to answer every prompt.
    Does a completed checklist mean I am ready?
    No. It only records completed preparation actions. Rest, accessibility, interview conditions, role fit and employer decisions are outside the count.
