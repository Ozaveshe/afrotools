FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the fee data come from?
The public feed may be empty or unavailable. When records exist, each row identifies its contributor source, proof link if supplied, and moderation state. Your manual quotes come from the schedules you enter.
Is a published fee a current school quote?
No. Confirm the latest fee schedule, included costs, deadlines and refund rules directly with the school.
How does the quick school-fee check work?
It adds annual tuition and annual extras, divides the total by 12, and compares that reserve with the monthly support entered using disclosed planning bands.
Does the quick check upload or save my numbers?
No. The quick check runs in the browser and saves only when you explicitly choose Save on this device.
What costs should be included?
Include tuition plus known registration, books, uniforms, transport, meals, levies and other recurring extras.
Can parents, students or schools contribute?
Signed-in contributors can submit a fee record. Public proof should be a safe, public or redacted HTTPS link and must not expose private student information.
