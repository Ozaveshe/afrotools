Private feeding and nappy log

Record breastfeeding, expressed-milk and nappy events in this open page. The log cannot measure milk transfer, diagnose low supply or decide whether a baby is feeding adequately.

Session onlyNo adequacy scoreLocal export

A log must never delay help

Seek urgent local medical help if a baby is not feeding normally and you are worried, cannot feed or has stopped feeding well, is hard to wake, has difficult breathing, has blue/pale/grey skin, has a seizure, or has nappies much drier than usual. Get prompt professional advice for poor weight gain or feeding concerns. For the breastfeeding parent, seek care for a hot, swollen, painful breast with fever or flu-like illness, or if symptoms worsen.

    Use a record, not a verdict

    Can the log show whether a baby gets enough milk?

    No. A log can be incomplete and cannot assess attachment, swallowing, weight trend, urine output in clinical context or the baby. Ask a qualified professional early when concerned.

    Does a short feed mean something is wrong?

    Not by itself. Duration varies and does not measure transfer. The route deliberately does not label a feed normal or abnormal.

    Where is the log stored?

    Only in memory in the open page. It disappears on refresh or close unless you explicitly download a local file.
