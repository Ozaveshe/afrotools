Frequently Asked Questions

Does every institution use the same formula?

No. Use only the formula and weights published by your chosen institution for the current admission cycle.

Does a result above a benchmark guarantee admission?

No. Programme requirements, valid credentials, screening, available places, and the official admission process still apply.

Where should I check programme eligibility?

Use JAMB IBASS for programme, O-Level, and UTME subject requirements, then check the institution's official admission portal.
