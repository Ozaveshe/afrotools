Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a complete isiZulu dictionary?

No. It is a fixed table of 132 draft phrase rows in 15 categories, with no claim of comprehensive vocabulary coverage.

Can the written click cues teach pronunciation?

No. The c, q and x orientation is only a written reminder. It cannot convey all click, voicing, aspiration, nasalisation, timing or connected-speech distinctions.

Does device speech prove pronunciation?

No. Voice quality and regional behaviour depend on the installed browser or operating-system voice. If no isiZulu voice is reported, this tool deliberately does not play an unrelated fallback voice.

Does this phrasebook teach isiZulu tone?

No. The displayed pronunciation cues use approximate Latin stress hints and do not encode lexical or grammatical tone. Use fluent-speaker audio and feedback when pronunciation matters.
