Frequently Asked Questions

Are festival dates official?

No. Confirm every date and access rule with the relevant organiser or authority.

Why can a festival date move?

Local calendars, organiser decisions, weather and public arrangements can change it.

What should visitors verify?

Verify date, venue, access, dress, photography, transport and accommodation.
