Privacy

Nothing is remembered

The engine runs locally. The page does not save dates to browser storage, accounts, URLs or analytics. TXT and PDF files are created only after you request them and contain the entered date, so handle them as sensitive health information.

Important limits

Why not calculate from “conception date”?

Outside documented assisted reproduction, the exact fertilization date is usually uncertain. This route avoids presenting an assumed date as known.

Can a scan change this estimate?

Yes. A maternity professional can compare menstrual history and early ultrasound dating. Use the EDD documented by your care team for clinical decisions.

Does the 42-week date mean it is safe to wait until then?

No. It is only a calendar boundary. Follow your maternity team's monitoring and urgent-care instructions.
