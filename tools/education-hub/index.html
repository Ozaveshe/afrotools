How it works — methodology & sources

Updated for 2026. Here is how the dashboard organises saved details and what to verify before you act.

Methodology & how this hub is compiled
Education Hub is an organiser, not a calculator or adviser. It reads education details and lists previously saved through AfroTools on this device; signed-in users may also retrieve their saved account profile. Profile completion counts filled optional fields. Scholarship overlap counts compare those fields with fields published in the AfroTools scholarship feed; they are not eligibility or award predictions. Dates and saved objects are ordered for convenience. The Africa Exam Radar links to official board sources and planning tools.
Limitations & what to verify
This is an informational planning workspace only — not an official admission decision, scholarship award, visa result, fee invoice, school record or readiness assessment. Deadlines, requirements, exam dates and fees change. Verify them with the relevant official body before applying, paying or submitting documents.
Sources & references
Exam windows link to official examination boards, including KNEC Kenya ↗, WAEC Ghana ↗, NECTA Tanzania ↗, and ECZ Zambia ↗. Scholarship records come from the AfroTools scholarship feed. Confirm the current timetable, criteria and rules at the linked official source. Page reviewed 2026.
