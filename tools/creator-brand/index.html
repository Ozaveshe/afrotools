Questions

Does CreatorBrand send my mission or audience to AI?

No. It has no AI action. The sample posts are built locally from the values in the form.

Does an exported guide include uploaded logos?

No logo upload is advertised in this version. The guide contains text, colors, typography choices and voice examples.

Is the contrast result official certification?

No. It applies the standard WCAG relative-luminance formula to the chosen text and primary colors; review the full design separately.
