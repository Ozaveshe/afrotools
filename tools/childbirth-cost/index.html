Cost planning must not delay needed maternity care

Ask the clinical team what care is needed; ask the provider and payer what it costs. A budget cannot decide a delivery mode, show that a facility is safe or replace emergency planning. Seek urgent maternity care when advised or when warning signs occur, even if a quote is incomplete.

Before relying on a quote

Ask what is included

  • Facility, professional, theatre and anaesthesia fees.
  • Medicines, supplies, blood, laboratory tests and imaging.
  • Extra stay, unexpected procedure, transfer or higher level of care.
  • Newborn assessment, treatment, admission and postnatal follow-up.
  • Payer authorization, facility network, limits, exclusions and co-payments.
  • Quote validity date, refund policy and who can update the figure.

Get written confirmation where possible. A zero field means “nothing entered,” not “free.”
