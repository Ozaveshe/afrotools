Method and limits

Does “near me” mean a nearby station price?

No. The finder selects the closest maintained market reference on this device. Each result says whether it is a city reference or national benchmark. AfroTools does not claim station availability or the cheapest pump.

What happens to my location?

Your browser supplies coordinates only after you press “Use my location” and grant permission. The page compares them locally with maintained market coordinates. Raw coordinates are not transmitted, stored, logged, placed in analytics or added to the URL.

Why can a market show “price unavailable”?

A price is unavailable only when required numeric values, sources or dates are invalid. Older and expired records remain visible as historical references: choose them explicitly for a labelled planning estimate, or enter a locally verified pump price.
