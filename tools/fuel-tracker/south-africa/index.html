    How to use these South Africa fuel prices

    AfroFuel shows South Africa prices in local currency first because households, drivers, shops, and procurement teams usually pay locally. The USD values are secondary comparison figures for checking South Africa against the wider African table.

    In this snapshot, South Africa petrol is 7% above the Southern Africa average. Prices may still differ between Johannesburg, Cape Town, rural stations, bulk suppliers, and border corridors.

    This snapshot is dated Mar 1, 2026 and does not establish today’s pump price. Prices may vary by city, station, supplier, and timing. Use this as a planning estimate and verify locally before buying fuel or quoting transport.
