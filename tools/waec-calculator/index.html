Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Nigeria best-five number official?
No. It is a planning index that organises the five lowest point values you entered. JAMB and each institution decide which subjects, sittings and combinations count.
How is the Ghana planning aggregate calculated?
It adds English, Core Mathematics, Integrated Science for a science programme or Social Studies for a non-science programme, and the best three entered electives. Confirm the exact rule for your programme.
Does this page decide university eligibility?
No. For Nigeria, use JAMB IBASS and the institution portal. For Ghana, use the institution's current programme requirements. This page prepares a result summary for those checks.
Can O-Level grades be converted to university GPA?
Not reliably. GPA systems measure university coursework and vary by institution. The grade reference here only explains the planning points used on this page.
