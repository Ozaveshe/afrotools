What this check can say

Observable: total and unique words, sentences, paragraphs, average sentence length, exact repeated word sequences, exact repeated sentences, and sentences over 35 words.

  • Case and punctuation are ignored for repetition comparison.
  • Phrases do not cross sentence boundaries.
  • Exact matches can be intentional terminology or necessary wording.
  • Lexical variety is descriptive; it is not an originality measure.

Not detectable here: plagiarism, uncited ideas, close paraphrasing, matching publications, AI authorship, writing quality or misconduct. Use your institution’s approved process for submission decisions.

Why repetition cannot become an originality score

Plagiarism is about attribution and source use, not simply repeated wording. A draft may repeat technical terms and still be properly cited; another may paraphrase a source closely without repeating an exact phrase. Without a relevant corpus and academic context, a percentage would create false certainty.

Use these counts as editing prompts. Review whether repeated phrases are necessary, whether a long sentence should be split, and whether borrowed ideas are quoted or cited according to your institution’s rules. A clean local repetition report is not proof that a draft is original.

Frequently asked questions

Does a repeated phrase mean plagiarism?

No. It means the same normalized word sequence occurs more than once inside this draft. It may be intentional, unavoidable or worth revising; the tool cannot decide which.

Does lexical variety measure originality?

No. It is the share of distinct normalized words in the draft. Length, topic and terminology affect it, so it is presented only as a descriptive writing statistic.

Can this compare my essay with the internet or Turnitin?

No. The page has no external corpus or institutional database. Use an approved licensed service when your school requires source comparison.

Will exports contain my essay?

Never the full essay. By default, exports contain counts only. Exact repeated phrase and sentence examples are included only when you explicitly select the include-examples checkbox.
