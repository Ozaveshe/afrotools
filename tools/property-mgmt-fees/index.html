Nigeria

NIESV's published scale proposes 10% of gross rent for property management and 10% for letting; FIRS guidance states 7.5% VAT for taxable supplies. Confirm the exact engagement, invoice and current tax treatment.

NIESV professional charges · FIRS VAT clarification

Kenya

Current local agency guides commonly publish 8–12% for long-let management, while KRA publishes a 16% general VAT rate. EARB says only currently registered and licensed agents may practise. Renewal and letting fees remain quote-specific.

Goldstay fee guide · KRA VAT · EARB register guidance

South Africa

One current agency schedule publishes 12% for managed leases, 6% for unmanaged leases and 3% on a year-two renewal, all exclusive of VAT. SARS publishes a 15% standard VAT rate; PPRA provides practitioner certificate verification.

Fine & Country fee schedule · SARS VAT · PPRA certificate verification

Ghana

Section 25 of the Rent Act caps an agent or broker's consideration for procuring a grant, renewal, continuance or assignment of a tenancy at 5% of one year's recoverable rent. GRA says taxable supplies carry an effective 20% stack from 1 January 2026 (15% VAT plus 2.5% NHIL and 2.5% GETFund). Confirm scope before applying either figure.

Ghana Rent Act, 1963 · GRA E-VAT
