Frequently Asked Questions

How can I compare delivery budgets in Nairobi?
Compare the sample base and distance charges, then request a quote for the actual pickup, destination and package.
How are the sample figures calculated?
Each plan uses a base fee plus distance multiplied by a per-kilometre cost. The urgency multiplier applies to the total. Weight determines the displayed cost per kilogram.
What should I budget for cross-border delivery?
Request separate amounts for customs clearance, duties, waiting time and border fees. These items are outside the local delivery example.
