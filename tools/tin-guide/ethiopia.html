🇪🇹 Ethiopia TIN Registration Guide

Find Ethiopia’s taxpayer registration forms for individuals and organisations, diaspora guidance and the Ministry’s existing-TIN search.

🇪🇹 Ethiopia💰 Free⌛ 1-3 business days

Individual or sole proprietor

Use the individual taxpayer application. A sole proprietor attends the responsible revenue office in person for biometric registration. Confirm the documents and office for your address before visiting.

Get the individual registration form (PDF)

Company or organisation

Use the organisation application. A private limited company or share company is represented by its manager, whose biometrics are required. Contact the responsible tax office to arrange registration.

Get the organisation registration form (PDF)

Applying from abroad

Ethiopian diaspora members can consult the Diaspora Service for help obtaining a TIN. It lists a passport or ID, the application form and business documents when applicable. Other foreign applicants should confirm their route with the responsible revenue office.

Read the diaspora TIN guidance

Verify an existing TIN

The Ministry’s Verification Search accepts an existing TIN or subTIN to look up taxpayer information and receipt orders.

Open the Ministry’s TIN search

Find a replacement form or get help

On the Ministry website choose “Tax Payers Registration Forms” for individual, organisation and information-update forms. The Ministry lists 8199 as its toll-free number.
