🇹🇿 Tanzania TIN Registration Guide

Choose the TRA registration route for your identity documents and activity.

Open TRA’s registration menu

Open the TRA taxpayer portal. On a phone, open the menu at the top, then select “TIN REGISTRATION”. On the next screen choose an application type.

Open the TRA taxpayer portal

Individual applying with or without a NIN

Choose “TIN Application with NIN” if applying with a National Identification Number. Residents without a NIN can choose “TIN Application without NIN”, select Business or Non Business, and prepare a recognized identity document and valid mobile number. TRA requires an office visit for biometric enrolment on the without-NIN route.

Company or other entity

Choose “Entity TIN Application”. For an incorporated company, prepare the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, and evidence of the project location. TISEZA also lists shareholder identity documents. Check TRA’s instructions for your entity.

Check TISEZA’s company document list

Foreign principal or electronic service provider

TRA offers separate “Non Resident (Foreign Principal) TIN Application” and “Digital (Electronic Service Provider) TIN Application” options. Select the category that describes the activity.

Existing TIN or pending application

Choose “Taxpayer Portal Account Application using Existing TIN” if you already have a TIN and need portal access. To follow an application already submitted, use “Check TIN Application Status” on the registration screen.
