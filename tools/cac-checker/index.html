CAC Name Rules

Must not:

  • Be identical to an existing registered name
  • Be misleadingly similar to another company
  • Use restricted words without approval (Federal, National, Bank, Insurance, etc.)
  • Contain offensive or obscene words
  • Imply government connection without approval

Must include:

  • "Limited" or "Ltd" for private companies
  • "PLC" for public companies
  • No suffix needed for business names

Registration Steps

  • 1. Check name availability on CAC portal
  • 2. Reserve only after official confirmation; check the actual expiry (CAMA permits up to 60 days)
  • 3. Complete registration forms
  • 4. Upload MeMArt / Articles
  • 5. Pay fees online
  • 6. Follow your official application status; queries and document requirements affect processing

Required Documents

  • Valid ID (NIN, Int'l Passport, Voter's Card)
  • Passport photographs
  • Proof of address (utility bill)
  • Memorandum & Articles (for Ltd/PLC)
  • TIN (or apply alongside)

Registering a Business in Nigeria with CAC

CAC handles company and other entity registration under CAMA. Business-name registration has statutory conditions and exceptions, including the naming conditions in section 814; this checker does not decide which registration duties apply to your circumstances.

The first step in registering any business is conducting a name availability search. CAC will reject names that are identical or misleadingly similar to existing registered names. Certain words are restricted and require special approval: "Federal," "National," "Municipal," "Bank," "Insurance," "Assurance," "Building Society," and words implying government patronage. Industry-specific words like "Pharmacy," "University," or "Microfinance" may require sectoral approvals before CAC registration.

Use the official application workflow to establish the required documents, name reservation and registration status. A local wording check is not a registry search, reserved name or filing approval. Processing can depend on entity type, complete documents, raised queries and portal availability; this checker gives no guaranteed completion time.

Check Tax ID and applicable tax duties with the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS). Under the Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025, effective 1 January 2026, the small-business VAT framework considers annual gross turnover of at most ₦100 million and fixed assets below ₦250 million, excludes professional-service businesses and includes opt-in and other statutory scope rules. This is not a turnover-only test, and this name checker does not assess your VAT status. Confirm the applicable rules with NRS, CAC annual-return duties and your sector regulator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does CAC registration take?

Check the current service target and application status in the official portal. Targets depend on entity type, accepted documents and raised queries; this checker cannot promise a completion time or that an agent will make approval faster. Obtain any agent quote separately.

Can foreigners register a business in Nigeria?

Foreign participation can involve entity, sector, capital, business-permit and other requirements. This checker does not establish the current capital minimum, fees or eligibility. Confirm the current requirements with CAC and the relevant authorities, and use the Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission for investment guidance.

Business-name verification preparation

A business-name checker should do more than tell users to search. It should help them prepare alternatives, avoid prohibited words, and connect name availability to CAC filing and trademark risk.

Decisions this clarifies

  • Which local naming flags need official CAC or trademark verification
  • Whether regulated words need prior approval or sector evidence
  • Whether a similar domain, social handle or trademark creates brand conflict

Before you rely on it

  • Prepare alternative name drafts before the official CAC search
  • Check spelling, restricted words, existing company names and trademark conflicts
  • Move quickly from availability to reservation because name status can change

Red flags

  • Using a name because the domain is free while CAC or trademark search fails
  • Restricted words such as bank, insurance, university or government without approval
  • Letting an agent reserve a name under an account you cannot access
