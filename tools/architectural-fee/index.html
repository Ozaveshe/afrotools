How to use this estimate

  • Compare the range with a written appointment or quote; do not treat it as a mandatory scale.
  • Confirm every deliverable, revision allowance, approval, site visit, structural/QS/M&E service, tax, and disbursement.
  • Verify the professional is registered in the project country and for the relevant work category.
  • Keep JSON as the reopenable backup; PDF, CSV, and TXT are planning copies.

Common questions

Does the country select a fee rate?

No. Country supplies only a label and currency hint. Every rate comes from your inputs.

Is this an official quote or approval?

No. It is a planning estimate. The professional and local authority confirm registration, scope, fees, tax, and approvals.

Does my project data go to a server?

No. Calculation, draft storage, and exports stay in this browser.
