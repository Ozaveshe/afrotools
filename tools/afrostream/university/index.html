Do I need expensive equipment?

Start with what you have. The setup and mobile-workflow lessons help you plan an audio test, usable light, a stable connection and a backup before you buy anything.

Does this guarantee income?

No. The business lessons help you prepare an offer and understand the work involved. Platform eligibility, demand, rates and results vary.
