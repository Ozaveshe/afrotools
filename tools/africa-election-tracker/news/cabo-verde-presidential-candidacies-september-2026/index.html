Cabo Verde court admits five presidential candidacies; appeals were available

AfroTools civic desk27 September 2026Source-reviewed

The electoral commission's 22 September notice reports an admissibility decision for the 15 November presidential election. It is not a final post-appeal candidate roster.

Cabo Verde's National Electoral Commission reported on 22 September 2026 that the Constitutional Court had ruled on the admissibility of candidacies for the presidential election scheduled for 15 November. The court's order was dated 21 September.

According to the commission's notice, five candidacies were admitted, five were rejected, and one withdrawal was accepted. The notice says rejected applicants had 24 hours from notification to appeal to the full Constitutional Court.

This is a report of the admissibility decision, not a final post-appeal roster. Readers should check later CNE or court notices for any appeal decisions before treating the list as settled. AfroTools does not report an election outcome from this notice.
