What the labels mean

An official source does not always mean the date is final. Check both the source label and date-status label on every record.

  1. Election authorities come first for dates and candidate lists.
  2. Tentative dates remain marked until a dated notice confirms them.
  3. Results are not inferred from uploads or secondary reports.
