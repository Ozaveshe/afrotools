Coverage and editorial goals

Our mission is to make deep, source-linked election updates across Africa accessible every day. Daily review is the goal; publication depends on verified developments. The date of an article is always shown.

Country coverage is limited to the published ledger. A missing update does not mean nothing happened.

  • Daily: review active election sources and report what changed, why it matters and what happens next.
  • Depth: build country timelines, electoral-rule explainers, candidate evidence, and official results with their dispute status.
  • Reach: expand to all 54 African countries, with visible gaps until each country has sourced coverage.
  • Trust: keep source-check dates, corrections and failed checks visible; never infer winners from polling dates.

South Africa: Local councils

Official sourceDate: Official

Municipalities / Southern Africa / local government

The 4 November 2026 election remains formally proclaimed. On 16 September the IEC certified 136,790 candidates for 10,526 council seats after statutory review and said that 5,096 nominees had been disqualified for specified administrative or voter-registration requirements. Its 21 September notice opened special-vote applications, with a deadline of 17:00 on 12 October. Its 23 September keynote records the Electoral Code of Conduct signing and ballot-paper draw ceremony and says ballot printing would begin after the draw.
