South Africa: Local councils

Official sourceDate: Official

Municipalities / Southern Africa / local government

The 4 November 2026 election remains formally proclaimed. On 16 September the IEC certified 136,790 candidates for 10,526 council seats after statutory review and said that 5,096 nominees had been disqualified for specified administrative or voter-registration requirements. Its 21 September notice opened special-vote applications, with a deadline of 17:00 on 12 October. Its 23 September keynote records the Electoral Code of Conduct signing and ballot-paper draw ceremony and says ballot printing would begin after the draw.

Cabo Verde: President

Official sourceDate: Official

National / West Africa / presidential

Cabo Verde's CNE confirms the 15 November 2026 presidential election. Its 22 September notice reported five candidacies admitted by a 21 September Constitutional Court decision and an appeal window. On 27 September the CNE reported that Constitutional Court ruling 80/2026, dated 24 September, admitted Loisito Tavares de Sousa after his appeal, bringing the number of admitted candidacies to six. This is not presented as a final ballot order or election outcome.
