Frequently Asked Questions

How does this GPA worksheet calculate a result?
It multiplies each valid course grade point or score by its credits, adds those products, and divides by total credits.
What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?
GPA (Grade Point Average) is for a single semester. CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is your weighted average across all semesters, calculated using total grade points divided by total credit units from every semester.
Are the example grade tables official?
No. They are planning choices, not national or institution-wide rules. Check your current transcript, handbook, registrar, or official regulations.
Does proportional scale normalization convert my credential?
No. It only shows the same relative position on another numerical maximum. An institution or credential evaluator may use a different method.
Does the worksheet determine my degree classification?
No. Classification, rounding, failed-course, repeated-course, and credit rules vary, so the worksheet leaves the official decision to the institution.
Does a GPA result prove scholarship eligibility?
No. Scholarships can consider degree class, country, programme, experience, language, documents, dates, and other conditions.
Can I import transcript data?
Yes. Paste synthetic or redacted text, or import a CSV with course, credits, and grade point or score columns. Processing stays in the browser.
Is my academic data uploaded or automatically saved?
No. Core calculation and imports stay in the browser. Saving the action pack requires an explicit local-save action.
How does the target-average planner work?
It solves the weighted-average formula for the average needed across the upcoming credits you enter.
How does grade-replacement planning work?
It models a complete replacement of one course contribution. Confirm whether your institution replaces, averages, caps, or retains both attempts.
