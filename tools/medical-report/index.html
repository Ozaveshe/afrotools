    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is my medical data safe?
    Parsing, image OCR, PDF text extraction, and the first interpretation run in your browser with bundled assets. Optional AI is never contacted automatically; it requires the separate checkbox and button, and its local preview shows the bounded fields before any request.
    Can I upload a photo of my lab report?
    Yes. JPG, PNG, and WebP images up to 10 MB can be read locally with bundled English OCR. OCR can misread decimals, units, marker names, and ranges, so compare every parsed result with the original report.
    Is this a medical diagnosis?
    No. This tool is for educational purposes only. It helps you understand what your lab results mean in plain language, but it is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider about your results.
    What tests are supported?
    The deterministic parser recognizes a curated set of common numeric markers across CBC, lipids, liver, kidney, thyroid, glucose, iron, vitamins, cardiac markers, and urinalysis. Qualitative results and unrecognized markers are left for clinician review.
    Which reference range is used?
    The parser prioritizes a numeric range printed on the same report line as the result. If none is recognized, the marker is clearly labelled with a built-in general fallback. Always verify the unit and range against the original report and discuss the result with a qualified clinician.

    • Use verified lab reports or confirmed genotype/blood group records
    • Write down questions before acting on the result

    Do Not Ignore

    • Symptoms that do not match a reassuring calculator output
    • Marriage, pregnancy, or transfusion decisions without professional counselling
