Botswana car import quote worksheet

Budget for a private passenger car you plan to keep and drive in Botswana. Use your seller, carrier and clearing quotes to see the border cost and the total to get on the road.

Your quotes in Botswana pula (BWP)

Convert foreign quotes to pula using your provider’s rate, including any exchange fee. Enter a charge once. If your purchase price includes freight or insurance, enter 0 for those items. Leave an unknown charge blank. Enter 0 for a confirmed zero charge.

What to request from your clearing agent

Ask for an itemised assessment for your vehicle and intended clearance date showing the accepted customs value, tariff heading, duty, other levies and import VAT. Customs value can differ from your purchase price. Enter the purchase price as your cash cost and use the assessed amounts for customs charges.

Tell the agent the country of origin and whether the car has already cleared customs within the Southern African Customs Union. A transit port alone does not establish duty treatment. If relocating, ask BURS to confirm any personal vehicle rebate before entering a reduced charge.

Have your invoice or valuation, vehicle details, export clearance papers and police clearance documents ready. Confirm current documentation and the treatment of your shipment with BURS.
