    Frequently asked questions

    How do I estimate the landed cost of importing a car into Nigeria?

    Enter the purchase price, FOB or CIF value, or a make, model and year. The calculator estimates the customs value, uses the dated NTIP reference for supported used-petrol HS codes or your entered full customs assessment, then adds practical clearing, port, inland delivery and registration costs to show a real on-road budget.

    What charges are included in the Nigeria car import estimate?

    The estimate separates official charges — customs duty, levies and administrative fees on the CIF basis — from practical costs such as port handling, clearing agent fees, demurrage risk, inland delivery and registration, so you can see where the money goes.

    Is the Nigeria Car Landed Cost Calculator free and how accurate is it?

    Yes, it is completely free and works on any phone or computer. Accuracy depends on the values you enter and the current official tariff. The dated NTIP reference is not a current customs bill. Official levy and FOB notices conflict, and Green Tax is not automatically valued. Check the exact vehicle and use a full Customs or broker assessment before buying or shipping.
