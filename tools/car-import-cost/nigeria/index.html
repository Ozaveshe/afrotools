    Frequently asked questions

    How do I estimate the landed cost of importing a car into Nigeria?

    Enter the purchase price, FOB or CIF value, or a make, model and year. The calculator estimates the customs value, applies a saved Nigeria duty and levy scenario under 2026 policy review, then adds practical clearing, port, inland delivery and registration costs to show a real on-road budget.

    What charges are included in the Nigeria car import estimate?

    The estimate separates official charges — customs duty, levies and administrative fees on the CIF basis — from practical costs such as port handling, clearing agent fees, demurrage risk, inland delivery and registration, so you can see where the money goes.

    Is the Nigeria Car Landed Cost Calculator free and how accurate is it?

    Yes, it is completely free and works on any phone or computer. Accuracy depends on the values you enter and the current official tariff.
