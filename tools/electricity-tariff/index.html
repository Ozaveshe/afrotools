    Immediate tariff examples

    These crawlable examples use the same maintained records as the calculator and make the class assumptions explicit.

    Uganda standard: UGX 10,000

    At UEDCL’s Q3 2026 domestic standard rate of UGX 779.4/kWh, UGX 10,000 buys about 12.83 kWh before receipt-specific deductions.

    Uganda lifeline: 30 kWh

    For an eligible UEDCL lifeline account, the first 15 kWh at UGX 250 and the next 15 kWh at UGX 779.4 total about UGX 15,441.

    Tanzania D1: 100 kWh

    For a TANESCO D1 account, 75 kWh at TZS 100 plus 25 kWh at TZS 350 totals TZS 16,250.

    Coverage and limitations

    Automatic at release

    Uganda: UEDCL Q3 2026 domestic standard, eligible domestic lifeline and commercial average classes. Tanzania: TANESCO D1 low-usage domestic and T1 general-use classes from EWURA’s published schedule.

    Custom-rate only

    Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Zambia, Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal are not auto-calculated until exact current provider/class schedules are parsed and reviewed. A regulator homepage or national benchmark is not enough.

    Actual bills and token receipts may include taxes, levies, fixed charges, minimum charges, arrears, debt recovery, meter corrections or time-of-use pricing. Enter receipt deductions where known and verify the result before paying or disputing a charge.

    Electricity tariff questions

    How many prepaid electricity units will I get?

    Choose the country, provider and customer class, then enter the purchase amount. Actual units can differ when a receipt includes deductions not entered here.

    Why is my country not calculated automatically?

    Automatic estimates need an exact official provider-and-class schedule plus a passing validity and freshness check. Use a current local rate when that evidence is missing.

    Is this an official bill or token receipt?

    No. It is a planning estimate. Confirm the result with the regulator or utility schedule and your actual receipt.
