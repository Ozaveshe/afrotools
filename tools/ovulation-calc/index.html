Next-period window
A calendar projection from recent cycle lengths, not a promise that bleeding will start then.

A broad estimate based on ovulation often occurring about 12-16 days before the next period.
Pregnancy-possible span
An intentionally wider calendar span around the estimate. It does not label other days safe.

Privacy

Cycle dates stay off storage

No dates are saved to localStorage, sessionStorage, accounts, URLs or analytics. TXT and PDF files are created locally only after you request them. They contain sensitive cycle information, so store and share them carefully.

Use the estimate carefully

Does this confirm ovulation?

No. It applies date ranges to a cycle history. It does not measure hormones, temperature, cervical mucus, ultrasound findings or egg release.

Can this tell me whether I am pregnant?

No. A calendar cannot confirm pregnancy. Follow pregnancy-test instructions and contact a qualified service if you need individual guidance.

Can I use dates outside the span as contraceptive “safe days”?

No. Biological timing and cycle length vary. The tool deliberately does not mark any day safe for unprotected sex.
