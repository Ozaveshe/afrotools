Contract and outcome checks

Questions to answer before paying or signing

Full payment obligation

Compare the maximum amount under upfront, instalment, deferred-payment or income-share terms—not only the advertised deposit.

Time commitment

Confirm live sessions, assignments, time zone, attendance rules and whether the advertised duration includes career support.

Refund and exit terms

Check cooling-off periods, deferral, withdrawal, missed-payment consequences and what happens if the provider changes the cohort.

Outcome evidence

Treat placement and salary claims as provider marketing until the cohort, denominator, role type and measurement method are clear.

FAQ

Coding bootcamp comparison questions

Why are there no named “top bootcamps”?

A provider catalogue becomes misleading when fees, cohorts, formats and outcomes change. This worksheet compares the current offers you source.

What if the two offers use different currencies?

Convert every amount into the selected planning currency using the same dated FX source, then record the date above.

Should an ISA be entered as zero tuition?

No. Enter the total payment obligation you reasonably expect under the contract, and inspect the cap, income threshold, payment term and exit conditions.

Does the lower decision cost mean the programme is better?

No. It is one comparison dimension. Curriculum quality, instruction, support, accessibility, contract risk and verified outcomes still require judgment.
