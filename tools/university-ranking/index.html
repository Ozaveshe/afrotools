Decision checks

What a serious university comparison must verify

Programme and accreditation

Confirm the exact degree, campus, delivery mode and professional recognition with the university and relevant national or professional regulator.

Total cost and period

Separate tuition, compulsory fees, housing, insurance, visa, travel and deposits. Confirm whether quoted costs are per term, semester, year or full programme.

Admissions evidence

Check subject prerequisites, grade conversion, language tests, portfolio or interview requirements and the correct international-applicant deadline.

Outcomes and support

Treat salary and employment claims cautiously. Ask for programme-level methodology, sample size, response rate and graduation cohort before comparing outcomes.

Independent reference points

Start with official institutions and regulators

There is no single regulator for all African universities. Use the national authority for the institution’s country and the relevant professional regulator for regulated degrees.

FAQ

University comparison questions

Why is there no AfroTools rank number?

External rankings measure different things and change by edition. Combining them into an undocumented score would be misleading. This tool keeps each decision input visible.

Can I compare costs in different currencies?

Only after converting every figure to the selected currency using the same dated exchange-rate source. The worksheet performs no currency conversion.

Does “lowest entered cost” mean best value?

No. It means only that the numbers you entered add to the smallest first-year total. Quality, fit, accreditation and outcomes remain separate checks.

Will this predict admission or employment?

No. It does not calculate admissions probability, graduate employment, salary, visa approval or professional recognition.
