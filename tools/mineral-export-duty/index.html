South Africa diamond export levy

Estimate the gross levy on unpolished diamonds exported from South Africa. The SARS rate is 5% of the leviable value.

Use the leviable value from your DL 163 calculation after applicable import credits, relief and exemptions. If none apply, use the full qualifying export value.

Estimated gross levy

Prior levy overpayments or underpayments can change the amount payable on your return.

Coverage and official guidance

This calculation covers unpolished diamonds exported from South Africa. Other minerals, polished diamonds and exports from other countries require their own rules.

SARS introduced this levy on 1 November 2008. Rate checked 21 November 2026. See the SARS diamond export levy and the official DL 163 return guide for the qualifying deductions and filing steps.
