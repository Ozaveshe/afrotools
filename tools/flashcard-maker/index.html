Free Flashcard Maker with Private, Portable Study Decks

Create question-and-answer decks for school, university, professional study or any subject. Flip mode supports self-rating, quiz mode compares a typed answer, and match mode pairs terms with definitions. Cards marked Review Again are prioritised ahead of new and Got It cards in the next shuffled session.

The starter decks are editable prompts rather than official examination content. The tool does not predict questions, certify syllabus coverage or replace past papers, marking guides, teachers or examination-authority instructions. Decks remain in the current tab unless you explicitly enable on-device storage. CSV and printable review-pack exports provide a portable backup.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the review queue work?

Cards marked Review Again move ahead of new cards; Got It cards move later. This is a simple adaptive queue, not a calendar-based spaced-repetition algorithm.

Are the starter decks official exam content?

No. They are editable examples, not an official syllabus, marking guide, past paper or guarantee of exam coverage. Verify facts and scope with your teacher, textbook and the relevant examination authority.

Is my data saved?

Only if you enable “Remember on this device.” Otherwise decks remain in the current tab. Browser storage is local to this browser and can be cleared, so export important decks as CSV.

How do I import cards in bulk?

Click Bulk Import and paste one card per line. Use a tab or comma between front and back. Quoted CSV fields can contain commas.

What study modes are available?

Flip mode lets you see the front and reveal the back by clicking. Quiz mode asks you to type the answer for active recall practice. Match mode gives you a set of terms and definitions to pair together.
