    Important separation

    Do not buy an evaluation blindly. Ask the receiving institution, employer, regulator or immigration programme which provider, report type, delivery method and validity period it accepts.

    Why purpose matters

    One degree can have several decision owners

    Study

    The receiving university or college decides admission and credit. It may evaluate documents itself or specify an accepted evaluation service.

    Employment

    For non-regulated work, the employer sets its evidence policy. An evaluation does not guarantee a job or seniority.

    Immigration

    The immigration programme defines whether an assessment is required, which organisations are designated and how long reports remain valid.

    Licensed profession

    The competent regulator or assessing authority decides qualification, examinations, supervised practice and licensing requirements.

    FAQ

    Foreign qualification recognition questions

    Does this checker say what my degree equals?

    No. It routes you to the correct decision owner. An actual comparison requires document and institution verification.

    Can an ECA or statement guarantee my outcome?

    No. It may satisfy one evidence requirement, but it does not guarantee admission, employment, licensing or immigration approval.

    Is WES required everywhere?

    No. Some recipients accept or require specific providers; others evaluate documents directly. Confirm the accepted provider before paying.

    Are licensing and immigration assessments the same?

    Often not. A regulated profession may have a separate competent authority even when immigration also requires a credential or skills assessment.
