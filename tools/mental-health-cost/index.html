If there may be immediate danger

If you or someone else may act on thoughts of suicide or self-harm, cannot stay safe, or is in immediate danger, do not use this calculator. Contact your local emergency service now or go to the nearest emergency department. If possible, stay with a trusted person and move away from anything that could be used for harm. For a non-immediate crisis, contact a local crisis service or qualified mental-health professional as soon as possible.
