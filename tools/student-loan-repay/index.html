Method, limitations and verification

How the fixed-payment model works
For a positive fixed rate, monthly payment uses M = P × r(1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of payments. At 0%, principal is divided evenly across the term. The final payment is adjusted for rounding so the schedule ends at zero.
What this calculator deliberately does not model
It does not encode any country program or provider rules. It excludes income-contingent repayments, payroll deductions, affordability thresholds, grants, write-offs, grace periods, deferment, capitalization, fees, penalties, variable rates and payment holidays.
What to verify on an official statement
Confirm the current balance, whether the quoted rate is annual and fixed, the remaining number of payments, when interest begins, fees, payment frequency, how overpayments are applied and the provider’s current settlement figure. If your statement describes a statutory or income-linked method, use that official method instead of this calculator.
