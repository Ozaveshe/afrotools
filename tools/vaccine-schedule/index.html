Possible severe reaction after vaccination?

Get immediate local emergency care for trouble breathing, wheezing, swelling of the face, lips or throat, collapse, seizure, blue lips, shock, or unresponsiveness. Do not use this page to assess severity, choose treatment, or decide to wait. For other concerning symptoms, contact the vaccination provider or a qualified clinician promptly.

      How to use the handoff

      Bring the official record, not a reconstructed timeline

      Open the country programme source, then take the official vaccination record to an authorised provider. Ask them to reconcile the record against the current local programme, including campaigns or school-based doses. If the record is unavailable, say so; do not recreate dates from memory and treat them as proof.

      Why does this page not calculate a due date from a birth date?

      A single date calculation would ignore country revisions, product-specific minimum ages and intervals, campaign doses, previous records, contraindications and clinical context. A qualified local provider must reconcile those details.

      What if the official page looks old or is unavailable?

      Treat it as an incomplete handoff, not current truth. Use the WHO country-reported portal and contact the ministry, local clinic, or authorised provider for the current programme.

      Can I use the downloaded brief as proof of vaccination?

      No. It is a question sheet only. It is not a vaccination card, medical record, certificate, prescription, appointment, eligibility decision, or proof that any dose was given.
