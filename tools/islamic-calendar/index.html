Islamic Calendar Converter: quick guide

Enter a Gregorian or Hijri date to create a tabular planning conversion. This is not an astronomical observation or an official moon-sighting calendar.

  • What you get: a reversible date estimate with the calculation boundary shown.
  • How it works: the browser applies a civil tabular conversion without sending the date to a server.
  • What to check: confirm Ramadan, Eid, Hajj and other observances with the relevant local authority.

Method reviewed 27 July 2026. A local calendar may differ by one day because observance can depend on moon sighting.

Use the converter above. The additional planning panel below records the selected date and local-confirmation boundary; it does not create a second conversion.

Islamic Calendar Converter — Gregorian to Hijri FAQ

Is this an official Hijri date?

No. It is a tabular estimate for planning. Moon-sighting calendars and local authorities can differ by one day.

Can I convert in both directions?

Yes. Enter a complete Gregorian date or a complete Hijri date, then use the matching conversion button.

Does the converter send my date anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in the browser. Confirm observance dates separately before publishing or booking travel.
