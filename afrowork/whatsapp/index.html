Privacy & Security

      
Your privacy is non-negotiable

      
The bot is built for the worker who is checking their rights — not for data collection.


      

        
🔒

        

          
How messages and usage are handled

          

            The browser payroll preview calculates locally. WhatsApp messages pass through Meta and reach the bot with your number so it can reply. The bot’s operational usage records contain the command type, country, success, timing and response duration, without a phone number, phone hash, salary or message text. AI questions require explicit consent for that message and are sent to the configured AI provider. See the privacy notice for site analytics and provider handling.
