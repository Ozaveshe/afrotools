A practical, official-source guide to the 15% standard rate, the 2026 8% water and electricity rule, monthly Mod 106 filing, invoice evidence and VAT close controls.

Cabo Verde VAT compliance is more than applying a percentage to an invoice. A business must confirm its tax status, classify every supply, issue and retain the right evidence, reconcile output VAT with supported input VAT, complete the official Modelo 106 return and preserve proof of filing and payment. The arithmetic is the easy part. The risks usually sit in classification, missing documents, late corrections and a return that cannot be traced back to the books.

This guide was verified on September 2, 2026 against five primary sources from Cabo Verde's Ministry of Finance and Direção Nacional das Receitas de Estado, or DNRE. All five source URLs returned HTTP 200 on the verification date. The set includes the 2026 Budget Law, the updated 2026 fiscal calendar, DNRE VAT guidance, the official Mod 106 form and the order governing the return and its annexes.

Separate the 15% rate, 8% utility treatment and exemptions

The standard planning rate is 15%. The 2026 Budget Law creates a specific 8% treatment for the transmission of electricity and provision of water to final consumers. That wording should not be expanded to every energy sale, utility-related service, bottled water product, business input or infrastructure contract. The supplier should confirm the exact legal category before changing an invoice or system rate.

The same Budget Law also provides a 2026 exemption for ice supplied for preserving fish when the recipient is an operator properly licensed and accredited by the government department responsible for fisheries. This is a useful reminder that an exemption can depend on the product, purpose and recipient status together. A product description alone may not prove the treatment.

Zero-rated, exempt and non-taxable operations must not be merged into one generic zero-VAT bucket. The official Mod 106 provides separate lines for exempt transactions with a right to deduction, exempt transactions without that right and operations treated as non-taxable under the cited VAT rule. This separation matters because the effect on input tax can differ.

Build a classification register that records the supply description, customer type, selected treatment, legal or DNRE source, effective date, reviewer and any evidence condition. If the same item can have different treatment depending on use or buyer status, make the invoice process ask for that evidence before finalising the rate.

How to add and extract Cabo Verde VAT

For a VAT-exclusive amount at 15%, multiply the net amount by 0.15 and add the result to the net amount. For a VAT-inclusive amount, divide the gross total by 1.15 to find the net amount, then subtract the net amount from the gross total. At 8%, replace 0.15 with 0.08 and 1.15 with 1.08 only after the final-consumer water or electricity condition has been confirmed.

It does not claim that any particular product, customer or invoice qualifies for that rate.

Build the monthly Mod 106 workflow

DNRE describes Mod 106 as the monthly periodic VAT return. Taxpayers outside special regimes generally submit it by the last day of the month following the period. The 2026 fiscal calendar should be checked for the exact date. Do not rely on a remembered deadline when a public holiday, updated calendar or special notice may affect the operational date.

  1. Close the sales register. Record invoice number, date, customer NIF where required, description, net amount, VAT rate, VAT amount, gross total, exemption or non-taxable basis, credit notes and any reverse-charge treatment.
  2. Close the purchase register. Capture supplier details, invoice reference, transaction date, purchase purpose, net value, VAT, payment evidence and whether the input supports taxable, exempt or mixed activity.
  3. Complete the annexes. Portaria n.º 2/2014 links Mod 106 to customer, supplier and adjustment annexes. Reconcile annex totals to the source registers before transferring figures to the return.
  4. Reconcile output VAT. Compare invoices with the general ledger, payment records, point-of-sale totals and the invoice sequence. Investigate missing numbers, duplicated documents and late credit notes.
  5. Review input VAT. Remove duplicate, unsupported, private or otherwise ineligible claims. Check that each retained amount is tied to a valid supplier document and the business activity.
  6. Map totals to Mod 106. Use the official lines for standard or special-rate sales, exemptions, non-taxable operations, imported services, construction treatment, purchases, adjustments and credits. Do not force an unusual transaction into a convenient line.
  7. Review and authorise. A second reviewer should check rates, large adjustments, exempt sales, foreign services, credits carried forward and the reconciliation from return to ledger.
  8. Submit, pay and archive. Preserve the final return, annexes, acknowledgement, payment proof, reviewed registers, source documents, approvals and any DNRE correspondence together.

DNRE guidance says the return is required even if no taxable operations occurred during the month. A nil period should still have a short review showing that sales, purchases, bank activity and invoice sequences were checked before the nil return was filed.

The same guidance says VAT due is normally delivered with the return. It also describes later payment as attracting interest and a fine. Treat the filing date as the working payment deadline. Do not build a routine process around a penalty period.

Invoice, input tax and correction evidence

Every return figure should be reproducible from the books. Use a stable invoice sequence and retain the supplier identity, customer identity where required, transaction date, description, quantity, price, discount, net amount, VAT rate, VAT amount and gross amount. If a supply is exempt, non-taxable or subject to a special rule, store the reason and supporting source with the transaction.

For purchase VAT, a document showing tax is necessary but may not be sufficient. The business should also be able to show that the purchase belongs to the registered activity and supports supplies for which deduction is allowed. Match the invoice to the purchase register, ledger account, receipt of goods or services, supplier statement and payment evidence where relevant.

The Mod 106 package includes adjustment annexes. Corrections should identify the original document, original tax period, reason, replacement or credit note, amount changed, approver and return period where the correction is reported. Deleting or overwriting a closed invoice breaks the link between the books and the return.

DNRE's public guidance says accounting records, invoices, dispatch notes, returns and supporting documents must be kept in good order for five years. A business may need a longer period under another law, contract, audit or dispute. Apply the longest verified requirement and protect commercial, customer, supplier and banking data from unnecessary access.

A VAT credit is not automatically cash in the bank. The official form contains the return position and refund request fields, while DNRE guidance describes supporting documents for a refund. Keep the return history, supplier evidence, reconciliations and requested schedules ready, then follow the current DNRE process for the specific claim.

Read the special Mod 106 fields before filing

The official Mod 106 is useful evidence because it shows categories that a simple VAT calculator cannot cover. One line addresses services acquired from a provider without a seat, permanent establishment or domicile in Cabo Verde where the declarant accounts for the tax. Another addresses construction services where the declarant liquidates the VAT. These are return classifications, not optional arithmetic shortcuts.

If the business buys software, advertising, consulting, platform access or another service from abroad, do not assume that the absence of VAT on the supplier invoice ends the analysis. Review the place-of-supply and self-accounting position against the current VAT rules and DNRE guidance. Preserve the contract, supplier invoice, service evidence, payment and calculation.

Construction transactions also need careful separation. The return has a dedicated line because the person accounting for VAT can differ from the ordinary supplier-charges-customer pattern. Confirm the contract, parties, service type and statutory condition before using that field.

Imports of goods are separate again. DNRE says import VAT is paid at customs clearance. Keep the customs declaration, assessed value, duty and tax proof with the purchase records. Do not recreate an import assessment by applying 15% only to the supplier's invoice amount.

Controls that prevent monthly VAT errors

Use one rate and classification table. Give every treatment an authority source, effective date, owner and review date. Lock unusual rates until the required evidence is supplied.

Reconcile invoice sequences. Missing, duplicated or reused invoice numbers should be resolved before the return. Credit and debit notes must point back to the original transaction.

Separate standard, special, exempt and non-taxable totals. Do not let the accounting system merge them because Mod 106 reports them differently.

Reconcile annexes to the ledger. Customer, supplier and adjustment annex totals should agree with the reviewed registers and return lines. Record and approve every difference.

Review imported services and construction separately. These transactions can require the declarant to account for tax. Flag relevant ledger accounts for monthly review.

File while registered. A quiet month does not remove the return obligation. Preserve the nil-period check and submission proof.

Confirm the current calendar. This article records sources checked on September 2, 2026. Before each filing, recheck the DNRE fiscal calendar, form version, portal instructions and any special notice.

Protect taxpayer records. Limit access to invoices, NIFs, bank evidence and return files. Do not place raw tax records in analytics, public links, screenshots or unapproved AI tools.

Frequently asked questions

What is Cabo Verde's standard VAT rate in 2026?

The standard planning rate is 15%. The 2026 Budget Law separately applies 8% to electricity and water supplied to final consumers. Confirm the exact transaction classification before invoicing or filing.

When must Mod 106 be filed?

DNRE guidance says the monthly periodic return is generally due by the last day of the following month for VAT taxpayers outside special regimes. Check the current fiscal calendar for the exact period date.

Is a nil Mod 106 still required?

Yes. DNRE says the periodic return remains mandatory even when there are no taxable operations or no VAT is payable.

What supports the VAT return?

Keep the sales and purchase registers, issued and received invoices, customer and supplier annexes, adjustment documents, import records, classification evidence, return reconciliation, submission acknowledgement and payment proof.

Does AfroTools send data to DNRE?
