Senegal Employer Payroll Compliance 2026: VRS, IPRES, CSS and SENTAX

A practical payroll close for salary withholding, employer-side charges, social contributions, annual reporting and the September 2026 SENTAX transition.

Employer payroll in Senegal is not complete when the net salary reaches the employee. A controlled pay run must connect the employment record, taxable remuneration, salary withholding, employer-side taxes, social contributions, payslip, declarations, payments and official acknowledgements. Each figure should trace back to a source and each filing should trace back to the payroll ledger.

This guide was verified on September 26, 2026 against the Direction générale des Impôts et des Domaines, or DGID, current business pages, its 2025 General Tax Code resource, monthly and annual declaration calendars, the 2026 annual-statement extension notice, the SENTAX launch page, and official labour and social-security material from Senegal's Direction générale du Travail et de la Sécurité sociale, or DGTSS. The official portals can change procedures, ceilings and assigned filing channels, so the employer account remains the final operational check.

Use the Senegal PAYE Calculator to test confirmed salary inputs and deductions locally. Use the Staff Cost Planner to separate salary from verified employer obligations and benefits. These tools support planning. They do not register an employer, classify a worker, submit a declaration, pay an institution or replace payroll advice.

The main risk is mixing obligations that use different bases, rates, ceilings and portals. Employee income tax is not the same item as IPRES retirement, CSS contributions, an Institution de Prévoyance Maladie arrangement or the Contribution forfaitaire à la charge de l'employeur. Keep each line separate in the payroll register and general ledger even when one payment process groups several amounts.

Dates also depend on the taxpayer's regime and official instructions. The DGID monthly calendar gives a quarterly route for taxpayers under the simplified real regime when the withholding amount does not exceed XOF 20,000, with dates on the 15th of January, April, July and October. That exception should be used only when the employer's current regime and amount meet the published condition.

Set up the employer before the first pay run

Start with legal identity. The payroll file should hold the employer's registered name, NINEA, tax office, tax regime, assigned DGID unit, IPRES number, CSS number, unified IPRES-CSS number where issued, bank details and the authorised portal users. Do not wait until payment day to discover that a former accountant owns the only login or that the registered address is outdated.

The DGID declaration-of-existence page says a person opening an establishment or starting an activity that creates a tax obligation must declare that existence within 20 days. The official page lists identification evidence, NINEA and proof of the business address among the required records. An employer should connect that tax identity to its labour and social registrations rather than creating disconnected versions of the same entity.

The current shared CSS and IPRES employer sign-up page requests the NINEA, IPRES number, CSS number and unique IPRES-CSS number. That is useful operational evidence of the identifiers the social system expects. It is not proof that registration has been approved. Retain the application, assigned numbers, activation messages and any inspection or follow-up record issued by the competent institution.

For every worker, maintain a signed contract or appointment record, identity, start date, job, pay frequency, fixed salary, approved variable pay, benefits, bank details, family information used for tax where applicable, and the relevant social classification. Limit access to this file because it contains personal and payroll data. Payroll analytics should use counts or bands, not raw names, account numbers or salary details.

Calculate and remit salary withholding

Articles 181 onward of the General Tax Code place income tax on salaries and related remuneration under withholding at source. The Code states that when the employer is domiciled in Senegal, the tax is withheld for the Treasury when the payment is made. That makes the pay date, taxable components and employee record part of the filing evidence.

Build gross remuneration from the approved salary, taxable allowances, bonuses, commissions, benefits in kind and corrections for the period. Keep non-taxable reimbursements separate and supported by the expense policy and underlying evidence. Do not hide a recurring cash allowance inside an expense code merely because it is easier to process.

Apply the current salary-tax method to the employee facts held for that pay period. The Code contains rules on the taxable base, deductions and family quotient treatment. Because employee circumstances and official bands can change, save the source version or payroll-engine version used, the effective date and the inputs behind the result. A payslip total without a reproducible calculation is weak evidence.

The monthly DGID calendar calls salary withholding retenues sur salaires, or VRS. For a normal-regime employer, the published due date is no later than the 15th day of the month after payment. A September salary paid in September therefore belongs in the following month's control cycle, but the employer should confirm the period and due date displayed in its account before submission.

Separate the declaration from the payment proof. The close file should contain the submitted return or declaration data, official acknowledgement, payment reference, bank debit and a reconciliation showing that the paid amount matches the declared period. A bank transfer with no return reference does not prove the tax was allocated correctly.

Keep IPRES, CSS and health coverage distinct

Senegal's official DGTSS social-security reference states that the employer calculates and pays retirement contributions for its staff and produces annual and quarterly salary declarations. It describes an IPRES general retirement contribution of 14%, split 8.4% employer and 5.6% employee, and a supplementary cadres contribution of 6%, split 3.6% employer and 2.4% employee.

Those percentages do not remove the need to verify the current contribution ceilings and employee category. The same DGTSS reference says the contribution base is remuneration up to a ceiling set by the institution. Before a 2026 payroll goes live, obtain the current ceiling and classification from IPRES or the employer portal, record the effective date and test the calculation at the boundary.

CSS obligations should be controlled separately. The employer needs the current family-benefit and occupational-risk position, including the business's assigned risk treatment and applicable salary ceiling. Do not infer one employer's accident-risk contribution from another business, and do not treat an old spreadsheet ceiling as current authority.

Health coverage through an Institution de Prévoyance Maladie, or IPM, can add employee and employer deductions under the applicable arrangement. Store the governing plan, membership list, contribution rule and remittance evidence. Reconcile deductions withheld from employees to amounts actually paid to the institution. An unpaid deduction is both a payroll liability and an employee trust issue.

At month-end, the sum of employee deductions and employer contributions should reconcile to the liability accounts for IPRES, CSS and health coverage. The institution receipt should clear the matching liability, not a generic payroll suspense account. Investigate rejected employees, unmatched numbers and differences before the next pay run.

Handle the SENTAX transition carefully

DGID's current SENTAX page says the platform supports online registration, declarations, payments, tax-position tracking and notifications. It also says SENTAX replaced ETAX for taxpayers managed by the Direction des Grandes Entreprises and became mandatory for all their declarations and payments from September 1, 2026, with paper declarations no longer accepted by that unit.

That statement is specific to the DGE population described by DGID. It should not be expanded into a claim that every Senegal employer uses the same portal for every obligation. The DGID business page continues to list separate services, including E-Services for annual and quarterly third-party statements and annual salary statements. Confirm which service the employer account assigns to each obligation.

Before the next filing, test access without submitting a live return. Confirm the legal entity, user permissions, tax types, outstanding periods and payment method. Capture the support route for an activation or allocation problem. Do not share passwords in a payroll spreadsheet, email attachment or automation log.

During a platform transition, preserve both the calculation evidence and the submission evidence. If the portal is unavailable, record the time, error reference and official support contact, then follow the authority's published contingency process. Do not claim a filing succeeded because a browser reached the login page.

Close payroll in ten controlled steps

  1. Freeze master data. Approve starters, leavers, salary changes, bank changes and worker classifications before calculation.
  2. Validate attendance and variable pay. Tie overtime, leave, bonuses, commissions and deductions to authorised source records.
  3. Build gross remuneration. Separate fixed pay, taxable benefits, reimbursements and corrections.
  4. Calculate employee deductions. Apply the current income-tax, IPRES, health and other confirmed rules to the correct bases.
  5. Calculate employer costs. Keep CSS, employer IPRES, health, CFCE and other verified obligations outside employee net-pay deductions.
  6. Review exceptions. Investigate negative net pay, unusual changes, duplicate employees, missing identifiers and manual overrides.
  7. Approve and pay salaries. Match the approved bank file to the payroll register and record rejected or returned payments.
  8. Declare and fund liabilities. Use the assigned DGID and social channels, current periods and official due dates.
  9. Reconcile acknowledgements. Match each declaration, payment reference and receipt to the payroll liability.
  10. Archive securely. Retain the approved register, payslips, declarations, receipts, reconciliations and source version with restricted access.

A second-person review is valuable because many errors are classification errors rather than formula errors. Reviewers should be able to move from the bank total to the net-pay register, from gross pay to each liability, and from each liability to the official declaration and payment receipt.

Use change reports rather than only totals. Compare headcount, gross pay, net pay, VRS, IPRES, CSS, health contributions and employer costs with the prior period. A stable total can conceal an omitted employee and a duplicate employee of similar value.

Reconcile the annual salary statement

DGID's annual declaration calendar lists the summary statement of salaries and amounts paid to third parties, commonly associated with State 1024 and annexes. The official form records employee identity, employment details, payment period, annual remuneration, benefits and withholding categories. Build the annual file from the twelve approved monthly registers instead of retyping it from payslips.

For the 2025 exercise, DGID issued a specific notice extending the annual salary and third-party statement deadline to March 31, 2026. The same notice said regularisations under Article 186 still had to be declared and paid by February 15, 2026. That was an explicit extension for a named exercise, not a permanent change to every future calendar.

Before submission, reconcile annual gross pay, taxable benefits, VRS, employer-side tax and employee identifiers to the monthly payroll ledger. Explain leavers, corrections and any difference between payroll and accounting. Retain the submitted statement, acceptance evidence and reconciliation. If DGID announces a new filing link or extension, save the dated notice with the file.

Also reconcile the annual IPRES and CSS declarations to the same workforce and remuneration records, while respecting each institution's contribution base and ceiling. Differences can be legitimate, but they must be explained by rules such as capped bases, worker categories or excluded items rather than left as unexplained spreadsheet adjustments.

Evidence checklist for every payroll period

  • Employer legal identity, NINEA, DGID unit, IPRES, CSS and portal details are current.
  • Every worker has an approved contract, pay terms, classification and effective date.
  • Gross remuneration ties to salary approvals, attendance, benefits and variable-pay records.
  • VRS calculations are reproducible from the current rule set and employee facts.
  • IPRES, CSS and health bases, rates, ceilings and categories have dated sources.
  • Employee deductions and employer contributions are posted to separate liability accounts.
  • Salary bank files match approved net pay, with rejected payments resolved.
  • Declarations, acknowledgements, payment references and bank evidence agree by period.
  • Manual overrides are listed, justified, approved and carried into the annual reconciliation.
  • Payroll records are access-controlled and raw personal data is not copied into analytics or logs.

This guide is a planning and control resource, not an official filing service, legal opinion or guarantee of compliance. Current DGID, IPRES, CSS and labour instructions, plus the employer's facts, determine the actual treatment.

Senegal employer payroll questions

When is salary withholding normally due?

The DGID monthly calendar places VRS for normal-regime taxpayers by the 15th day of the month after salary payment. Check the employer account and any current notice before filing.

What changed for DGE taxpayers in September 2026?

DGID says SENTAX replaced ETAX for taxpayers managed by the DGE and became mandatory for their declarations and payments from September 1, 2026.

Who calculates and pays IPRES contributions?

The official DGTSS reference places calculation, payment and salary-declaration duties on the employer. Confirm the current ceiling and worker category directly with IPRES.

Is March 31 now the annual salary-statement deadline every year?

No. The March 31, 2026 date was a DGID extension for statements covering the 2025 exercise. A new year needs its own current calendar or notice.

Does AfroTools submit payroll declarations?

No. The calculator provides local planning arithmetic. It does not register an employer, classify a worker, submit a return or pay DGID, IPRES or CSS.
