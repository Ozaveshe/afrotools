Visa rules depend on nationality, passport type, destination, transit route, purpose and travel date. A social post or old list can be wrong even when it describes the same two countries.

Use a checker to identify the likely route, then confirm it with the destination immigration authority, embassy or consulate and the airline's document system before paying non-refundable costs.

African Visa Requirement Checker helps compare travel-document routes within Africa and shows source context where available.

Quick answer

Check the passport you will actually use, every transit airport and the purpose of travel. Save dated screenshots or PDFs of official instructions and recheck close to departure.

Start with the exact job you need to complete. A useful result is tied to a real application, meeting, agreement, trip, electricity purchase, job application, image export or academic record. Write down the destination, deadline and acceptance rule before changing any settings.

Visa-free entry can still require passport validity, blank pages, onward travel, funds, accommodation or health evidence.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Enter nationality and destination into the checker.
  2. Open the destination's official immigration or embassy source.
  3. Check transit rules for every airport and border.
  4. Verify passport validity, health, funds and supporting documents.
  5. Recheck before departure and carry the approval or evidence required.

Keep the original file or record unchanged. Work on a copy, label the output with the date and purpose, and compare it with the source before sending or uploading it. This makes mistakes reversible and gives another person enough context to review the decision.

Separate discovery from official confirmation

A checker is excellent for discovering the likely category, but it cannot approve entry. Border officials and authorised systems apply the current rule to the specific traveller.

An eVisa should be obtained only from the official domain or a clearly authorised provider. Search advertisements and lookalike websites can charge extra or collect passport data.

Airline boarding rules and destination entry rules interact. A traveller may also need transit permission even without leaving the airport.

Travelling with a child, diplomatic document, refugee travel document or emergency passport can trigger a different route. Check consent letters, birth records and document eligibility directly with the responsible authority.

Checks before you rely on the result

  • Nationality and passport type are correct.
  • Transit points and purpose are included.
  • The source is official and recently checked.
  • Approval names and passport details match exactly.
  • Non-refundable bookings wait until uncertainty is resolved.

Treat any estimate as a planning aid. Fees, entry rules, grading scales, document clauses, upload limits and utility deductions can vary by provider, country and date. The receiving institution or official authority remains the final source of truth.

Create a review trail

Record the source, date and version beside the result. If another person reviews the work, ask them to check the inputs and acceptance rule rather than only the final appearance. Save their correction separately so the original decision remains traceable.

When the receiving portal, authority, employer, utility or institution rejects the output, keep the rejection message and compare it with the rule you recorded. Correct the identified mismatch first. Repeating the same export with random settings makes the problem harder to diagnose and can create conflicting copies.

Privacy and safety

Use the minimum information needed. Do not paste identity numbers, private commercial terms, academic records or personal contact details into an untrusted service. AfroTools workflows described here are designed for practical preparation, but you should still review the page notice and export before sharing it.

Passport scans and visa applications contain sensitive identity data. Use official encrypted portals and avoid sending full documents to unknown agents.
