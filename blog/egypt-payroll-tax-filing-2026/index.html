Egypt Payroll Tax Filing 2026: Employer Guide

A practical employer workflow for monthly salary-tax calculations, quarterly Form 4, annual settlement and evidence that survives review.

Egyptian payroll tax filing is not one spreadsheet sent once a year. An employer needs a controlled record of employee identity, taxable pay, permitted deductions, tax calculated for the period, tax already reported, payments and later corrections. That record feeds the monthly salary-tax calculation, quarterly payroll declaration and annual settlement.

This guide was verified on September 27, 2026 against the Egyptian Tax Authority's current payroll forms page, March 2026 payroll-system FAQ, monthly and quarterly submission guides, 2025 annual-settlement guide and current income-tax law index. The ETA page lists a private-sector monthly calculation template specifically for 2026. Rules, enrolment decisions and portal deadlines can change, so preserve the source set used for each period and recheck the live ETA calendar before submission.

It does not enrol a company in the ETA payroll system, classify every payment, submit Form 4, make a tax payment or prove that the authority accepted a return.

The three filing layers should agree but do different jobs. The monthly record calculates current liability and supports payment. Quarterly Form 4 summarizes the three-month position and workforce movements. Annual settlement checks the final full-year position for each employee and the employer as a whole. Treating any one layer as a substitute for the others creates unexplained differences later.

The ETA FAQ says the electronic payroll system contains employee master data, insurance data, earnings, exemptions, deductions, cumulative tax from January to the current month, prior tax and the tax due for the submission month. A reliable payroll ledger should be able to produce the same bridge without manual guesswork.

Build one employer filing calendar

Start the year with one calendar that combines payroll cut-off, approval, payslip release, monthly calculation submission, payment, Form 4, annual settlement and any social-insurance actions. Assign a named preparer and reviewer for each step. The calendar should record both the normal legal cycle and any current official extension.

ETA's standing employer guidance says tax withheld from salaries is remitted within the first 15 days of the following month. It also describes quarterly declarations in January, April, July and October. These dates are the planning baseline, not a reason to ignore a later ETA notice. Holidays, system decisions and specific taxpayer instructions can affect an operational date.

Keep proof of submission and proof of payment as separate records. A prepared file is not a submitted form. A submitted form is not proof that payment settled. Store the acknowledgement number, submitted version, payment reference, amount, value date and bank confirmation. If the portal replaces or corrects a submission, link the corrected item to the original instead of deleting the audit trail.

Use a status field such as draft, reviewed, submitted, accepted, paid or corrected. Avoid using a colour alone to show status because it is easy to misread and difficult to export. A short written exception note should explain every late, rejected or revised item.

Close the monthly salary-tax calculation

The monthly close begins with employee master data. Match each employee's name and identifier to the records accepted by the ETA system. The payroll services page includes specific guidance for adding employees and the annual-settlement process warns employers to keep names aligned with national identity records. Resolve duplicated employees, invalid identifiers and unexplained start or end dates before calculating tax.

Build gross pay from the actual approved components for the month. Separate basic pay, variable pay, bonuses, allowances, benefits, arrears and one-off items. Do not hide a taxable payment inside a generic adjustment line. The classification should point to the policy, contract or legal treatment used.

ETA's current FAQ says the salary-tax calculation annualises monthly wages, considers the personal exemption, deducts the employee's qualifying social-insurance contribution and applies other permitted deductions within their legal limits before using the statutory rates. The same FAQ currently identifies an EGP 20,000 annual personal exemption. Because the employee's cumulative position matters, a change in pay or employment length can affect the current month's result.

The calculation file should show the bridge from gross pay to taxable annualised income, tax under the applicable band structure, cumulative tax, tax already withheld and the current-month amount. Preserve the software version or calculation sheet used. If the payroll engine is changed during the year, perform a parallel check and document how year-to-date balances moved.

Before submission, reconcile the monthly form to the approved payroll register, payslips, bank payment file and general ledger. Differences can arise from off-cycle payments, manual bonuses, leavers, reversed payments and late adjustments. Resolve them before the form becomes the source for Form 4.

  1. Lock employee data. Validate identity, employment dates, tax treatment and current insurance information.
  2. Approve earnings. Separate recurring, variable and one-off payments with supporting records.
  3. Approve deductions. Distinguish tax deductions from payroll recoveries that do not reduce taxable income.
  4. Calculate cumulatively. Reconcile year-to-date income, prior tax and the current period.
  5. Review exceptions. Investigate negative tax, zero tax on material pay, duplicate employees and large month-on-month changes.
  6. Submit and pay. Use the current ETA workflow, then store the acknowledgement and separate payment proof.

Reconcile quarterly Form 4

ETA's current quarterly guide says that an employer registered in the unified payroll calculation system submits quarterly payroll Form 4 through that system. The form brings together employee numbers, payroll amounts, tax withheld, tax paid and movements in the workforce. It is a reconciliation point, not a fourth monthly estimate.

Prepare a three-month bridge before opening the form. Total the submitted monthly calculations, payments and general-ledger balances. List employees who joined, left or changed status. Match the number of employees and total pay to the supporting register for each month. Investigate any payment posted in a different month from the related withholding.

If a monthly form was corrected after submission, make sure the quarter uses the corrected version and retains both acknowledgements. If the quarterly total does not equal the sum of monthly records, document the exact reason and authority for the adjustment. Do not enter a plug value just to make the declaration balance.

The quarter-end review is also the right point to check whether employee payslips show the same taxable pay and tax as the employer ledger. ETA provides employee guidance for retrieving monthly payslips through the system. A disagreement may indicate a mapping, identity or period issue that should be fixed before annual settlement.

Prepare annual payroll settlement

Annual settlement is a full-year reconciliation. ETA's 2025 annual guide, published in the current payroll-services library, walks users through selecting the settlement form, importing or entering annual data, validation and submission. The payroll forms page also publishes the related annual processing templates.

For each employee, reconcile total earnings, exempt items, permitted deductions, taxable income, annual tax, tax previously withheld and the final difference. Then reconcile the employee totals to the employer's monthly forms, quarterly declarations, payments and general ledger. The settlement should explain the year, not reproduce December's payroll.

Pay special attention to employees who joined or left during the year, received irregular payments, had retroactive changes, worked for more than one payer, or moved between payroll entities. ETA's FAQ specifically notes that the system is designed to handle cases where an employee receives amounts from another source. Confirm the applicable treatment rather than forcing every employee through the same template.

ETA's standing guidance places the annual payroll settlement deadline at the end of January. The current annual guide in the source library is for the 2025 settlement submitted after that year. Before relying on the planning date for a future settlement, check the ETA portal, current guide and any official extension that applies to the employer.

Check the simplified-tax-system exception

Egypt's ETA FAQ describes special treatment for businesses enrolled under Law No. 6 of 2025 and within its turnover conditions. The current FAQ says qualifying enrolled businesses may be relieved from monthly salary-tax payment and use an annual salary-tax settlement instead. This is not a general exemption for every small employer.

Before changing the normal filing cycle, retain proof that the business is actually enrolled, confirm the current turnover and eligibility conditions, and record the exact ETA instruction applied. A company should not stop monthly payroll compliance only because its own estimate of turnover falls below a threshold.

The current income-tax law index also lists 2026 amendments, including Law No. 151 of 2026. The reviewed provisions of that law focus on other income-tax matters and do not justify inventing a new salary-tax process. Use the ETA payroll library and any later official payroll instruction as the operational source for salary filings.

Frequently asked questions

What payroll tax forms do Egyptian employers manage in 2026?

The ETA payroll system supports monthly salary-tax calculation submissions, quarterly Form 4 and an annual settlement. The exact workflow depends on the employer's enrolment and tax profile.

When is salary tax remitted in Egypt?

ETA's published employer guidance states that tax withheld from employees is remitted within the first 15 days of the following month. Confirm the live ETA calendar and any official extension before filing or paying.

When is quarterly payroll Form 4 filed?

ETA guidance places quarterly payroll declarations at the ends of January, April, July and October. Confirm the current portal period and deadline before submission.

Does the monthly submission replace annual payroll settlement?

No. ETA materials distinguish monthly calculation submissions, quarterly Form 4 and annual settlement. The annual process reconciles the full year's payroll tax position.

Can AfroTools submit an ETA payroll return?

No. The Egypt PAYE Calculator provides a local planning estimate. It does not enrol an employer, submit forms, make payments or confirm that a filing has been accepted.
