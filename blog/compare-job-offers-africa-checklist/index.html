How to Compare Two Job Offers in Africa

AfroTools TeamAug 20, 202610 min read

A larger monthly salary does not automatically make one job offer better than another. The payment frequency, cash allowances, pension or insurance support, bonus conditions, commute, remote-work arrangement, hours, contract duration and notice terms can change the decision. The safest comparison begins with two written offers and separates confirmed terms from assumptions.

Use the AfroTools Job Offer Evaluator to compare figures, ratings and decision weights privately in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent by the tool. It does not infer tax, market pay, promotion probability or a winner. The official employment sources in this guide were checked on August 20, 2026.

Decision boundary: This is a preparation worksheet, not legal, tax or career advice. Employment rules differ by country, sector, worker category and contract. Confirm important terms in writing and check the current law and professional guidance that apply to the actual employer and place of work.

Do not compare promises with written terms

Start by copying only information that appears in the offer, contract draft, benefit schedule or employer policy supplied to you. Put verbal explanations in a separate questions list. If a recruiter says an allowance, bonus, remote day or salary review is normal but it is not documented, mark it unconfirmed rather than silently adding it to the package.

This distinction matters because official employment laws often require written particulars, but the required detail and timing are not identical. Kenya's current Employment Act text lists the job description, start date, contract form and duration, place and hours of work, remuneration method, other benefits and payment intervals among the particulars for written contracts covered by its rules. South Africa's Basic Conditions of Employment Act lists written particulars including work, place, start date, hours, wage calculation, overtime rate, other cash or in-kind payments, payment frequency, deductions, leave and notice or end date.

Ghana's Ministry of Labour, Jobs and Employment publishes the Labour Act and Labour Regulations through its official laws library. The ILO's employment-relationship resources also stress that the existence and terms of work should be assessed under national law and practice. These sources support a disciplined document check, not one universal African contract template.

Put both offers on one annual basis

The Job Offer Evaluator uses a transparent planning formula. For each offer, annual value equals 12 multiplied by monthly pay, monthly cash allowances and the monthly benefit estimate, less monthly work costs, then adds the expected annual bonus and subtracts one-off costs. This is a comparison structure, not a forecast that every amount will be received.

Enter the same type of amount on both sides. If Offer A states monthly gross pay and Offer B states annual gross pay, convert one before entry. If one offer pays thirteen times a year or uses weekly pay, document the calculation instead of forcing it into a monthly label. Keep the original offer beside the worksheet so another person can reproduce the annualization.

Do not mix currencies inside one comparison. When offers use different currencies, choose one reporting currency, record the exchange-rate source and date, convert every cash field consistently, and retain the original amounts. Then run a second comparison with a less favorable exchange rate. Currency conversion still does not answer where income tax, social insurance or transfer costs arise.

A bonus needs its own evidence check. Ask whether it is guaranteed, target-based or discretionary; which performance period and measures apply; whether new joiners qualify; when it is paid; and whether the employee must remain employed on the payment date. If those points are unclear, use a conservative estimate or zero and compare again. The same caution applies to commissions, profit share, equity and retention awards.

Calculate take-home pay separately

The worksheet deliberately does not estimate PAYE, pension, health contributions, housing levies or other country deductions. Run each written gross package through the relevant current calculator, such as the Nigeria PAYE Calculator, Kenya PAYE Calculator, Ghana PAYE and SSNIT Calculator or South Africa PAYE Calculator. Check the tax year and source date before relying on the result.

Keep gross contractual value and estimated take-home pay as separate lines. Gross pay may control pension, severance, overtime, leave pay or other entitlements under the applicable rules, while take-home pay answers the immediate cash question. A salary sacrifice, employer contribution, reimbursement or in-kind benefit may receive different treatment, so do not label every employer expense as employee income.

If the job crosses borders, remote work does not automatically remove local obligations. Confirm the employing entity, payroll country, physical work location, right to work, tax residence, social-security position, payment route and currency terms. Those are professional review items, not assumptions for a scoring tool.

Measure the costs created by the job

An offer can increase gross pay and still leave little extra usable cash if the role creates higher recurring costs. Record only costs that differ meaningfully between the offers: transport, fuel, tolls, parking, work meals, data, backup power, required clothing, childcare created by the schedule, second accommodation or unreimbursed travel. Separate employer reimbursements from allowances because reimbursement may depend on receipts and policy limits.

Use measured inputs where possible. A commute estimate can come from route distance, expected office days and a current fuel-cost calculation. A remote role can still require data, electricity and workspace costs. A relocation package should state which items are paid directly, reimbursed after evidence or recoverable if you leave early.

One-off costs belong outside the monthly line. Examples include required credential verification, a move, equipment you must provide, an early lease exit or a work-permit charge that the contract lawfully assigns. Never pay a recruiter or supposed employer merely to secure a job. Verify the employer and recruitment channel independently before sharing identity documents or money.

Value benefits without inventing cash

A benefit estimate is useful only when the benefit is real, documented and comparable. Ask for the plan name, coverage, waiting period, employee contribution, dependants, exclusions, limits, claim process and what happens when employment ends. For pension or provident support, distinguish the employee deduction from the employer contribution and confirm the contribution base.

Do not use the employer's headline cost as your personal value. A training budget has value only if it is available to your role and usable for relevant learning. A company vehicle is not the same as unrestricted cash. Equity is not guaranteed salary. Extra leave should be compared with the written leave and approval process, not converted automatically into cash unless the applicable contract and rules support that treatment.

When a value is uncertain, use a low estimate and repeat the worksheet with zero. If the preferred offer changes when one uncertain benefit is removed, that benefit deserves a written clarification before acceptance.

Score the non-financial terms yourself

The evaluator lets you rate career growth, work-life fit, stability and culture on a 0 to 10 scale, then assign your own decision weights. These ratings are your judgments. The tool normalizes the weights by their total and combines them with the financial score. It preserves the order of Offer A and Offer B and never declares a winner.

Define each rating before entering it. Career growth can be supported by a named manager, role scope, learning access, progression criteria and evidence from current employees. Work-life fit can use documented hours, office days, travel, on-call expectations and commute. Stability can consider contract duration, funding or business model without pretending that any employer is risk-free. Culture is better assessed through specific reporting, feedback and decision practices than slogans.

Run more than one weighting set. First use your honest priorities. Then increase the weight on the factor you are most uncertain about. If a small rating change reverses the result, the worksheet has found a decision-sensitive question. Resolve that question with evidence instead of treating the score as precision.

Compare contract conditions before accepting

Review the start date, probation, fixed-term end date, renewal wording, notice, leave, working hours, overtime, mobility, outside-work restrictions, intellectual property, confidentiality, deductions, equipment responsibility and dispute process. Ask for changes in writing. A friendly email summary is not a substitute for an updated offer or contract when the formal document says something else.

Country rules can set minimum rights or limit contract terms. That is why the Kenya, South Africa, Ghana and ILO sources in this guide are used only to show the kinds of particulars that deserve attention. They do not prove that one country's rule applies to another country or to every worker category. If a clause affects immigration status, repayment, non-competition, intellectual property, termination or a substantial bonus, obtain appropriate local advice before signing.

Use the Employment Contract Builder only as a drafting and review aid for its supported countries. It does not certify an employer's contract. Keep the original offer, questions, written answers, final signed document and any incorporated policies together in a private file.

Make the final decision auditable

Save one worksheet version before clarification and another after the employer answers. The Job Offer Evaluator can export CSV, JSON and PDF locally. Give each file a date and avoid storing raw offer details in a public link, shared computer or uncontrolled messaging thread.

Before accepting, confirm that the chosen offer still works when discretionary bonus is set to zero, uncertain benefits are reduced, recurring costs rise and your most important non-financial rating is one point lower. This is not pessimism. It reveals whether the decision depends on one fragile assumption.

Then write a short decision note: the offer selected, the decisive confirmed terms, unresolved risks, the source date for tax or currency estimates, and the signed-document location. The useful outcome is not the highest score. It is a decision that can be traced back to written terms, measured costs, declared priorities and explicit uncertainty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I compare gross salary or take-home pay?

Record the written gross package first, then calculate take-home pay separately with current country rules. The AfroTools worksheet does not infer PAYE, pension, social insurance or other deductions.

How should I value a bonus that is not guaranteed?

Do not treat a discretionary or target bonus as guaranteed cash. Ask for the written eligibility, measure, payment date and recent policy, then enter a conservative user estimate or zero and test the decision again.

Can the Job Offer Evaluator choose the better job for me?

No. It annualizes figures you enter and applies your ratings and weights. It preserves Offer A and Offer B order and does not declare an automatic winner.

Can I compare offers in different currencies?

Convert both offers outside the worksheet using one documented exchange-rate source and date, then test a weaker exchange rate. Also review tax, transfer, work-permit and social-security issues separately.
