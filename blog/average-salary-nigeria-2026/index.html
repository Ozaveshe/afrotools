Average Salary in Nigeria 2026: Survey Data and Limits

Data check: September 27, 2026. There is no single monthly salary figure that represents everyone earning a living in Nigeria. Paylab's live, opt-in employee salary survey displayed an average gross salary of NGN 334,369 per month for its Nigerian job-position data. It showed the central 80% of reported monthly gross pay between NGN 145,473 and NGN 569,500. Those are survey figures for participating employees, not a nationally representative average for all Nigerian workers. They will change as Paylab updates its data.

That distinction matters: the National Bureau of Statistics' Q2 2024 Labour Force Survey found that 85.6% of employed people were self-employed and 14.4% were employees. An employee-only salary survey misses most of that employment mix. Use its figures to frame a formal-job comparison, not to describe the income of all households or self-employed workers.

What the survey measures

Paylab says its figures come from employees who voluntarily complete an anonymous salary questionnaire. Its methodology describes cleaning duplicate and extreme responses and modeling salary levels by position, region, company size, education, experience and age. That makes it a useful job-market signal, but participation is self-selected and the public country page does not establish a representative sample for every Nigerian occupation or state.

The mean and median answer different questions. The mean is pulled upward by high salaries; the median marks the middle observation. Age-band numbers mix industries, roles, cities and experience. Someone comparing a job offer should narrow the comparison to the actual role and total package before treating any of these as a target.

Why role and industry change the answer

The live Paylab country page showed a software-engineer average of NGN 1,585,870 per month and a nursery-school teacher-assistant average of NGN 139,733. Both are survey averages for named positions on that page, not minimums, offers or guaranteed market rates. Their gap illustrates why a single national employee average is a weak benchmark for either job. Position pages can be more relevant, but check the sample and definitions before using them in a negotiation.

Within one title, duties still vary: a software engineer's scope, seniority, employer, equity, on-call work and benefits can change compensation. A salary labelled “gross” may include bonuses and allowances, while take-home depends on payroll deductions. For regulated, public-sector or unionized roles, official pay schedules or a documented employer offer may be stronger evidence than an open survey.

Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt: compare like with like

The Paylab country-wide figures above do not establish a reliable Lagos-versus-Abuja-versus-Port-Harcourt premium. Avoid applying a generic percentage to a national average. Instead, gather dated offers or salary listings for the same role, seniority and employer type in each city. Record whether the number is base pay, total cash compensation or expected net pay.

For a move, compare the complete monthly budget: rent and deposits, transport, utilities, health cover, childcare if relevant, and any employer-funded benefits. The city with a higher gross offer may still leave less disposable income. Keep the actual quotes and dates beside your estimate; housing prices and job offers change too quickly for a static city table to stay reliable.

Entry-level pay and the NYSC distinction

Experience can influence pay, but the increase is not a fixed 60% or 100% at a certain year. Compare responsibilities and the employer's pay structure alongside years worked. A role that manages a team or scarce technical systems is different from a title with the same number of years and narrower duties.

NYSC allowance should be kept separate from a graduate salary. A federal announcement dated 25 March 2025 says the monthly federal allowance rose from NGN 33,000 to NGN 77,000 effective March 2025. State or place-of-primary-assignment support can differ. Our NYSC allowance guide explains the payment components and budgeting questions. Do not use the service allowance as a market salary benchmark.

Remote and foreign-currency roles

A foreign-currency offer can look large when converted to naira, but the conversion is only one part of the comparison. Confirm whether it is employment or contracting, which party pays tax, how frequently currency is converted, who covers equipment, internet, health insurance and pension, and whether there is paid leave. Use the exchange rate on the actual pay or decision date, not an undated rate embedded in a salary article.

There is no trustworthy universal “remote premium” for Nigerian workers. For a specific offer, compare the documented foreign-currency amount after fees, taxes and missing benefits with a dated local offer for the same responsibilities. If you freelance, allow for unpaid time between contracts and business expenses. The side-hustle tax tool can help with an initial scenario; confirm tax treatment with the relevant authority or adviser.

How to benchmark your own salary

  1. Define the comparison. Record the exact job title, responsibilities, experience, location, employer type and whether you are comparing monthly gross, annual gross or take-home pay.
  2. Collect dated evidence. Use current offers or salary listings, a position-specific survey and, where available, an official pay scale. Mark whether each number includes bonuses, allowances, pension or equity.
  3. Prefer a range to one average. The central part of a credible distribution is more informative than a single mean. Ask how many responses support the role and location.
  4. Estimate net pay separately. Run the documented gross offer through the Nigeria Salary Tax Calculator using the correct tax regime and deductions. The calculator estimates PAYE and net pay; it does not rank your salary against other professionals.
  5. Compare the full package. Put benefits, work costs, contract security and paid time off beside the cash offer before deciding.

For a negotiation, lead with the responsibilities you can perform and the strongest role-matched evidence, then present a range with its source and date. The Paylab country average can be a starting point for context, but a recruiter will usually need evidence closer to the job on offer.
