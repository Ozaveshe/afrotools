Togo payroll brings two statutory systems into the same pay run. The Office Togolais des Recettes, or OTR, administers personal income tax on employment income under the Impôt sur le revenu des personnes physiques, commonly shortened to IRPP. The Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale, or CNSS, administers compulsory social security contributions. Employers have to calculate the employee amounts, add the employer contribution, report them to the correct institution and retain evidence that connects each payment to the payroll register.

This guide was verified on August 16, 2026 against the OTR consolidated General Tax Code and Tax Procedures Book updated in 2025, the OTR 2026 fiscal handbook, and current CNSS employer pages. The consolidated code supplies the employment deductions, dependent rules, annual tax bands, withholding duties and tax deadlines. CNSS supplies the current 4% employee and 17.5% employer contribution rates, the contribution base and the monthly declaration timetable.

Calling the entire deduction package “PAYE” is convenient, but it can hide important accounting differences. Employee CNSS reduces employee pay and is relevant to the tax computation. Employer CNSS is an additional business cost. IRPP is tax withheld for OTR. The CNSS declaration and OTR tax remittance are separate obligations even when both fall on the 15th.

What Enters Togo Taxable Employment Income

The tax code's employment income provisions cover salaries, wages, allowances, emoluments and benefits connected with employment. The code also identifies benefits in kind, including housing, domestic staff, utilities, telephone, a vehicle, travel or leave costs, and food. Payroll should not treat a non-cash benefit as non-taxable merely because it never passes through the employee's bank account. The correct value and treatment depend on the statutory provision and the employer's records.

The CNSS contribution base is also broader than basic salary alone. The CNSS employer page describes remuneration including salaries, allowances, bonuses, commissions and benefits in kind, while excluding stated items such as genuine expense reimbursements and family benefits. A payroll setup with one “basic salary” field and no classification of other payments can therefore produce both a tax error and a social contribution error.

The tax code allows compulsory social security and health contributions in the employment income calculation. It then provides a 28% professional deduction after those contributions, limited to the relevant income fraction up to XOF 10 million. That sequence matters. Taking 28% from gross pay before the compulsory employee contribution, or applying the percentage without the ceiling, changes taxable income.

Dependent relief is not an automatic allowance for every person named by an employee. The code defines who qualifies, limits the count to six and provides XOF 10,000 per month for each qualifying dependent. For a full year, that equals XOF 120,000 for one dependent. Employers need a consistent declaration and supporting-document process, plus an effective date when a dependent is added or ceases to qualify.

Classify irregular items before calculating. Bonuses, arrears, termination amounts, director payments, second employments and benefits can require a different timing or evidence analysis from ordinary monthly salary. Record the legal basis and OTR guidance used instead of forcing an unusual payment through a generic recurring-pay field.

Togo IRPP Bands For Employment Income

Article 74 of the consolidated tax code sets the progressive annual schedule below. “Progressive” means each rate applies only to the taxable income slice inside that band. An employee whose taxable income enters the 20% band does not pay 20% on the entire taxable amount.

The code also specifies rounding. Taxable income is rounded down to the nearest XOF 1,000 before the bands are applied, and the resulting tax is rounded down to the nearest XOF 10. Payroll software should make those steps explicit. Ordinary arithmetic rounding or rounding each band independently can create small differences that accumulate across a workforce.

OTR And CNSS Employer Deadlines

The Tax Procedures Book states that an employer withholds the tax at each taxable payment and records the deduction on the payslip. Article 27 requires the employer to remit the withholding by the 15th of the following month with the prescribed statement. The same provision addresses annual regularisation where an amount was not withheld or was adjusted, with a January 15 date for the following year. Article 28 sets January 31 for the annual salary declaration.

CNSS separately requires the employer's declaration, commonly called the Appel de Cotisations or ADC, and contribution payment within the first 15 days of the next month. Its current pages list a 5% late increase for unpaid contributions under the cited social security rule. Employers should confirm the assessed amount directly with CNSS because late-payment consequences depend on the liability and timing.

When the 15th is close, do not assume one portal acknowledgement proves both obligations. Preserve the OTR declaration, OTR payment reference, CNSS ADC, CNSS payment reference and bank evidence as separate records. A payment without a matched declaration can remain difficult to allocate, while a submitted declaration without settlement still leaves an unpaid balance.

The Tax Procedures Book also requires records on which those payments are recorded to be retained for ten years. That legal retention duty does not justify uncontrolled access. Payroll files contain salary and identity information, so store them in a restricted system with an audit trail and a documented disposal process after the applicable period.

A Controlled Monthly Payroll Workflow

1. Freeze the approved inputs. Record starters, leavers, salary changes, bonuses, allowances, benefits, unpaid leave and dependent changes with effective dates. Never overwrite a closed period to make the current month balance.

2. Classify every earning. Separate base salary, cash allowances, reimbursed business expenses, benefits in kind and exceptional payments. Map each item to its IRPP and CNSS treatment, supported by the current official source.

3. Calculate in the statutory order. Build the CNSS base, calculate the 4% employee share, apply allowed employment deductions, dependent relief and statutory rounding, then apply the progressive bands. Calculate the 17.5% employer contribution separately.

4. Reconcile four views. Gross earnings minus employee deductions must equal net pay. Net pay must equal the approved bank file. OTR withholding must equal the tax return and tax control account. Employee plus employer CNSS must equal the ADC and CNSS liability.

5. Review exceptions. Investigate zero tax above an expected threshold, negative net pay, duplicate bank details, missing CNSS identifiers, unusually large month-to-month changes, manual overrides, benefits without values and employee deductions that do not match the control accounts.

6. Submit, pay and archive. Complete the OTR and CNSS processes by the applicable date. Save the approved payroll register, payslips, exception report, declarations, acknowledgements, payment receipts, bank confirmation and correction log under the same period reference.

7. Version the rules. Record the source URL, date checked, rates, thresholds, ceilings, rounding method and approver for each payroll configuration. When a rule changes, add a new effective-dated version instead of rewriting historic calculations.

These controls also protect employees. A payslip should show gross earnings, employee CNSS, taxable income or a clear tax basis, IRPP, any other authorised deduction and net pay. The employer contribution can be shown as employer information, but it must not look like an employee deduction.

Togo PAYE Questions

What is the employee CNSS rate in Togo?

The current official CNSS page lists a 4% employee share. The employer withholds that amount from the contribution base and adds its separate 17.5% share.

When are PAYE and CNSS due?

The OTR procedure rules require employment tax withheld in a month to be remitted by the 15th of the next month. CNSS also sets its employer declaration and contribution payment within the first 15 days of the following month. They remain separate filings and payments.

What is the highest Togo employment tax rate?

The top IRPP rate is 35% on the annual taxable income slice above XOF 20 million. It is a marginal rate, so it does not apply to all taxable income.

How much dependent relief is available?

The code provides XOF 10,000 per month for each qualifying dependent, up to six. A full year equals XOF 120,000 per qualifying dependent, subject to the code's definitions and evidence.
