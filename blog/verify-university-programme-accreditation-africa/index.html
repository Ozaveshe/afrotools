Verify a University and Course Before You Pay

AfroTools TeamSep 27, 202612 min read

A university name can be genuine while a particular course, satellite campus or delivery arrangement is not approved. A private college can use an affiliation claim that is incomplete or out of date. An official-looking offer can also send a student to a personal account or an unrelated payment page. The safest decision comes from matching the offer to current records held by the responsible education authorities.

This guide builds that verification file before you accept admission or pay. It covers university-level study in Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa as practical examples, but it is not a substitute for the regulator in your country. Primary sources and current regulator notices were checked on September 27, 2026. Recheck them on the day you make a decision because institutional, programme, campus and registration status can change.

Verify six separate facts

Do not reduce the task to "is this school accredited?" The offer contains several facts that must agree. A result for one fact does not automatically prove the others.

FactWhat to matchPrimary evidence
InstitutionLegal name, institution type and current statusNational regulator or education ministry register
ProgrammeExact award title and current approval or accreditationProgramme register, accreditation result or regulator confirmation
SiteCampus, learning centre or partner location named in the offerApproved-site record or written regulator confirmation
Delivery modeFull-time, part-time, distance, online, blended or franchise arrangementApproval that covers the offered mode
Award ownerInstitution that will issue the certificate or degreeApproved affiliation, collaboration or award arrangement
Payment routeOfficial fee schedule, invoice, beneficiary and receipt processInstitution portal and written finance-office confirmation

Record the exact wording. "Bachelor of Science in Computer Science" may not be interchangeable with a similarly named diploma, conversion programme or professional certificate. A programme approved at a main campus is not automatically approved at every study centre. An institution authorised to operate is not proof that every advertised award is included in its current scope.

Build a fact sheet from the offer

Copy the offer details into a private worksheet before searching. Keep the institution's legal name, trading name, programme title, qualification level, campus, country, intake, duration, study mode, awarding body, admissions contact, fee amount and payment beneficiary. Save the offer email or portal confirmation as received. Do not upload identity documents or bank details to an unverified checker.

Search using the legal name first. Social-media names, abbreviations and local centre names may not match a regulator record. If the institution changed its name, ask for the legal history and compare it with an official notice. Where a register provides a reference number, qualification identifier or approval year, copy it exactly and keep the page URL plus the date checked.

Use the institution's own official website only after the regulator check. Confirm that the admissions domain, telephone number and finance office match the institution record. Contact details copied into a message by an agent are not independent proof. Type the official domain yourself or follow the link from the regulator where one is supplied.

Nigeria: match the university and the exact programme

The National Universities Commission provides the Nigerian University System portal as a verified source for universities, approved programmes, affiliations and quality assurance information. Start by finding the institution under its official name and ownership category. Open its record rather than relying on a screenshot of a general list.

Then look for the exact academic programme and any affiliation that appears in the offer. The NUC also publishes programme accreditation reports. Read the institution, programme, site and result shown in the relevant report. A result for one programme or affiliated provider does not prove another programme with a similar title.

If an offer comes from a college that says a university will award the degree, verify both sides of the arrangement. Confirm the college, the awarding university, the named programme and the approval for that affiliation. Ask the university through its official admissions or registry channel to confirm that the intake and centre are authorised. Do not pay because the college displays the university's logo.

Kenya: check authorised universities and approved programmes

The Commission for University Education publishes separate records for university status and programmes. Its accreditation section listed approved universities dated September 15, 2026 and accredited academic programmes dated July 31, 2026 when this guide was checked. Use the newest file or searchable record available on the day of your decision.

First confirm the institution in CUE's status of universities record. Then search the academic programmes offered by universities for the exact award. Record the university, title, level and any approval detail shown.

Collaboration and foreign-university arrangements need their own check. CUE's status page advises the public to confirm whether an institution offering a programme under collaboration has authority to do so before enrolment. Match the local institution, foreign or awarding institution, programme and site. A foreign university's recognition in its home country alone does not prove that its Kenyan collaboration is authorised.

Ghana: check the institution, centre and current notices

Use the Ghana Tertiary Education Commission's institution explorer to identify the institution category and current record. Then verify the exact programme and any affiliation directly with GTEC if the public record does not show enough detail. Ask the institution for the accreditation reference and approval period, but confirm those details with GTEC rather than treating the supplied letter as final proof.

Check current public notices as well as the positive register. GTEC published an unrecognised institutions notice dated August 28, 2026. It also published an unaccredited learning centres and campuses notice dated July 8, 2026 and told prospective students to verify institutions, centres and academic programmes directly with the Commission before applying.

This distinction matters. A known university name can appear beside a centre that is not approved for the advertised delivery. Match the location on the offer, not only the parent institution. If teaching happens online or at a partner site, obtain confirmation that the mode and site are covered.

South Africa: registration, accreditation and qualification records

South Africa uses several connected checks. The Council on Higher Education explains that only programmes accredited by the Higher Education Quality Committee can be offered by a higher education institution, and private providers must also be licensed and registered with the Department of Higher Education and Training. The CHE post-school education guidance directs users to the DHET register for private higher education institutions.

The DHET private higher education register was last updated February 28, 2026 when checked. Read the explanatory material, institution status, registered name, sites and qualifications. The register itself warns against publishing or using a list without the related explanatory information and registered qualifications.

For the qualification, search the SAQA registered qualification database. Match the qualification title, identifier, level, status and provider information where shown. A qualification appearing on the National Qualifications Framework does not by itself prove that any business advertising it is authorised to deliver it. The institution and programme checks must still agree.

Treat online, branch and affiliated offers as separate routes

Distance and online delivery can be legitimate, but the delivery mode must be part of the verified offer. Ask whether the programme is approved for online or blended delivery, where examinations take place, which institution holds student records, and which institution awards the final qualification. Confirm any mandatory physical attendance, laboratory, clinical, teaching-practice or internship component.

For a branch campus or learning centre, verify the exact street location and name. Do not assume that approval for a main campus extends to a rented centre in another city. For an affiliated college, confirm who teaches, who admits, who examines, who keeps transcripts and who awards. A marketing partnership, referral relationship or logo licence is not the same as an approved academic affiliation.

Cross-border offers add another layer. Confirm the award in the home jurisdiction and the authority to recruit or deliver in the student's jurisdiction. If you expect to use the qualification for migration, further study or a regulated profession, check the receiving authority before enrolment. The Foreign Degree Recognition Route Checker can identify the decision owner for common study, work, immigration and licensing routes, but it does not issue an equivalency verdict.

Verify the money trail before paying

Accreditation does not make every payment request genuine. Obtain the current fee schedule and invoice through the official student or applicant portal. Confirm the beneficiary name, bank, account reference, currency, deadline, refund terms and receipt process with the institution's finance office using contact details taken from its official domain.

  • Do not pay a personal account because an agent says the institution's account is slow.
  • Do not use a changed beneficiary sent only through chat without independent confirmation.
  • Separate application, acceptance, tuition, accommodation, insurance and agent charges.
  • Ask which charges are refundable and which event triggers a refund or forfeiture.
  • Keep the invoice, transfer confirmation, portal status and official receipt together.

Compare the complete written cost with the School Fees Comparator or the Study Abroad Cost Worksheet. Enter only verified amounts. These tools help organise a private budget; they do not supply current fees or confirm that an offer is genuine.

Create a dated evidence packet

Keep a small, readable file that another person can audit. Save the offer, regulator pages, programme result, site or collaboration evidence, official contact confirmation, fee schedule, invoice, refund terms and receipts. Name each file with the institution, subject and verification date. Use the PDF Workspace locally in your browser to merge or reorder non-sensitive copies where that helps.

Do not place passport numbers, bank credentials or full identity files in a public share link. Redact information that a reviewer does not need. Keep originals unchanged in a secure location and make a separate review copy. If a regulator page changes, your dated record should show what you relied on, but the saved page is not proof that the status remains current later.

Evidence itemMinimum detailRefresh point
Institution recordLegal name, category, status, URL and checked dateBefore acceptance and before first major payment
Programme recordExact title, level, site, mode and approval detailBefore enrolment and if the offer changes
Official confirmationNamed office, official domain, question and dated responseWhen the public record is incomplete
Payment recordInvoice, beneficiary, reference, transfer and receiptFor every payment
Recognition routeReceiving authority and required evidenceBefore relying on the award for another purpose

Stop when the evidence does not agree

Pause the decision if the programme is absent, the centre is not listed, the regulator record is expired or unclear, the award owner denies the arrangement, or the payment beneficiary does not match the confirmed route. Absence from a searchable page is not always final proof of illegality, but it is a reason to seek written confirmation from the regulator before paying.

Do not let a short deadline replace verification. Ask the institution to hold the offer while the regulator answers. If an agent refuses to provide the legal name, programme reference, awarding body, refund terms or official payment route, keep the funds and escalate through the institution and regulator's published channels.

Final prepayment checklist

  • The legal institution name appears in the current official register.
  • The exact programme and qualification level appear in an approved record or written regulator confirmation.
  • The campus, learning centre and delivery mode match the offer.
  • Any affiliation or collaboration is confirmed by the regulator and awarding institution.
  • The admissions contact and applicant portal use the institution's official domain.
  • The fee schedule, invoice, beneficiary and refund terms came through a confirmed route.
  • The evidence packet records every URL and verification date.
  • The receiving authority was checked if the qualification will support migration, licensing, employment or further study.
Decision rule: pay only when the institution, exact programme, site, mode, award owner and money trail agree. If one part remains unverified, pause and ask the responsible regulator.

Frequently asked questions

Is a registered university proof that every course is accredited?

No. Institution status and programme approval are separate checks. Confirm the exact programme, qualification, campus and delivery mode with the responsible regulator.

Can a university logo or offer letter prove accreditation?

No. Verify the legal institution name and programme in the regulator's own current register, then confirm the offer through the institution's official admissions channel.

What should I check for an affiliated college?

Confirm the teaching centre, awarding university, exact programme, collaboration or affiliation approval, approved site and current approval period with the regulator and awarding institution.

Does AfroTools decide whether a course is accredited?

No. AfroTools helps organise the official verification route and evidence. Only the responsible regulator, institution and other competent authorities can confirm current status.
