Which rate applies?

For residential accounts using up to and including 5 m³ per month, the rate is GH₵5.985381 per m³. Above 5 m³, the entire volume is charged at GH₵10.589413 per m³. The monthly residential service charge is GH₵10.

Commercial accounts pay GH₵32.051425 per m³ plus a GH₵20 monthly service charge. Both estimates include a 3% levy on the water charge. Your bill may also include arrears or account adjustments.

PURC tariff schedule effective 1 October 2026, in force until reviewed. Check your customer class and compare the estimate with the PURC water tariff reckoner.
