Which tariff applies?

Cape Town’s 2026/27 Water Wise residential schedule applies from 1 July 2026. For a single metered home on the standard non-indigent tariff, the first 6 kL cost R25.05 per kL, the next 4.5 kL cost R34.42 per kL, the next 24.5 kL cost R54.60 per kL and additional usage costs R109.98 per kL. These prices include VAT.

The city adds a monthly water charge based on your meter diameter and adjusts the consumption bands for the actual meter-reading period. This estimate uses one average month. Registered indigent households, shared or larger meters, and business accounts have different terms. Check your account classification and the full schedule. Sanitation, property rates, arrears and account adjustments are excluded.
