Ethiopia Water Bill Calculator — What You Need to Know

Water tariffs depend on the provider, customer class and effective period. The former national default and automatic 8% charge have been withdrawn because they were not supported by an applicable verified schedule.

The calculation is usage × your flat rate + your other charges. Daily figures assume 30 days. Keep your dated bill or quotation with the result. There is no automatic tax, sewerage, arrears or tier calculation.
