Zaɓi abin da kake son shiryawa a tafiyarka

Kayan aikin da ke ƙasa suna Turanci. Wannan bayanin Hausa yana taimaka maka zaɓar aikin da ya dace; ba kalkuletar kuɗin tafiya ta Hausa ba ce. Ba za mu sauya harshen shafi kai tsaye ba.

Shirya kasafin jirgin sama a Afirka — Turanci

Yi amfani da wannan kayan aiki don tsara kuɗin jirgin sama. Ka duba iyakokin ƙididdiga da bayanan da shafin yake bayarwa; ka nemi sabon farashi daga kamfanin jirgin sama kafin biyan kuɗi.
