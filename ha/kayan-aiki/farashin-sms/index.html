Kwatanta farashin aika SMS da yawa

Shigar da farashin da kamfanoni biyu suka ba ka na ƙasa da hanyar sadarwa ɗaya. Za ka ga jimillar kuɗin wata, kuɗin kowane saƙo da bambancin farashin.

Shirin aika saƙonni

Saƙo ɗaya ga mutane 500 yana nufin saƙonni 500. Idan kamfani ya ƙirga saƙon a matsayin sassa biyu, za a caje sassa 1,000. Tabbatar da adadin sassa daga tsarin kamfanin, musamman idan saƙon yana da dogon rubutu ko haruffa na musamman.

Yi amfani da farashin sashe na wannan ƙasa da wannan nau’in saƙo. Idan an riga an rage farashin, bar rangwame a sifili. Ƙara kuɗin wata da sauran cajin da kamfani ya tabbatar a wuri guda.
