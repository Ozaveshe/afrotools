Abincin dabbobi

Tsara rabon abinci ga shanu, awaki ko tumaki bisa nauyi, matakin samarwa da sinadaran da ake samu. Injin abincin dabbobi na AfroTools yana kiyasta busasshen abinci, furotin, TDN da kuɗi.

Rabon rana ga dabba ɗaya

    Tushe, zato da iyaka

    Tushe
    FAO AFRIS, ka'idojin NRC, da farashin sinadaran Najeriya da aka adana a AfroTools.
    Ranar dubawa
    8 Agusta 2026; farashin ma'ajiyar 2024–2025 ne, ba farashin kai tsaye ba.
    Zato
    Nauyi, busasshen abinci, furotin da TDN sun bi ajin dabbar da aka zaɓa; ingancin sinadari matsakaici ne.
    Matakin tabbaci
    Matsakaici don kasafin farko; ƙwararren ciyarwa ya duba rabo, ma'adanai da lafiyar dabba.
    Iyaka
    Ba magani ko shawarar likitan dabbobi ba ne. Ciki, nono, zafi, cuta da ainihin sinadaran abinci na sauya buƙata.
    Sirri
    Ana yin lissafi a burauzar nan; ba a aika shigarwa zuwa sabar ko URL ba.
