Tushe

Sabuntawa

Tabbaci

Zato
Caji = tsayayyen caji + kaso; canjin mai bayarwa = matsakaicin canji × (1 − ragi).
Iyaka
Wannan kimantawa ce ta shiri, ba sanarwar hukuma, tayin farashi, shawarar kudi ko garantin sakamako ba.
