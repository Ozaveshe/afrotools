Jinsi ya Kupanga Mtiririko wa Fedha wa Biashara

Biashara inaweza kupata faida kwenye karatasi lakini ikakosa fedha ya kulipa supplier au mshahara. Sababu ni muda: mteja anaweza kulipa baada ya siku nyingi wakati stock, kodi ya pango, ushuru na deni vinahitaji fedha mapema. Makisio ya mtiririko wa fedha yanaonyesha fedha zinazoingia na kutoka kwa tarehe zinazotarajiwa.

Faida si fedha iliyopo

Faida hupima mapato ukiondoa gharama kwa kipindi cha uhasibu. Mtiririko wa fedha hupima pesa iliyoingia au kutoka. Invoice inaweza kuonyesha mauzo leo lakini mteja akalipa mwezi ujao. Stock inaweza kulipwa leo na kuuzwa polepole.

IFRS Foundation, IAS 7 Statement of Cash Flows. IAS 7 hutenganisha operating, investing na financing cash flows. Biashara ndogo inaweza kutumia mantiki hiyo kutenganisha mauzo ya kawaida, ununuzi wa equipment na mikopo.

Tumia wiki 13 na miezi 12

Makisio ya wiki 13 yanafaa kwa uamuzi wa karibu. Onyesha salio la mwanzo, mapato yanayotarajiwa, malipo, tofauti na salio la mwisho kila wiki. Makisio ya miezi 12 yanaonyesha misimu, ada za mwaka, tax, bima na uwekezaji.

Sogeza makisio mbele kila wiki. Hii ni document ya uendeshaji, si file ya kuandaa mara moja kwa mkopo.

Anza na salio lililohakikiwa

Linganisha benki, cash, M-Pesa au wallet nyingine na platform za malipo. Usihesabu overdraft ambayo haijatumika kama cash. Tenganisha fedha iliyozuiliwa au inayosubiri settlement.

Andika tarehe ya reconciliation. Salio la mwanzo likiwa kosa, wiki zote zinazofuata zitakuwa na picha isiyo sahihi.

Panga fedha zinazoingia

Tenganisha mauzo ya cash, mobile money, bank transfer, malipo ya invoice, deposit ya mteja, mkopo na fedha ya mwenye biashara. Tumia tarehe ambayo fedha inaweza kupatikana, si tarehe ya kutuma invoice.

Kwa mteja anayechelewa mara kwa mara, tumia tabia yake halisi. Tender, grant au order ambayo haijathibitishwa iwe scenario, si base case.

Panga fedha zinazotoka

Weka stock, supplier, usafiri, mishahara, pango, umeme, data, kodi, leseni, marejesho ya mkopo, maintenance na fedha anazochukua owner kwa tarehe ya malipo. Usifiche gharama za mwaka katika miscellaneous.

Kwa import, tenganisha deposit, balance, freight, insurance, duty, clearing na local delivery. Hii inaonyesha mwezi wenye shinikizo la cash.

Tengeneza scenario tatu

Base case itumie ushahidi bora. Downside icheleweshe mteja mkubwa au ipunguze mauzo. Action case ionyeshe hatua: deposit, order ndogo, supplier terms, collection ya invoice au kuahirisha equipment isiyo ya lazima.

Weka kiwango cha chini cha cash. Kila wiki chini ya kiwango hicho iwe na action, mtu wa kuwajibika na deadline.

Simamia stock, wateja na supplier pamoja

Stock nyingi inafunga fedha. Wateja wanaochelewa kuongeza muda wa kusubiri. Supplier anayehitaji malipo mapema anaongeza gap. Angalia mambo haya kama mfumo mmoja wa working capital.

Growth inaweza kutumia cash kabla ya kuleta cash. Order kubwa inahitaji stock, wafanyakazi na delivery kabla mteja hajalipa.

Linganisha forecast na actual

Kila wiki, linganisha kilichotarajiwa na kilichotokea. Eleza tofauti kama timing, volume, price, cost, exchange rate au unexpected payment. Usibadilishe history ili forecast ionekane sahihi.

Makosa yanayojirudia yanaonyesha sehemu ya kuboresha: invoicing, collection, purchasing, pricing au records.

Makosa ya kawaida

  • Kuhesabu invoice kama cash.
  • Kuhesabu loan au tender kabla fedha kupatikana.
  • Kusahau settlement delay na ada za malipo.
  • Kusahau tax, repairs, licence na owner drawings.
  • Kutabiri growth bila stock au capacity.
  • Kufungua forecast wakati wa crisis tu.

Mipaka

Makisio haya ni ya kupanga. Hayabadilishi accounting, sheria ya kodi, masharti ya mkopo au ushauri wa kifedha. Tumia data ya biashara yako na hakiki wajibu wa eneo lako.

Muundo wa spreadsheet

Weka column kwa wiki au mwezi na row kwa aina ya cash flow. Assumptions ziwe sehemu tofauti. Kwa invoice kubwa, hifadhi mteja, invoice, expected date, confidence na collection action. Kwa supplier, hifadhi commitment date, due date na priority.

Linda formula na hifadhi version yenye tarehe. Jedwali liwe rahisi kusoma kwenye simu au kuchapishwa kwa meeting.

Growth inaweza kutumia cash

Mauzo mengi yanaweza kuhitaji stock, packaging, temporary staff, transport na promotion kabla ya mteja kulipa. Unganisha sales forecast na purchasing plan. Vinginevyo forecast itaonyesha mapato lakini isionyeshe fedha ya kuyatengeneza.

Kwa service business, angalia hours, contractor, travel na software capacity kabla ya kutabiri project nyingi.

Collection ni process

Tuma invoice sahihi mapema, hakikisha mteja amepokea documents na tatua dispute kabla ya due date. Gawa wateja kwa tabia ya malipo badala ya kutumia assumption moja kwa wote.

Deposit na milestone payment zinaweza kupunguza gap ikiwa ziko wazi kwenye agreement. Lengo ni masharti yanayolingana na delivery.

Maswali ya meeting

  • Receipt gani tatu zinaathiri wiki nne zijazo?
  • Payment gani haiwezi kuahirishwa?
  • Cash buffer itavunjwa lini?
  • Assumption gani imebadilika?
  • Action gani inalinda cash bila kuharibu trust?
  • Season gani inahitaji fedha mapema?

Mpango wa siku 30

Wiki 1, reconcile cash, benki na wallet na uteue owner wa data. Wiki 2, tengeneza list ya receivables, supplier, mshahara, tax na debt. Wiki 3, jenga forecast ya wiki 13 na miezi 12 na downside scenario. Wiki 4, fanya variance meeting na gawa action.

Model ya kwanza haitakuwa perfect. Pima forecast hitilafu badala ya kuongeza formula nyingi. Baada ya weekly cycles nne, timing ya wateja na supplier itakuwa na evidence bora.

Early warning signs

Angalia cash buffer kushuka, invoice delays kuongezeka, purchase zisizopangwa, stock polepole, owner drawings na tax inayokusanyika. Signal moja si diagnosis, lakini signal kadhaa kwa pamoja zinahitaji review.

Andika maelezo kwa kila alert. Tenganisha season ya kawaida, delay ya muda na gap ya kudumu inayohitaji price, cost, financing au business-model decision.

Nyaraka za forecast

  • Bank, cash na wallet reconciliation.
  • Invoice list na expected dates.
  • Supplier commitments na due dates.
  • Payroll, tax na debt calendar.
  • Stock purchase plan.
  • Assumption log.
  • Action register yenye owner na deadline.
