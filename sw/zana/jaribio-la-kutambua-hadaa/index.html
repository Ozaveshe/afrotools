Faragha na AI

Usibandike ujumbe halisi, account data au credentials. App hutumia mifano yake tu; export/save ina answer indexes na score, si scenario contacts.

Hakuna AI. Kuchanganua ujumbe halisi kwa AI kungehitaji kuonyesha exact content na ridhaa ya wazi; quiz ya ndani ingebaki.

Ujumbe wa shaka
