## Product and formula repair

The previous English runtime depended on inline logic and a `PENSION_SYSTEMS` object whose schema did not match the page: the page expected `growthRates` and `currencySymbol`, while the data supplied `fundGrowth` and `symbol`; string notes were iterated as characters. It also supplied fallback country returns and a fixed 4% drawdown despite the source contract forbidding supplied country rates, forecasts, benefits, annuities, or drawdowns.

The readable DOM-free owner is now `engines/src/pension-fund-projection-engine.js`; `engines/pension-fund-projection-engine.js` is generated by `scripts/minify.js`. English and Swahili use the same controller and engine. Country selection supplies display context and a changeable ISO currency only. The user must enter and confirm:

- current and retirement ages;
- monthly salary, current balance, combined contribution rate, and salary growth;
- gross return, annual fee, inflation, and illustrative drawdown assumptions;
- the statement, provider rule, or scheme source checked and its date.

The engine compounds monthly after fee drag, grows salary annually, separates contributions from investment growth, reports nominal and inflation-adjusted balances, calculates only the user-entered illustrative drawdown, and produces a deterministic minus/plus two-percentage-point range. Sources older than 366 days, missing confirmations, invalid ages, and invalid numeric ranges fail closed.
