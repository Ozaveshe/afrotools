## Reproduction and change

Before the change, delaying `assets/js/pages/mobile-money-quote-parity.js` on the French page while entering synthetic quotes and using Enter/pointer produced three document requests: the initial route and two native `?` reloads. The regression test failed its one-document-request assertion. The controller could be cancelled before attaching the submit listener.

The generated EN/FR/SW family now embeds its small, readable readiness guard in the head without an extra network dependency. Both forms start with disabled action buttons and localized loading feedback. Quote inputs remain editable. Capturing submit protection prevents native submission before initialization. The third-quote toggle is enabled with the controller so an early toggle cannot be lost. Initialization enables actions without resetting entered values. Tariff actions wait for the catalogue independently of manual quote comparison.

Failed engine/controller requests leave actions disabled, retain drafts, and explain that users should copy their entries before reloading to retry. A 15-second fallback covers stalled requests or initialization exceptions; a later successful initialization can still make the form ready. No automatic reload or transmission of input was added. JavaScript-disabled users receive localized guidance and can read existing tariff tables/source links.

No fee/rate/catalogue, calculation engine, source date, numerical oracle expectation, route, privacy consent, or export format changed.
