## South Africa: confirmed model mismatch

Primary text read: Constitutional Court, *Van Wyk*, [2025] ZACC 20, decided 3 October 2025, [Parliament-hosted judgment](https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/OISD/ISD_Related_Constitutional_Court_Judgements/2026/5-may/14-05-2026/judgment-for-issuance-cct-309-23-cct-308-23-werner-van-wyk-others-v-minister-for-employment-and-labour-cge-and-others.pdf).

Printed pages 3–5, order paragraphs 4–5: invalidity is suspended for 36 months, with substituted BCEA wording pending remedial legislation. A single parent or only employed parent has four consecutive months; two employed parents share four months plus ten days. Allocation, birth-related protections and notification conditions apply. A fixed independent maternity-week/paternity-day model cannot reproduce this order. Benefits are a separate matter.

The [Department of Employment and Labour statement of 27 February 2026](https://www.labour.gov.za/Media-Desk/Media-Statements/Pages/Labour-Law-Amendment-Bills-and-Notice-published-by-the-Department-benefitting-Employees-and-Workers--.aspx), read in full, describes proposed amendment bills. It is not evidence of enactment or commencement. Do not implement its adoption-age proposal as enacted law.

Before a current-law calculator repair: establish whether remedial legislation has commenced since that statement; record employee scope, calendar-month arithmetic and allocation conditions; add household employment/allocation inputs rather than silently assigning the shared amount to each parent. Preserve UIF/payment uncertainty separately. Existing free-duration planning remains useful but is not statutory verification.
