## Record policies and compatibility

- Invoice/expense amounts must be finite, non-negative, at most two decimal places and within safe cent arithmetic. Genuine zero values require a note and remain draft/review or unpaid; payments must be positive. An invalid value never becomes 1000 or another fabricated amount.
- Payments reference an existing selected invoice, match its currency and cannot exceed remaining value. Editing reuses IDs and recomputes received/status from recorded allocations. Legacy received balances without underlying payment records require reconciliation instead of guessed allocation. Multi-line/discount legacy invoices are preserved; this simple editor refuses to flatten them.
- CSV uses the existing eight-column expense contract with quoted-field handling, a 1 MB limit, explicit row errors and a deliberate Save valid rows only action. Zero handling is explicit. Canonical row keys prevent retry duplicates; all valid rows are reviewed, with no former silent 30-row truncation.
- Books/HR existing storage keys and extension fields are preserved. Backups are additive, not destructive migrations. Read-only previews export original bytes including malformed history. Exported unknown history is not proof of genuine operations.
- Manual account actions remain explicit. Failed save/pull, local quota errors and tab conflicts do not authorize replacing records. There is no automatic account upload.
- Legacy device storage is shared across signed-in accounts on that device. Account/client/workspace isolation is **not proven** by this work. Assigning ownership or namespacing legacy records requires a reviewed migration and account-binding validation; no permission or schema change is made here.
- Public routes/canonicals/noindex app boundaries, free calculator formulas, existing exports, auth/session keys and subscription prices/IDs are preserved. Newly added continuation stores only route context.
- Analytics use existing events plus non-sensitive offer/source/app/action metadata. No names, record amounts, record contents, emails, tokens or financial/document payloads are added to measurement. Events are not revenue or delivered-service evidence.
