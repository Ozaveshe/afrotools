# Car price evidence and expansion

## Public workflow

`/cars/` is the buyer entry point. It shows 25 older source-budget starter vehicles and source-linked local asking-price observations where available. Its full search covers those 25 plus 456 more make/model/year options from the import-duty catalog (481 total) and opens an editable quote in `/tools/car-import-cost/` for one of six supported destinations. The 481 options are catalog identities, not 481 verified market prices. `/tools/import-duty/` remains the general-goods calculator; its vehicle selector hands off to the car import workspace.

## Price evidence contract

- `data/cars/market-observations.json` holds small, dated aggregates of asking prices. Keep make, model, year, sampled variant when narrower than the catalog model, market, condition, currency, sample count, quartiles, reviewed date, source URL, method, and limitations together. Check exact-year results and exclude wrong-year, wrong-condition, contradictory, or duplicate ads. Record independent marketplace checks separately rather than blending their selections. Set `searchIndexEligible: false` when a useful buyer snapshot is too thin or poorly matched to warrant a crawlable vehicle page; the price remains visible in the directory and detail view.
- A listing ask is neither a completed sale nor an assessed customs value. Default to "no sourced local sample" when a country/vehicle pair lacks a traceable observation. Do not promote `data/cars/import-duty-vehicle-estimates.csv` comparable estimates as observed local prices.
- The current observation freshness review window is 14 days. An old observation may remain as a dated historical snapshot; never move its date forward without reviewing the source again. Run `npm run cars:market:observations:check` to see stale and missing coverage.
- The 2026-09-27 Lagos review covers eleven starter vehicles. The second batch added 2018 Hyundai Elantra, 2016 Honda CR-V, and 2014 Honda Accord; the CR-V sample excludes a listing whose displayed ask conflicts with its description. A third batch added 2017 Mercedes E-Class (E300 offers), 2017 Lexus RX (RX350 offers), and 2016 Lexus ES (ES350 offers). The Lexus ES record also shows a separate five-listing Lagos check from Autochek. A fourth review added ten 2005 Camry Local Used asks; two ambiguous or wrong-year ads were excluded, and this older car's local price must not be read as a viable new-import scenario. A fifth review retained five 2020 CR-V Foreign Used asks after excluding a wrong-year description and one near-duplicate silver offer. Its trim and mileage match is weak, so it is buyer-visible but `noindex`. Each observation is a small search snapshot, not a quote for a particular car or a different engine variant.
- The [2020 Honda CR-V Jiji search](https://jiji.ng/lagos/cars/honda-cr-v-2020) exposed seven offers in the cached page; one described a 2021 car and two silver ads had near-identical copy. One of those two was retained, leaving five asks. [Autochek's Lagos foreign-used CR-V results](https://autochek.africa/ng/cars-for-sale/honda/cr-v/foreign/lagos) showed only one 2020 vehicle with substantially higher mileage, so it was not blended or called corroboration. The [2017 Kia Sportage search](https://jiji.ng/lagos/cars/kia-sportage-2017) repeated seller copy and had too few distinct offers for a stable band. The [2016 Prado search](https://jiji.ng/lagos/cars/toyota-land-cruiser-prado-2016) was mainly V6 or upgraded vehicles, unlike the 2.7/3.0 seed source budget. The [2015 X-Trail search](https://jiji.ng/lagos/cars/nissan-x-trail-2015) had only one matching Foreign Used offer. Keep these unresolved pairs in the research queue; do not turn a category result count into a price sample.
- Jiji and other marketplaces retain rights to listing photos, descriptions, and seller details. Store original aggregates and source links only. Use approved/licensed AfroTools car imagery from the media library, labelled as illustrative. Do not collect phone numbers or seller identities.
- Source-market seed budgets and FX conversions are planning estimates. The car import calculator must allow an actual purchase quote and show its duties, freight, port, clearing, registration, FX, and rule-pack assumptions. Customs and tax changes need verified authority evidence through the trade source lane.

## Scheduled collection

The existing `transport-source-freshness-sweep` Codex cron runs daily at 11:00 Asia/Tashkent. It reviews a bounded car batch against accessible marketplaces, checks the observation audit, stages only source-linked aggregate changes, runs the focused car tests, and submits a producer handoff. It does not publish itself. Do not schedule `cars:market:refresh` unattended: its current refresh script can write scraped samples directly into the import estimate CSV, without the observation review gate. The publisher owns integration and production proof.

## SEO and product backlog

The 2026-09-08 GSC sample includes car import links to unsupported country routes that returned 404. The directory now only links to supported six-country calculator paths; confirm those old URLs redirect correctly on the deployed site. Keep the root, countries with dated local price evidence, and evidence-rich vehicle pages crawlable. Country pages without such evidence, make/model, duplicate import-vs-local, and unobserved year pages remain user routes with `noindex`; adding more near-identical pages is not a substitute for fresh market data.

The French car generator still keeps roughly 1,081 user routes, but only the hub, Nigeria country page, and the ten search-eligible Nigeria model-year pages are indexable in the 2026-09-27 source snapshot. The thin 2020 CR-V snapshot is visible to buyers but `noindex`; unobserved French pages show an honest empty state for local price. Nonindex pages are removed from reciprocal English/French hreflang. The French hub is an observation picker linking to the same editable import quote; it no longer presents a second calculation. `npm run fr:surface:build` regenerates these pages from the reviewed observation JSON, and `npm run cars:fr:check` checks the evidence/search contract. A source observation over 14 days old loses model-page indexability on the next generation; its dated historical value is not silently refreshed.

Recheck the final sitemap, route canonicals, mobile browsing flow, structured data, Search Console coverage, and deployed URLs after publishing. Repository output alone cannot prove Google has recrawled or ranked the revised pages.
