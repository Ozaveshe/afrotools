# Car price evidence and expansion

## Public workflow

`/cars/` is the buyer entry point. It shows 27 priced starter vehicles and source-linked local asking-price observations where available. Most source-market bands are older planning estimates; the 2018 Camry and RAV4 have dated UAE asking-price samples. Its full search covers those 27 plus 455 more make/model/year options from the import-duty catalog (482 unique options total) and opens an editable quote in `/tools/car-import-cost/` for one of six supported destinations. The 482 options are catalog identities, not 482 verified market prices. `/tools/import-duty/` remains the general-goods calculator; its vehicle selector hands off to the car import workspace.

## Price evidence contract

- `data/cars/market-observations.json` holds small, dated aggregates of asking prices. Keep make, model, year, sampled variant when narrower than the catalog model, market, condition, currency, sample count, quartiles, reviewed date, source URL, method, and limitations together. Check exact-year results and exclude wrong-year, wrong-condition, contradictory, or duplicate ads. Record independent marketplace checks separately rather than blending their selections. Set `searchIndexEligible: false` when a useful buyer snapshot is too thin or poorly matched to warrant a crawlable vehicle page; the price remains visible in the directory and detail view.
- `data/cars/source-market-observations.json` holds the same reviewed aggregate evidence for purchase markets, plus source snapshot date and currency conversion provenance. `npm run cars:catalog:refresh` projects both ledgers into the interactive price pack. The audit checks the projections and flags local or source-market snapshots older than 14 days; it does not silently refresh or automatically publish them.
- A listing ask is neither a completed sale nor an assessed customs value. Default to "no sourced local sample" when a country/vehicle pair lacks a traceable observation. Do not promote `data/cars/import-duty-vehicle-estimates.csv` comparable estimates as observed local prices.
- The current observation freshness review window is 14 days. An old observation may remain as a dated historical snapshot; never move its date forward without reviewing the source again. Run `npm run cars:market:observations:check` to see stale and missing coverage.

## Scheduled collection

The existing `transport-source-freshness-sweep` Codex cron runs daily at 11:00 Asia/Tashkent in an isolated worktree. It reviews three to five missing or aging car pairs against accessible marketplaces, checks both local and source-market observation ledgers, records exact variant, condition, sample selection, source date and currency conversion, stages only reviewed source-linked aggregates, runs the focused car tests, and submits a producer handoff. A new active priced vehicle also needs a matching reviewed source-market band and approved licensed or project-owned illustration. It does not publish itself. The `trade-desk-source-automation` cron reviews customs sources on Tuesdays at 10:00; a marketplace asking price must not alter its official-rule data. Do not schedule `cars:market:refresh` unattended: its current refresh script can write scraped samples directly into the import estimate CSV, without the observation review gate. The publisher owns integration and production proof.

## SEO and product backlog

The [AfroTools domain performance report filtered to URLs containing `/cars/`](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Aafrotools.com&num_of_days=28&page=*%2Fcars%2F) showed 57 Web search clicks, 9.65K impressions, 0.6% CTR, and average position 13.5 for the last complete 28 days (2026-08-28 through 2026-09-24). Search Console displayed 983 page rows for this filter and warns that filtered totals and tables may be partial. The high-impression pages included `/cars/ethiopia/mercedes-benz/g-wagon/` (302 impressions, 0 clicks), `/cars/rwanda/mercedes-benz/` (264, 1), `/cars/ghana/toyota/corolla/2018/` (262, 0), and `/cars/zambia/mercedes-benz/g-wagon/2022/` (214, 1). The Nigeria hub had 100 impressions and 1 click. High-impression queries included “g wagon price in ethiopia 2026” (130, 0), “g wagon price in zambia” (110, 0), and “toyota corolla 2018 price in ghana” (62, 0). This is a pre-release baseline for the source branch, not proof that its SEO changes have improved ranking.

The live [Nigeria car directory](https://afrotools.com/cars/nigeria/) still displayed the older 25-car list and source-market seed bands dated 2026-03-16 when checked on 2026-09-27. The 27-car, source-linked revision and the `noindex` policy for unsupported country/model/year pages exist only in the isolated branch until published. After the exact branch build is released, recheck live page content and robots/canonical tags, then compare the same Search Console filter after recrawl. Impressions on thin pages show demand, but they do not establish that an unsupported price estimate deserves an indexable landing page; add verified local evidence before making those pages search-facing.

The 2026-09-08 GSC sample includes car import links to unsupported country routes that returned 404. The directory now only links to supported six-country calculator paths; confirm those old URLs redirect correctly on the deployed site. Keep the root, countries with dated local price evidence, and evidence-rich vehicle pages crawlable. Country pages without such evidence, make/model, duplicate import-vs-local, and unobserved year pages remain user routes with `noindex`; adding more near-identical pages is not a substitute for fresh market data.

The French car generator keeps 1,084 user routes, but only the hub, Nigeria country page, and the twelve search-eligible Nigeria model-year pages are indexable in the 2026-09-27 source snapshot. The French hub is an observation picker linking to the same editable import quote; it no longer presents a second calculation. `npm run fr:surface:build` regenerates these pages from the reviewed observation JSON, and `npm run cars:fr:check` checks the evidence/search contract. A source observation over 14 days old loses model-page indexability on the next generation; its dated historical value is not silently refreshed.

Recheck the final sitemap, route canonicals, mobile browsing flow, structured data, Search Console coverage, and deployed URLs after publishing. Repository output alone cannot prove Google has recrawled or ranked the revised pages.
