# Original SSCE Physics practice: source and answer review

Reviewed 2026-09-28 for questions p13–p24. These are AfroTools-authored, yearless practice items. No question, numerical scenario, option set, graph or answer was taken from an examination paper. The WAEC pages below informed topic selection only; they do not make the items official WAEC or NECO coverage.

## Topic signals

- [WAEC Physics Paper 2 examiner report, school candidates 2019](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/Physics/Phys228mw.html) identifies difficulty with velocity–time graph interpretation and missing units. That informed p13–p15 and the unit-bearing answer choices.
- [WAEC Physics Paper 3 examiner report, private candidates second series 2019](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/Physics/Phys229cw.html) identifies slope, graph scale and unit errors. That informed the separate slope and area reasoning in p13–p15.
- [WAEC Physics Paper 3A examiner report, school candidates 2015](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/Physics/Phys3224mw.html) identifies difficulty evaluating tabulated values, plotting graphs and making deductions. That informed the fully stated force–extension data in p16.

The in-bank guidance links follow each item: p13–p15 use the 2019 WAEC graph report; p16 uses the 2015 WAEC practical report; p17 uses [OpenStax speed and velocity](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/2-2-speed-and-velocity); and p18–p20 use [OpenStax Newton’s second law](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/4-3-newtons-second-law-of-motion). These links support study of the concepts, not provenance of a question or a claim of official paper coverage.
