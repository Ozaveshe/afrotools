## Official source boundary

The [Federal Ministry of Youth Development Youth Initiative article](https://yid.fmyd.gov.ng/nysc-monthly-allowance-increased-to-%E2%82%A677000-a-milestone-in-youth-empowerment/), published25March2025, reports₦77,000 from March2025. The federal figure is an editable historical source-backed starting assumption, not a newly verified current individual payment. An [official Federal Ministry of Information report dated7November2025](https://fmino.gov.ng/fgs-nysc-allowance-increase-spurs-abias-%E2%82%A650000-boost-for-corps-members/) also refers to the federal₦77,000 figure and illustrates that state support has different eligibility conditions. No state amount or blanket state entitlement is inferred or prefilled. Users enter confirmed state/PPA amounts and periods themselves, as in the maintained English owner. No NYSC account, bank, private record or live AfroTools API is accessed.

The official article was found through primary-domain search during this source review; direct article opens had timeouts. Retrieval of a dated announcement is not a live-payroll or new-policy freshness guarantee. The dated premise is stated on page and in TXT; later rate changes must come through reviewed source updates. The route does not encode service-policy reform details beyond the existing1–12month planning range.
