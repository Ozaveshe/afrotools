Straight answers about limits

What it will not do

  • Outputs are planning estimates, not tax, accounting, legal, or filing advice.
  • Rates and statutory rules change with finance acts — verify with the revenue authority before you file or pay.

Privacy, plainly

Payroll and invoice details are sensitive. The calculators and invoice builder run in your browser, and salaries, client names, invoice contents, and tax numbers are not included in the usage data AfroTools collects.

Questions people actually ask

Can AfroTools AI file my taxes or payroll?

No. It prepares the figures and the review checklist; filing stays with you, your accountant, or the revenue authority's own portal. That separation is deliberate.

Are the payroll numbers on this page real?

Yes — the worked example is computed in your browser when the page loads, by the same engine the PAYE calculator uses, on the assumptions listed under it. Open the calculator and change the assumptions, and you will see the numbers move.

Do sponsors or partners change the numbers?

Never. Sponsored placements are labeled and cannot alter a calculation, an eligibility result, or a source label anywhere on AfroTools.
