Straight answers about limits

What it will not do

  • Import estimates are planning figures — the final customs assessment can differ, and HS classification needs professional confirmation.
  • AfroTools does not replace a licensed clearing agent, customs broker, or the revenue authority's own valuation.

Privacy, plainly

What you plan to import is commercial information. AfroTools records the broad category and country of a trade question, not the product, supplier, or amounts you enter.

Questions people actually ask

Are the duty numbers on this page real?

The worked example is computed in your browser by the same engine behind the Import Duty Calculator, on the inputs listed under it. The 20% band is a planning default — the whole point is that you can edit it once you confirm your item's band.

Why does it keep telling me to confirm with customs?

Because classification and valuation are decisions the authority makes, not the calculator. A tool that presented its defaults as final would misprice shipments — ours shows its assumptions and makes every rate editable instead.

Can it find my HS code?

The HS Code Lookup gets you to a likely heading fast, and the advisor carries it into the duty estimate. Final classification still belongs to customs or your broker — treat the lookup as the starting point it is.
