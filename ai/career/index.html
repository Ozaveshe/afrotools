Straight answers about limits

What it will not do

  • AfroTools cannot verify your work history, degrees, or references — generated text must stay truthful and edited by you.
  • Do not paste sensitive CV content into optional AI features unless you understand and consent to what is sent.

Privacy, plainly

Your CV is sensitive. The CV and cover-letter tools run in your browser, and your CV text is never put into the page address or into a link you share. Optional AI writing help runs only when you ask for it.

Questions people actually ask

Will it write my whole CV for me?

It sets up the structure, suggests wording, and checks readability — but it will not invent employers, dates, degrees, or achievements. The truthful content is yours; the assistant makes it read well.

Is my CV uploaded anywhere?

The CV Builder runs in your browser and your CV text is never placed in a URL or share link. Optional AI writing help is the one exception, and it asks for your consent before any text is sent.

What does the ATS check actually test?

It checks the things applicant-tracking systems commonly trip on — structure, headings, parseable dates, and keyword coverage against the role you named — and shows you what to fix, not just a score.
