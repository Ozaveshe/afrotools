Straight answers about limits

What it will not do

  • AfroTools is not a licensed architect, engineer, or quantity surveyor — outputs are planning notes for a qualified professional.
  • Material prices and labour rates vary by location and date; the bundled rates are defaults to replace with local quotes.

Privacy, plainly

Property details and budgets stay private to your device. Keep exact addresses, client names, and private plans out of any link you share from this page.

Questions people actually ask

Can I build from these plans?

No — they are planning drawings and quantity estimates, not approved construction documents. Take them to an architect or engineer for the real thing; they make that conversation faster and cheaper.

How accurate is the cost estimate?

As accurate as planning-grade defaults can be — which is why every export marks its rates as figures to replace with supplier and contractor quotes. The value is the structure and the quantities, not the last digit.

Does it handle renovations, not just new builds?

Yes — the Lagos kitchen example on this page is a renovation run. Room-by-room renovation budgeting has its own tool, and the router sends you there when the question is about an existing building.
