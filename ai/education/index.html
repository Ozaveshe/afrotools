Straight answers about limits

What it will not do

  • AfroTools does not guarantee admission, funding, visa approval, or scholarship availability.
  • Deadlines, fees, and proof-of-funds rules change quickly — verify on the university, funder, and government pages before you apply.

Privacy, plainly

Study plans include personal academic details. Matching scholarships and destinations happens on this page without sending anything to an AI model. If you want a written study-plan brief, you are asked first and can skip it.

Questions people actually ask

Can AfroTools AI apply or get me admitted?

No. It matches scholarships, prices the route, and builds your document checklist. Applications, admissions, and visa decisions stay with the institutions — no tool changes that.

Where do the scholarship listings come from?

From the public scholarship feed AfroTools maintains, with each award carrying its deadline and eligibility notes. The feed's coverage state is shown rather than hidden — if something looks stale, the date tells you.

Does it work without IELTS or final grades?

Yes. Tell it what you are missing — the Cameroon-to-Germany example on this page starts with no IELTS — and the plan includes the test or document as a step instead of blocking on it.
