Straight answers about limits

What it will not do

  • Solar sizing and payback are planning estimates, not installer quotes or engineering designs.
  • Fuel prices and tariffs move faster than any bundled dataset — the tools show review dates, and you should confirm locally before spending.

Privacy, plainly

A business electricity bill and its running hours say a lot about the business. Those figures stay on your device unless you choose to use the optional AI write-up.

Questions people actually ask

Will it tell me exactly what system to buy?

No — it gives you a rough size, a payback estimate, and the installer questions that protect you: load audit, site survey, warranties, after-sales support. The final design belongs to a qualified installer.

Are the fuel prices live?

They are dated snapshots, and the date is always shown. Pump prices move faster than any dataset, which is why the generator tools ask for your local price instead of assuming one.

Does it work for homes as well as businesses?

Yes — the same maths runs for a home, shop, office, school, or farm. The Nairobi example on this page is a home running a generator four hours a day.
