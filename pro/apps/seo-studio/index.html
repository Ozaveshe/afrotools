Search performance

Use your own evidence to decide what to review next. Import two single-period English Search Console Pages or Queries CSVs with the same filters and equal-length, non-overlapping date ranges.

How to prepare the exports
  1. Open Search Console → Performance → Search results. Select Web, your country/device filters and the previous date range.
  2. Export the Pages or Queries table as CSV. Repeat for the current date range with identical filters.
  3. Enter the exact inclusive dates below. Pages must match this project's origin. Export coverage can omit anonymized queries and less frequent rows; it is not the full site total.

Each file: at most 2 MB and 10,000 rows. Comparison exports, localized headings and ZIP files are not supported. Pages and Queries are separate aggregate views and are never joined into invented query-to-page matches.
