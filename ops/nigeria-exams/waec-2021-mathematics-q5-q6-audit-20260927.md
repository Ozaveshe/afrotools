## Independent arithmetic

| Guide | Verified source givens | Independent result | Common learner trap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q5(a) | Five shares 5%, 15%, 10%, 45%, 25%; draw a pie chart | Multiply each by 3.6°: 18°, 54°, 36°, 162°, 90°. Their sum is 360°. | Drawing sectors without calculating or checking all five angles |
| Q5(b) | 5 red, 3 green, 4 blue beads; two red draws without replacement | Total 12; (5/12) × (4/11) = 20/132 = 5/33 ≈ 0.1515. | Reusing 5/12 on the second draw |
| Q6(a) | 80 learners; 3/4 Biology, 3/5 Physics, all study at least one | Biology 60, Physics 48, union 80; intersection 60 + 48 − 80 = 28. Biology only 32, or 32/80 = 2/5 of the class. Venn regions 32 + 28 + 20 = 80. | Using Biology's 60 as denominator for the requested class fraction |
| Q6(b) | 15 m × 8 m office floor; GH¢890/m² carpeting; GH¢216,120 combined spend | Area 120 m²; carpet GH¢106,800; painting GH¢216,120 − GH¢106,800 = GH¢109,320. | Subtracting the unit price rather than total carpet cost |

The worked images agree with these results. Automated tests recompute all four calculations from the givens and keep the English, French and Swahili result strings/source links aligned.
