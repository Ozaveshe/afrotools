# JAMB Mathematics 2022: first reviewed publisher-collection batch

Checked 2026-09-26. The [Myschool filtered collection](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2022) labels these as JAMB Mathematics 2022. Its list positions are publisher order, **not authenticated UTME question numbers or a complete sitting**. AfroTools publishes sixteen original adapted prompts and explanations, not copies of the collection pages, source images, videos or publisher prose. No official key, exam-board approval or publisher licence is asserted. The owner authorised use of existing past-question sources; that does not authenticate the publisher's date or confer third-party rights.

The source check used the collection's [page 1](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2022), [page 3](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2022&page=3), [page 4](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2022&page=4), [page 5](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2022&page=5) and [page 6](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2022&page=6). Individual item pages were available for positions 1, 4, 5, 13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 23, 25 and 26. Position 2's individual page was unavailable to the web reader, but the collection entry and matching [EduPadi 2022 entry](https://edupadi.com/classroom/lessons/jamb/mathematics/2022/page/1) independently display its binary input and choices. The page 6 collection itself displays the full prompts and choices for positions 27–30; individual item links could not be fetched by the web reader. Those four receive mathematical, not publisher-key, answer verification. This is sufficient for an adapted collection item but not an original-paper authenticity claim.
