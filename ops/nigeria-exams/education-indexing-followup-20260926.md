The largest not-indexed reasons were 10,724 redirects, 2,378 alternate pages with proper canonical tags, 2,342 404s, and 1,010 noindex pages. Those categories are not one repair queue. The visible 404 examples mix old or malformed URLs (`/mwaka</td></tr>`, an `/undefined` suffix and a wildcard route), an inaccessible data directory, and country-specific car-import URLs.
