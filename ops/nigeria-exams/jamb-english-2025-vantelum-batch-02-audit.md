## Held positions

- **23:** The publisher keys *their dear lives* while another offered choice is the established fixed idiom *for dear life*. A [Nigerian English grammar discussion](https://punchng.com/between-for-dear-life-and-for-their-lives/) explains the distinction. No unique dependable answer survives.
- **28:** The publisher calls *disgusted with* uniquely correct, but [Cambridge examples](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/example/english/disgust) show both *disgusted by* and *disgusted with* in comparable contexts; *at* also occurs. The prompt does not separate the meanings.
- **38:** Formal singular *neither of ... is* is valid, but [Cambridge grammar](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/neither-neither-nor-and-not-) also accepts informal plural agreement. The publisher does not specify a register, so the alternative is not uniquely wrong.

This ten-item batch raises the local publisher-labelled 2025 English collection from 55 to 65 reviewed practice items after generated pages are rebuilt. It does **not** close the authenticated recent-year past-paper acquisition gap.
