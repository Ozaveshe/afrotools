## WAEC 2022 General Mathematics Paper 2

- [Q1(a)](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/Mathematics/maths235mq1.html): original algebraic terms are lost in HTML. The official worked image implies terms 7−2x, 9 and 5x+17, giving 10x²−x−38=0 and x=2 or x=−19/10. Verify the prompt against an original paper before publication.
- [Q2](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/Mathematics/maths235mq2.html): official image gives y=(pr/m−p²r)^(−3/2) with y=−8, m=1, p=3. Worked r=−1/24 yields y=+8 over the reals. Hold.
- [Q5](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/Mathematics/maths235mq5.html): part (a) asks for p+√q, while the solution is 1−√3; part (b) omits a union condition, so overlap of 15 glasses wearers and 10 watch wearers among 20 women can be 5–10. Hold.
- [Q8](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/Mathematics/maths235mq8.html): the official page supplies the percentage table and parts (a)–(c); its [worked image](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-Learning/Mathematics/maths235mq8_clip_image_001.png) confirms that part (c) applies the 15% savings rate to after-tax pay. Independent checks: listed spending percentages total 85%, so savings are 15%; sector angles are 126°, 27°, 36°, 54°, 63° and 54°, summing to 360°; 12% of $28,800 is $3,456 annual or $288 monthly tax; after-tax monthly pay is $2,112; monthly savings are $316.80. The existing Q8(a–b) guide plus the new Q8(c) guide cover all three selected parts in adapted form. This does not establish a complete paper.
