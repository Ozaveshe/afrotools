# JAMB Mathematics 2021: year-label collision

Checked 2026-09-26 as a possible next Mathematics expansion after the 2024 and 2025 publisher lists became nearly exhausted. The [Myschool 2021 Mathematics filter](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2021) has eight list pages, but an item-level cross-year check found exact collisions with material that the same publisher also labels JAMB 2004. The 2021 list cannot be treated as a distinct 2021 paper or used to fill a recent-year gap by count alone.

| Myschool 2021 item | Conflicting older-year record | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Position 2 / item 60619](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics/60619?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2021): choose two students from five in a debating competition | [Myschool JAMB 2004 item 8193](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics/8193), also shown at [2004 collection position 19](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2004&page=4) | Same complete prompt, four options and answer `C(5,2)=10`. It is already represented as `mathematics-2004-46-4c78954e8c6a` in the legacy raw bank. A second 2021 label would double-count the concept and create conflicting year provenance. |
| [2021 position 3](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2021): rate of change of a hemisphere's volume at radius 2 | [Myschool JAMB 2004 collection position 12](https://myschool.ng/classroom/mathematics?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2004&page=3) | Same prompt and four choices. Its independent derivative is `d[(2/3)πr³]/dr=2πr²=8π` at `r=2`; both year lists show `8π` as an option. The legacy pool also contains the stem under 2004. |

The correct mathematical answers do not resolve which, if either, year label reflects an actual UTME sitting. The two collisions are enough to reject a bulk 2021 import; they do not prove that every item in the 2021 collection is reused. **Accepted from this screen: zero.** An individual 2021 candidate can be reconsidered only with a separate dated post-exam source or another item-level record that resolves the year, plus a complete prompt, unique independently solved answer and a duplicate search across the existing bank. Until then, original syllabus practice belongs outside a 2021 past-question count.
