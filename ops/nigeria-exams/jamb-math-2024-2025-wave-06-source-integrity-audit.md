## Release decision

**Accepted in this wave: 0. Held: 8 Myschool items; two misleading alternative year labels and one damaged, duplicate upload excluded.** No source-pool, answer ledger, year page, or public count has changed. Publishing a numeric increase from these candidates would weaken the recent-year and answer-verification claims.

To extend these years, obtain a legible source figure for each visual hold or a separately traceable post-exam paper/collection with date and item-level provenance. Compare its actual questions with current IDs before adapting any new item. Independently solve its options, add a worked explanation and executable calculation receipt, and keep original-sitting and reuse claims limited to the evidence. New topic practice without paper provenance belongs in a separate non-year-labelled bank.
