# English 2024 publisher collection: bounded source audit

Observed 2026-09-24. The [Myschool collection](https://myschool.ng/classroom/english-language?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2024&page=1)
labels its entries JAMB 2024 and spans 44 five-item pages. That count describes a
publisher collection, not one authenticated UTME sitting or official paper
order. Its answer letters are comparison inputs only. The eight brief adapted
questions in `jamb-english-2024-curated.json` have separate answer checks from
the assigned novel, Cambridge pronunciation, or Cambridge grammar. The questions
are attributed as **publisher-labelled revision**, with `num: null`.

The 50 previously accepted English 2022–23 questions in `ops/jamb/source-pool.json`
were compared by distinctive stem and answer concepts on 2026-09-24. None of
the eight new prompts duplicates one of those questions. Source item 58 was
nonetheless omitted because its *diminished* meaning is close to the existing
2022 Poscholars item 47.

| Webpage position | Evidence and reason |

| [9](https://myschool.ng/classroom/english-language/69886?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2024&page=2) | This asks who the "quiet one" is; accepted item 11 tests the character with a more instructive distinction between his name and nickname. |
| [10](https://myschool.ng/classroom/english-language/69887?page=1723&qtid=793339) | The answer *hearse* matches *urn* phonetically, but the source discussion includes comments dated 1 May and 17 June **2023**. Its 2024 year label is contradicted by the page's own history. |
| [56](https://myschool.ng/classroom/english-language?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2024&page=12) | The intended spelling pair *expatiate/playwright* would leave "expatiate the point of view" without the preposition *on*. It is not a sound sentence completion. |
| [58](https://myschool.ng/classroom/english-language/69989?exam_type=jamb&exam_year=2024&page=12) | *Diminished* is answerable, but the same diminution concept already appears in accepted English 2022 item 47; omitted from this small batch for topic diversity. |

Items outside the eight selected and six held positions were not exhaustively
reviewed. The absence of an item here is **not** a positive answer review.

## Other claimed-2024 collections excluded

- [SureSuccess English 2024](https://suresuccess.ng/jamb-prep/english-jamb-questions-and-answers-2024/) reuses matching questions and options from a [2015/16 UNIZIK Post-UTME PDF](https://9jabaz.ng/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/UNIZIK_ENGINEERING_AND_ENVIROMENTAL_SCIENCE.pdf) and an older [JAMB bank posted in 2017](https://jalaloadedshuks.blogspot.com/2017/01/jamb-past-question-use-of-english.html). Its blanket "genuine 2024" claim is unsafe for a year-specific intake.
- [Vantelum English 2024](https://vantelum.com/cbt/jamb/use-of-english/2024/answers) repeats questions with the same options found together in [LarnEdu's 1983–2004 English compilation](https://www.larnedu.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/JAMB-Use-Of-English-Past-Questions-1983-2004-By-LarnEdu.pdf). Its 2024 label cannot authenticate those items.
- [EduPadi English 2024](https://edupadi.com/classroom/lessons/jamb/english/2024/page/1) mirrors the Myschool sequence and has contradictory generated explanations: it keys the mango-tree answer but says balcony in prose, and it keys *vineyard* while incorrectly describing its vowel. Its explanations were not imported.

This is an internal editorial audit. It must not be surfaced as learner-facing
correction history; learner explanations discuss only the subject matter.
