Mining tools — frequently asked questions

What mining tools are live on AfroTools?

Seven working routes are linked from this hub: Mining Royalty, Mining License Fee, Commodity Reference, Diamond Valuation, Oil Well Production, Oil and Gas Revenue, and Artisanal Mining Income. Six are Mining-owned registry rows; Commodity Trade Tracker remains Trade-owned and is cross-linked without duplicate app credit.

How current are the mining royalty rates?

Royalty defaults are planning inputs and may change with legislation, budgets, commodity prices or project terms. Check the displayed review date, follow the cited source where available, and confirm the current treatment with the responsible authority. The result is not legal or tax advice.

Which African countries does the Mining Royalty Calculator cover?

Eighteen mining jurisdictions: Ghana, Tanzania, South Africa, Zambia, DR Congo, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia, Nigeria, Kenya, Mali, Burkina Faso, Egypt, Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia, Madagascar and Gabon — across gold, diamonds, copper, cobalt, coltan and other minerals. Jurisdictions we have not yet bound to an official source are recorded as known gaps rather than shown as fact.

Are these mining tools free?

The seven linked routes can be opened without signup. Their coverage differs by workflow and jurisdiction; each result should be treated as a planning estimate and verified against current local requirements.
