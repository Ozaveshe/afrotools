Question 12

Which of the following is a factor of 15 + 7x − 2x²?

  1. x − 3
  2. x + 3
  3. x − 5
  4. x + 5
Answer and explanation

C: x − 5

By the factor theorem, x − a is a factor when substituting x = a gives zero. At x = 5, 15 + 35 − 50 = 0. Equivalently, 15 + 7x − 2x² = (5 − x)(2x + 3) = −(x − 5)(2x + 3), so x − 5 is a factor.

Question 14

Solve x² + y − 5 = 0 and y − 7x + 3 = 0 for x.

  1. −2, 4
  2. 2, 4
  3. −1, 8
  4. 1, −8
Answer and explanation

D: 1, −8

The second equation gives y = 7x − 3. Substitution into the first gives x² + 7x − 8 = 0, or (x − 1)(x + 8) = 0. Hence x = 1 or x = −8. Their corresponding y-values are 4 and −59, and both pairs satisfy the original equations.

Question 18

Divide x³ + 7x² − x − 7 by x² − 1.

  1. −x³ + 7x² − x − 7
  2. −x³ − 7x + 7
  3. x − 7
  4. x + 7
Answer and explanation

D: x + 7

Group the terms: x³ + 7x² − x − 7 = x²(x + 7) − (x + 7) = (x² − 1)(x + 7). The polynomial quotient is x + 7 and the remainder is zero. If treated as a fraction, the original expression is undefined at x = ±1.

Question 20

Solve the inequality y² − 3y > 18.

  1. −2 < y < 6
  2. y < −3 or y > 6
  3. y > −3 or y > 6
  4. y < −3 or y < 6
Answer and explanation

B: y < −3 or y > 6

Move 18 to the left and factor: y² − 3y − 18 = (y − 6)(y + 3). The product is positive when both factors have the same sign: y < −3 or y > 6. The boundary values −3 and 6 give equality, so neither is included.

Question 23

If k + 1, 2k − 1 and 3k + 1 are consecutive terms of a geometric progression, find the possible common ratios.

  1. 0, 8
  2. −1, 5/3
  3. 2, 3
  4. 1, −1
Answer and explanation

B: −1, 5/3

For three consecutive geometric terms, (2k − 1)² = (k + 1)(3k + 1). Expanding and simplifying gives k² − 8k = 0, so k = 0 or 8. At k = 0 the terms are 1, −1, 1 and the ratio is −1. At k = 8 they are 9, 15, 25 and the ratio is 15/9 = 5/3.
