Question 10

The polynomial pm² + qm + 1 leaves remainder 2 on division by m − 1 and remainder 4 on division by m + 1. Find p and q.

  1. 2, −1
  2. −1, 2
  3. 3, −2
  4. −2, 3
Answer and explanation

A: 2, −1

The remainder theorem gives p + q + 1 = 2 and p − q + 1 = 4. Thus p + q = 1 and p − q = 3, yielding p = 2 and q = −1.

Question 19

  1. −4, 2
  2. −2, 4/11
  3. −11/4, 2
  4. 5, −3
Answer and explanation

C: −11/4, 2

Twice the middle term equals the sum of the outer terms:4p − 20 = p + 2 − 4p². Thus 4p² + 3p − 22 = 0, giving p = 2 or−11/4.

Question 20

The first three terms of a convergent geometric progression sum to half its nonzero infinite sum. Find its positive common ratio.

  1. 1/4
  2. 1/2
  3. 1/∛3
  4. 1/∛2
Answer and explanation

D: 1/∛2

For common ratio r, the first-three-term sum is(1 − r³) times the infinite sum. Hence 1 − r³ = 1/2, so r = ∛(1/2) = 1/∛2.

  2. 5/16

Answer and explanation

A: 5/8
