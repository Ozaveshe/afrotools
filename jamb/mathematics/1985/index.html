Question 6

Simplify 1/2 + 1/(2 + 1/(2 − 1/(4 + 1/5))).

  1. 3/4
  2. −1/3
  3. 169/190
  4. 13/15
  5. 1 21/169
Answer and explanation

C: 169/190

Work from the innermost fraction: 4 + 1/5 = 21/5, so 2 - 1/(21/5) = 2 - 5/21 = 37/21. Next, 2 + 1/(37/21) = 2 + 21/37 = 95/37. The whole expression is therefore 1/2 + 37/95 = 95/190 + 74/190 = 169/190, option C.

Question 13

  1. 0, 12
  2. 1, 2
  3. 21, 0
  4. 4, 5
  5. 3, 4
Answer and explanation

A: 0, 12

Equal roots require discriminant zero: (p-2)^2 - 4(2p+1) = p^2 - 12p = p(p-12). Thus p is 0 or 12, A.

Question 16

The cost per head of a meal is partly constant and partly inversely proportional to the number of people. It is 30 kobo for 100 people and 60 kobo for 40 people. Find the cost per head for 50 people.

  1. 15 kobo
  2. 45 kobo
  3. 20 kobo
  4. 50 kobo
  5. 40 kobo
Answer and explanation

D: 50 kobo

Cost per head C = a + b/n. Subtracting the conditions gives 30 = b(1/40 - 1/100), so b = 2000 and a = 10. At 50 people, C = 10 + 2000/50 = 50 kobo.

Question 24

Solve the simultaneous equations 2x − 3y + 10 = 5 and 10x − 6y = 5.

  1. x = 2 1/2, y = 3 1/3
  2. x = 3 1/2, y = 2 1/3
  3. x = 2 1/4, y = 3
  4. x = 3 1/2, y = 2 1/5
  5. x = 2 1/2, y = 2 1/3
Answer and explanation

A: x = 2 1/2, y = 3 1/3

First rearrange 2x - 3y + 10 = 5 to get 2x - 3y = -5. Double it: 4x - 6y = -10. Subtract this from 10x - 6y = 5 to obtain 6x = 15, so x = 5/2. Substituting gives y = 10/3. Thus x = 2 1/2 and y = 3 1/3, option A.

Question 35

An integer is chosen uniformly at random from 1 to 10 inclusive. What is the probability that it is prime or a multiple of 3?

  1. 7/10
  2. 3/5
  3. 4/5
  4. 1/2
  5. 3/10
Answer and explanation

B: 3/5

Primes in 1..10 are 2,3,5,7. Multiples of three are 3,6,9. Their union is 2,3,5,6,7,9: six outcomes out of ten, so 3/5. Count 3 only once; 1 is not prime.
