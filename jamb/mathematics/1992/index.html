Question 6

Question 10

Make t the subject of the formula s = ut + ½at².

  1. (u ± √(u² − 2as))/a
  2. (−u ± √(u² − 2as))/a
  3. (u ± √(u² + 2as))/a
  4. (−u ± √(u² + 2as))/a
Answer and explanation

D: (−u ± √(u² + 2as))/a

Rearrange to at² + 2ut − 2s = 0. For a ≠ 0, the quadratic formula gives t = (−2u ± √(4u² + 8as))/(2a) = (−u ± √(u² + 2as))/a. Real solutions require u² + 2as ≥ 0; a physical time must also satisfy the conditions of the motion.

Question 12

Solve the equation y − 11√y + 24 = 0.

  1. 8, 3
  2. 64, 9
  3. 6, 4
  4. 9, −8
Answer and explanation

B: 64, 9

Put z = √y, so z ≥ 0. Then z² − 11z + 24 = (z − 8)(z − 3) = 0. Thus √y is 8 or 3, giving y = 64 or 9. Both values satisfy the original equation.

Question 19

What is the nth term of the sequence 2, 6, 12, 20, …?

  1. 4n − 2
  2. 2(3n − 1)
  3. n² + n
  4. n² + 3n + 2
Answer and explanation

C: n² + n

The terms are 1×2, 2×3, 3×4 and 4×5. The pattern is n(n + 1) = n² + n, starting at n = 1. It gives 2, 6, 12 and 20, matching all four terms.
