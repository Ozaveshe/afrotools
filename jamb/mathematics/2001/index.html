Question 5

Given p = 1 + √2 and q = 1 − √2, evaluate (p² − q²)/(2pq).

  1. −2(2 + √2)
  2. 2(2 + √2)
  3. −2√2
  4. 2√2
Answer and explanation

C: −2√2

We have p + q = 2, p − q = 2√2 and pq = −1. Hence p² − q² = 4√2, and division by 2pq = −2 gives −2√2.

Question 10

Solve m² + n² = 29 and m + n = 7. Give the ordered pairs (m,n).

  1. (5,2) and (5,3)
  2. (5,3) and (3,5)
  3. (2,3) and (3,5)
  4. (2,5) and (5,2)
Answer and explanation

D: (2,5) and (5,2)

Squaring the sum gives 49 = 29 + 2mn, so mn = 10. Thus m and n are roots of t² − 7t + 10 = 0, namely 2 and 5, in either order.

Question 11

Divide a^(3x) − 26a^(2x) + 156a^x − 216 by a^(2x) − 24a^x + 108, where the divisor is nonzero and a > 0.

  1. a^x − 18
  2. a^x − 6
  3. a^x − 2
  4. a^x + 2
Answer and explanation

C: a^x − 2

Let t = a^x. The numerator equals (t − 2)(t² − 24t + 108), as expansion confirms. Cancelling the nonzero divisor gives t − 2 = a^x − 2.

Answer and explanation

C: (1,1), (1,2), (2,1)

Question 27

Find the complete locus of a point whose perpendicular distance from y = 4 is a constant k ≥ 0.

  1. y = 4 + k
  2. y = k − 4
  3. y = k ± 4
  4. y = 4 ± k
Answer and explanation

D: y = 4 ± k

Distance from a horizontal line is the absolute difference of y coordinates. Thus |y − 4| = k, giving the two parallel lines y = 4 + k and y = 4 − k.

Question 36

Find dV/dr for a sphere when its radius r = 1.

  1. 4π
  2. 8π
  3. 12π
  4. 24π
Answer and explanation

A: 4π

A sphere has volume V = 4πr³/3. Differentiating with respect to its radius gives dV/dr = 4πr². At r = 1, the rate is 4π.

Question 37

Find the dimensions of the rectangle with greatest area for a fixed positive perimeter p.

  1. A square of side p/4
  2. A square of side p/2
  3. A square of side p
  4. A square of side 2p
Answer and explanation

A: A square of side p/4

If one side is x, the other is p/2 − x. The area x(p/2 − x) is a downward-opening quadratic with its maximum at x = p/4. Both sides are therefore p/4.

Question 38

Evaluate ∫2(2x − 3)^(2/3) dx.

  1. 2x − 3 + k
  2. 2(2x − 3) + k
  3. (6/5)(2x − 3)^(5/3) + k
  4. (3/5)(2x − 3)^(5/3) + k
Answer and explanation

D: (3/5)(2x − 3)^(5/3) + k

Let u = 2x − 3, so du = 2 dx. The integral becomes ∫u^(2/3) du = (3/5)u^(5/3) + k. Substituting back gives the stated result.

Question 46

If Q₁, Q₂ and Q₃ denote the lower quartile, median and upper quartile of a distribution, which expression gives its interquartile range?

  1. Q₃ − Q₁
  2. Q₃ − Q₂
  3. Q₂ − Q₁
  4. (Q₃ − Q₁)/2
Answer and explanation

A: Q₃ − Q₁

The interquartile range measures the span of the middle half of the data. Subtract the lower quartile from the upper quartile: IQR = Q₃ − Q₁.

Question 49

A bowl contains 1 blue, 2 black, 4 yellow, 5 white and 3 brown beads. A bead is chosen uniformly at random. Find the probability it is blue or white.

  1. 1/15
  2. 1/3
  3. 2/5
  4. 7/15
Answer and explanation

C: 2/5

There are 15 beads in total. The blue and white groups contain 1 + 5 = 6 beads, so their combined probability is 6/15 = 2/5.
