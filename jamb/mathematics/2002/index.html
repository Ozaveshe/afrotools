Question 2

Simplify (√0.7 + √70)².

  1. 217.7
  2. 168.7
  3. 84.7
  4. 70.7
Answer and explanation

C: 84.7

Since √70 = 10√0.7, the bracket equals 11√0.7. Squaring gives 121 × 0.7 = 84.7. Both radicals are nonnegative real numbers.

Question 10

XZ is a diameter of a circle of radius 15/2 cm. Point Y lies on the circle and XY = 12 cm. Find the area of triangle XYZ.

  1. 75 cm²
  2. 54 cm²
  3. 45 cm²
  4. 27 cm²
Answer and explanation

B: 54 cm²

The angle at Y is 90° because it subtends a diameter. XZ = 15 cm, so YZ = √(15² − 12²) = 9 cm. Area = ½ × 12 × 9 = 54 cm².

Question 17

Triangle PQR has angle PQR = 50° and an exterior angle at R of 128°. Classify the triangle.

  1. A scalene triangle
  2. An isosceles triangle
  3. An equilateral triangle
  4. An obtuse-angled triangle
Answer and explanation

A: A scalene triangle

The interior angle at R is 180° − 128° = 52°. The third angle is 180° − 50° − 52° = 78°. All three angles differ, so all three sides differ and the triangle is scalene.

Question 25

In a class of 80 students, 30 offer Music, 40 offer History and 20 offer neither. Find the probability that a randomly chosen student offers Music only.

  1. 0.13
  2. 0.25
  3. 0.38
  4. 0.50
Answer and explanation

B: 0.25

The union contains 80 − 20 = 60 students. Therefore the overlap is 30 + 40 − 60 = 10. Music only contains 30 − 10 = 20 students, giving 20/80 = 0.25.

Question 32

The ninth term of an arithmetic progression is five times its fifth term. Find the relationship between first term a and common difference d.

  1. a + 2d = 0
  2. a + 3d = 0
  3. 3a + 5d = 0
  4. 2a + d = 0
Answer and explanation

B: a + 3d = 0

The condition is a + 8d = 5(a + 4d). Rearranging gives 4a + 12d = 0; dividing by four gives a + 3d = 0.

Question 39

Solve x³ − 5x² − x + 5 = 0.

  1. 1, 1 or 5
  2. −1, 1 or −5
  3. 1, 1 or −5
  4. 1, −1 or 5
Answer and explanation

D: 1, −1 or 5

Group the terms as x²(x − 5) − (x − 5) = (x − 5)(x² − 1). Factoring again gives (x − 5)(x − 1)(x + 1), so the roots are 5, 1 and −1.

Question 43

  1. 3/2
  2. 5/2
  3. 10/3
  4. 11/3
Answer and explanation

A: 3/2

This is a geometric series with first term 1 and common ratio 1/3. Since the ratio has magnitude below one, its sum is 1/(1 − 1/3) = 3/2.
