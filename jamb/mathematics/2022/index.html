For real k, (1/8)^(k+2)=1. Find k.

  1. −3
  2. −2
  3. 2
  4. 0
Answer and explanation

B: −2

A positive base other than 1 gives a result of 1 only when its exponent is zero. Set k+2=0, so k=−2.

Cube the binary number 101₂ and express the result in base two.

  1. 1111011₂
  2. 111101₂
  3. 1111101₂
  4. 1111111₂
Answer and explanation

C: 1111101₂

The binary value 101₂ is 5 in base ten. Cubing gives 125, and 125=64+32+16+8+4+1, so its binary digits are 1111101₂.

Myschool 2022 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

A fair coin is tossed twice. What is the probability of at least one head?

  1. 1/4
  2. 1/2
  3. 3/4
  4. 1
Answer and explanation

C: 3/4

The equally likely outcomes are HH, HT, TH and TT. Three contain at least one head, so the probability is 3/4; equivalently subtract P(TT)=1/4 from 1.

Six numbers are 2, 5, x+1, x+2, 7 and 9. Their mean is 6. Find their median.

  1. 5.5
  2. 6
  3. 6.5
  4. 7
Answer and explanation

C: 6.5

A mean of 6 requires a total of 36. The six expressions sum to 26+2x, giving x=5. In order the values are 2, 5, 6, 7, 7, 9; the middle pair averages to 6.5.

How many different arrangements use all seven letters of ZOOLOGY?

  1. 420
  2. 840
  3. 2520
  4. 5040
Answer and explanation

B: 840

There are seven positions, but the three O letters are indistinguishable. Divide the 7! possible orderings by 3!, giving 5040/6=840 distinct arrangements.

Which expression is an antiderivative of (2x+1)^3?

  1. (2x+1)^4/8+C
  2. (2x+1)^4/4+C
  3. (2x+1)^4/6+C
  4. (2x+1)^3/6+C
Answer and explanation

A: (2x+1)^4/8+C

Set u=2x+1, so du=2 dx. Then the integral is one half of u^4/4, or (2x+1)^4/8+C. Differentiating this result returns (2x+1)^3.

  1. 2.5
  2. 3
  3. 3.5
  4. 4
Answer and explanation

C: 3.5

Write 8 as 2³ and √2 as 2^(1/2). Their product is 2^(7/2), so its base-two logarithm is 7/2=3.5.

  3. 9

A particle has displacement s=3t²+5t−10 metres. What is its velocity at t=0?

  1. 3 m/s
  2. 5 m/s
  3. 6 m/s
  4. 10 m/s
Answer and explanation

B: 5 m/s

Velocity is the derivative ds/dt=6t+5. At t=0 this equals 5 m/s; the constant displacement term has no effect on velocity.
