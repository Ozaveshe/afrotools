Question 4

Evaluate 0.24633 / 0.0306, correct to one decimal place.

  1. 0.8
  2. 1.8
  3. 8.0
  4. 8.1
Answer and explanation

D: 8.1

The exact quotient is 8.05: 0.0306 × 8.05 = 0.24633. To round to one decimal place, inspect the hundredths digit, which is 5. Round the tenths digit up, giving 8.1.

Question 5

Taiwo and Kehinde own a store in the ratio 11:9. Kehinde sells two-thirds of her share to Taiwo for ₦720. What is the value of the whole store?

  1. ₦1,080
  2. ₦2,400
  3. ₦3,000
  4. ₦3,600
Answer and explanation

B: ₦2,400

Kehinde owns 9/(11 + 9) = 9/20 of the store. She sells (2/3) × (9/20) = 3/10 of the whole store for ₦720. Therefore the whole store is worth ₦720 ÷ (3/10) = ₦2,400.

Question 9

Given log₁₀(2) = 0.3010 and log₁₀(3) = 0.4771, evaluate log₁₀(4.5) using these values, without logarithm tables.

  1. 0.3010
  2. 0.4771
  3. 0.6532
  4. 0.9542
Answer and explanation

C: 0.6532

Write 4.5 = 9/2 = 3²/2. Then log₁₀(4.5) = 2log₁₀(3) − log₁₀(2) = 2(0.4771) − 0.3010 = 0.9542 − 0.3010 = 0.6532. This uses the supplied rounded logarithms.

Question 11

An 800-page book is 18 mm thick. Calculate the thickness of one leaf in metres, expressed in standard form. Treat the thickness as that of the paper leaves alone.

  1. 2.25 × 10⁻⁴ m
  2. 4.50 × 10⁻⁴ m
  3. 2.25 × 10⁻⁵ m
  4. 4.50 × 10⁻⁵ m
Answer and explanation

D: 4.50 × 10⁻⁵ m

Each leaf has two pages, so 800 pages correspond to 400 leaves. The thickness of one leaf is 18/400 = 0.045 mm. Dividing by 1,000 converts millimetres to metres: 0.045/1,000 = 0.000045 m = 4.50 × 10⁻⁵ m.

Question 13

If 1/p = (a² + 2ab + b²)/(a − b) and 1/q = (a + b)/(a² − 2ab + b²), find p/q. Assume a ≠ b and a ≠ −b.

  1. (a + b)/(a − b)
  2. 1/(a² − b²)
  3. (a − b)/(a + b)
  4. a² − b²
Answer and explanation

B: 1/(a² − b²)

Factor the expressions: 1/p = (a + b)²/(a − b) and 1/q = (a + b)/(a − b)². Therefore p/q = (1/q)/(1/p) = [(a + b)/(a − b)²] × [(a − b)/(a + b)²] = 1/[(a − b)(a + b)] = 1/(a² − b²). The stated exclusions keep the original expressions and their reciprocals defined.

Question 14

x varies inversely as the cube root of y. Given x = 1 when y = 8, find y when x = 3.

  1. 1/3
  2. 2/3
  3. 8/27
  4. 4/9
Answer and explanation

C: 8/27

Inverse variation gives x = k/∛y. When x = 1 and y = 8, k = 1 × ∛8 = 2. For x = 3, ∛y = 2/3. Cubing both sides gives y = (2/3)³ = 8/27.

Question 16

If g(y) = (y − 3)/11 + 11/(y² − 9), find g(y + 3), where y ≠ 0 and y ≠ −6.

  1. y/11 + 11/[y(y + 6)]
  2. y/11 + 11/[y(y + 3)]
  3. (y + 30)/11 + 11/[y(y + 3)]
  4. (y + 3)/11 + 11/[y(y − 6)]
Answer and explanation

A: y/11 + 11/[y(y + 6)]

Replace every occurrence of y in the definition by y + 3. The first term becomes [(y + 3) − 3]/11 = y/11. The second denominator becomes (y + 3)² − 9 = y² + 6y = y(y + 6). Hence g(y + 3) = y/11 + 11/[y(y + 6)]. This is defined when y is neither 0 nor −6.

Question 21

Solve 3x − 5y − 3 = 0 and 2y − 6x + 5 = 0. Give the ordered pair (x, y).

  1. (−1/8, 19/24)
  2. (8, 24/10)
  3. (−8, 24/19)
  4. (19/24, −1/8)
Answer and explanation

D: (19/24, −1/8)

Double the first equation to obtain 6x − 10y = 6. The second equation is −6x + 2y = −5. Adding gives −8y = 1, so y = −1/8. Substituting in 3x − 5y = 3 gives 3x = 19/8 and x = 19/24. The ordered pair is (19/24, −1/8).

Question 23

Simplify 1/(x² + 5x + 6) + 1/(x² + 3x + 2), where x ≠ −1, −2, −3.

  1. (x + 3)/[(x + 1)(x + 2)]
  2. 1/[(x + 1)(x + 2)(x + 3)]
  3. 2/[(x + 1)(x + 3)]
  4. 4/[(x + 1)(x + 3)]
Answer and explanation

C: 2/[(x + 1)(x + 3)]

Factor the denominators as (x + 2)(x + 3) and (x + 1)(x + 2). Over their common denominator, the numerator is (x + 1) + (x + 3) = 2(x + 2). Cancelling x + 2 gives 2/[(x + 1)(x + 3)]. The original restriction x ≠ −2 still applies, along with x ≠ −1 and x ≠ −3.

Question 24

Simplify (4a² − 49b²)/(2a² + 5ab − 7b²), where the denominator is nonzero.

  1. (a − b)/(2a + b)
  2. (2a + 7b)/(a − b)
  3. (2a − 7b)/(a + b)
  4. (2a − 7b)/(a − b)
Answer and explanation

D: (2a − 7b)/(a − b)

Factor the numerator as (2a − 7b)(2a + 7b), a difference of two squares. Factor the denominator as (2a + 7b)(a − b). Cancel the common nonzero factor 2a + 7b to obtain (2a − 7b)/(a − b), option D. The original restrictions remain: a ≠ b and 2a + 7b ≠ 0.

Question 25

Solve x² − x − 1 = 0, giving the roots to one decimal place.

A: x = 1.6 and x = −0.6

The quadratic formula gives x = (1 ± √5)/2. The roots are approximately 1.618 and −0.618, which round to 1.6 and −0.6. Their exact sum is 1 and their exact product is −1.

Question 27

The solutions of x² − 2x − 1 = 0 are the x-coordinates where two graphs intersect. If one graph is y = 2 + x − x², which is the second graph?

  1. y = 1 − x
  2. y = 1 + x
  3. y = x − 1
  4. y = 3x + 3
Answer and explanation

A: y = 1 − x

At a solution of x² − 2x − 1 = 0, we have x² = 2x + 1. Substituting into y = 2 + x − x² gives y = 2 + x − (2x + 1) = 1 − x. Conversely, equating 2 + x − x² and 1 − x gives the required quadratic.

Question 32

Which of these can be the exterior angle of a regular polygon? (i) 35°; (ii) 18°; (iii) 115°.

  1. i and ii
  2. ii only
  3. ii and iii
  4. iii only
Answer and explanation

B: ii only

For a regular polygon with n sides, each exterior angle is 360°/n, where n is an integer of at least 3. We get 360/35 = 72/7 and 360/115 = 72/23, neither an integer. But 360/18 = 20, so only (ii) is possible.

Question 39

A man is 14√3 metres horizontally from a tree on level ground. From a point 2 metres above the ground, he measures an angle of elevation of 30° to the top of the tree. How tall is the tree?

  1. 12 m
  2. 14 m
  3. 14√3 m
  4. 16 m
Answer and explanation

D: 16 m

The height above the observation point is 14√3 × tan 30° = 14√3 × (1/√3) = 14 m. Add the observation height of 2 m: the tree is 14 + 2 = 16 m tall.

Question 44

A metal pipe is 10 cm long, has an external diameter of 12 cm and a wall thickness of 1 cm. Find the volume of metal in the pipe.

  1. 120π cm³
  2. 110π cm³
  3. 60π cm³
  4. 50π cm³
Answer and explanation

B: 110π cm³

The outer radius is 12/2 = 6 cm, and the inner radius is 6 − 1 = 5 cm. Subtract the inner cylinder from the outer cylinder: volume = π(6² − 5²) × 10 = π(36 − 25) × 10 = 110π cm³.

Question 48

Let x be the mean and y the median of 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21. Find x/y, correct to one decimal place.

  1. 1.6
  2. 1.2
  3. 1.1
  4. 1.0
Answer and explanation

D: 1.0

The ten values sum to 156, so the mean is x = 156/10 = 15.6. The middle two values are 15 and 16, so the median is y = 15.5. Thus x/y = 15.6/15.5 = 156/155 ≈ 1.00645, which rounds to 1.0 to one decimal place.

Question 49

A score distribution is given by these (score, frequency) pairs: (0, 7), (1, 11), (2, 6), (3, 7), (4, 7), (5, 5), (6, 3). Find the mode and the median, respectively.

  1. (1, 3)
  2. (1, 2)
  3. (3, 3)
  4. (0, 2)
Answer and explanation

B: (1, 2)

Score 1 has the largest frequency, 11, so the mode is 1. There are 46 observations; the median is the average of the 23rd and 24th values. The cumulative frequencies through scores 0, 1 and 2 are 7, 18 and 24. Both middle values are therefore 2, so the median is 2. The answer is (1, 2).

Question 50

Two fair six-sided dice are thrown independently. What is the probability that their total score is at least 10?

  1. 1/6
  2. 1/12
  3. 5/6
  4. 11/12
Answer and explanation

A: 1/6

There are 6 × 6 = 36 equally likely ordered outcomes. A total of 10 occurs in three ways: (4,6), (5,5), (6,4). A total of 11 occurs in two ways, and 12 in one way. Thus the probability is (3 + 2 + 1)/36 = 6/36 = 1/6.
