Question 2

Find the highest common factor of a²bx + abx² and a²b − b³.

  1. b
  2. a + b
  3. a(a + b)
  4. abx(a² − b²)
Answer and explanation

A: b

Factor both expressions: a²bx + abx² = abx(a + x), and a²b − b³ = b(a − b)(a + b). The only polynomial factor common to both expressions is b. The other factors involve independent variables and are not shared by both expressions.

Question 3

Evaluate 241.34 × (3 × 10⁻³)² and give the answer to four significant figures.

  1. 0.0014
  2. 0.001448
  3. 0.0022
  4. 0.002172
Answer and explanation

D: 0.002172

First square the entire bracket: (3 × 10⁻³)² = 9 × 10⁻⁶. Multiplying by 241.34 gives 0.00217206. The first four significant digits are 2, 1, 7 and 2; the next digit is 0, so the answer is 0.002172. Leading zeros do not count as significant digits.

Question 4

At what annual simple-interest rate would ₦100 deposited for five years earn ₦7.50 interest?

  1. 1½%
  2. 2½%
  3. 15%
  4. 25%
Answer and explanation

A: 1½%

For simple interest, I = Prt/100 when r is the annual percentage rate. Hence 7.50 = 100 × r × 5/100 = 5r, so r = 1.5% per year. This is option A, written as 1½%.

Question 5

Three children share a basket of mangoes. The first takes ¼ of the mangoes and the second takes ¾ of the remainder. What fraction of the original basket does the third child receive?

  1. 3/16
  2. 7/16
  3. 9/16
  4. 13/16
Answer and explanation

A: 3/16

After the first child takes ¼, the fraction left is ¾. The second child takes ¾ of that remainder, leaving ¼ of ¾ for the third child: ¼ × ¾ = 3/16. As a check, ¼ + 9/16 + 3/16 = 1.

Question 7

Three brothers share a profit. The first receives ⅓ of the total and the second receives ⅔ of the remainder. The third receives the remaining ₦12,000. What was the total profit?

  1. ₦60,000
  2. ₦54,000
  3. ₦48,000
  4. ₦42,000
Answer and explanation

B: ₦54,000

After the first brother receives ⅓, the remainder is ⅔. The second takes ⅔ of that remainder, leaving ⅓ × ⅔ = 2/9 of the original profit. Therefore 2/9 of the total is ₦12,000, so the total is ₦12,000 × 9/2 = ₦54,000.

Question 17

If a² + b² = 16 and 2ab = 7, find all possible values of a − b.

  1. 3 and −3
  2. 2 and −2
  3. 1 and −1
  4. 3 and −1
Answer and explanation

A: 3 and −3

Use (a − b)² = a² + b² − 2ab = 16 − 7 = 9. Taking both square roots gives a − b = 3 or −3. Both signs are possible because exchanging a and b preserves the given equations and reverses their difference.

Question 18

Divide x³ − 2x² − 5x + 6 by x − 1.

  1. x² − x − 6
  2. x² − 5x + 6
  3. x² − 7x + 6
  4. x² − 5x − 6
Answer and explanation

A: x² − x − 6

Polynomial division gives the quotient x² − x − 6 with zero remainder. Check by multiplying: (x − 1)(x² − x − 6) = x³ − x² − 6x − x² + x + 6 = x³ − 2x² − 5x + 6.

Question 21

Solve x − 8√x + 15 = 0 over the real numbers.

  1. 3 and 5
  2. −3 and −5
  3. 9 and 25
  4. −9 and 25
Answer and explanation

C: 9 and 25

Because √x is real, x ≥ 0. Substitute u = √x, so u ≥ 0 and x = u². Then u² − 8u + 15 = (u − 3)(u − 5) = 0, giving u = 3 or 5. Squaring gives x = 9 or 25. Both satisfy the original equation: 9 − 24 + 15 = 0 and 25 − 40 + 15 = 0.

Question 23

A rectangular lawn has perimeter 24 m and area 35 m². Find its width, taking the width as the shorter side.

  1. 5 m
  2. 7 m
  3. 12 m
  4. 14 m
Answer and explanation

A: 5 m

If the sides are l and w, then 2(l + w) = 24, so l + w = 12, and lw = 35. The positive numbers 5 and 7 have sum 12 and product 35. Thus the shorter side, or width, is 5 m. The perimeter check is 2(5 + 7) = 24 m.

Question 25

Simplify x/(x + y) + y/(x − y) − x²/(x² − y²), where x ≠ y and x ≠ −y.

  1. x²/(x² − y²)
  2. y²/(x² − y²)
  3. x/(x² − y²)
  4. y/(x² − y²)
Answer and explanation

B: y²/(x² − y²)

Use the common denominator (x + y)(x − y) = x² − y². The numerator becomes x(x − y) + y(x + y) − x² = x² − xy + xy + y² − x² = y². Therefore the result is y²/(x² − y²). The restrictions keep every original denominator nonzero.

Question 27

Find the sum of the first twenty terms of the arithmetic progression log a, log(a²), log(a³), …, where a > 0 and the logarithms have the same valid base.

  1. log(a²⁰)
  2. log(a²¹)
  3. log(a²⁰⁰)
  4. log(a²¹⁰)
Answer and explanation

D: log(a²¹⁰)

The terms are log a, 2 log a, 3 log a, and so on. Their sum is (1 + 2 + … + 20) log a = [20 × 21/2] log a = 210 log a = log(a²¹⁰).

Question 28

Find the sum of the first 18 terms of the progression 3, 6, 12, ….

  1. 3(2¹⁷ − 1)
  2. 3 × 2¹⁸ − 1
  3. 3(2¹⁸ + 1)
  4. 3(2¹⁸ − 1)
Answer and explanation

D: 3(2¹⁸ − 1)

This is a geometric progression with first term 3 and common ratio 2. The sum is S₁₈ = 3(2¹⁸ − 1)/(2 − 1) = 3(2¹⁸ − 1). Multiplying the sum by 2 and subtracting the original sum also leaves 3 × 2¹⁸ − 3.

In trapezium PQRS shown, QR is parallel to PS. Its area is 73.5 cm² and its perpendicular height is 10.5 cm. Find PS if QR is one-third of PS.

  1. 21 cm
  2. 17½ cm
  3. 14 cm
  4. 10½ cm
Answer and explanation

D: 10½ cm

The area of a trapezium is half the sum of its parallel sides multiplied by its height. Let PS = x cm, so QR = x/3 cm. Then 73.5 = ½(x + x/3) × 10.5 = 7x, giving x = 10.5 cm. Check: QR = 3.5 cm and ½(10.5 + 3.5) × 10.5 = 73.5 cm².

Question 32

A sector of a circle has radius 10.5 cm and central angle 48°. Calculate its perimeter to one decimal place.

  1. 8.8 cm
  2. 25.4 cm
  3. 25.6 cm
  4. 29.8 cm
Answer and explanation

D: 29.8 cm

The arc length is (48/360) × 2π × 10.5 = 2.8π cm, approximately 8.8 cm. The perimeter includes both radii, so it is 10.5 + 10.5 + 2.8π ≈ 29.8 cm.

  1. 8 cm

  3. 4 cm

B: 6 cm

The marked angles give ∠XQP = ∠XZY, and the triangles share the angle at X. Thus triangles XQP and XZY are similar, with Q corresponding to Z and P corresponding to Y. Question 35

Find the length of a side of a rhombus whose diagonals are 6 cm and 8 cm.

  1. 8 cm
  2. 5 cm
  3. 4 cm
  4. 3 cm
Answer and explanation

B: 5 cm

The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles. Half-diagonals of 3 cm and 4 cm form the legs of a right triangle whose hypotenuse is a side of the rhombus. The side length is √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 cm.

Question 39

If cos θ = 12/13, find 1 + cot² θ.

  1. 169/25
  2. 25/169
  3. 169/144
  4. 144/169
Answer and explanation

A: 169/25

Use sin² θ = 1 − cos² θ = 1 − 144/169 = 25/169. Therefore cot² θ = cos² θ/sin² θ = 144/25, and 1 + cot² θ = 169/25. Equivalently, 1 + cot² θ = 1/sin² θ. Squared values make the result independent of the sign of sin θ.

Answer and explanation

C: 4√2 cm

The angles in a triangle sum to 180°, so angle XZY = 180° − 30° − 105° = 45°. By the sine rule, YZ/sin 30° = 8/sin 45°. Therefore YZ = 8 × (1/2)/(√2/2) = 4√2 cm.

Question 49

Two distinct numbers are drawn at random without replacement from 1, 2, 3 and 4. What is the probability that their sum is even?

  1. 2/3
  2. ½
  3. 1/3
  4. ¼
Answer and explanation

C: 1/3

The six equally likely unordered pairs are {1,2}, {1,3}, {1,4}, {2,3}, {2,4} and {3,4}. Only {1,3} and {2,4} have even sums, so the probability is 2/6 = 1/3. An even sum requires two odd numbers or two even numbers.

Question 50

An integer is selected uniformly at random from 41 to 56 inclusive. What is the probability that it is a multiple of 9?

  1. 1/8
  2. 2/15
  3. 3/16
  4. 7/8
Answer and explanation

A: 1/8

There are 56 − 41 + 1 = 16 integers in the inclusive range. The multiples of 9 are 45 and 54, so there are two favourable outcomes. The probability is 2/16 = 1/8.
