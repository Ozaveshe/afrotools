Question 2

Find the least length of a rod which can be cut into exactly equal strips, each of either 40cm or 48cm in length.

  1. 120 cm
  2. 240 cm
  3. 360 cm
  4. 480 cm
Answer and explanation

B: 240 cm

The length must be a multiple of both 40 and 48. Since 40 = 2³ × 5 and 48 = 2⁴ × 3, their least common multiple is 2⁴ × 3 × 5 = 240 cm. This gives six 40 cm strips or five 48 cm strips. A length of 480 cm also works, but is not the least.

Answer and explanation

Question 6

If the length of a square is increased by 20% while its width is decreased by 20% to form a rectangle, what is the ratio of the area of the rectangle to the area of the square?

  1. 6:5
  2. 25:24
  3. 5:6
  4. 24:25
Answer and explanation

D: 24:25

Let the original side length be s. The rectangle has length 1.2s and width 0.8s, so its area is 0.96s². Compared with the original area s², the ratio is 0.96:1 = 96:100 = 24:25. The area decreases by 4%.

Question 7

Two brothers invested a total of ₦5,000.00 on a farm project. The farm yield was sold for ₦ 15, 000.00 at the end of the season. If the profit was shared in the ratio 2:3, what is the difference in the amount of profit received by the brothers?

  1. ₦2,000.00
  2. ₦4,000.00
  3. ₦6,000.00
  4. ₦10,000.00
Answer and explanation

A: ₦2,000.00

The profit is ₦15,000 − ₦5,000 = ₦10,000. There are 2 + 3 = 5 equal shares, so each share is ₦2,000. The brothers receive ₦4,000 and ₦6,000, a difference of ₦2,000.

Question 8

Peter earns ₦20 per week for the first 20 weeks and ₦36 per week for the next 24 weeks. His average weekly wage for all 52 weeks is ₦30. Find his average weekly wage for the remaining 8 weeks.

  1. ₦37.00
  2. ₦35.00
  3. ₦30.00
  4. ₦5.00
Answer and explanation

A: ₦37.00

Total annual wages are 52 × ₦30 = ₦1,560. The first 44 weeks account for 20 × ₦20 + 24 × ₦36 = ₦400 + ₦864 = ₦1,264. The remaining eight weeks therefore account for ₦296, giving an average of ₦296 ÷ 8 = ₦37 per week.

Question 10

By selling 20 oranges for ₦1.35, a trader makes a profit of 8%. What is the percentage gain or loss if he sells the same 20 oranges for ₦1.10?

  1. 8%
  2. 10%
  3. 12%
  4. 15%
Answer and explanation

C: 12%

An 8% profit means ₦1.35 is 108% of the cost. The cost is ₦1.35 ÷ 1.08 = ₦1.25. Selling for ₦1.10 gives a loss of ₦0.15. The percentage loss is (₦0.15 ÷ ₦1.25) × 100 = 12%.

Question 18

The formula Q = 15 + 0.5n gives the cost Q, in naira, of feeding n people for a week. Find, in kobo, the extra cost of feeding one additional person.

  1. 350 kobo
  2. 200 kobo
  3. 150 kobo
  4. 50 kobo
Answer and explanation

D: 50 kobo

For one extra person, the increase is Q(n + 1) − Q(n) = [15 + 0.5(n + 1)] − [15 + 0.5n] = 0.5 naira. Since one naira equals 100 kobo, the extra cost is 50 kobo.

Question 20

Make y the subject of Z = x² + 1/y³, for real values with Z ≠ x².

  1. y = 1/(Z − x²)³
  2. y = 1/∛(Z + x³)
  3. y = 1/∛(Z − x²)
  4. y = 1/(∛Z − ∛(x²))
Answer and explanation

C: y = 1/∛(Z − x²)

Subtract x² to obtain Z − x² = 1/y³. Taking reciprocals gives y³ = 1/(Z − x²), so y = 1/∛(Z − x²). This requires Z ≠ x². A cube root does not distribute over subtraction: ∛(Z − x²) is not generally ∛Z − ∛(x²).

Question 21

Find the values of m for which x² + 2(m + 1)x + m + 3 is a perfect square.

  1. −1, 1
  2. −1, 2
  3. 1, −2
  4. 2, −2
Answer and explanation

C: 1, −2

A monic perfect square with middle term 2(m + 1)x is [x + (m + 1)]². Its constant term must satisfy m + 3 = (m + 1)². This gives m² + m − 2 = (m + 2)(m − 1) = 0, so m = −2 or 1. These give (x − 1)² and (x + 2)² respectively.

Question 23

Find the two values of y that satisfy the simultaneous equations x + y = 5 and x² − 2y² = 1.

  1. 12, −2
  2. −12, 12
  3. −12, 2
  4. 2, −2
Answer and explanation

C: −12, 2

From x + y = 5, x = 5 − y. Substitution gives (5 − y)² − 2y² = 1, so 25 − 10y − y² = 1. Rearranging gives y² + 10y − 24 = (y + 12)(y − 2) = 0. Thus y = −12 or 2; the matching x values are 17 and 3.

Question 26

Solve the inequality x − 1 > 4(x + 2).

  1. x > −3
  2. x < −3
  3. 2 < x < 3
  4. −3 < x < −2
Answer and explanation

B: x < −3

Expand the bracket: x − 1 > 4x + 8. Rearranging gives −3x > 9. Dividing by −3 reverses the inequality, so x < −3. The boundary x = −3 is excluded because the original inequality is strict.

In triangle PTS, Q lies on PT and R lies on PS. QR is parallel to TS, and QR:TS = 2:3. Find the ratio of the area of triangle PQR to the area of trapezium QRST.

  1. 4:9
  2. 4:5
  3. 1:3
  4. 2:3
Answer and explanation

B: 4:5

Triangles PQR and PTS are similar because QR is parallel to TS. Their corresponding lengths have ratio 2:3, so their areas have ratio 4:9. The trapezium is the part of the larger triangle outside the smaller triangle: it has 9 − 4 = 5 area parts. Thus triangle PQR : trapezium QRST = 4:5.

Triangle XYZ is right-angled at Y. T lies on XZ and YT is perpendicular to XZ. Given XT = 9 cm and TZ = 16 cm, find YZ.

  1. 25 cm
  2. 20 cm
  3. 16 cm
  4. 9 cm
Answer and explanation

B: 20 cm

The hypotenuse is XZ = 9 + 16 = 25 cm. Similarity of triangles XYZ and YTZ gives YZ/XZ = TZ/YZ, so YZ² = XZ × TZ = 25 × 16 = 400. Therefore YZ = 20 cm. Equivalently, the altitude satisfies YT² = 9 × 16 = 144, and Pythagoras gives YZ² = 144 + 16² = 400.

Rectangle PQRS has width 2h cm and height 3h cm. Its shaded top strip and two side strips are each 2 cm thick. The unshaded rectangular opening reaches the bottom edge, as shown. If the shaded area is 72 cm², find h in centimetres.

  1. 12 cm
  2. 10 cm
  3. 8 cm
  4. 5 cm
Answer and explanation

D: 5 cm

The outer area is (2h)(3h) = 6h². The opening has width 2h − 4 and height 3h − 2, so the shaded area is 6h² − (2h − 4)(3h − 2) = 16h − 8. Set 16h − 8 = 72: then 16h = 80 and h = 5 cm. Check: the outer area is 150 cm² and the opening is 6 × 13 = 78 cm², leaving 72 cm².
