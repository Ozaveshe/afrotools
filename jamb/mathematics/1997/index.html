Question 6

If 8^(x/2) = 2^(3/8) × 4^(3/4), find x.

  1. 3/8
  2. 3/4
  3. 4/5
  4. 5/4
Answer and explanation

D: 5/4

Writing everything in base2 gives 2^(3x/2) = 2^(3/8 + 3/2) = 2^(15/8). Thus3x/2 = 15/8 and x = 5/4.

Question 19

If Sₙ = n² − 1 is the sum of the first n terms, find the nth term for n ≥ 2.

  1. 4n + 1
  2. 4n − 1
  3. 2n + 1
  4. 2n − 1
Answer and explanation

D: 2n − 1

For n ≥ 2, subtract successive partial sums: aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁ = n² − 1 − [(n − 1)² − 1] = 2n − 1. The first term is separately S₁ = 0.

Question 36

  1. √23 cm
  2. √13 cm
  3. 2√5 cm
  4. 2√3 cm
Answer and explanation

B: √13 cm

The cosine rule gives XZ² = 3² + 4² − 2×3×4×cos 60° = 25 − 12 = 13. Therefore XZ = √13 cm. The positive square root is required because a side length is positive.
