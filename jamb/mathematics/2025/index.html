Solve 2x+3>5x+8.

  1. x>5/3
  2. x>−5/3
  3. x<−5/3
  4. x<5/3
Answer and explanation

C: x<−5/3

Subtract 2x and 8 to obtain −5>3x, so x<−5/3. No inequality reversal is needed because the final division is by positive 3.

Define a*b=(a+b)/(ab)+(a−b). Find 3*2.

  1. −6
  2. 11/6
  3. 6/11
  4. −11/6
Answer and explanation

B: 11/6

Use the given rule with a=3 and b=2. The fraction is (3+2)/(3×2)=5/6, while the difference is 3−2=1, so their sum is 11/6.

If cos A=12/13 and A is acute, find tan A.

  1. 5/12
  2. 12/13
  3. 5/13
  4. 13/12
Answer and explanation

A: 5/12

Use a right triangle with adjacent side 12 and hypotenuse 13. Its opposite side is √(13²−12²)=5, so tan A=5/12.

How many distinct arrangements can be made from the letters of SCHOOL?

  1. 600
  2. 360
  3. 480
  4. 300
Answer and explanation

B: 360

SCHOOL has six letters, with O repeated twice. Divide all 6! arrangements by 2! for the repeated O: 720/2=360.

Answer and explanation

A varies directly as b². If A=4 when b=1, find A when b=2.

  1. 12
  2. 14
  3. 16
  4. 11
Answer and explanation

C: 16

Direct-square variation means A=kb² for a constant k. The first pair gives 4=k×1², so k=4. At b=2, A=4×2²=16.
