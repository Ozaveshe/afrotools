Question 15

If w varies inversely as uv/(u + v), and w = 8 when u = 2 and v = 6, find the relationship between u, v and w.

  1. uvw = 16(u + v)
  2. 16uv = 3w(u + v)
  3. uvw = 12(u + v)
  4. 12uvw = u + v
Answer and explanation

C: uvw = 12(u + v)

Inverse variation gives w = k(u + v)/(uv). Substituting the given values, 8 = k(2 + 6)/(2 × 6), so k = 12. Therefore uvw = 12(u + v). The relation applies where u, v and u + v are nonzero.

Question 19

Which of the following is a factor of rs + tr − pt − ps?

  1. p − s
  2. s − p
  3. r − p
  4. r + p
Answer and explanation

C: r − p

Group the terms: rs + tr − pt − ps = r(s + t) − p(t + s) = (r − p)(s + t). Therefore r − p is a factor.

Question 20

Find the two values of y that satisfy the simultaneous equations 3x + y = 8 and x² + xy = 6.

  1. −1 and 5
  2. −5 and 1
  3. 1 and 5
  4. 1 and 1
Answer and explanation

A: −1 and 5

From the first equation, y = 8 − 3x. Substitute into the second: x² + x(8 − 3x) = 6, so x² − 4x + 3 = 0. Factor to obtain (x − 1)(x − 3) = 0. When x = 1, y = 5; when x = 3, y = −1. Thus the two values of y are −1 and 5.

Question 21

Find the range of x that satisfies x/2 + x/3 + x/4 < 1.

  1. x < 12/13
  2. x < 13
  3. x < 9
  4. x < 13/12
Answer and explanation

A: x < 12/13

Use the common denominator 12: x/2 + x/3 + x/4 = (6x + 4x + 3x)/12 = 13x/12. Multiplying the inequality by the positive number 12/13 gives x < 12/13. The boundary value is excluded because the inequality is strict.

Question 23

Solve the equation (x − 2)(x − 3) = 12.

  1. 2 and 3
  2. 3 and 6
  3. −1 and 6
  4. 1 and 6
Answer and explanation

C: −1 and 6

Expand and collect terms: x² − 5x + 6 = 12, so x² − 5x − 6 = 0. Factor to obtain (x − 6)(x + 1) = 0. Hence x = 6 or x = −1. Substituting either value into the original equation gives 12.

Question 24

Simplify (√(1 + x) + √x)/(√(1 + x) − √x).

  1. 1 − 2x − 2√(x(1 + x))
  2. 1 + 2x + 2√(x(1 + x))
  3. √(x(1 + x))
  4. 1 + 2x − 2√(x(1 + x))
Answer and explanation

B: 1 + 2x + 2√(x(1 + x))

Multiply the numerator and denominator by √(1 + x) + √x. The denominator becomes (1 + x) − x = 1. Expanding the numerator gives 1 + x + x + 2√(x(1 + x)) = 1 + 2x + 2√(x(1 + x)). For real square roots, x ≥ 0.

Question 28

What is the nth term of the progression 27, 9, 3, …?

  1. 27(1/3)^(n − 1)
  2. 3^(n + 2)
  3. 27 + 18(n − 1)
  4. 27 + 6(n − 1)
Answer and explanation

A: 27(1/3)^(n − 1)

This is a geometric progression with first term 27 and common ratio 9/27 = 1/3. Its nth term is ar^(n − 1), so the required term is 27(1/3)^(n − 1), for n = 1, 2, 3, …. It gives 27, 9 and 3 for the first three terms.

Question 31

The angles of a quadrilateral are (5x − 30)°, (4x + 60)°, (60 − x)° and (3x + 61)°. Which is the smallest angle?

  1. (5x − 30)°
  2. (4x + 60)°
  3. (60 − x)°
  4. (3x + 61)°
Answer and explanation

C: (60 − x)°

The interior angles of a quadrilateral sum to 360°. Thus 11x + 151 = 360, giving x = 19. The four angles are 65°, 136°, 41° and 118°. The smallest is 41°, represented by (60 − x)°.

Question 32

The area of a square is 144 cm². Find the length of its diagonal.

  1. 11√3 cm
  2. 12 cm
  3. 12√2 cm
  4. 13 cm
Answer and explanation

C: 12√2 cm

The side length is √144 = 12 cm. A diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with both shorter sides equal to 12 cm. By Pythagoras, d² = 12² + 12² = 288, so d = 12√2 cm.

Question 33

One angle of a rhombus is 60°. The shorter diagonal is 8 cm. Find the length of the longer diagonal.

  1. 8√3 cm
  2. 16/√3 cm
  3. 5√3 cm
  4. 10/√3 cm
Answer and explanation

A: 8√3 cm

The diagonals of a rhombus bisect its angles and meet at right angles. In the resulting 30°–60°–90° triangle, half of the shorter diagonal is 4 cm. The longer half-diagonal is 4√3 cm, so the full longer diagonal is 8√3 cm.

Question 35

From a point P outside a circle, PT is a tangent. Two secants pass through the circle in the orders P–M–N and P–Q–R. If PM = 5 cm, PN = 12 cm and PQ = 4.8 cm, find PR and PT respectively, to the nearest 0.1 cm.

  1. 7.3 cm and 5.9 cm
  2. 7.7 cm and 12.5 cm
  3. 12.5 cm and 7.7 cm
  4. 5.9 cm and 7.3 cm
Answer and explanation

C: 12.5 cm and 7.7 cm

For secants from the same external point, PM × PN = PQ × PR = PT². The common product is 5 × 12 = 60 cm². Hence PR = 60/4.8 = 12.5 cm and PT = √60 ≈ 7.7 cm.

Question 40

Simplify cos²x(sec²x + sec²x tan²x).

  1. tan x
  2. tan x sec x
  3. sec²x
  4. cosec²x
Answer and explanation

C: sec²x

Factor out sec²x: cos²x sec²x(1 + tan²x). Since cos²x sec²x = 1 and 1 + tan²x = sec²x, the expression equals sec²x wherever the original expression is defined.

Question 44

PQRS is a square of side 8 cm, with its vertices named in order. U lies on PQ, V on QR and W on RS. Given UQ = 4 cm, QV = 6 cm and RW = 2 cm, find the area of triangle UVW.

  1. 64 cm²
  2. 54 cm²
  3. 50 cm²
  4. 10 cm²
Answer and explanation

D: 10 cm²

PU = 4 cm, SW = 6 cm and RV = 2 cm. The trapezium PUWS has area ½ × (4 + 6) × 8 = 40 cm². The corner triangles UQV and VRW have areas ½ × 4 × 6 = 12 cm² and ½ × 2 × 2 = 2 cm². Hence area UVW = 64 − 40 − 12 − 2 = 10 cm².

Question 45

Find the total surface area of a solid cylinder whose base radius is 4 cm and height is 5 cm.

  1. 56π cm²
  2. 72π cm²
  3. 96π cm²
  4. 192π cm²
Answer and explanation

B: 72π cm²

The two circular ends have total area 2πr² = 32π cm². The curved surface has area 2πrh = 40π cm². Adding them gives 72π cm².

Question 47

  2. 10.8° and 224.6°

Answer and explanation

Question 48

Fifty boxes, each containing 50 balls, were inspected. The numbers of defective balls per box were 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9, with corresponding numbers of boxes 2, 7, 17, 10, 8 and 6. Find the mean (to one decimal place) and median number of defective balls per box, respectively.

  1. 6.7 and 6
  2. 6.7 and 6.5
  3. 6 and 6.7
  4. 6.5 and 6.7
Answer and explanation

A: 6.7 and 6

The frequencies total 50. The weighted total is 4×2 + 5×7 + 6×17 + 7×10 + 8×8 + 9×6 = 333, so the mean is 333/50 = 6.66, or 6.7 to one decimal place. There are 9 values below 6 and 26 values at or below 6. Both the 25th and 26th values are 6, so the median is 6.
