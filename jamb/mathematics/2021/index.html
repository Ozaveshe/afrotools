Practice question

Myschool 2021 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Factorize m³ − m² + 2m − 2.

  1. (m² + 1)(m − 2)
  2. (m − 1)(m + 1)(m + 2)
  3. (m − 2)(m + 1)(m − 1)
  4. (m² + 2)(m − 1)
Answer and explanation

D: (m² + 2)(m − 1)

Group the first two terms and the last two: m²(m − 1) + 2(m − 1). Both groups contain m − 1, so the result is (m² + 2)(m − 1). Expanding this product gives m³ − m² + 2m − 2.

Practice question

Myschool 2021 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Find the set of all integers x satisfying −2 < 2x − 6 < 4.

  1. {2, 3, 4, 5}
  2. {2, 3}
  3. {2, 5}
  4. {3, 4}
Answer and explanation

D: {3, 4}

Add 6 to each part to obtain 4 < 2x < 10. Divide by positive 2 to get 2 < x < 5. The inequalities are strict, so neither endpoint is allowed. The only integers in this interval are 3 and 4.

Practice question

Myschool 2021 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

X is due east of Y. Z is 6 km due south of Y, and XZ is 12 km. What is the bearing of Z from X?

  1. 240°
  2. 150°
  3. 60°
  4. 270°
Answer and explanation

A: 240°

The east-west and north-south directions form a right angle at Y. Seen from X, Z lies southwest. If θ is the angle south of west, sin θ = 6/12 = 1/2, so θ = 30°. West has bearing 270°, hence the bearing toward Z is 270° − 30° = 240°.

Practice question

Myschool 2021 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

A group of market sellers each sells at least one of maize, yam and plantain. In the group, 12 sell maize, 10 sell yam and 14 sell plantain. 5 sell both plantain and maize, 4 sell both yam and maize, 2 sell yam and plantain only, and 3 sell all three. How many sellers are in the group?

  1. 25
  2. 19
  3. 18
  4. 17
Answer and explanation

A: 25

The total who sell both yam and plantain is 2 + 3 = 5, including those who sell all three. Apply inclusion-exclusion: 12 + 10 + 14 − 5 − 4 − 5 + 3 = 25. Subtracting the three pair totals removes the triple group too often, so add its 3 sellers back once.

Practice question

Myschool 2021 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Let P(x) = Lx + 2kx² + 24. If x + 2 and x − 1 are factors of P(x), find L and k.

  1. L = −12, k = −6
  2. L = −2, k = 1
  3. L = −2, k = −1
  4. L = 0, k = 1
Answer and explanation

A: L = −12, k = −6

The factors give P(−2) = 0 and P(1) = 0. Thus −2L + 8k = −24 and L + 2k = −24. Double the second equation and add it to the first: 12k = −72, so k = −6 and L = −12. Then P(x) = −12(x + 2)(x − 1), confirming both factors.
