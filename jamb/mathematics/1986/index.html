Question 1

Evaluate (212)₃ − (121)₃ + (222)₃. All subscripts indicate base three.

  1. (313)₃
  2. (1000)₃
  3. (1020)₃
  4. (1222)₃
Answer and explanation

C: (1020)₃

Convert to base ten: (212)₃ = 2×9 + 1×3 + 2 = 23; (121)₃ = 1×9 + 2×3 + 1 = 16; (222)₃ = 2×9 + 2×3 + 2 = 26. Then 23 − 16 + 26 = 33. Since 33 = 1×27 + 0×9 + 2×3 + 0, the result is (1020)₃, option C. Base three uses only the digits 0, 1 and 2.

Question 9

Udoh deposited ₦150.00 in a bank. After 5 years, the simple interest earned was ₦55.00. What was the annual interest rate?

  1. 11%
  2. 7⅓%
  3. 5%
  4. 3½%
Answer and explanation

B: 7⅓%

For simple interest, I = Prt/100, where r is the annual percentage rate. Therefore r = 100I/(Pt) = (100 × 55)/(150 × 5) = 22/3 = 7⅓%. The correct answer is B. Use the interest earned, ₦55, rather than the final balance.

Question 11

The ages of Tosan and Isa differ by 6 years, and their product is 187. Write the ages as (x, y), where x > y.

  1. (12, 9)
  2. (23, 17)
  3. (17, 11)
  4. (18, 12)
Answer and explanation

C: (17, 11)

Let the younger age be y. Then the older age is y + 6, so y(y + 6) = 187. Rearranging gives y² + 6y − 187 = (y − 11)(y + 17) = 0. An age cannot be negative, so y = 11 and x = 17. Check: 17 − 11 = 6 and 17 × 11 = 187. The ages are (17, 11), option C.

Question 17

First factor out a: a(8 + 125x³). Since 8 = 2³ and 125x³ = (5x)³, use u³ + v³ = (u + v)(u² − uv + v²). This gives a(2 + 5x)(4 − 10x + 25x²), option B. Question 19

Find all real numbers x satisfying (x + 1)/3 − 1 > (x + 4)/5.

  1. x < 11
  2. x < -1
  3. x > 6
  4. x > 11
Answer and explanation

D: x > 11

Multiply both sides by 15, a positive number, so the inequality direction stays the same: 5(x + 1) − 15 > 3(x + 4). Thus 5x − 10 > 3x + 12, giving 2x > 22 and x > 11. The boundary x = 11 is excluded because the inequality is strict. Answer D.

Question 20

Question 23

At which points does the curve y = −x² + 3x + 4 intersect the coordinate axes?

  1. (4, 0), (0, 0), (−1, 0)
  2. (−4, 0), (0, 4), (1, 1)
  3. (0, 0), (0, 1), (1, 0)

On the y-axis, x = 0, so y = 4 and the intercept is (0, 4). On the x-axis, y = 0, giving x² − 3x − 4 = 0. Factorizing gives (x − 4)(x + 1) = 0, so x = 4 or −1. The three intercepts are (0, 4), (4, 0) and (−1, 0), option D.

At an intersection, the two expressions for y are equal: 2x + 1 = 2x² + 5x − 1. Hence 2x² + 3x − 2 = (2x − 1)(x + 2) = 0, so x = 1/2 or −2. Substituting into y = 2x + 1 gives y = 2 or −3 respectively. The points are (−2, −3) and (1/2, 2), option A.

Question 38

Answer and explanation

For arc length s and radius r, the sector area is A = rs/2. Here A = (6 × 8)/2 = 24 cm², option B. Equivalently, θ = s/r = 4/3 radians and A = r²θ/2. The 8 cm measurement is the curved arc length, not a chord.

Question 44

  2. ₦112.00

Answer and explanation

The open box has no top. Its outside consists of a 4 × 3 = 12 m² base, two 4 × 4 = 16 m² sides and two 3 × 4 = 12 m² sides. Total area = 12 + 2(16) + 2(12) = 68 m². At ₦2.00 per m², the cost is 68 × 2 = ₦136.00, option C.

Question 47

An integer is selected uniformly at random from 40 to 50 inclusive. What is the probability that it is prime?

  1. 3/11
  2. 5/11
  3. 3/10
  4. 4/11
Answer and explanation

A: 3/11

There are 50 − 40 + 1 = 11 integers in the inclusive range. The primes are 41, 43 and 47: exactly three numbers. In particular, 49 = 7 × 7 is not prime. Therefore the probability is 3/11, option A.
