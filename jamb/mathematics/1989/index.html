Question 2

Evaluate (2,700,000 × 0.03) / 18,000.

  1. 4.5 × 10⁰
  2. 4.5 × 10¹
  3. 4.5 × 10²
  4. 4.5 × 10³
Answer and explanation

A: 4.5 × 10⁰

2,700,000 × 0.03 = 81,000. Dividing by 18,000 gives 4.5. Since 10⁰ = 1, this is 4.5 × 10⁰, option A. An exponent of 1 would instead give 45.

Question 3

Which list contains all the distinct prime factors of 2,520?

  1. 2, 9, 5
  2. 2, 9, 7
  3. 2, 3, 5, 7
  4. 2, 3, 7, 9
Answer and explanation

C: 2, 3, 5, 7

Repeated division gives 2,520 = 2³ × 3² × 5 × 7. The distinct prime factors are therefore 2, 3, 5 and 7. The number 9 is not prime because 9 = 3 × 3.

Question 6

Find the absolute difference between 0.007685 rounded to three significant figures and the same number rounded to four decimal places.

  1. 10⁻⁵
  2. 7 × 10⁻⁴
  3. 8 × 10⁻⁵
  4. 10⁻⁶
Answer and explanation

A: 10⁻⁵

To three significant figures, 0.007685 rounds to 0.00769. To four decimal places, it rounds to 0.0077. Their absolute difference is 0.007700 − 0.007690 = 0.000010 = 10⁻⁵. The leading zeros do not count as significant figures.

Question 11

Find p in terms of q if log₃(p) + 3log₃(q) = 3, with p > 0 and q > 0.

  1. (3/q)³
  2. ∛(q/3)
  3. (q/3)³
  4. ∛(3/q)
Answer and explanation

A: (3/q)³

Use 3log₃(q) = log₃(q³), then combine the logarithms to obtain log₃(pq³) = 3. Therefore pq³ = 3³ = 27, giving p = 27/q³ = (3/q)³. Positivity is required for the original real logarithms.

Question 12

Find all real values of y satisfying 9ʸ − 4(3ʸ) + 3 = 0.

  1. −1 and 0
  2. −1 and 1
  3. 1 and 3
  4. 0 and 1
Answer and explanation

D: 0 and 1

Since 9ʸ = (3ʸ)², substitute u = 3ʸ. The equation becomes u² − 4u + 3 = (u − 1)(u − 3) = 0. Thus 3ʸ = 1 or 3, giving y = 0 or 1. The function 3ʸ is one-to-one, so there are no other real solutions.

Question 13

Make R the subject of S = √[(2R + T)/(2RT)], using the principal square root and values for which the original expression is defined.

  1. R = T/(TS² − 1)
  2. R = T/[2(TS² − 1)]
  3. R = T/(TS² + 1)
  4. R = T/[2(TS² + 1)]
Answer and explanation

B: R = T/[2(TS² − 1)]

Square both sides: S² = (2R + T)/(2RT). Multiply by 2RT to obtain 2RTS² = 2R + T. Collect the terms containing R: 2R(TS² − 1) = T. Therefore R = T/[2(TS² − 1)]. The original formula requires R and T to be nonzero and S ≥ 0; the rearranged denominator requires TS² ≠ 1.

Question 15

The cost of dinner for a group of students is partly constant and partly varies directly with the number of students. It costs ₦74.00 for 20 students and ₦96.00 for 30 students. Find the cost for 15 students.

  1. ₦68.50
  2. ₦63.00
  3. ₦60.00
  4. ₦52.00
Answer and explanation

B: ₦63.00

The extra 10 students cost ₦96 − ₦74 = ₦22, so the variable cost is ₦2.20 per student. The fixed cost is ₦74 − 20 × ₦2.20 = ₦30. For 15 students, the total is ₦30 + 15 × ₦2.20 = ₦63.00.

Question 16

If f(x) = 2x² − 5x + 3, find f(x + 1).

  1. 2x² − x
  2. 2x² − x + 10
  3. 4x² + 3x + 2
  4. 4x² + 3x + 12
Answer and explanation

A: 2x² − x

Replace every x in the definition with x + 1: f(x + 1) = 2(x + 1)² − 5(x + 1) + 3. Expand to get 2x² + 4x + 2 − 5x − 5 + 3 = 2x² − x.

Question 21

Factorize 4a² + 12ab − c² + 9b².

  1. 4a(a − 3b) + (3b − c)²
  2. (2a + 3b − c)(2a + 3b + c)
  3. (2a − 3b − c)(2a − 3b + c)
  4. 4a(a − 3b) + (3b + c)²
Answer and explanation

B: (2a + 3b − c)(2a + 3b + c)

Group the first, second and fourth terms: 4a² + 12ab + 9b² = (2a + 3b)². The expression is therefore (2a + 3b)² − c². Applying the difference-of-squares identity gives (2a + 3b − c)(2a + 3b + c).

Question 22

Find K and L, respectively, if ½(3y − 4x)² = 8x² + Kxy + Ly².

  1. (−12, 9/2)
  2. (−6, 9)
  3. (6, 9)
  4. (12, 9/2)
Answer and explanation

A: (−12, 9/2)

Expand the square before multiplying by one half: ½(9y² − 24xy + 16x²) = 8x² − 12xy + (9/2)y². Comparing coefficients gives K = −12 and L = 9/2.

Question 23

Solve 2x⁻¹ − 3y⁻¹ = 4 and 4x⁻¹ + y⁻¹ = 1 for x and y, respectively, where x and y are nonzero.

  1. (−1, 2)
  2. (1, 2)
  3. (2, 1)
  4. (2, −1)
Answer and explanation

D: (2, −1)

Substitute u = 1/x and v = 1/y. The equations become 2u − 3v = 4 and 4u + v = 1. Add the first equation to three times the second to obtain 14u = 7, so u = 1/2. Then v = 1 − 4(1/2) = −1. Thus x = 2 and y = −1. Substitution gives 1 + 3 = 4 and 2 − 1 = 1, checking both equations.

Question 24

What value of Q makes 4x² + 5x + Q a perfect square?

  1. 25/16
  2. 25/64
  3. 5/8
  4. 5/4
Answer and explanation

A: 25/16

Write the perfect square as (2x + c)² = 4x² + 4cx + c². Matching the coefficient of x gives 4c = 5, so c = 5/4 and Q = c² = 25/16. Hence the expression is (2x + 5/4)².

Question 25

For positive a, b and c, find the range of r satisfying r/a + r/b + r/c > 1.

  1. r > abc/(bc + ac + ab)
  2. r > abc
  3. r > 1/a + 1/b + 1/c
  4. r > 1/(abc)
Answer and explanation

A: r > abc/(bc + ac + ab)

Factor out r: r(1/a + 1/b + 1/c) > 1. Since a, b and c are positive, their reciprocal sum is positive, so division keeps the inequality direction unchanged. Therefore r > 1/(1/a + 1/b + 1/c) = abc/(bc + ac + ab).

Question 30

If −8, m, n and 19 are consecutive terms of an arithmetic progression, find (m, n).

  1. (1, 10)
  2. (2, 10)
  3. (3, 13)
  4. (4, 16)
Answer and explanation

A: (1, 10)

There are three equal steps from −8 to 19. The common difference is [19 − (−8)]/3 = 27/3 = 9. The terms are −8, 1, 10, 19, so m = 1 and n = 10.

Question 37

In triangle XYZ, angles Y and Z are each 30° and XZ = 3 cm. Find YZ.

  1. √3/2 cm
  2. 3√3/2 cm
  3. 3√3 cm
  4. 2√3 cm
Answer and explanation

C: 3√3 cm

Angle X is 180° − 30° − 30° = 120°. By the sine rule, YZ/sin 120° = XZ/sin 30°. Thus YZ = 3(√3/2)/(1/2) = 3√3 cm. The side opposite 120° must be the longest, consistent with this result.

Question 41

An aeroplane flies horizontally at a height of 10 km toward a landmark on level ground. At two positions before reaching it, the angles of depression are 35° and 55°, respectively. Find the distance between the observation positions.

  1. 10(sin 35° − sin 55°) km
  2. 10(cos 35° − cos 55°) km
  3. 10(tan 35° − tan 55°) km
  4. 10(cot 35° − cot 55°) km
Answer and explanation

D: 10(cot 35° − cot 55°) km

The angle of depression equals the angle of elevation from the landmark. At angle θ, the horizontal distance to the landmark is 10/tan θ = 10 cot θ. As the aircraft approaches, this distance decreases. The distance flown is therefore 10(cot 35° − cot 55°) km, approximately 7.28 km. This uses the constant-height, level-ground geometry described in the question.

Question 48

The average age of a family of 21 people is 14 years. Excluding the grandfather, the average age of the remaining 20 people is 12 years. How old is the grandfather?

  1. 35 years
  2. 40 years
  3. 42 years
  4. 54 years
Answer and explanation

D: 54 years

The total age of all 21 people is 21 × 14 = 294 years. The total for the other 20 people is 20 × 12 = 240 years. The grandfather is therefore 294 − 240 = 54 years old.
