Question 1

If M represents the median and D the mode of the measurements 5, 9, 3, 5, 8 then (M,D) is

  1. (6,5)
  2. (5,8)
  3. (5,7)
  4. (5,5)
  5. (7,5)
Answer and explanation

D: (5,5)

Put the values in ascending order: 3, 5, 5, 8, 9. The median is the middle value, 5. The mode is the most frequent value, also 5. Therefore (M, D) = (5, 5), option D.

Question 4

P sold a bicycle to Q at a profit of 10%. Q then sold it to R for ₦209 at a loss of 5%. How much did the bicycle originally cost P?

  1. ₦200
  2. ₦196
  3. ₦180
  4. ₦205
  5. ₦150
Answer and explanation

A: ₦200

Q sold the bicycle for 95% of the amount Q paid. Therefore Q paid ₦209 ÷ 0.95 = ₦220. This was 110% of P’s original cost, so P paid ₦220 ÷ 1.10 = ₦200, option A. Check: ₦200 × 1.10 × 0.95 = ₦209.

Question 5

If x = 1 is a root of x³ − 2x² − 5x + 6 = 0, find the other roots.

  1. -3 and 2
  2. –2 and 2
  3. 3 and –2
  4. 1 and 3
  5. –3 and 1
Answer and explanation

C: 3 and –2

Dividing by x − 1 gives x² − x − 6 = (x − 3)(x + 2). Thus the polynomial is (x − 1)(x − 3)(x + 2). Apart from x = 1, the roots are 3 and −2: option C.

Question 5

If the price of each orange were raised by ½ kobo, a customer spending ₦2.40 would be able to buy 16 fewer oranges. What is the price of an orange before the increase?

  1. 2½ kobo
  2. 3½ kobo
  3. 5½ kobo
  4. 20 kobo
  5. 21½ kobo
Answer and explanation

A: 2½ kobo

Convert the budget to 240 kobo. Let the original price be p kobo. Then 240/p − 240/(p + 0.5) = 16. Combining the fractions gives p(p + 0.5) = 7.5, or (p − 2.5)(p + 3) = 0. A price must be positive, so p = 2.5 kobo, option A. The customer could buy 96 oranges before the increase and 80 afterwards: 16 fewer.

Question 9

Bola chooses an integer at random from 1 through 300 inclusive, with each integer equally likely. What is the probability that it is divisible by 4?

  1. 1/3
  2. 1/4
  3. 1/5
  4. 4/300
  5. 1/300
Answer and explanation

B: 1/4

The multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, …, 300. There are 300 ÷ 4 = 75 such integers among 300 equally likely choices. The probability is 75/300 = 1/4, option B.

Question 10

If (x + 2) and (x − 1) are factors of ℓx³ + 2kx² + 24, find ℓ and k.

  1. ℓ = −6, k = −9
  2. ℓ = −2, k = 1
  3. ℓ = −2, k = −1
  4. ℓ = 0, k = 1
  5. ℓ = 6, k = 0
Answer and explanation

A: ℓ = −6, k = −9

A factor x + 2 gives f(−2) = 0, so −8ℓ + 8k + 24 = 0, or ℓ − k = 3. A factor x − 1 gives f(1) = 0, so ℓ + 2k = −24. Substitute ℓ = k + 3 to get 3k = −27. Thus k = −9 and ℓ = −6, option A. Both −2 and 1 make the repaired cubic equal to zero.

Question 14

y varies partly as the square of x and partly as the inverse of the square root of x. Find the expression for y if y = 2 when x = 1 and y = 6 when x = 4.

  1. y = 10x²/31 + 52/(31√x)
  2. y = x² + 1/√x
  3. y = x² + 1/x
  4. y = x²/31 + 1/(31√x)
  5. y = (10/31)(x² + 1/√x)
Answer and explanation

A: y = 10x²/31 + 52/(31√x)

Write y = ax² + b/√x. The first condition gives a + b = 2. The second gives 16a + b/2 = 6, or 32a + b = 12. Subtracting gives 31a = 10, so a = 10/31 and b = 52/31. Therefore y = 10x²/31 + 52/(31√x), option A. Substitution gives 2 at x = 1 and 6 at x = 4.

Question 19

Question 21

Tunde and Shola can finish a piece of work together in 18 days. Tunde can do it alone in x days, while Shola would take 15 days longer. Which equation is satisfied by x?

  1. x² − 5x − 18 = 0
  2. x² − 20x + 360 = 0
  3. x² - 21x − 270 = 0
  4. 2x² + 42x − 190 = 0
  5. 3x² − 31x + 150 = 0
Answer and explanation

C: x² - 21x − 270 = 0

Their daily work rates are 1/x and 1/(x + 15). Together, 1/x + 1/(x + 15) = 1/18. Multiplying by 18x(x + 15) gives 18(2x + 15) = x(x + 15), hence x² − 21x − 270 = 0, option C. Its positive solution is x = 30; working with Shola’s 45-day rate gives 1/30 + 1/45 = 1/18.

Question 23

Question 23

Which quadratic equation has roots 1 − √13 and 1 + √13?

  1. x² + (1 - √13)x + 1 + √13 = 0
  2. x² + (1 - √13)x + 1 - √13 = 0
  3. x² + 2x + 12 = 0
  4. x² − 2x + 12 = 0
  5. x² − 2x − 12 = 0
Answer and explanation

E: x² − 2x − 12 = 0

The roots add to 2. Their product is (1 − √13)(1 + √13) = 1 − 13 = −12. A monic quadratic with these roots is x² − (sum)x + product = 0, so x² − 2x − 12 = 0, option E.

Question 24

  1. –28, 7
  2. 6, -28
  3. 6, -1
  4. –1, 7
  5. 3, 2
Answer and explanation

C: 6, -1

From y + 5x − 2 = 0, y = 2 − 5x. Substitute into x² + y − 8 = 0: x² − 5x − 6 = 0. Factor: (x − 6)(x + 1) = 0, so x = 6 or x = −1. These are the two x-values in option C; the corresponding y-values are −28 and 7.

Question 29

A triangle has sides (x + 4) cm, x cm and (x − 4) cm. If the cosine of its largest angle is 1/5, find x.

  1. 24 cm
  2. 20 cm
  3. 28 cm
  4. 88/7 cm
  5. 0 cm
Answer and explanation

A: 24 cm

The largest angle is opposite x + 4. By the cosine rule, 1/5 = [x² + (x − 4)² − (x + 4)²]/[2x(x − 4)] = (x − 16)/[2(x − 4)]. Thus 5x − 80 = 2x − 8, so 3x = 72 and x = 24, option A. The resulting sides 20, 24 and 28 form a valid triangle.
