Question 4

Aaron' . All I need really is a place in an Art school, engineering can go hang itself. The dominant figure of speech in the excerpt above is

What is the dominant figure of speech?

  1. metonymy
  2. synecdoche
  3. personification
  4. metaphor
Answer and explanation

C: personification

Engineering is treated as though it were a person capable of hanging itself. Attributing that human action to an abstract field of study creates personification.

Question 19

Around which central character is the action of Nineteen Eighty-Four organised?

  1. Winston Smith
  2. O'Brien
  3. Julia
  4. Big Brother
Answer and explanation

A: Winston Smith

Winston Smith is the central character whose work, doubts, relationship and eventual capture organise the narrative. Other named characters influence his story but do not replace him as its focal character.

Question 22

Which description best fits the vivid action and tension in Rubadiri’s An African Thunderstorm?

  1. didactic
  2. dramatic
  3. traditional
  4. satirical
Answer and explanation

B: dramatic

The rapidly changing storm, bending trees and hurried village activity create an unfolding scene full of tension and action. Dramatic describes this vivid presentation; it is not a direct moral lesson or a sustained exercise in ridicule.

Question 23

In Kunene’s A Heritage of Liberation, the speaker addresses the ancestors: “Since it was you who in all these thin seasons”. Which device is used?

  1. apostrophe
  2. allusion
  3. anecdote
  4. aside
Answer and explanation

A: apostrophe

The speaker directly addresses the ancestors as though they could hear the present appeal. Such an address to absent or departed beings is apostrophe; it is not an anecdote or an aside to a theatre audience.

Question 24

"Let me ask for what reason or rhyme women refuse to marry? Woman cannot exist except by man, what is there in that to vex some of them so? The lines above from Give Me The Minstrel's Seat is an example of

Which device do these questions exemplify?

  1. pathetic fallacy
  2. chiasmus
  3. ironical statement
  4. rhetorical question
Answer and explanation

D: rhetorical question

The speaker asks questions to press an argument rather than to obtain factual information. That persuasive use of questioning is rhetorical, rather than a reversal of grammatical order.

Question 36

Which figure of speech conveys the opposite of what the words literally say?

  1. satire
  2. irony
  3. paradox
  4. metaphor
Answer and explanation

B: irony

Verbal irony expresses a meaning that contrasts with the literal words, often their opposite. Satire is a broader critical mode, while paradox presents an apparent contradiction.

Question 37

Action without speech in a play is

  1. soliloquy
  2. aside
  3. epilogue
  4. mime
Answer and explanation

D: mime

Mime communicates action through bodily movement and expression without spoken words. A soliloquy, aside or epilogue normally involves speech rather than silent enactment.

Question 40

What is a character’s speech to themselves on stage called?

  1. Monologue
  2. Epilogue
  3. Aside
  4. Soliloquy
Answer and explanation

D: Soliloquy

A soliloquy voices a character’s thoughts to themselves for the audience to hear. A monologue is any extended speech, whereas soliloquy names this particular self-directed dramatic convention.
