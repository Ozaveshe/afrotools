In J. C. de Graft’s Sons and Daughters, why does Maanan express dislike for Lawyer B?

  2. his recent advances towards her

  4. the betrayal of his wife's trust.

B: his recent advances towards her

Maanan resists Lawyer Bonu’s unwanted sexual advances. Her objection concerns his conduct towards her, rather than simply disagreement over her career or his obligations to other people.

Question 7

William Shakespeare’s Romeo and Juliet is predominantly written in which verse form?

  1. blank verse
  2. free verse
  3. metres
  4. foot.
Answer and explanation

A: blank verse

Much of Romeo and Juliet uses blank verse: unrhymed iambic pentameter. Free verse does not follow that regular metrical pattern, though the play also contains prose and rhymed passages.

Question 8

In Romeo and Juliet, whom does Juliet describe as “O serpent heart, hid with a flowering face!”?

  1. Juliet
  2. Romeo
  3. Tybalt
  4. Benvolio.
Answer and explanation

B: Romeo

Juliet speaks about Romeo after learning that he has killed Tybalt. The contrast between a beautiful face and a serpent’s heart expresses her shocked conflict about her husband.

Question 10

  2. He marries Juliet without parental consent
  3. He attends Capulet’s party uninvited

Answer and explanation

Romeo kills Tybalt after Tybalt has killed Mercutio. The Prince punishes Romeo with banishment; his secret marriage and attendance at the party are not the offence that causes this sentence.

Question 27

The poet persona in Marvell's To His Coy Mistress is willing to praise the lady's eyes for

  1. thirty thousand years
  2. six decades
  3. two centuries
  4. a century.
Answer and explanation

D: a century.

The speaker imagines spending one hundred years praising her eyes and gazing on her forehead. One hundred years is a century; the larger numbers refer to other imagined acts of praise.

Question 32

In conventional dramatic plot structure, which feature belongs to both comedy and tragedy?

  1. happy ending
  2. climax
  3. tragic hero
  4. stanza
Answer and explanation

B: climax

In conventional dramatic structure, both comedy and tragedy build towards a climax, the high point of conflict. They differ in typical outcomes, and a stanza is a unit of verse rather than a dramatic requirement.

Question 34

The narrative style in which the hero tells his own story directly is the .

  1. Objective
  2. Subjective
  3. First-person
  4. Third-person
Answer and explanation

C: First-person

A first-person narrator tells events using a personal perspective, commonly with I or we. When the hero narrates their own story directly, that is first-person narration.

Question 35

The physical, historical or cultural background of a literary work is referred to as

  1. episode
  2. plot
  3. time
  4. setting
Answer and explanation

D: setting

Setting includes where and when events occur and their social or cultural surroundings. Plot instead concerns the sequence of events and an episode is a particular incident.

Question 37

Pun as a literary device deals with

  1. placing two opposite phrases
  2. placing words side by side
  3. playing on words
  4. arrangement of words
Answer and explanation

C: playing on words

A pun plays on a word’s different meanings or on similar-sounding words. Merely arranging words side by side does not create the double meaning that makes a pun.

Question 38

In a narrative poem, the poet attempts to

  1. summarize a story
  2. describe a place
  3. preach a sermon
  4. tell a story
Answer and explanation

D: tell a story

A narrative poem tells a story through verse, presenting events and often characters. Description or reflection may appear within it, but storytelling defines the narrative form.

Question 39

The account of experiences of an individual during the course of a journey is known as

  1. a travelogue
  2. an autobiography
  3. a catalogue
  4. a memoir
Answer and explanation

A: a travelogue

A travelogue records a traveller’s experiences on a journey. An autobiography covers a life more broadly, while a catalogue lists items rather than narrating travel.

Question 44

Read these four lines: (1) That age is best which is the first, / (2) When youth and blood are warmer, / (3) But being spent, the worse, and worst / (4) Time still succeed the former. What is the rhyme scheme?

  1. bbaa
  2. aabb
  3. abab
  4. abba.
Answer and explanation

C: abab

The first and third line endings, first and worst, rhyme; warmer and former link the second and fourth. Alternating these two sounds produces the pattern ABAB.

Question 45

But the towering earth was tired sitting in one position. She moved, suddenly, and the houses crumbled, the mountains heaved horribly, and the work of a million years was lost. The subject matter of the extract above is

What is the subject matter of the passage?

  1. storm
  2. sea waves
  3. house movement
  4. earthquake.
Answer and explanation

D: earthquake.

The sudden movement of the ground, collapsing houses and heaving mountains describe an earthquake. The personified earth does not change the physical event being represented.
