Question 3

What is the central setting of Frank Ogodo Ogbeche’s Harvest of Corruption?

  1. Mabu
  2. Gbossa
  3. Darkin
  4. Jabu
Answer and explanation

D: Jabu

The principal action of Harvest of Corruption takes place in the fictional city of Jabu. Specific venues such as the hotel belong within the play’s wider setting and do not replace the city as its central location.

Question 6

“Ill-starred wench, pale as thy smock, / When we shall meet at compt.” — Shakespeare, Othello.

Which device appears in pale as thy smock?

  1. Simile
  2. Pun
  3. Metaphor
  4. Paradox
Answer and explanation

A: Simile

Pale as thy smock compares the woman’s pallor with her clothing using as. This explicit comparison is a simile rather than a direct metaphor or a play on word meanings.

Question 8

In Othello, which prejudice helps explain Brabantio’s opposition to his daughter’s marriage?

  1. He prefers Iago
  2. Othello is a Moor
  3. Roderigo wooed her first
  4. Desdemona is too young
Answer and explanation

B: Othello is a Moor

Brabantio’s objections express prejudice against Othello as a Moor and disbelief that Desdemona freely chose him. The play exposes this prejudice through Brabantio’s accusations of enchantment.

Question 22

Which figure of speech presents panic as a winged creature?

  1. onomatopoeia
  2. metaphor
  3. personification
  4. apostrophe

Question 25

(1) But such a tide as moving seems asleep, / (2) Too full for sound and foam, / (3) When that which drew from out the boundless deep / (4) Turns again home. — Tennyson, Crossing the Bar.

What is the rhyme scheme of these four lines?

  1. abba
  2. abab
  3. abed
  4. aabb
Answer and explanation

B: abab

Asleep and deep rhyme in the first and third lines, while foam and home link the second and fourth. Alternating those sounds gives the rhyme pattern ABAB.

Question 35

The continuation of meaning without pause, from one line to the next is

  1. enjambment
  2. synecdoche
  3. alliteration
  4. melodrama
Answer and explanation

A: enjambment

Enjambment carries a sentence or phrase across a verse line break without a full syntactic pause. It concerns the relation between sentence structure and lineation.

Question 40

In literary criticism, the vocabulary or language used by a writer is generally known as

  1. figure of speech
  2. diction
  3. expression
  4. rhythm
Answer and explanation

B: diction

Diction refers to a writer’s choice and use of words. Rhythm concerns patterned movement or sound, while a figure of speech is a particular figurative expression.

  4. the natural by the artificial
