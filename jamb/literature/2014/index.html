Question 6

In The Tempest, which role identifies Ariel among these choices?

  1. Leader of the spirits
  2. Prospero’s daughter
  3. Alonso’s wife
  4. Assistant to Sycorax
Answer and explanation

A: Leader of the spirits

Ariel is a spirit serving Prospero and organising other spirits in his tasks. Miranda is Prospero’s daughter; Ariel is neither a human wife nor Sycorax’s willing assistant.

Question 7

In The Tempest, how long have Prospero and Miranda lived on the island before the opening shipwreck?

  1. two decades
  2. twelve years
  3. forty days
  4. eighteen months.
Answer and explanation

B: twelve years

Prospero tells Miranda that twelve years have passed since he was Duke of Milan and they were driven away. This establishes the length of their residence before the play’s shipwreck.

  4. child quest of an African woman

Pokuwaa’s desire to have a child drives the central struggle in A Woman in Her Prime. Her success as a farmer does not remove the pressure she experiences from a community that places great value on motherhood.

Question 14

In Purple Hibiscus, which change does Father Benedict introduce at St. Agnes’ church?

  1. there must be fasting every month
  2. the Credo must be recited in lgbo
  3. the Kyrie must be rendered only in Latin
  4. everyone must take holy communion
Answer and explanation

C: the Kyrie must be rendered only in Latin

Father Benedict insists on Latin for the Kyrie and Credo, while allowing Igbo for offertory songs. The requirement for the Kyrie therefore matches the change described in the opening of the novel.

Question 18

Which broad human struggle is exemplified by Santiago’s endurance in The Old Man and the Sea?

  1. attempt to catch fish
  2. desire to understand life
  3. influence of the sea on man
  4. struggle of man against-defeat
Answer and explanation

D: struggle of man against-defeat

Santiago persists through exhaustion, pain and the loss inflicted by sharks. His endurance gives the fishing struggle a wider meaning as resistance to defeat, beyond the practical attempt to catch a fish.

Question 27

Which device describes the direct address to the sun in Donne’s The Sun Rising?

  1. invocation
  2. ellipsis
  3. enjambment
  4. apostrophe
Answer and explanation

D: apostrophe

The speaker directly addresses the sun as though it could listen and answer. Address to this nonhuman object is apostrophe, rather than omission of words or a line-break technique.

Question 28

In Raleigh’s The Soul’s Errand, how is the soul portrayed?

  1. friend of suffering masses
  2. fearless message-bearer
  3. restorer of lost glory
  4. messenger of hope and peace
Answer and explanation

The speaker sends the soul to expose falsehood in powerful institutions and tells it not to fear confronting the influential. That makes it a fearless bearer of an unwelcome message, rather than a messenger promising peace.

Question 31

An action in a play that stimulates the audience to pity a character is

  1. pathos
  2. parody
  3. pyrrhic
  4. props
Answer and explanation

A: pathos

Pathos is the quality that evokes pity or sympathetic sorrow. A parody imitates for comic effect, and props are physical objects used during a performance.

Question 32

Purgation of emotion, pity and fear is

  1. epilogue
  2. exposition
  3. catharsis
  4. catastrophe
Answer and explanation

C: catharsis

Catharsis names the purgation or clarification of emotions traditionally associated with tragedy, especially pity and fear. An epilogue is a concluding speech rather than this emotional effect.

Question 37

In literature, a flat character can be described as one who

  1. dies abruptly
  2. achieves greatness
  3. is undeveloped
  4. undergoes changes
Answer and explanation

C: is undeveloped

A flat character is presented through a limited range of traits rather than complex psychological development. Undeveloped is the closest option; becoming great or dying does not define flatness.

Question 39

The speech made at the end of a dramatic performance is generally called

  1. a dirge
  2. a monologue
  3. a prologue.
  4. an epilogue
Answer and explanation

D: an epilogue

An epilogue follows the main dramatic action and provides a concluding address or comment. A prologue precedes the action, while a dirge is a lament.

Question 42

He is a faithful liar The above is an example of

What figure of speech does the phrase exemplify?

  1. epigram
  2. oxymoron
  3. euphemism
  4. antithesis
Answer and explanation

B: oxymoron

Faithful usually suggests trustworthiness, while liar denies truthfulness. Bringing these apparently opposing qualities together creates an oxymoron in this reading of the phrase.
