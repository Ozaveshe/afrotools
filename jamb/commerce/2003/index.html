Question 3

The right of ownership with respect to goods or property is

  1. right of occupancy
  2. proxy
  3. title
  4. certificate of occupancy
Answer and explanation

C: title

Title is the legal ownership right over goods or property. A proxy concerns authority to act for someone else, while a certificate is a document that may provide evidence of a particular right.

Question 4

The main motive of chambers of commerce and trade association is to

  1. assist the government in achieving its objectives
  2. protect the interest of consumers
  3. maximize profit
  4. protect and promote the interests of members
Answer and explanation

D: protect and promote the interests of members

These associations collectively represent their members and promote their business interests. They are not formed primarily to earn profits for themselves or act as consumer organisations.

Question 8

Ojo purchased on credit 200 cartons of milk at ₦1,000 each, 150 bags of sugar at ₦500 each, and 50 bags of rice at ₦3,000 each from Sani. Sani allowed 10% trade discount and a cash discount of 50% for payment within the agreed period. Ojo paid within that period. How much is the trade discount alone?

  1. ₦42,500
  2. ₦27,500
  3. ₦22,500
  4. ₦20,000
Answer and explanation

A: ₦42,500

The list total is 200×₦1,000 + 150×₦500 + 50×₦3,000 = ₦425,000. The trade discount alone is 10% of this total, which is ₦42,500.

Question 10

The concept that refers to the sale, transfer or exchange of goods and services is

  1. Industry
  2. Trade
  3. Tariff
  4. Marketing
Answer and explanation

B: Trade

Trade is the exchange, purchase or sale of goods and services. Industry concerns production, a tariff is a charge on trade, and marketing includes wider activities around customers and products.

Question 13

The payment made periodically in respect of an insurance policy entered into is known as

  1. Bond
  2. Commission
  3. Premium
  4. Surrender value
Answer and explanation

A premium is the payment the policyholder makes for insurance cover. Commission remunerates an intermediary, and surrender value is an amount potentially paid when certain policies end early.

  2. Audience

Question 24

Among these groups in their stated capacity, whose creditor claim ranks ahead of shareholders’ capital claims when a company is liquidated?

  1. Debenture holders
  2. Members of the board
  3. Preference shareholders
  4. Ordinary shareholders
Answer and explanation

A: Debenture holders

Debenture holders are creditors, so their debt claims rank ahead of shareholders’ capital claims. This comparison is among the listed groups; it does not mean debenture holders outrank every other possible claim.

Question 46

Balances: paid-up capital ₦200,000; retained earnings ₦50,000; creditors ₦50,000; bills payable ₦50,000; dividends payable ₦70,000. Assets: buildings ₦50,000; plant and machinery ₦35,000; debtors ₦50,000; fixtures ₦20,000; stocks ₦145,000; bank ₦120,000. Both sides total ₦420,000. Find owner’s equity.

  1. ₦250,000
  2. ₦300,000
  3. ₦420,000
  4. ₦200,000
Answer and explanation

A: ₦250,000

Owner’s equity is paid-up capital plus retained earnings: ₦200,000 + ₦50,000 = ₦250,000. It also equals assets ₦420,000 less the ₦170,000 owed to creditors and other payees.

Question 47

Balances: paid-up capital ₦200,000; retained earnings ₦50,000; creditors ₦50,000; bills payable ₦50,000; dividends payable ₦70,000. Assets: buildings ₦50,000; plant and machinery ₦35,000; debtors ₦50,000; fixtures ₦20,000; stocks ₦145,000; bank ₦120,000. Both sides total ₦420,000. Find the current ratio to two decimal places.

  1. 1.53:1
  2. 1.85:1
  3. 0.87:1
  4. 0.50:1
Answer and explanation

B: 1.85:1

Current assets are debtors ₦50,000 + stocks ₦145,000 + bank ₦120,000 = ₦315,000. Current liabilities total ₦170,000, so the ratio is 315,000/170,000 = 1.85:1 to two decimal places.

Question 50

In the traditional classification of banking functions, public-debt operations for government are associated with which bank?

  1. Merchant bank
  2. Central bank
  3. Commercial bank
  4. Development bank
Answer and explanation

B: Central bank

A central bank can act as banker and fiscal agent to government, including public-debt operations. This is a central-banking function among the listed bank types, without implying exclusive control of all debt management.
