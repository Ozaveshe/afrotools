Question 1

Commerce is defined as the study of how

  1. man utilizes the resources in his physical environment
  2. man produces, distributes and consumes his goods and services
  3. man buys, sells and distributes goods and services
  4. raw materials are changed into finished goods.
Answer and explanation

C: man buys, sells and distributes goods and services

Commerce concerns buying, selling and distributing goods and services, including activities that support exchange. Production and consumption together describe a wider field of economic activity.

Question 2

The type of activity which turns processed raw materials into consumer and industrial goods is described as

  1. extractive
  2. manufacturing
  3. constructive
  4. processing
Answer and explanation

B: manufacturing

Manufacturing transforms processed materials into finished goods for consumers or industry. Extraction obtains natural resources, while construction assembles structures such as roads and buildings.

Question 6

Which of the following takes place when firms producing at different stages in the same industry combine?

  1. Conglomeration
  2. Vertical integration
  3. Horizontal integration
  4. Cartel.
Answer and explanation

B: Vertical integration

Vertical integration joins businesses at different stages of the same supply chain, such as a producer and its distributor. Horizontal integration combines businesses at the same stage.

Question 7

Among the following groups, whose claims rank ahead of shareholders when a company is liquidated?

  1. Preference shareholders
  2. Debenture holders
  3. Ordinary shareholders
  4. Cumulative preference shareholders
Answer and explanation

B: Debenture holders

Debenture holders lend money to the company and have creditor claims. These claims rank ahead of distributions to owners, including preference and ordinary shareholders.

Question 8

Compulsory dissolution of a business can arise from

  1. An agreement by the owners
  2. A declaration by a court of law
  3. The termination of its life
  4. An unfavourable economic climate
Answer and explanation

B: A declaration by a court of law

Compulsory winding up is imposed through a court order. An agreement by the owners is voluntary, while poor economic conditions alone do not constitute a legal dissolution order.

Question 12

What are fixtures and fittings in a balance sheet?

  1. Liquid capital
  2. Current assets
  3. Fixed assets
  4. Working capital
Answer and explanation

C: Fixed assets

Fixtures and fittings are kept for continuing use in running the business. They are therefore fixed assets, rather than inventory, liquid funds or the net working-capital balance.

Question 13

Governments impose import duties for the following reasons EXCEPT which?

  1. To raise revenue for other services
  2. To act as a retaliatory measure against other governments
  3. To discourage the redistribution of income at home
  4. To protect home industries from external competition
Answer and explanation

Import duties can raise public revenue, protect domestic producers or respond to another country’s trade restrictions. Discouraging domestic income redistribution is not a standard purpose of such duties.

Question 16

What is the major function of the wholesaler?

  1. Buying from small-scale producers and selling to exporters.
  2. Buying in bulk and selling in small quantities
  3. Granting of credit facilities to retailers
  4. Providing information to manufacturers on market situation.
Answer and explanation

B: Buying in bulk and selling in small quantities

Wholesalers buy large quantities and divide them into smaller lots for retailers. Credit and market information may support this role, but bulk buying and breaking bulk are its central trading function.

Question 17

The rate at which a country’s exports exchange for its imports is called

  1. balance of payments
  2. balance of trade
  3. terms of payment
  4. terms of trade.
Answer and explanation

D: terms of trade.

Terms of trade compare export prices with import prices. They describe how much imports a country can obtain for its exports, rather than the monetary trade balance.

Question 18

The difference between the value of a country’s exports and imports within a given period is called what?

  1. balance of payments
  2. balance of trade
  3. comparative cost advantage
  4. comparative advantage
Answer and explanation

B: balance of trade

The balance of trade compares export receipts with import expenditure. The balance of payments is broader, also recording international income, transfers and financial transactions.

Question 22

Goods discharged from ships on which import duties have not been paid are kept in the

  1. ordinary warehouse
  2. public warehouse
  3. private warehouse
  4. bonded warehouse.
Answer and explanation

D: bonded warehouse.

A bonded warehouse holds imported goods under customs control while duties remain unpaid. Storage there postpones duty payment until the goods are released under the applicable customs procedure.

Question 24

One of the effects of the devaluation of the Naira is that the

  1. goods that can be imported with the Naira have become cheaper
  2. value of other currencies relative to the Naira have become cheaper
  3. goods that can be exported from Nigeria have become costlier
  4. imports that can be bought with the Naira have become costlier.
Answer and explanation

D: imports that can be bought with the Naira have become costlier.

Devaluation lowers the domestic currency’s value relative to foreign currencies. With foreign prices unchanged, more naira are needed for the same imported goods, making imports costlier in naira.

Question 36

Amusa offers to sell Bello a hat for ₦30. Bello replies, “I will pay ₦29.” Which describes the exchange?

  1. an offer and acceptance
  2. an implied contract.
  3. a complete simple contract.
  4. an offer but no acceptance.
Answer and explanation

D: an offer but no acceptance.

The offer is to sell for ₦30. Replying with ₦29 proposes a different price, so it is a counter-offer rather than acceptance of the original terms. The exchange has not formed an agreed bargain.

Question 45

The principle of span of control stipulates which of the following?

  1. an employee should report to only one superior
  2. managers must demand accountability from their subordinates
  3. resources have to be economized within the various divisions
  4. the number of employees reporting to one superior should be kept to a workable maximum.
Answer and explanation

D: the number of employees reporting to one superior should be kept to a workable maximum.

Span of control is the number of subordinates directly supervised by one manager. Keeping that number manageable supports effective supervision; reporting to one boss is unity of command.
