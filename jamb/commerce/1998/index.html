Question 3

Extractive industries may also be denominated as

  1. direct industries
  2. servicing industries
  3. primary industries
  4. indirect industries.
Answer and explanation

C: primary industries

Extractive activities obtain resources directly from nature, such as minerals or timber. They belong to primary industry, supplying materials that secondary manufacturing can transform.

Question 7

Obi originally makes all decisions and takes all profits from his agricultural business. He and Musa agree to pool resources. Abayomi joins on condition that his liability is limited to his investment; they register Obi, Musa and Abayomi Agricultural Ventures. Which form of business does this describe?

  1. sole proprietorship
  2. general partnership
  3. limited partnership
  4. joint venture.
Answer and explanation

C: limited partnership

The business combines general partners with a partner whose liability is limited to his investment. That combination is characteristic of a limited partnership rather than an ordinary general partnership.

Question 13

SONU balances: plant ₦2,300; motor van ₦2,000; stock ₦1,250; debtors ₦480; bank ₦3,000; cash ₦20; creditors ₦1,000; accruals ₦187.50; capital ₦6,000; net profit ₦2,000; drawings ₦137.50. Find the current ratio.

  1. 4:1
  2. 4.5:1
  3. 5:1
  4. 25.5:1
Answer and explanation

A: 4:1

Current assets total stock ₦1,250 + debtors ₦480 + bank ₦3,000 + cash ₦20 = ₦4,750. Current liabilities are ₦1,187.50, giving ₦4,750 ÷ ₦1,187.50 = 4:1.

Question 15

The form of capital which is easily transferred into the form desired is known as

  1. working capital
  2. liquid capital
  3. circulating capital
  4. capital employed.
Answer and explanation

B: liquid capital

Liquid capital is readily available in money or a form easily converted for another use. It differs from capital tied up in machinery or other assets that cannot quickly be redirected.

Question 16

Turnover is ₦15,000 and cost of goods sold is ₦10,000. Find gross profit as a percentage of sales, to one decimal place.

  1. 23.3%
  2. 33.3%
  3. 50.0%
  4. 66.6%
Answer and explanation

B: 33.3%

Gross profit is ₦15,000 − ₦10,000 = ₦5,000. Divide by sales, not cost: ₦5,000 ÷ ₦15,000 × 100 = 33.3% to one decimal place.

Question 19

Entrepot trade is said to have taken place when goods are

  1. held at the port pending payment of custom duty
  2. sold in the ship under special regulation
  3. imported and then re-exported
  4. kept for further processing before sales.
Answer and explanation

C: imported and then re-exported

Entrepot trade involves importing goods and subsequently exporting them again. Merely holding goods while duties are pending is customs storage, not by itself re-export trade.

Question 24

The two main categories under which marine losses fall into are

  1. particular loss and average loss
  2. actual loss and general loss
  3. total loss and partial loss
  4. voyage policy loss and time policy loss.
Answer and explanation

C: total loss and partial loss

Marine losses are broadly classified as total or partial. Total loss may be actual or constructive, while particular and general average concern classifications within marine-loss treatment.

Question 25

What is the principle of indemnity intended to provide, subject to policy terms and limits?

  1. only half of the loss suffered
  2. double the value of the loss suffered
  3. the total sum of the premiums paid prior to the loss
  4. damage claims equal to the loss suffered.
Answer and explanation

D: damage claims equal to the loss suffered.

Indemnity aims to restore the insured to the financial position before the covered loss, rather than create a profit. Actual payment remains subject to the policy’s cover, limits and excess.

Question 28

  2. cheques, bank drafts, warrants and promissory notes
  3. bonds ivory, treasury bills, debentures and warrants
  4. stocks, shares, bonds and warrants.
Answer and explanation

D: stocks, shares, bonds and warrants.

Question 31

Product X was introduced into the market at N4.00 with its cost of production at N3.90 while competing products are selling at N4.50. What pricing policy are producers of X adopting?

  1. Market skimming
  2. Market penetration
  3. Product live promotion
  4. Above the market pricing.
Answer and explanation

B: Market penetration

The launch price of ₦4 is below competing products at ₦4.50 and only slightly above cost. This low introductory pricing is consistent with penetration pricing to attract buyers and build market share.

Question 32

An oral presentation in conversation with prospective buyers for the purpose of making sales is called what?

  1. branding of goods
  2. personal selling
  3. packaging
  4. publicity.
Answer and explanation

B: personal selling

Personal selling uses direct conversation with prospective buyers to explain the offer, answer questions and seek a sale. Branding and packaging identify or present a product rather than conduct that conversation.

Question 46

A business flow runs from the external environment into the internal environment, then back to the external environment. Which sequence fits the three stages?

  1. People → control → ideas
  2. Material → production → ideas
  3. Finance → marketing → product
  4. Material → production → product
Answer and explanation

D: Material → production → product

Materials enter from the external environment, production transforms them inside the organisation and products leave for external users. This is the input–transformation–output sequence.
