1995 · Question 3

In which of the following sets of occupations can an industrial worker be found?

  1. Extraction, commerce and construction
  2. Manufacturing, extraction and banking.
  3. Construction, quarrying and insurance.
  4. Manufacturing, extraction and construction.
Answer and explanation

D: Manufacturing, extraction and construction.

Manufacturing makes products, extraction obtains natural resources and construction builds structures. All three are industrial activities; banking, insurance and commerce provide services supporting economic exchange.
