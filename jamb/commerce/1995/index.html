Question 3

In which of the following sets of occupations can an industrial worker be found?

  1. Extraction, commerce and construction
  2. Manufacturing, extraction and banking.
  3. Construction, quarrying and insurance.
  4. Manufacturing, extraction and construction.
Answer and explanation

D: Manufacturing, extraction and construction.

Manufacturing makes products, extraction obtains natural resources and construction builds structures. All three are industrial activities; banking, insurance and commerce provide services supporting economic exchange.

Question 4

Construction activities include the building of houses and roads as well as

  1. bricklaying
  2. shoemaking
  3. blacksmithing
  4. car assembling.
Answer and explanation

A: bricklaying

Bricklaying creates walls and other parts of buildings, making it a construction activity. Shoemaking, blacksmithing and car assembly instead manufacture or work on movable products.

Question 5

Under the default Partnership Act 1890 rule, if partners have not agreed how to share profits, how are profits shared?

  1. in the ratio of the value of business secured by each partner for the partnership
  2. in the ratio of partners’ capital contributions
  3. in an equal proportion, irrespective of partners’ capital contributions
  4. according to duties performed by each partner.
Answer and explanation

C: in an equal proportion, irrespective of partners’ capital contributions

Under the default Partnership Act rule, partners share profits equally unless they agree otherwise. Unequal capital contributions do not by themselves replace that equal-sharing rule.

Question 6

In the traditional memorandum-and-articles framework, which listed information concerns internal governance in the articles of association?

  1. Rights and obligations of directors
  2. Objectives of the company.
  3. Amount of share capital.
  4. Limitation of liability of shareholders.

A: Rights and obligations of directors

Articles set rules for the company’s internal governance, including directors’ responsibilities and procedures. The traditional memorandum describes its constitutional objects, capital and liability framework.

Question 7

The merger of two companies producing the same type of products is an example of

  1. vertical integration
  2. horizontal integration
  3. lateral merger
  4. an acquisition.
Answer and explanation

B: horizontal integration

Combining companies making the same type of product at the same stage is horizontal integration. A vertical merger instead links different stages such as manufacturing and distribution.

Question 8

One of the implications of the incorporation of a company is that

  1. it is more difficult for the company to raise loans
  2. the company can sue but cannot be sued
  3. the company’s property becomes distinguished from that of its member
  4. its members become liable for all the debts of the company.
Answer and explanation

C: the company’s property becomes distinguished from that of its member

Incorporation establishes a legal entity distinct from its members. Company property belongs to that entity rather than directly to the shareholders, and the company can both sue and be sued.

Question 9

The main advantage of a sole trader is the freedom to

  1. employ anyone he likes
  2. seek advice from any source
  3. take quick decisions
  4. plough all the profits back into the business.
Answer and explanation

C: take quick decisions

A sole trader does not need partner or board agreement for routine business choices. That concentrated control allows quick decisions, although the owner must also bear responsibility for them.

Question 11

When a company uses more of loans than equity to finance its business, the company is said to be

  1. bankrupt
  2. solvent
  3. highly geared
  4. in a strong liquid position.
Answer and explanation

C: highly geared

Gearing compares debt finance with owners’ equity. Heavy reliance on loans gives high gearing and greater debt-service commitments, but it does not automatically mean the business is bankrupt.

Question 15

The financial index which compares current assets directly with current liabilities is known as current

  1. turnover
  2. capital
  3. ratio
  4. balance
Answer and explanation

C: ratio

The current ratio is current assets divided by current liabilities. It compares short-term resources with short-term obligations; it is a ratio rather than a money balance.

Question 16

Rights issue means the

  1. issue of shares to the directors of a company on favourable terms
  2. issue of shares by a company only to the founders of the company
  3. right of shareholders to vote on any issue
  4. issue of shares to shareholders on favourable terms.
Answer and explanation

D: issue of shares to shareholders on favourable terms.

A rights issue offers existing shareholders the opportunity to buy additional shares, commonly at a favourable subscription price. It concerns new capital, not merely voting rights or an offer only to directors.

Question 18

Opening stock ₦40,000; purchases ₦115,000; closing stock ₦60,000; sales ₦250,000. Find cost of goods sold.

  1. ₦155,000
  2. ₦95,000
  3. ₦50,000
  4. ₦30,000
Answer and explanation

B: ₦95,000

Goods available cost opening stock ₦40,000 plus purchases ₦115,000 = ₦155,000. Deduct closing stock ₦60,000 to obtain cost of goods sold of ₦95,000.

Question 19

In the channel of distribution, which of the following sets is entirely made up of middlemen?

  1. Wholesalers, retailers and agents.
  2. Manufacturers, consumers and retailers.
  3. Wholesalers, agent and consumers.
  4. Retailers, manufacturers and wholesalers.
Answer and explanation

A: Wholesalers, retailers and agents.

Wholesalers, retailers and agents act between producers and final buyers in distribution. Manufacturers create the goods and consumers use them, so those two groups are not middlemen.

Question 20

Goods imported into a country for the purpose of reexporting attracts a rebate known as

  1. customs draw back
  2. export royalty
  3. incentive
  4. export rebate.
Answer and explanation

A: customs draw back

Customs drawback is repayment of qualifying import duties when the relevant goods are subsequently exported. It is a duty-refund mechanism rather than an export royalty paid for resource rights.

  2. Refinancing and Recording Facility
  3. Refinancing and Rediscounting Facility

Question 24

Freight note is a document

  1. used by government to transport goods from one country to another
  2. issued by a shipping company giving details of charges
  3. used for payment for imported goods.
  4. given by an importer to pay for goods at a future period.
Answer and explanation

B: issued by a shipping company giving details of charges

A freight note sets out the carrier’s transport charges. It concerns payment for carriage, rather than the purchase price of the goods or a promise by the importer to pay the supplier later.

Question 25

An invoice for ₦600 allows discounts of 5% within one month, 2½% within three months and 1% within five months. How much is payable if settled in the second month?

  1. ₦570.00
  2. ₦585.00
  3. ₦594.00
  4. ₦600.00
Answer and explanation

B: ₦585.00

Payment in month two falls after the one-month 5% deadline but within the three-month 2½% period. Discount is ₦600 × 0.025 = ₦15, leaving ₦585 payable.

Question 26

Spot market goods are those which are available for

  1. immediate delivery
  2. short-term delivery
  3. long-term delivery
  4. end of year delivery
Answer and explanation

A: immediate delivery

A spot transaction concerns goods available for immediate or prompt delivery. A forward transaction instead agrees delivery at a future date, so long-term or year-end delivery is not the defining feature.

Question 27

The main advantage of road transport is that it is

  1. convenient and flexible
  2. cheap and direct
  3. common and fast
  4. safe and available.
Answer and explanation

A: convenient and flexible

Road transport can change routes and reach individual premises, giving flexible door-to-door service. Its cost, speed and safety depend on the journey, so those are not universal advantages.

Question 28

An insurer accepts a large risk and then shares part of that risk with other insurers. What is this called?

  1. subrogation
  2. contribution
  3. re-insurance
  4. indemnity.
Answer and explanation

C: re-insurance

Reinsurance transfers part of an insurer’s accepted risk to another insurer. It helps spread exposure to large claims; contribution instead concerns overlapping policies covering the same loss.

  1. International Credit Union

  3. International Union of Credit and Investment Insurers
  4. Export Credit Guarantee Scheme.
Answer and explanation

The Berne Union is the International Union of Credit and Investment Insurers. Its members work in export-credit and investment insurance, rather than forming a consumer credit union.

Question 36

The breaking down of a market into separate and identifiable elements each with its own special product requirements is known as market

  1. differentiation
  2. Segmentation
  3. penetration
  4. identification.
Answer and explanation

Market segmentation divides potential customers into identifiable groups with different needs or characteristics. This enables products and marketing efforts to be tailored to each group.
