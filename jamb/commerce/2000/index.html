Question 4

The measure of independence available to the individual buisness units that come together is the main difference between

  1. vertical and horizontal integration
  2. merges and acquisitions
  3. trust and cartel
  4. consortium and amalgamation
Answer and explanation

C: trust and cartel

A cartel coordinates selected policies while its member firms retain their separate identity. A trust combines control more closely, so the degree of independence is a key distinction in the traditional classification.

Question 10

On liquidation of a public limited liability company, the residual owners are the

  1. Creditors
  2. debenture holders
  3. preference shareholders
  4. ordinary share holders
Answer and explanation

D: ordinary share holders

Ordinary shareholders hold the residual ownership claim. They receive only what remains after creditors and any preferential capital entitlements have been satisfied in liquidation.

Question 14

Which traditional insurance principle requires disclosure of material information affecting the risk?

  1. Indemnity
  2. Caveat emptor
  3. Subrogation
  4. Uberrimae fidei
Answer and explanation

D: Uberrimae fidei

Uberrimae fidei means utmost good faith. In the traditional insurance principle, material information affecting the risk must be disclosed so the insurer can assess the proposed cover.

Question 18

A computer represents data using continuously varying physical quantities and linear electronic circuits. Which type is this?

  1. digital computer
  2. hybrid computer
  3. mainframe computer
  4. Analogue computer
Answer and explanation

D: Analogue computer

An analogue computer represents quantities through continuously varying physical signals. A digital computer instead processes discrete values, while a hybrid combines analogue and digital techniques.

Question 19

Ocean liners are subdivided into

  1. cargo liners and tramp liners
  2. coastal liners and cargo liners
  3. passenger liners and cargo liners
  4. passenger liners and tramp liners
Answer and explanation

C: passenger liners and cargo liners

Ocean liners follow scheduled services and can be classified by their main cargo as passenger or cargo liners. Tramp shipping instead operates without the same regular liner schedule.

Question 22

A property worth ₦1,000 is insured for ₦800 with an average clause. Fire causes a ₦400 loss. Calculate the insurer’s payment under that clause.

  1. ₦560
  2. ₦460
  3. ₦420
  4. ₦320
Answer and explanation

D: ₦320

The average clause reduces the claim in the proportion insured to actual value. The property is insured for 800/1,000 = 80%, so payment on a ₦400 loss is ₦320.

Question 23

Which listed function distinguishes Nigeria’s central bank from commercial banks?

  1. renders services to customers
  2. carries out foreign exchange transactions
  3. issues currencies
  4. discounts bills
Answer and explanation

C: issues currencies

In Nigeria, issuing legal-tender currency is a central-bank function. Commercial banks provide payments, foreign-exchange services and bill discounting, but do not issue the national currency.

Question 24

Tourists with no fixed address in a town may receive their letters from the post office through a

  1. Poste restante
  2. Recorded delivery
  3. Parcel post
  4. Postmaster
Answer and explanation

A: Poste restante

Poste restante holds mail at a post office for collection by the addressee. It serves travellers or others without a fixed local address, rather than providing a home-delivery address.

Question 25

An invoice can be described as a

  1. contractual agreemnt for the sale of goods
  2. receipts containing necessary information about the goods
  3. document specifying quantity, description, prices and total value of purchases
  4. letter specifying the contractul sales of goods between two parties.
Answer and explanation

C: document specifying quantity, description, prices and total value of purchases

An invoice itemises the goods or services supplied, quantities, unit prices and total amount payable. It requests or records a charge and does not by itself prove payment as a receipt does.

Question 26

Question 46

Nationalisation and indigenisation differ directly in which of these respects?

  1. efficiency
  2. profit
  3. Technology
  4. shareholding
Answer and explanation

D: shareholding

Nationalisation transfers ownership to the state, whereas indigenisation transfers ownership or participation to local citizens. Their direct distinction is therefore the identity and pattern of shareholding.
