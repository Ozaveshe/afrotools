Question 6

In Nigeria in 2002, which agency implemented the government’s privatisation and commercialisation programme?

  1. securities and exchange commission
  2. technical committee on privatization and commercialization
  3. Nigeria stock exchange
  4. bureau of public enterprises
Answer and explanation

D: bureau of public enterprises

The Bureau of Public Enterprises implements the government’s privatisation programme and serves the National Council on Privatisation. The earlier technical committee had been replaced before the 2002 examination.

Question 12

The commercialization of public enterprises is aimed at

  1. increasing efficiency and making the enterprises self-sufficient
  2. advertising and promoting the goods and services of the enterprise
  3. increased assistance and patronage of the enterprises by the public
  4. selling the goods and services of the enterprises
Answer and explanation

A: increasing efficiency and making the enterprises self-sufficient

Commercialisation applies business principles to public enterprises so they operate efficiently and cover their costs. It does not simply mean advertising or selling more products.

Question 13

What is the established legal term for protection of an author’s original literary works, whether published or unpublished?

  1. authors right
  2. constitutional right
  3. patent right
  4. copyright
Answer and explanation

D: copyright

Copyright is the established term for the rights protecting an author’s original literary and other creative works. A patent protects inventions rather than the expression in a written work.

Question 14

What is the payment due under an endowment policy at maturity, or to the beneficiary on earlier death, called?

  1. indemnity
  2. surrender value
  3. lump sum benefit
  4. insurance premium
Answer and explanation

C: lump sum benefit

An endowment policy pays its agreed benefit at maturity or on earlier death, according to the policy. This is a lump-sum benefit; a premium is money paid for cover and surrender value concerns early termination.

Question 15

The machine used for sending telex message is known as a

  1. radar
  2. fax machine
  3. Dictaphone
  4. teleprinter
Answer and explanation

D: teleprinter

A teleprinter sends and receives typed messages over a telex connection. A fax sends document images, a Dictaphone records speech, and radar detects objects using radio waves.

Question 16

Which of these insurance principles requires a close connection between the actual loss suffered and the risk insured?

  1. Indemnity
  2. proximate cause
  3. contribution
  4. subrogation
Answer and explanation

B: proximate cause

Proximate cause identifies the effective cause of the loss and whether it is an insured peril. Contribution divides liability among insurers; subrogation concerns recovery rights after payment.

Question 18

Which of the following is used to inform the addressee that a registered parcel is ready for collection?

  1. Express label
  2. counterfoil
  3. telegram
  4. Slip
Answer and explanation

D: Slip

A collection slip notifies the addressee that an item is waiting at the post office. A counterfoil is the retained portion of a document, while an express label identifies a service rather than collection readiness.

Question 21

The temporary insurance certificate issued to the insured before drawing up a policy is a

  1. cover note
  2. testimonial
  3. time policy
  4. proposal form
Answer and explanation

A: cover note

A cover note provides temporary evidence of insurance while the full policy is being prepared. A proposal form is the application for cover, so it does not itself confirm that insurance has begun.

Question 24

In the traditional money-market/capital-market classification, which securities characterise a stock exchange?

  1. long-term securities are sold
  2. all types of securities are sold
  3. short term securities are sold
  4. medium-term securities are sold
Answer and explanation

A: long-term securities are sold

In the traditional money-market and capital-market distinction, a stock exchange trades long-term securities such as shares and bonds. Short-term borrowing instruments belong primarily to the money market.

Question 28

I. Retail; II. Export; III. Transport; IV. Import; V. Insurance; VI. Banking and finance; VII. Wholesale; VIII. Communication. Which are aids to trade?

  1. I, II, III and VIII
  2. III, V, VI and VIII
  3. I, II, III and VI
  4. II, V, VI and VII
Answer and explanation

B: III, V, VI and VIII

Transport, insurance, banking and communication facilitate buying and selling, so they are aids to trade. Retail, wholesale, import and export are forms of trade itself.

  4. issued capital

Question 36

In traditional stock-exchange terminology, members dealing in securities on their own account are called what?

  1. bull
  2. jobbers
  3. broker
  4. stagers
Answer and explanation

B: jobbers

In traditional stock-exchange terminology, jobbers deal in securities on their own account. Brokers act for clients, while bulls are identified by expectations of rising prices rather than that market-making role.

Question 39

Which of the following industries is normally located near the source of its raw materials?

  1. Footwear
  2. cement factory
  3. textile industry
  4. automobile assembly
Answer and explanation

Question 41

In traditional securities terminology, securities recorded in a register without issuing certificates to buyers are called what?

  1. authorized securities
  2. inscribed securities
  3. bearer securities
  4. registered securities
Answer and explanation

In the traditional distinction, inscribed stock records ownership in a register without issuing a negotiable certificate. Bearer securities instead rely on possession of the instrument.

Question 44

What does E&OE stand for on a commercial document?

  1. errors of exception
  2. errors and omissions excepted
  3. estimated and order error
  4. end of error
Answer and explanation

B: errors and omissions excepted

E&OE expands to errors and omissions excepted. It indicates that an accidental error or omission may be corrected; it is not a calculation instruction or a guarantee of accuracy.

An important issue for consideration in the product element of the marketing mix is the

  1. adequate promotion of the product
  2. channel of distribution of the product
  3. price of the product
  4. planning and development of the product
Answer and explanation

Question 49

An advantage of hire purchase to the consumer is the

  1. Economies of scale in production
  2. Possession of goods before full payment
  3. Increase in turnover and profits
  4. Low interest rate chargeable
Answer and explanation

B: Possession of goods before full payment

Hire purchase lets a customer possess and use goods while paying instalments, before the whole price has been paid. Ownership normally follows completion of the agreed conditions; low interest is not guaranteed.
