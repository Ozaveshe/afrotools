Question 1

Ships that sail across the ocean and operate on scheduled timetables are

  1. ferries
  2. ocean liners
  3. tramp steamers
  4. coastal liners.
Answer and explanation

B: ocean liners

Ocean liners provide regular services across oceans according to schedules. Tramp vessels seek cargo without a fixed regular timetable; coastal liners serve coastal rather than ocean-crossing routes.

Question 2

Which is a speculative risk normally excluded from ordinary insurance cover?

  1. gambling
  2. damage to property
  3. death
  4. marine problems.
Answer and explanation

A: gambling

Gambling deliberately creates a speculative chance of gain or loss. Ordinary insurance covers specified fortuitous losses rather than reimbursing a person’s gambling losses.

Question 3

An operating system belongs to which category?

  1. microprogram
  2. system flowchart
  3. system software
  4. syntax.
Answer and explanation

C: system software

An operating system is system software: it manages hardware and provides services for applications. A flowchart represents a process, and syntax describes the rules for writing a language.

Question 5

  1. Insurance
  2. Assurance
  3. Warranty
  4. Reinsurance
Answer and explanation

Question 13

Consumer rights include the right to

  1. credit
  2. free samples of a product
  3. redress
  4. a share of profits.
Answer and explanation

C: redress

Redress means access to a remedy when consumer rights are breached, such as replacement or refund where appropriate. Consumers have no general right to free samples, credit or a share of business profits.

Question 23

The instruments of credit include

  1. billboards and postal stamps
  2. payment vouchers and statement of account
  3. bills of exchange and promissory notes
  4. bills of exchange and salary vouchers.
Answer and explanation

C: bills of exchange and promissory notes

A bill of exchange orders payment and a promissory note promises payment. Both can embody credit obligations, unlike vouchers and account statements, which primarily record transactions.

Question 26

In international trade, documentary credit is also known as

  1. credit note
  2. letters of credit
  3. bankers' drafts
  4. letter of hypothecation.
Answer and explanation

B: letters of credit

A documentary credit is a letter of credit under which the bank undertakes payment against complying documents. A credit note adjusts an invoice and a bank draft is a different payment instrument.
