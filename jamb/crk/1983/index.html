Question 1

Who said this, ‘there shall be neither dew nor rain these years except by my word?’

  1. Elisha
  2. Ezekiel
  3. Elijah
  4. Obadiah
  5. Nehemiah
Answer and explanation

C: Elijah

In 1 Kings 17:1, Elijah announces the drought to Ahab. Elisha is a different prophet who appears later in the narrative.

Question 2

  1. giving the living child to her opponent.
  2. killing the living child
  3. joint ownership of the living child.
  4. king Solomon having the living child.
  5. state ownership of the living child.
Answer and explanation

B: killing the living child

In 1 Kings 3:26, the woman whose son had died accepts dividing the living child. The living child’s mother instead asks that he be spared and given to the other woman.

Question 31

  3. the kingdom of God will expand world-wide from what appears to be an insignificant beginning.
  4. It is not certain when the kingdom of God will come.
  5. The kingdom of God is within us.
Answer and explanation

C: the kingdom of God will expand world-wide from what appears to be an insignificant beginning.

Matthew 13:31-32 contrasts a very small seed with a plant large enough for birds to nest in. The intended comparison is growth from a small beginning to a much larger reach.
