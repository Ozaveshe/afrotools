Question 1

“Our sister, be the mother of thousands and ten thousands…” Who was the sister?

  1. Rachel
  2. Rebekah
  3. Hannah
  4. Peniah
Answer and explanation

B: Rebekah

Genesis 24:59–60 names Rebekah as the sister being sent away. Her family blesses her with the hope of numerous descendants.

Genesis 21:4 explicitly says Abraham circumcised Isaac at eight days old as God had commanded him. The stated reason is obedience to that command.

Question 11

  2. God commanded them to keep it
