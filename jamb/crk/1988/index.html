Question 1

The reference in Genesis chapter 6 to the union between the ‘Sons of God’ and the ‘Daughters of Men’ explains the origin of

  1. hermaphrodites
  2. mighty men of renown
  3. gods and goddesses
  4. dwarfs.
Answer and explanation

B: mighty men of renown

Genesis 6:4 associates the children from these unions with the mighty men of old, men of renown. The answer follows the account’s own description.

Question 2

‘Two nations in Rebekah’s womb refers to the

  1. Ammonites and Moabites
  2. Moabites and Edomites
  3. Edomites and Israelites
  4. Israelites and Ishmaelites.
Answer and explanation

C: Edomites and Israelites

The two children are Esau and Jacob. Genesis identifies Esau with Edom and gives Jacob the name Israel, linking the brothers with Edomites and Israelites.
