Question 1

“Behold, I have given you every plant yielding seed which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree with seed in its fruit; ...” In the statement above, God made all provision for man to

  1. tend and preserve
  2. have enough food
  3. make a beautiful garden
  4. look at and admire
Answer and explanation

B: have enough food

The remainder of Genesis 1:29 says these seed-bearing plants and fruit trees are given for food.
