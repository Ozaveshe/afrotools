Question 1

What was upon the face of the deep on the first day of creation?

  1. Light
  2. Cloud
  3. Water
  4. Darkness
Answer and explanation

D: Darkness

Genesis 1:2 describes darkness over the surface of the deep before the command for light. Darkness is the condition asked about.
