Question 1

“I heard the sound of thee in the garden, and I was afraid, because I was naked; and I hid myself.” The question that immediately followed this statement was

  1. “What is this that you have done?”
  2. “Who told you that you are naked?”
  3. “Where are you?”
  4. “Have you eaten of the tree of which I commanded you not to eat?”
Answer and explanation

B: “Who told you that you are naked?”

Genesis 3:11 first asks who told the man that he was naked. The question about eating from the tree follows immediately afterwards.

Question 2

One of the rules given to Noah after the flood was that he should

  1. cultivate the land
  2. not eat the flesh with its blood
  3. send out the dove
  4. remain in the ark.
Answer and explanation

B: not eat the flesh with its blood

Genesis 9:4 prohibits eating flesh with its life, identified as its blood. The restriction is given in the instructions after the Flood.
