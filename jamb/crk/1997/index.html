Question 1

In describing God’s sovereignty, Amos compares the movement of the land to the Nile, which he describes as

  1. rising and sinking
  2. drying and flooding
  3. the source of the waters of the sea
  4. the source of Egypt’s power.
Answer and explanation

A: rising and sinking

Amos 9:5 describes the land rising and sinking like the River of Egypt. The comparison presents the land’s response when the Lord touches it.

Question 2

By keeping God’s covenant, the people of Israel would become

  1. the conquerors of nations
  2. a kingdom of priests to God
  3. the inheritors of the earth
  4. a nation where God would manifest Himself.
Answer and explanation

B: a kingdom of priests to God

Exodus 19 connects obeying God’s voice and keeping the covenant with becoming a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.

Question 4

  1. a proof of his superiority over them
  2. an opportunity to avenge their evil deeds
  3. a time to test their honesty
  4. a means of preserving their lives.
Answer and explanation

D: a means of preserving their lives.

Joseph tells his brothers God sent him ahead to preserve life and secure their survival. He interprets his position as a means of saving them.

Question 15

  1. show that nobody is above temptation
  2. demonstrate that he controls the world
  3. lure Jesus Christ from his mission

  1. preaching and hearing the word
  2. hearing and obeying the word
