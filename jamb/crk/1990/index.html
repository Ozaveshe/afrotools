Question 1

“Be fruitful and multiply ...” This statement occurs first in Genesis following the creation of

  1. day and night
  2. the earth and the seas
  3. living creatures and birds
  4. Adam and Eve
Answer and explanation

C: living creatures and birds

Genesis 1:22 first gives the blessing to the sea creatures and birds. The similar instruction to human beings comes later.

Question 3

The four promises of God to Abram were land,

  1. Greatness, riches and blessings
  2. Fame, circumcision and many descendants
  3. Many descendants, greatness and being a means of blessing
  4. Greatness, salvation and blessings.
Answer and explanation

C: Many descendants, greatness and being a means of blessing

Genesis 12 promises land, a great nation, a great name and blessing extending through Abram to others. Many descendants, greatness and being a means of blessing fit that promise.

Question 7

  1. command the sea to be divided
  2. throw his rod into the sea
  3. strike the sea twice
  4. stretch out his hand over the sea
Answer and explanation

D: stretch out his hand over the sea

Exodus 14:16 instructs Moses to lift his rod and stretch out his hand over the sea. It does not tell him to throw the rod into the water.

Question 12
