Question 1

According to Genesis Chapter 1, on the 6th day, God created

  1. the firmament
  2. light
  3. dry land and sea
  4. man and living creatures
  5. the sun, moon and stars.
Answer and explanation

D: man and living creatures

Genesis 1:24-31 describes land animals and human beings and concludes with the sixth day. The heavenly lights belong to an earlier day.

Question 2

According to Genesis 7:24, the waters prevailed on the earth during Noah’s flood for

  1. two weeks
  2. ten weeks
  3. ten days
  4. a hundred days
  5. a hundred and fifty days
Answer and explanation

E: a hundred and fifty days

Genesis 7:24 specifies one hundred and fifty days for the prevailing waters. This is distinct from the forty days of rain and the later departure from the ark.
