Question 1

What effect did the sudden death of Ananias and Sapphira have on the early disciples?

  1. Many more disciples were won to God
  2. Great fear gripped them all
  3. The disciples became more united
  4. Apostle peter was highly respected
Answer and explanation

B: Great fear gripped them all

Acts records great fear among those who hear and throughout the church after the deaths of Ananias and Sapphira.

Question 3

“We must work the works of him who sent me, while it is day; night comes, when no one can work.” Jesus made the statement above before healing the

  1. centurion’s servant
  2. ten lepers
  3. man born blind
  4. paralytic man
Answer and explanation

C: man born blind

John 9:4–7 places the statement before Jesus makes mud and sends the man born blind to wash in the pool of Siloam. The man returns able to see.

Question 12

  1. their devotion of prayer
  2. their suffering and endurance
  3. the grace given to them
  4. enablement by the spirit.
Answer and explanation

C: the grace given to them

Romans 12:4–6 compares believers to members of one body with different functions. Their gifts differ according to the grace given to them, while they belong to one body in Christ.
