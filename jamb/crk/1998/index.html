Question 2

God commanded the earth to bring forth living creatures according to their kinds on the

  1. 3rd day
  2. 4th day

The command for the earth to bring forth living creatures occurs in the section ending with the sixth day.

When God heard the groaning of the people of Israel in Egypt, He

  1. got angry with the Pharaoh
  2. sent a warning to the Pharaoh
  3. remembered His covenant with Abraham

Exodus 2:24 says God heard Israel’s groaning and remembered his covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.
