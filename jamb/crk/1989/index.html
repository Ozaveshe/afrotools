Question 2

The first generation of mankind was destroyed in the great flood of water because of

  1. corruption and violence
  2. disobedience to the Torah
  3. worship of idols
  4. immoral sex relationships.
Answer and explanation

A: corruption and violence

Genesis 6:11-13 describes corruption and violence and gives these as the reason for the approaching destruction by the Flood.

Question 3

Which of the following is the sign of the covenant between God and Abraham?

  1. The sprinkling of blood on door posts
  2. Going to Jerusalem every year.
  3. Keeping of the Passover
  4. Circumcision in the flesh of the foreskins.
Answer and explanation

D: Circumcision in the flesh of the foreskins.

Genesis 17:10-11 identifies circumcision as the sign of the covenant with Abraham. The Passover belongs to a later account.
