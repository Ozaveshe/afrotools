Question 1

When Elijah escaped to the brook Cherith, he was fed by

  1. angels
  2. doves
  3. ravens
  4. sparrows,
Answer and explanation

C: ravens

First Kings 17 says God commanded ravens to feed Elijah. They brought bread and meat while he drank from the brook.

Question 2

The contest on Mount Carmel was to prove that

  1. Baal was the god of Israel
  2. Baal controlled nature
  3. God was supreme to other gods
  4. Israel had faith in God
Answer and explanation

C: God was supreme to other gods

The test was to identify the God who answered by fire. When the fire fell, the people confessed that the LORD is God.

  3. it was his only vineyard

Naboth refuses Ahab’s offer because the vineyard is the inheritance of his fathers. His answer names the ancestral inheritance as the reason.

Question 5

  2. sweeping reforms of the religious life of his people
  3. curtailing the Assyrian aggressions

Second Kings 23 describes Josiah removing idolatrous worship and renewing obedience to the law. These extensive religious reforms distinguish his reign.
