Question 1

After the Flood, God allowed all food to be eaten except

  1. olive leaves.
  2. flesh with its blood in it.
  3. animals with cloven hooves.
  4. reptiles.
  5. birds of prey.
Answer and explanation

B: flesh with its blood in it.

Genesis 9:3-4 permits animals as food but excludes flesh with its life, identified as its blood. This is the restriction stated after the Flood.
