Question 1

‘This at last is bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh; she shall be called Woman because she was …

  1. The first woman
  2. The mother of all living
  3. The man’s partner
  4. Taken out of man.
Answer and explanation

D: Taken out of man.

Genesis 2:23 completes the statement by saying that the woman was taken out of man. This is the explanation given within that creation narrative.
