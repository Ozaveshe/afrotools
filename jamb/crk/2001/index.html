Question 1

What did Saul do after his defeat of the Amalekites:

  1. He set up a monument for himself
  2. He returned to Shiloh
  3. He offered sacrifices to god
  4. He repented to his sins
Answer and explanation

A: He set up a monument for himself

First Samuel 15:12 reports that Saul went to Carmel and set up a monument for himself after the campaign.
