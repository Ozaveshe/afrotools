Question 1

According to the creation account of Genesis 1, God charged man to

  1. rest on the Sabbath day
  2. worship Him
  3. have dominion over all the earth
  4. obey all His commandments.
Answer and explanation

C: have dominion over all the earth

Genesis 1:26-28 gives humanity dominion over the living creatures and the earth. This is the charge represented in option C.

Question 3

When God told Abraham that Sarah would have a child, Abraham laughed because

  1. Hagar prompted him
  2. his wife Sarah laughed
  3. they were too old to have a child
  4. he already had a child called Ishmael.
Answer and explanation

C: they were too old to have a child

Genesis 17:17 records Abraham asking whether a hundred-year-old man and ninety-year-old Sarah could have a child. Their ages explain his laughter.

  2. give a tenth of all he would have to God
