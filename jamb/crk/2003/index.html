Question 2

The two sons of Samuel who became judges in Israel were

  1. Joel and Abijah
  2. Abimelech and Abidjah
  3. Hophni and Phinehas
  4. Joel and Abimelech
Answer and explanation

A: Joel and Abijah

First Samuel 8:1–2 names Samuel’s firstborn son as Joel and his second son as Abijah. Samuel appoints them as judges, and they serve in Beersheba.
