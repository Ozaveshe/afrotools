Question 1

“Let us make man in our image...” In the statement above, ‘man’ refers to

  1. Eve
  2. Adam
  3. male
  4. male and female
Answer and explanation

D: male and female

Genesis 1:27 explains the human creation in God’s image as male and female. The term in the preceding verse includes both.

  2. the law brings about salvation

  4. the works of the law are of the devil.

Galatians requires complete observance of the law and says no one is justified by it. Paul’s account of universal sin explains the failure to meet that requirement.
