Practice question

Myschool 2024 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

In The Life Changer, who supplied Salma with the notes she called her 'Notes of Salvation'?

  1. Tomiwa
  2. Kolawole Abdul
  3. The female invigilator
  4. Ummi
Answer and explanation

B: Kolawole Abdul

Kolawole was the student beside Salma who passed her the notes during the examination. 'Notes of Salvation' was Salma's name for those notes.

Practice question

Myschool 2024 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Which word rhymes with boys?

  1. Moist
  2. Elbows
  3. Noise
  4. Stays
Answer and explanation

C: Noise

Boys and noise both end in /ɔɪz/. Moist ends in /ɔɪst/, while elbows and stays have different vowel sounds.

Practice question

Myschool 2024 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Which pattern shows the main stress in productivity?

  1. First syllable: PROductivity
  2. Second syllable: proDUCtivity
  3. Third syllable: producTIvity
  4. Fourth syllable: productiVIty
Answer and explanation

C: Third syllable: producTIvity

The main stress falls on the syllable 'tiv': produc-TIV-i-ty. The earlier 'prod' has only secondary stress.

Practice question

Myschool 2024 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Which word contains the same first vowel sound as the 'u' in busy?

  1. Vineyard
  2. Automobile
  3. Twilight
  4. Island
Answer and explanation

A: Vineyard

The first vowel in busy is /ɪ/, as in the first syllable of vineyard. Twilight and island begin with /aɪ/ instead.

Practice question

Which word ends with the same voiced 'th' sound as clothe?

  1. Cloth
  2. Smooth
  3. Good
  4. Thesis
Answer and explanation

B: Smooth

Clothe and smooth end in /ð/. Cloth and thesis use /θ/, while good ends in /d/.

Practice question

Myschool 2024 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

___ of his late arrival, Okupe still benefited from the coaching.

  1. Despite
  2. Instead
  3. In spite
  4. In case
Answer and explanation

C: In spite

'In spite of' expresses the intended contrast. The word 'of' is already in the sentence, so 'despite of' would be ungrammatical.
