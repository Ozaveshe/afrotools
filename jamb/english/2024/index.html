In The Life Changer, who supplied Salma with the notes she called her 'Notes of Salvation'?

  1. Tomiwa
  2. Kolawole Abdul
  3. The female invigilator
  4. Ummi
Answer and explanation

B: Kolawole Abdul

Kolawole was the student beside Salma who passed her the notes during the examination. 'Notes of Salvation' was Salma's name for those notes.

Which word rhymes with boys?

  1. Moist
  2. Elbows
  3. Noise
  4. Stays
Answer and explanation

C: Noise

Boys and noise both end in /ɔɪz/. Moist ends in /ɔɪst/, while elbows and stays have different vowel sounds.

Which pattern shows the main stress in productivity?

  1. First syllable: PROductivity
  2. Second syllable: proDUCtivity
  3. Third syllable: producTIvity
  4. Fourth syllable: productiVIty
Answer and explanation

C: Third syllable: producTIvity

The main stress falls on the syllable 'tiv': produc-TIV-i-ty. The earlier 'prod' has only secondary stress.
