Practice question

Myschool 2025 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Complete the familiar saying: 'What goes around ____ around.'

  1. is coming
  2. comes
  3. came
  4. come
Answer and explanation

B: comes

The established saying is 'What goes around comes around.' The singular subject 'what' takes 'comes' in this fixed expression.

Practice question

Myschool 2025 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Each of the responses ____ correct.

  1. have been
  2. were
  3. is
  4. are
Answer and explanation

C: is

Each is the singular subject; the plural noun after 'of' does not control the verb. Use 'is'.

Practice question

Myschool 2025 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Reports show that the ____ of theft has increased this year.

  1. accidence
  2. accident
  3. incident
  4. incidence
Answer and explanation

D: incidence

Incidence means the rate at which something happens. An incident is a single event, so 'incidence of theft' fits a statement about its increasing frequency.

Practice question

Myschool 2025 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

Yesterday, the doctor ____ the patient to take the medicine regularly.

  1. adviced
  2. advised
  3. advise
  4. advice
Answer and explanation

B: advised

Advised is the past tense of the verb advise. Advice is a noun, while 'adviced' is a misspelling; 'yesterday' calls for a past-tense verb here.
