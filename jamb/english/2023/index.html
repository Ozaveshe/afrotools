A reliable interpreter became indispensable to the meeting. What does 'indispensable' mean here?

  1. essential
  2. unpopular
  3. replaceable
  4. absent
Answer and explanation

A: essential

Someone indispensable is so important that the work cannot be managed without them. The interpreter was essential to the meeting, not merely popular or present.

Which word contains the same voiced 'th' sound /ð/ heard at the end of 'smooth'?

  1. wealth
  2. path
  3. brother
  4. think
Answer and explanation

C: brother

The final sound of 'smooth' is voiced /ð/, and 'brother' has /ð/ between its vowels. The 'th' in wealth, path and think is voiceless /θ/.
