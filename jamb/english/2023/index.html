Practice question

Poscholars 2023 collection · Original sitting and question number unconfirmed.

A runner trained hard but missed the marathon qualifying time by a whisker. Which statement is accurate?

  1. She qualified with ease
  2. She did not practise
  3. She fell just short despite serious preparation
  4. She abandoned the race
Answer and explanation

C: She fell just short despite serious preparation

A whisker is a very small margin, so she narrowly failed to meet the target even after hard training.
