Question 1

There are many different approaches to conflict management, each of which may have utility in particular circumstances. A great deal of scholarship has been devoted to analysing how and in what situations different approaches can be applied most effectively. Conflict management approaches can be classified into two broad categories: firstly, on the basis of the level of escalation at which the particular conflict is being managed. In this sense one can distinguish between the peaceful and the military approaches. The trademarks of the peaceful approach are negotiation, verbal persuasion, use of inducements, denial of privileges, and subtle manipulations short of the use of physical force, while those of the military approach relate to the use of physical coercion. The use of physical force could be by a party to the conflict or a third party, to promote one side’s interest, impose a settlement, or create a situation in which diplomatic negotiations can occur. Secondly, conflict management approaches can also be classified according to the status of the participants in the bargaining process. For example, a conflict could be managed through negotiation, that is, direct bargaining by the parties involved in the conflict; or through mediation, that is, with the help of a third party.

The expression “third party”, as used in the passage, means

  1. politician
  2. intruder
  3. conformist
  4. mediator
Answer and explanation

D: mediator

The final paragraph contrasts direct bargaining with mediation, in which an additional participant helps the conflicting parties. That assisting participant is a mediator. The passage does not define this person as a politician, an intruder or a conformist.

Question 3

There are many different approaches to conflict management, each of which may have utility in particular circumstances. A great deal of scholarship has been devoted to analysing how and in what situations different approaches can be applied most effectively. Conflict management approaches can be classified into two broad categories: firstly, on the basis of the level of escalation at which the particular conflict is being managed. In this sense one can distinguish between the peaceful and the military approaches. The trademarks of the peaceful approach are negotiation, verbal persuasion, use of inducements, denial of privileges, and subtle manipulations short of the use of physical force, while those of the military approach relate to the use of physical coercion. The use of physical force could be by a party to the conflict or a third party, to promote one side’s interest, impose a settlement, or create a situation in which diplomatic negotiations can occur. Secondly, conflict management approaches can also be classified according to the status of the participants in the bargaining process. For example, a conflict could be managed through negotiation, that is, direct bargaining by the parties involved in the conflict; or through mediation, that is, with the help of a third party.

Which of the following statements can be inferred from the passage?

  1. The approach to employ in conflict management depends on the state of affairs.
  2. Only one conflict management approach can be applied in all situations
  3. All conflict management approaches can be applied in all situations
  4. There is a general disagreement among scholars on conflict management.
Answer and explanation

A: The approach to employ in conflict management depends on the state of affairs.

The opening says that different approaches are useful in particular circumstances. A captures that dependence on the situation. B and C wrongly make approaches universal, while D asserts a disagreement among scholars that the passage does not mention.
