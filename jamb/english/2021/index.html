choose the most appropriate option nearest in meaning to the word(s) underlined. The matter was considered a private concern of the chairman to handle Target: private.

  1. personal
  2. known
  3. public
  4. open
Answer and explanation

A: personal

Private means personal or concerning an individual. The chairman is handling his own concern, rather than a publicly known or open matter.
