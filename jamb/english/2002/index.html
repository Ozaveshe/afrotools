Question 96

Choose the option opposite in meaning to “extemporaneous”: The President gave another extemporaneous speech last Friday.

  1. fascinating
  2. unfavourable
  3. unprepared
  4. planned.
Answer and explanation

D: planned.

An extemporaneous speech is delivered without advance preparation. Planned is the opposite, while unprepared has a similar meaning and fascinating concerns audience interest.

Question 97

Choose the option opposite in meaning to “averred”: The witness averred that she had seen Dosun at the scene of the crime.

  1. confirmed
  2. argued
  3. affirmed
  4. denied
Answer and explanation

D: denied

Averred means stated firmly or asserted as true. Denied reverses the assertion; affirmed and confirmed support it, while argued does not necessarily contradict it.

Question 99

Choose the option opposite in meaning to “indomitable”: The athlete has unexpectedly become indomitable.

  1. unruly
  2. unconquerable
  3. weak
  4. disoriented.
Answer and explanation

C: weak

Indomitable describes someone who is very difficult to defeat or discourage. Weak is the closest opposing quality; unconquerable is similar in meaning, while unruly concerns discipline.
