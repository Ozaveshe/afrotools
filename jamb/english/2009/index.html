Question 26

Choose the most appropriate interpretation: It was our collective idea but Ado was doing everything to steal the show.

  1. Ado was not part of the planning but he tried to rob us of the benefit of it
  2. We planned the show together but Ado was trying to abort it
  3. It was a joint project but Ado is trying to attract the most attention
  4. We collated the idea for the show but Ado was trying to steal the programme
Answer and explanation

C: It was a joint project but Ado is trying to attract the most attention

To steal the show is to attract more attention or admiration than the other participants. Ado is trying to take the spotlight in a shared project, not cancel the project or physically steal its programme.

Question 27

  1. He has walked slowly to the shop
  2. He stole some bread from the shop but we could not stop him
  3. He broke into the bread shop before our very eyes
  4. He went quickly to the shop for some bread
Answer and explanation

D: He went quickly to the shop for some bread

To pop along to a place is to make a quick, casual visit there. The sentence says he went quickly to the shop for bread; it does not imply theft or a break-in.

Question 33

Choose the most appropriate interpretation: He was injured by friendly fire.

  1. He was wounded in a war by a friend of his who fired a shot mistakenly
  2. He was injured by a gun shot that was fired by a friend
  3. He was hit in a war by a war weapon fired by his own side
  4. It was a war and he was hit by a bomb fired by an unknown soldier
Answer and explanation

C: He was hit in a war by a war weapon fired by his own side

Friendly fire is fire from one’s own side that injures or kills allied personnel. Friendly identifies the military side involved; it does not mean that the person firing is a personal friend.

Question 34

Choose the most appropriate interpretation: Kola visits us on occasions, and I don't like that.

  1. I do not like Kola because he visits us only when an event is being celebrated
  2. I hate the fact that Kola only visits us when something is being celebrated
  3. That Kola does not visit us often is something I object to
  4. Kola seldom visits us and that is because he is a fair-weather friend
Answer and explanation

C: That Kola does not visit us often is something I object to

On occasions means sometimes rather than frequently. The speaker dislikes Kola’s infrequent visits. The sentence does not say the visits are limited to celebrations or explain Kola’s motives.

Question 36

  1. bitter
  2. stringy
  3. tough
  4. tender
Answer and explanation

D: tender

Leathery meat is tough and difficult to chew. Tender describes meat that is soft and easy to chew, so it gives the opposite meaning. Stringy and tough do not provide that contrast.
