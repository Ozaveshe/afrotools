Question 26

Choose the most appropriate interpretation: If the rain hadn't fallen, we wouldn't have missed the match.

  1. The rain is falling, so we will miss the match
  2. The rain fell, so we didn't watch the match
  3. The rain didn't fell, so we didn't watch the match
  4. The rain didn't fall, so we didn't watch the match
Answer and explanation

B: The rain fell, so we didn't watch the match

The third conditional imagines a different past: without the rain, they would not have missed the match. It therefore implies that rain did fall and they missed the match, which B expresses.

Question 29

  3. Unless the trader paid in full his order would be rejected
  4. As the trader did not increase his order, he did not need to pay in full
Answer and explanation

A: The trader who made full payment did not have his order reduced

Pruning down an order means reducing its size. The sentence says full payment prevented that reduction, which A preserves; it does not say the order was rejected or increased.
