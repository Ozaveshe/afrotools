Question 30

Choose the most appropriate interpretation: The next village is at least 30 kilometers away.

  1. The next village is 30 kilometres away, possibly less
  2. The next village is approximately 30 kilometres away
  3. The next village is only 30 kilometres away
  4. The next village is 30 kilometres away, or possibly more
Answer and explanation

D: The next village is 30 kilometres away, or possibly more

At least sets a minimum rather than an exact or approximate value. The next village is therefore 30 kilometres away or farther; a distance below 30 kilometres is excluded.

Question 32

Choose the most appropriate interpretation: My advice to Tolu was: Look before you leap.

  1. Tolu was advised to consider her opinions before reaching any decisions
  2. Tolu was advised to consider the possible consequences before taking action
  3. Tolu was advised to weigh her decisions by looking and leaping
  4. Tolu was advised to leap only after looking
Answer and explanation

B: Tolu was advised to consider the possible consequences before taking action

Look before you leap advises someone to think through possible consequences before acting. It is a warning against an impulsive decision, not an instruction about literal looking and jumping.

Question 36

  1. be recalcitrant
  2. be compromising
  3. be unrepentant
  4. be relenting
Answer and explanation

A: be recalcitrant

To steer a middle course is to take a moderate position and seek compromise. Being recalcitrant means resisting advice or cooperation, which contrasts with that approach; compromising and relenting move towards agreement.

Question 82

Choose the option that best completes the sentence: There was _____ any traffic as he drove home.

  1. hardly
  2. normally
  3. usually
  4. no
Answer and explanation

A: hardly

Hardly any means almost none and fits naturally before the uncountable noun traffic. No cannot precede any here, while normally and usually describe frequency rather than this small amount.
