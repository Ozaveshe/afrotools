Question 27

Choose the most appropriate interpretation: The computer operator was sacked for incompetence

  1. The computer operator was sacked for his foolishness
  2. The computer operator was sacked for his lazı- ness
  3. The computer operator was sacked for lack of skill
  4. The computer operator was sacked for lateness
Answer and explanation

C: The computer operator was sacked for lack of skill

Incompetence means lacking the skill or ability needed to perform a task properly. Lack of skill is the nearest explanation; it does not specifically mean laziness, lateness or foolishness.

  2. He was an expert who gave advice to government
  3. He was an expert that government did not listen to
  4. As an expert government must have taken to his advice

B: He was an expert who gave advice to government

Question 33

Choose the most appropriate interpretation: She wriggled out of her wedding gown

  1. She could not take off her gown herself so she was assisted.
  2. her dress got torn because of the snake-like pattern
  3. No one could have imagined that her dress was so tight
  4. It took her some time to remove her close-fitting dress.
Answer and explanation

D: It took her some time to remove her close-fitting dress.

Wriggling involves twisting the body to move through or out of a tight space. Removing a close-fitting dress with some effort is the nearest interpretation; the sentence does not say the gown tore or that someone helped her.

Choose the option with the same consonant sound as the q in bouquet.

  1. bucket
  2. banquet
  3. liquid
  4. squeeze.
Answer and explanation

A: bucket

The q in bouquet has the sound /k/, as does ck in bucket. The qu spelling in banquet, liquid and squeeze is pronounced with /kw/, so bucket is the intended sound match.
