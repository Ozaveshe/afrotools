Question 4

Seed plants are divided into

  1. tracheophytes and ferns
  2. angiosperms and gymnosperms
  3. monocotyledons and dicotyledons
  4. thallophytes and bryophytes
Answer and explanation

B: angiosperms and gymnosperms

Seed plants include gymnosperms, whose ovules are not enclosed in ovaries, and angiosperms, the flowering plants. Both groups reproduce using seeds, unlike ferns and bryophytes.

Question 12

Which of the following can cause shrinkage of living cells?

  1. Hypotonic solution
  2. Isotonic solution
  3. Deionized water
  4. Hypertonic solution
Answer and explanation

D: Hypertonic solution

A hypertonic solution draws water out of a cell by osmosis and can cause the cell contents to shrink. A hypotonic solution instead favours water entry, while an isotonic solution causes no net osmotic change.

Question 13

Which statement describes mammalian leucocytes?

  1. they are respiratory pigments
  2. they are most numerous and ramify all cells
  3. they are large and nucleated
  4. they are involved in blood clotting
Answer and explanation

C: they are large and nucleated

Leucocytes are nucleated white blood cells involved in defence. They are generally larger than red blood cells, which are far more numerous in normal mammalian blood and lack nuclei when mature.

Question 14

The conversion of a nutrient into a molecule in the body of a consumer is referred to as

  1. digestion
  2. assimilation
  3. absorption
  4. inhibition
Answer and explanation

B: assimilation

Assimilation incorporates absorbed nutrients into the body’s own materials. Digestion breaks food down, while absorption transfers digested molecules across the intestinal lining before they can be used by cells.

  1. locomotion

  3. growth
  4. taxis

Question 19

Three tubes contain sucrose: I, sucrose and water; II, sucrose and dilute acid; III, sucrose and its hydrolysing enzyme. Under suitable laboratory conditions, which tubes produce detectable glucose after catalysed hydrolysis?

  1. I and II only
  2. II and III only
  3. I only
  4. I, II and III
Answer and explanation

B: II and III only

Dilute acid or the appropriate enzyme can hydrolyse sucrose into glucose and fructose. Water alone does not appreciably hydrolyse sucrose during the usual laboratory test period, so tubes II and III are positive.

Question 22

The path followed by air as it passes through the lungs in mammals is

  1. trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli
  2. bronchi → trachea → alveoli → bronchioles
  3. trachea → bronchioles →bronchi→ alveoli
  4. bronchioles → alveoli → bronchi →trachea
Answer and explanation

A: trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli

Inhaled air passes down the trachea into bronchi, then progressively smaller bronchioles and finally the alveoli. The alveoli provide a large thin surface for exchange with pulmonary capillary blood.

Question 23

The movement response of a cockroach away from a light source can be described as

  1. positive phototaxism
  2. negative phototaxism
  3. negative phototropism
  4. positive phototropism
Answer and explanation

B: negative phototaxism

Movement away from light is negative phototaxis. Taxis is directional movement of an organism relative to a stimulus; tropism usually describes a directional growth response, particularly in plants.

Question 24

The vascular tissues in higher plants are responsible for

  1. the movement of food and water
  2. suction pressure
  3. transpiration pull
  4. the transport of gases and water
Answer and explanation

A: the movement of food and water

Xylem transports water and mineral ions, while phloem distributes sugars and other organic solutes. Together these vascular tissues connect sources, storage organs and growing parts of the plant.

Question 25

Which of the following organs regulates the levels of water, salts, hydrogen ions and urea in the mammalian blood?

  1. Liver
  2. Kidney
  3. Bladder
  4. Colon
Answer and explanation

B: Kidney

The kidneys filter blood and adjust tubular reabsorption and secretion. These processes regulate water, electrolytes and acid-base balance while allowing nitrogenous wastes such as urea to leave in urine.

Question 26

The usual one-way flow of water for gas exchange in a bony fish is

  1. operculum → gills → mouth
  2. gills → operculum → mouth
  3. mouth → operculum → gills
  4. mouth → gills → operculum
Answer and explanation

D: mouth → gills → operculum

In a typical bony fish, water enters the mouth, passes over the gills and exits through the opercular openings. Oxygen diffuses from this water into blood across the thin gill surfaces.

Question 43

At a single flower-colour locus, crossing true-breeding red and white plants produces pink heterozygotes. This illustrates

  1. codominance
  2. incomplete dominance
  3. mutation
  4. linkage
Answer and explanation

B: incomplete dominance

Incomplete dominance produces a heterozygous phenotype intermediate between the two homozygotes, such as pink flowers from red and white parental lines. Both alleles remain distinct rather than permanently blending.

Question 50

The independent evolution of wings for powered flight in birds and bats illustrates

  1. convergent evolution
  2. continuous variation
  3. coevolution
  4. divergent evolution
Answer and explanation

A: convergent evolution

Bird and bat wings evolved independently as flight structures. Their underlying forelimbs are homologous through shared vertebrate ancestry, but the independent acquisition of wings for powered flight illustrates convergence.
