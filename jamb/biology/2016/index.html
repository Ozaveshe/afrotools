Question 1

Which of the following structures is a protective adaptive feature of the Agama Lizard to the environment?

  1. Nuchal crest
  2. Claws
  3. Scaly skin
  4. Gular fold
Answer and explanation

C: Scaly skin

Keratinised scales protect lizard skin and help limit water loss. This covering supports life on land, unlike the generally more permeable skin of amphibians.

Question 13

I. Tissue II. System III. Cell IV Organ The correct sequence of increasing level of complexity is

  1. IV-II-III
  2. I-II-III-IV
  3. IV-III-I-II
  4. III-I-IV-II
Answer and explanation

D: III-I-IV-II

Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs cooperate in organ systems. The sequence is therefore III, I, IV, II.

Question 17

Bacteria which add atmospheric nitrogen to the soil are

  1. putrefying bacteria
  2. nitrifying bacteria
  3. nitrogen fixing bacteria
  4. denitrifying bacteria
Answer and explanation

C: nitrogen fixing bacteria

Nitrogen-fixing bacteria convert atmospheric nitrogen into biologically usable nitrogen compounds. This introduces nitrogen into soil food webs rather than returning it to the atmosphere.

Question 32

  1. the surface area to volume ratio is small
  2. they have lungs for diffusion
  3. materials have to move over long distance
  4. the surface area to volume ratio is large

Question 43

In photosynthesis, oxygen is liberated during

  1. conversion of energy
  2. photolysis
  3. splitting of carbon (IV)oxide
  4. glycolysis
Answer and explanation

The oxygen released during photosynthesis comes from water split in the light-dependent reactions. This photolysis supplies electrons and protons as well as oxygen.
