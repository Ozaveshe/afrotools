Question 1

Which of the following structures is a protective adaptive feature of the Agama Lizard to the environment?

  1. Nuchal crest
  2. Claws
  3. Scaly skin
  4. Gular fold
Answer and explanation

C: Scaly skin

Keratinised scales protect lizard skin and help limit water loss. This covering supports life on land, unlike the generally more permeable skin of amphibians.

Question 2

Which structure is directly specialised for processing or taking in food in an insect?

  1. suitable mouthparts
  2. paired antennae
  3. segmented body
  4. jointed appendages
Answer and explanation

A: suitable mouthparts

Insect mouthparts are modified for tasks such as biting, sucking or piercing food. Their form is closely related to how an insect obtains its diet.

Question 4

Which of the following is NOT part of the carbon cycle?

  1. Organic carbon
  2. Decomposition
  3. Nitrates formation
  4. Photosynthesis
Answer and explanation

C: Nitrates formation

Nitrate formation belongs primarily to the nitrogen cycle. Photosynthesis, decomposition and organic carbon storage are directly involved in movement of carbon through ecosystems.

Question 5

I. Tissues II. System III. Cell IV. Organs Which of the above is the level of organization of a leaf?

  1. IV
  2. I
  3. III
  4. II
Answer and explanation

A: IV

A leaf is an organ containing several tissues that work together. Epidermal, vascular and photosynthetic tissues contribute different functions within that one structure.

Question 6

In cellular respiration, energy is stored in the form of

  1. heat energy
  2. adenosine diphosphate
  3. adenosine monophosphate
  4. adenosine triphosphate
Answer and explanation

D: adenosine triphosphate

Cellular respiration transfers some energy from fuels into ATP, adenosine triphosphate. Cells then couple ATP reactions to processes requiring energy.
