Question 5

Which of the following is the lowest category of classification?

  1. Class
  2. Species
  3. Family
  4. Genus
Answer and explanation

B: Species

Species is the lowest rank among class, family, genus and species. A genus contains related species; families contain genera, and classes are still broader groupings.

Question 6

Plants that show secondary growth are usually found among the

  1. thallophytes
  2. pteridophytes
  3. monocotyledons
  4. dicotyledons
Answer and explanation

D: dicotyledons

Typical secondary growth in many dicots increases stem and root girth through lateral meristems. Vascular cambium adds secondary vascular tissues, and cork cambium contributes the outer protective covering.

Question 9

An animal body divisible into similar halves by multiple planes through its central axis has which specific type of symmetry?

  1. radially symmetrical
  2. bilaterally symmetrical
  3. asymmetrical
  4. symmetrical
Answer and explanation

A: radially symmetrical

Radial symmetry arranges body parts around a central axis so several planes through that axis divide the body into similar halves. Bilateral symmetry instead has one principal left-right plane.

Question 13

Which young plant organ normally lacks a waterproof epidermal cuticle in its water-absorbing region?

  1. leaf
  2. stem
  3. root
  4. shoot
Answer and explanation

C: root

Young absorbing roots generally lack the thick waterproof cuticle found on aerial organs. This permits water and dissolved minerals to enter; older roots may develop other protective barriers.

  1. urethra
  2. ureter
  3. bladder

Answer and explanation

Question 21

In the mammalian kidney, glomerular ultrafiltrate first enters the space in

  1. uriniferous tubule
  2. Bowman's capsule
  3. loop of Henle
  4. renal tubule
Answer and explanation

B: Bowman's capsule

Ultrafiltrate crosses the glomerular filtration barrier into the capsular space of Bowman’s capsule. This is the first stage of urine formation, before tubular reabsorption and secretion modify the filtrate.

Question 22

Which option names both lateral meristems responsible for typical secondary growth in woody plants?

  1. Collenchyma and xylem cells
  2. Vascular cambium
  3. Vascular cambium and cork cambium
  4. Cork cambium and sclerenchyma
Answer and explanation

C: Vascular cambium and cork cambium

Vascular cambium produces secondary xylem and phloem, while cork cambium contributes the protective periderm. Together these lateral meristems account for typical secondary growth of woody stems and roots.

Question 30

  2. survive only in water with low salinity
  3. withstand wide fluctuations in salinity
  4. feed only on phytoplankton and dead organic matter
Answer and explanation

Question 42

For an X-linked recessive colour-vision trait, an affected man (XcY) and carrier woman (XCXc) have a child. What is the probability that the child is affected?

  1. 25%
  2. 50%
  3. 75%
  4. 100%
Answer and explanation

B: 50%

For XcY × XCXc, the equally likely children are XCXc, XcXc, XCY and XcY. Two of these four combinations express the recessive colour-vision trait, giving a probability of 50%.

Question 43

The correct base pairing for DNA is

  1. adenine → thymine and guanine → cytosine
  2. adenine → guanine and thymine → cytosine
  3. adenine → cytosine and guanine → thymine
  4. adenine → adenine and cytosine → cytosine
Answer and explanation

A: adenine → thymine and guanine → cytosine

In ordinary double-stranded DNA, adenine pairs with thymine and guanine pairs with cytosine. These complementary pairs help preserve the sequence relationship between the two strands during replication.
