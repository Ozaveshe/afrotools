Question 1

In the usual pig intermediate host, the cysticercus of Taenia solium is found in the

  1. alimentary canal of cattle
  2. muscles of pig
  3. alimentary canal of pig
  4. muscles of cattle
Answer and explanation

B: muscles of pig

In the usual life cycle of Taenia solium, pigs act as intermediate hosts and cysticerci develop in their tissues, especially muscle. The adult tapeworm, by contrast, lives in the human intestine.

Question 3

Scales, shelled eggs and a bony skull are features found in both

  1. birds and reptiles
  2. fishes and birds
  3. reptiles and fishes
  4. birds and molluscs
Answer and explanation

A: birds and reptiles

Birds and reptiles both include animals with scales, shelled eggs and a bony skull. Bird scales can be seen on the legs and feet. These features are not the same as saying every species in each group lays eggs.

Question 4

Which arthropod group has no antennae?

  1. Crustacea
  2. Chilopoda
  3. Arachnida
  4. Diplopoda
Answer and explanation

C: Arachnida

Arachnids lack antennae. Their anterior appendages include chelicerae and pedipalps rather than the antennae found in insects, centipedes, millipedes and crustaceans.

Question 6

Which of the following is an organ?

  1. guard cell
  2. liver
  3. xylem bundle
  4. blood
Answer and explanation

B: liver

The liver is an organ composed of several tissue types working together. A guard cell is a single cell, blood is connective tissue and a xylem bundle is part of a conducting tissue system.

Question 9

A body arrangement in which one longitudinal plane divides the body into mirror-image left and right halves is called

  1. bilateral symmetry
  2. transverse section
  3. radial symmetry
  4. longitudinal section
Answer and explanation

A: bilateral symmetry

Bilateral symmetry means that one longitudinal plane divides the body into matching left and right halves. A section is a cut through a body, whereas symmetry describes how its parts are arranged.

Question 10

In which organism can ordinary vegetative cells combine nutrition, growth and reproductive functions?

  1. Rhizopus
  2. Dryopteris
  3. Brachymenium
  4. Spirogyra
Answer and explanation

D: Spirogyra

Spirogyra has a filament of relatively similar photosynthetic cells. Ordinary vegetative cells can grow and participate in reproduction, including conjugation, rather than being separated into the organs of a fern or moss.

Question 13

In the mature bark of woody stems, gaseous exchange takes place through

  1. micropyles
  2. stomata
  3. lenticels
  4. vessels
Answer and explanation

C: lenticels

Lenticels are regions in the bark through which gases can pass between internal tissues and the surrounding air. They provide exchange routes through the otherwise relatively impermeable outer tissues of a woody stem.

Question 14

Substances manufactured by the leaves are transported to other parts of the plant through the

  1. xylem
  2. companion cells
  3. sieve tubes
  4. cambium
Answer and explanation

C: sieve tubes

Sieve tubes are the main conducting pathways in angiosperm phloem for sugars and other organic solutes. Companion cells support their activity but are not the longitudinal sieve-tube conduits.

Question 15

During active absorption of glucose from a digested yam meal, which listed vessel is expected to carry the highest glucose concentration?

  1. hepatic artery
  2. pulmonary vein
  3. hepatic portal vein
  4. posterior vena cava
Answer and explanation

C: hepatic portal vein

During absorption of glucose from digested yam, blood from the small intestine enters the hepatic portal vein before reaching the liver. This route carries the newly absorbed glucose and can have a higher glucose concentration than the other listed vessels.

Question 17

Mammals can acclimatize to reduced oxygen partial pressure at high altitude by

  1. the stimulation of marrow to reduce the amount of erythrocytes produced
  2. increasing the rate at which erythrocytes are destroyed
  3. the stimulation of marrow to produce more erythrocytes
  4. increasing the breakdown of the protein portion of the erythrocytes
Answer and explanation

C: the stimulation of marrow to produce more erythrocytes

At high altitude, reduced oxygen partial pressure can stimulate erythropoietin signalling and increase red blood cell production in the bone marrow. More erythrocytes increase the blood’s oxygen-carrying capacity during acclimatization.

Question 19

Among these ordinary unfortified foods, which pair is richest in provitamin A carotenoids?

  1. red palm oil and groundnut oil
  2. red palm oil and carrots
  3. rice and groundnut oil
  4. oranges and carrots
Answer and explanation

B: red palm oil and carrots

Red palm oil and carrots supply substantial provitamin A carotenoids, which the body can convert to vitamin A. Ordinary unfortified groundnut oil and rice are much poorer sources; oranges are less concentrated sources than red palm oil.

Question 20

The force that holds water together in the form of a stream within the xylem tube is the

  1. cohesion of water molecules to one another by hydrogen bonds
  2. force of gravity attracting the water molecules through the hydrogen bonds
  3. attraction between the water and xylem by osmotic force
  4. normal flow of water from the ground with the help of gravitational force
Answer and explanation

A: cohesion of water molecules to one another by hydrogen bonds

Hydrogen bonds between water molecules produce cohesion. This helps maintain a continuous column of water in xylem as transpiration creates tension. Attraction to the vessel walls is adhesion, a different interaction.

Question 22

The lymphatic system of mammals rejoins the blood circulatory system at the

  1. hepatic vein
  2. subclavian vein
  3. renal vein
  4. common iliac vein
Answer and explanation

B: subclavian vein

Lymph returns to the venous circulation near the junctions of the subclavian and internal jugular veins. The main lymphatic ducts discharge there, returning fluid collected from the tissues to the bloodstream.

Question 24

Fertilization in humans usually takes place in the

  1. lower part of the uterus
  2. upper part of the uterus
  3. lower part of the oviduct
  4. upper part of the oviduct
Answer and explanation

D: upper part of the oviduct

Human fertilization usually occurs in the ampulla, the widened part of the uterine tube toward the ovary. The early embryo then travels toward the uterus, where implantation normally occurs later.

Question 25

Many foraging insects visit flowers in order to

  1. feed on the nectar
  2. deposit pollen on the stigma
  3. pollinate the flowers
  4. transfer pollen from anthers
Answer and explanation

A: feed on the nectar

Many insects visit flowers to obtain food such as nectar. Pollen can be transferred during feeding, but pollination is a consequence of the visit rather than the insect’s food-seeking purpose.

Question 26

In epigeal germination, the cotyledons are

  1. carried above the ground by the elongating hypocotyls
  2. pulled underground by the elongating hypocotyls
  3. pulled underground by the elongating epicotyl
  4. carried above the ground by the elongating epicotyl
Answer and explanation

A: carried above the ground by the elongating hypocotyls

In epigeal germination, elongation of the hypocotyl lifts the cotyledons above the soil. The hypocotyl is the embryonic axis below the cotyledons; the epicotyl lies above them.
