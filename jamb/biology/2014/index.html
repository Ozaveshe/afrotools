Question 4

The lowest level of organization in living organisms is

  1. organ
  2. cell
  3. system
  4. tissue
Answer and explanation

B: cell

The cell is the smallest organisational level listed that can function as a basic unit of life. Tissues comprise cells, organs comprise tissues and organ systems contain cooperating organs.

  2. Spirogyra
  3. Amoeba
  4. Euglena
Answer and explanation

B: Spirogyra

Spirogyra forms filaments containing many cells joined end to end. The other listed organisms are ordinarily single-celled forms, despite differences in their organelles and nutrition.

Question 9

In bryophytes, sex organs are produced in the

  1. protonema
  2. sporophyte
  3. gametophyte
  4. rhizoid
Answer and explanation

C: gametophyte

Bryophyte antheridia and archegonia are borne by the haploid gametophyte. The diploid sporophyte produces spores and typically remains attached to the gametophyte.

Question 18

The use of moist skin for respiration in amphibians is known as

  1. cellular respiration
  2. cutaneous respiration
  3. buccal respiration
  4. pulmonary respiration
Answer and explanation

B: cutaneous respiration

Cutaneous respiration is gas exchange across the skin. A moist surface allows respiratory gases to dissolve before diffusing across the thin exchange barrier.

Question 19

The specific botanical term for water-vapour loss from a plant is

  1. diffusion
  2. osmosis
  3. evaporation
  4. transpiration
Answer and explanation

D: transpiration

Transpiration is the loss of water vapour from a plant, especially through stomata. Evaporation and diffusion are physical processes involved in that overall biological loss.

Question 35

Under comparable conditions, which mineral-soil texture generally retains the most total water?

  1. Clayey soil
  2. Stoney soil
  3. Sandy soil
  4. Loamy Soil
Answer and explanation

A: Clayey soil

Clay-rich soils generally retain more total water than coarse sandy soils because of their fine particles and large surface area. Not all retained water is equally available to plant roots.

  2. Euglena
  3. Amoeba
