Question 1

The umbrella-shaped cap of a fully developed mushroom is the

  1. mycelium
  2. basidium
  3. pileus
  4. stipe
Answer and explanation

C: pileus

The pileus is the expanded cap of a typical mushroom. The stipe supports it, while the mycelium is the network of fungal hyphae and a basidium is a microscopic spore-producing cell.

Question 2

A frog is partly submerged in water. Arrow I points outwards from its exposed back into the air, and arrow II points from the surrounding water into its submerged skin. The processes at I and II respectively are

  1. evaporation and osmosis
  2. exhalation and osmosis
  3. osmosis and diffusion
  4. urination and diffusion
Answer and explanation

A: evaporation and osmosis

Water can evaporate from a frog’s moist skin exposed to air. Water can enter the submerged skin by osmosis when the surrounding water has a higher water potential than the body fluids.

Question 4

The similarity among organisms belonging to the same group will be least within each

  1. order
  2. family
  3. species
  4. kingdom
Answer and explanation

D: kingdom

A kingdom is the broadest taxonomic rank listed and contains organisms from many lower groups. Members of a species are generally much more similar than members of the same kingdom.

Question 16

  1. III
  2. IV
  3. II
  4. I
Answer and explanation

B: IV

The ratios correspond to 0.5, about 0.33, 0.25 and 0.20 units of surface area per unit volume. IV has the least exchange surface relative to its volume and therefore the greatest transport challenge on this criterion.

Question 22

Metabolic production of urea is carried out in the

  1. urinary bladder and kidney
  2. pancreas
  3. kidney and malphigian tubule
  4. liver
Answer and explanation

D: liver

The liver converts nitrogenous products of amino-acid breakdown into urea. Urea then circulates to the kidneys for excretion; the bladder mainly stores urine.

  1. ureter
  2. testes

  4. urethra
Answer and explanation

The male urethra provides a route for urine and also for semen at different times. The ureters carry urine to the bladder, while the vas deferens transports sperm toward the ejaculatory pathway.

Question 25

The response of plants to external stimuli in a non- directional manner is known as

  1. nastic movement
  2. phototropism
  3. taxis
  4. geotropism
Answer and explanation

A: nastic movement

Nastic movement responds to a stimulus without its direction being determined by the direction from which the stimulus comes. This differs from directional growth responses such as phototropism and gravitropism.

Question 28

Homologous pairs of chromosomes separate during

  1. meiosis
  2. cytolysis
  3. mitosis
  4. cleavage
Answer and explanation

A: meiosis

Homologous chromosomes separate during the first meiotic division. Sister chromatids separate later in meiosis II; in ordinary mitosis it is sister chromatids rather than homologous pairs that separate.

Question 36

  3. Sahel savanna → tropical rain forest → estuarine → Guinea savanna

Answer and explanation

B: Sahel savanna → Sudan savanna → Guinea savanna → tropical rain forest → estuarine

Across the broad north-to-south climatic gradient, Nigerian vegetation passes from drier savanna zones toward wetter forest and coastal environments. Sahel, Sudan and Guinea savannas precede tropical rain forest and estuarine areas in that order.
