Question 2

The process in which complex substances are broken down into simpler ones is referred to as

  1. anabolism
  2. catabolism
  3. metabolism
  4. tropism
Answer and explanation

B: catabolism

Catabolism breaks larger molecules into smaller ones and often releases usable energy. Anabolism builds more complex molecules; metabolism includes both kinds of reaction.

Question 4

The organelles present in cells that are actively respiring and photosynthesizing are

  1. lysosomes and ribosomes
  2. Golgi apparatus and endoplasmic reticulum
  3. nucleus and centrioles
  4. mitochondria and chloroplast
Answer and explanation

D: mitochondria and chloroplast

Mitochondria support aerobic cellular respiration, while chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis. A photosynthetic plant cell can use both organelles because photosynthesis does not replace its need for respiration.

Which listed animal is the usual intermediate host of Taenia solium?

  3. dog

Answer and explanation

D: pig

Pigs are the usual intermediate hosts for Taenia solium, the pork tapeworm. Larval cysts can occur in pig tissues; adult worms live in the human intestine after infected pork is eaten.

Question 11

  1. mesophytes
  2. hydrophytes
  3. epiphytes
  4. xerophytes
Answer and explanation

D: xerophytes

Xerophytes are plants adapted to dry environments. Their adaptations may include reduced leaves, water storage or physiological mechanisms that limit water loss; different species use different combinations.

Question 14

The structural adaptation of desert plants for water conservation is

  1. broad leaves with numerous stomata
  2. spongy mesophyll
  3. spiny leaves
  4. prominent stomata in leaves
Answer and explanation

Question 17

Long-distance transport of substances between different parts of a plant is broadly termed

  1. osmosis
  2. translocation
  3. transpiration
  4. diffusion
Answer and explanation

B: translocation

Translocation is the transport of substances from one part of a plant to another, especially sugars through phloem. Water and mineral ions also move through the vascular system, chiefly in xylem.

Question 19

  3. parasitic
  4. saprophytic
Answer and explanation

Question 20

  1. autotropism
  2. photosynthesis
  3. photolysis
  4. respiration

Question 21

Which listed feature is consistent with fungi obtaining organic food instead of photosynthesising?

  1. are filamentous
  2. lack chlorophyll
  3. have mycelium
  4. lack roots
Answer and explanation

B: lack chlorophyll

Fungi lack chlorophyll and obtain organic carbon by absorbing materials from other organisms or their remains. Their heterotrophy is defined by this dependence on organic food, not by the shape of their bodies.

Question 24

  2. heart, aorta, capillaries and veins
  3. heart, aorta, arteries and veins
  4. heart, vena cava, arteries and veins
Answer and explanation

A: heart, arteries, capillaries and veins

  3. sebaceous gland
  4. horny layer

  3. Sweat
  4. Mucilage

  1. cone

  3. rod
  4. iris
Answer and explanation
