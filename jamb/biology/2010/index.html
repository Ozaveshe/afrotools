Question 2

Which of the following characterizes a mature plant cell?

  1. the cytoplasm fills up the entire cell space
  2. the nucleus is pushed to the centre of the cell
  3. the cell wall is made up of cellulose
  4. the nucleus is small and irregular in shape
Answer and explanation

C: the cell wall is made up of cellulose

A typical plant cell has a cell wall containing cellulose. In many mature cells, a large central vacuole occupies much of the volume and displaces the cytoplasm and nucleus toward the edge.

Question 4

The dominant phase in the life cycle of a typical fern is the

  1. gametophyte
  2. prothallus
  3. sporophyte
  4. antheridium
Answer and explanation

C: sporophyte

The familiar leafy fern is the diploid sporophyte, the larger and longer-lived generation. The gametophyte is usually a small prothallus, which bears the sexual reproductive organs.

Question 11

Which statement is true of a transverse section of a typical dicot stem?

  1. the epidermis is completely encircled by the cortex
  2. the xylem is more interiorly located than the phloem
  3. the cambium lies between the cortex and the vascular bundles
  4. the vascular bundles are randomly scattered within the cortex
Answer and explanation

B: the xylem is more interiorly located than the phloem

In a typical dicot stem vascular bundle, xylem lies toward the centre and phloem toward the outside. Cambium is between them, and the bundles form a ring inside the outer tissues.

Question 16

When a food sample is warmed with Benedict’s solution, an orange to brick-red precipitate forms. This indicates the presence of

  1. reducing sugar
  2. fatty acid
  3. sucrose
  4. amino acid
Answer and explanation

A: reducing sugar

A reducing sugar can reduce copper ions in warmed Benedict’s reagent, forming an orange to brick-red copper(I) oxide precipitate. The colour depends on the amount present and the test conditions.

Question 17

Which listed structure pumps blood into the mammalian systemic circulation?

  1. veins
  2. right auricle
  3. arteries
  4. left ventricle
Answer and explanation

D: left ventricle

The left ventricle ejects blood into the aorta, supplying the systemic circuit. The right ventricle supplies the pulmonary circuit, so the two sides of the heart serve different circulatory pathways.

Question 18

The blood circuit supplying body tissues, as distinct from the pulmonary gas-exchange circuit, is

  1. the pulmonary artery
  2. systemic circulation
  3. the lymphatic system
  4. pulmonary circulation
Answer and explanation

B: systemic circulation

Systemic circulation supplies tissues through the aorta and its branches and returns blood to the right side of the heart. The pulmonary circuit carries blood between the heart and the lungs for gas exchange.

Question 19

Yeast carries out alcoholic fermentation to convert simple sugar to carbon dioxide and

  1. alcohol
  2. acid
  3. oxygen
  4. water
Answer and explanation

A: alcohol

During alcoholic fermentation, yeast produces ethanol and carbon dioxide from the products of glycolysis. Regeneration of NAD+ permits glycolysis to continue when oxygen is unavailable.

Question 30

  1. placenta
  2. liver
  3. umbilical cord
  4. uterine wall
Answer and explanation

A: placenta

Maternal and fetal blood are normally separated by placental tissues while gases, nutrients and wastes pass across the exchange barrier. The placenta is an exchange organ, not a completely impermeable wall.

Question 46

The chromosome number of a cell before and after the process of meiosis is conventionally represented as

  1. 2n → 2n
  2. n → n
  3. n → 2n
  4. 2n → n
Answer and explanation

D: 2n → n

Meiosis reduces the chromosome-set number from diploid, 2n, to haploid, n. DNA replicates before the first division, but replication does not itself double the number of chromosome sets.

Question 47

For a single autosomal trait with complete dominance, both parents are heterozygous (Aa). What is the probability that an offspring expresses the recessive trait?

  1. 3⁄4
  2. 1⁄2
  3. 1⁄4
  4. 1
Answer and explanation

C: 1⁄4

For Aa × Aa, each parent produces A and a gametes equally. The four equally likely combinations are AA, Aa, aA and aa, giving a recessive aa phenotype in one quarter of offspring.

Question 50

The independently evolved similarity of whale flippers and fish fins as swimming structures illustrates

  1. divergent evolution
  2. coevolution
  3. continuous variation
  4. convergent evolution
Answer and explanation

D: convergent evolution

Whales and fishes independently evolved body features suited to swimming. Their similarly functioning external swimming structures illustrate convergence, although deeper vertebrate ancestry links parts of their skeletal history.
