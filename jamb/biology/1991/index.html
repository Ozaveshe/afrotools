In the numbered animal-cell diagram, structure 5 is involved in

  1. protein synthesis
  2. energy production
  3. secretion
  4. excretion
Answer and explanation

C: secretion

Question 3

Angiosperms and gymnosperms belong to the plant group known as

  1. schizophyta
  2. bryophyta
  3. pteridophyta
  4. spermatophyta
Answer and explanation

D: spermatophyta

Angiosperms and gymnosperms are seed plants, traditionally grouped as Spermatophyta. Flowering plants enclose seeds within fruits, while gymnosperms have seeds not enclosed in an ovary-derived fruit.

Question 7

To facilitate gaseous exchange, breathing roots have

  1. stomata
  2. mitochondria
  3. cuticle
  4. lenticels
Answer and explanation

D: lenticels

Breathing roots project into air above oxygen-poor soil or mud. Lenticels allow gases to pass between the atmosphere and internal tissues, supporting respiration in the submerged root system.
