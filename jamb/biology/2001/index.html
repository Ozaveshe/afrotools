Question 1

The association between a leguminous plant and Rhizobium bacteria in its root nodules is known as

  1. commensalism
  2. mycorrhiza
  3. parasitism
  4. symbiosis
Answer and explanation

D: symbiosis

Rhizobium bacteria in legume root nodules obtain carbon compounds and a suitable habitat from the plant. In return, nitrogen fixation supplies nitrogen compounds useful to the plant. This is a mutualistic symbiosis.

Question 2

Which option combines the usual aquatic and terrestrial habitats of amphibians?

  1. on dry land and in water
  2. in water and on moist land
  3. on moist land
  4. in water
Answer and explanation

B: in water and on moist land

Amphibians are commonly associated with aquatic habitats and moist land. Their skin and, in many species, reproduction depend on moisture, making dry terrestrial conditions less suitable for typical amphibian activity.

Question 3

Viviparity occurs mainly in the

  1. mammals
  2. reptiles
  3. aves
  4. amphibians
Answer and explanation

A: mammals

Live birth is characteristic of most mammals, including marsupials and placental mammals. Egg-laying monotremes are exceptions. Some reptiles and amphibians also bear live young, so the feature is not exclusive to mammals.

Question 7

Water fleas, wood lice and barnacles belong to the group

  1. arachnida
  2. crustacea
  3. insecta
  4. chilopoda
Answer and explanation

B: crustacea

Water fleas, woodlice and barnacles are crustaceans despite their very different appearances and habitats. Woodlice are terrestrial members of the group, while barnacles are highly modified attached forms.

Question 8

The mode of feeding in Amoeba and Hydra is

  1. heterotrophic
  2. holophytic
  3. autotrophic
  4. symbiotic
Answer and explanation

A: heterotrophic

Amoeba and Hydra obtain organic food from other organisms. This makes their feeding heterotrophic, rather than producing organic food from inorganic carbon by photosynthesis.

Question 16

  1. calcium hydroxide
  2. copper sulphate
  3. calcium carbonate
  4. sodium hydroxide
Answer and explanation

Carbon dioxide reacts with calcium hydroxide solution, or limewater, to form a cloudy calcium carbonate precipitate. This visible change can identify the carbon dioxide released during aerobic respiration.

Question 18

The enzyme invertase will hydrolyze sucrose to give

  1. maltose and glucose
  2. glycerol and fatty acid
  3. glucose and fructose
  4. mannose and galactose
Answer and explanation

C: glucose and fructose

Sucrose contains glucose and fructose joined by a glycosidic bond. Invertase catalyses hydrolysis of that bond, releasing one glucose molecule and one fructose molecule.

Question 20

Which listed blood cells play the main cellular role in transporting oxygen and carbon dioxide in mammals?

  1. leucocytes
  2. thrombocytes
  3. phagocytes
  4. erythrocytes
Answer and explanation

D: erythrocytes

Erythrocytes contain haemoglobin, which transports most blood oxygen and part of the carbon dioxide. They also help convert carbon dioxide into bicarbonate, much of which is carried in plasma.

Question 28

  1. 35%
  2. 16%
  3. 46%
  4. 23%
Answer and explanation

Question 32

In a grazing food chain, the greatest amount of energy available for transfer occurs at the level of the

  1. secondary consumers
  2. decomposers
  3. producers
  4. primary consumers
Answer and explanation

Producers introduce energy into a grazing food chain by fixing it into organic matter. Only a fraction passes to each higher trophic level, so the energy available is greatest at the producer level.

Question 40

Considering ABO compatibility alone for red-cell transfusion, which statement is correct?

  1. Group O is the universal recipient
  2. Group A can donate to group A only
  3. Group AB is the universal recipient
  4. Group B can donate to group B only
Answer and explanation

C: Group AB is the universal recipient

In the ABO model for red cells, group AB lacks anti-A and anti-B antibodies and can receive cells from any ABO group. Rh type, other antibodies and compatibility testing also matter in real transfusions.
