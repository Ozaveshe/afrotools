Question 2

An adaptation common in many submerged freshwater hydrophytes is

  1. waxy cuticle on shoot surface
  2. poor development of roots and xylem tissues
  3. well-developed roots and supporting system
  4. leaves reduced to spines
Answer and explanation

B: poor development of roots and xylem tissues

Many submerged aquatic plants have reduced roots and xylem because water is readily available and surrounding water supports their bodies. Hydrophyte anatomy varies with whether plants are submerged, floating or emergent.

Question 4

The theory which supports the view that the large muscles developed by an athlete will be passed on to the offspring was proposed by

  1. Mendel
  2. Darwin
  3. Lamarck
  4. Pasteur
Answer and explanation

C: Lamarck

Lamarck’s historical proposal included inheritance of changes acquired during an individual’s life. In that account, increased use of muscles could enlarge them and the enlargement was proposed to pass to descendants.

Question 5

The chromosomes of prokaryotes historically grouped as Monera lie in a nucleoid region within the

  1. nucleoplasm
  2. nucleus
  3. nucleolus
  4. cytoplasm
Answer and explanation

D: cytoplasm

Organisms historically grouped as Monera are prokaryotes, with chromosomes in a nucleoid region of the cytoplasm. They lack a membrane-bound nucleus and therefore do not contain nuclear nucleoplasm.

Question 7

The pancreas secretes enzymes for the digestion of

  1. fats, proteins and carbohydrates
  2. fats, vitamins and cellulose
  3. fats, carbohydrates and vitamins
  4. proteins, cellulose and minerals
Answer and explanation

A: fats, proteins and carbohydrates

The pancreas releases enzymes that digest carbohydrates, proteins and fats in the small intestine. Vitamins and minerals generally do not need this kind of enzymatic breakdown.

Question 16

Which analysis can compare a biological sample from an investigation with a person’s genetic profile?

  1. RNA analysis
  2. blood group test
  3. behavioural traits test
  4. DNA analysis
Answer and explanation

D: DNA analysis

DNA profiling compares variable DNA markers in biological samples. It can provide evidence about whose biological material is present, but does not by itself establish what happened or prove guilt.

Question 17

  1. croaker

  3. shark
  4. cat fish
Answer and explanation

B: African lungfish

African lungfish can aestivate when their aquatic habitat dries, reducing activity and surviving within a protective cocoon. They use air breathing during this dormant period.

Question 25

  2. melanin and haemoglobin

  4. carotenoids and haemoglobin

Question 29

Which of the following is an example of parasitism?

  1. a squirrel living in an abandoned nest of a bird
  2. mistletoe growing on an orange tree
  3. fungi growing on a dead tree branch
  4. cattle egrets taking ticks from the body of cattle
Answer and explanation

B: mistletoe growing on an orange tree

Mistletoe obtains water and mineral resources from its living host and can reduce the host’s resources. This is parasitism, even though mistletoe can also photosynthesise.

Question 33

Which organelle carries out oxidative phosphorylation in a typical plant cell?

  1. nucleus
  2. nucleolus
  3. endoplasmic reticulum
  4. mitochondrion
Answer and explanation

D: mitochondrion

Mitochondria carry out the citric-acid cycle and oxidative phosphorylation in eukaryotic cells. Glycolysis occurs in the cytosol, so respiration is not confined entirely to mitochondria.
