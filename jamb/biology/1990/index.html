Question 1

Which property of viruses is also a characteristic of living organisms?

  1. possess transmittable characters
  2. move from one place to another
  3. respond to stimulation
  4. ingest food materials
Answer and explanation

A: possess transmittable characters

Viruses contain genetic material whose characteristics can pass to new virus particles when they replicate inside suitable host cells. Heredity is a life-associated property, although viruses do not carry out independent cellular metabolism.

Question 2

Which structure is common to freshwater Amoeba and Paramecium?

  1. Oral groove
  2. Trichocyst
  3. Contractile vacuole
  4. Cilia
Answer and explanation

C: Contractile vacuole

Freshwater Amoeba and Paramecium possess contractile vacuoles that collect and expel excess water. This helps maintain water balance as water enters from their relatively dilute surroundings.

Question 3

Hydra removes undigested food by

  1. passing it through the anus
  2. passing it through the mouth
  3. means of a contractile vacuole
  4. egesting it through the body surface
Answer and explanation

B: passing it through the mouth

Hydra has a gastrovascular cavity with a single opening. Food enters through this mouth, and undigested remains leave through the same opening because there is no separate anus.

Question 4

Hydra, which reproduces asexually by budding, belongs to which of these traditionally named groups?

  1. Arthropoda
  2. Annelida
  3. Mollusca
  4. Coelenterata
Answer and explanation

D: Coelenterata

Hydra and several other cnidarians form buds that develop into new individuals. Coelenterata is the older grouping name used here for these familiar budding animals.

Question 7

In bryophytes, sex organs are produced in the

  1. gametophyte
  2. rhizoid
  3. protonema
  4. sporophyte
Answer and explanation

A: gametophyte

The gametophyte is the haploid generation that bears sex organs in bryophytes. These organs produce gametes; the dependent sporophyte instead produces spores following meiosis.

Question 10

Which listed animal can have homodont dentition?

  1. Rat
  2. Man
  3. Lizard
  4. Pigeon
Answer and explanation

C: Lizard

Some lizards have numerous similarly shaped teeth, the homodont pattern. Humans and rats have differentiated tooth types, while a pigeon has a toothless beak rather than homodont dentition.

Question 11

In which of the following does external fertilization take place?

  1. Toad
  2. Lizard
  3. Bird
  4. Cockroach
Answer and explanation

A: Toad

In typical toads, eggs are released into water and fertilised outside the female’s body as the male releases sperm. The other listed animals normally use internal fertilisation.

Question 12

In the tapeworm, the suckers on the scolex are used for

  1. sucking the blood of the host
  2. holding fast to the host
  3. reproduction
  4. locomotion
Answer and explanation

B: holding fast to the host

The suckers on a tapeworm’s scolex anchor it to the host’s intestinal wall. The worm absorbs nutrients through its body surface; the suckers are attachment structures rather than mouths for sucking blood.

Question 14

The nephridia in the earthworm form part of the

  1. reproductive system
  2. respiratory system
  3. circulatory system
  4. excretory system
Answer and explanation

D: excretory system

Earthworm nephridia collect fluid, reclaim useful substances and eliminate metabolic wastes. They form the excretory system rather than the reproductive, respiratory or circulatory system.

Question 15

Which listed disease is mosquito-borne rather than transmitted by housefly contamination?

  1. Yellow fever
  2. Dysentery
  3. Typhoid fever
  4. Poliomyelitis
Answer and explanation

A: Yellow fever

Yellow fever is a viral infection transmitted by infected mosquitoes, not by houseflies. Houseflies can mechanically contaminate food or surfaces, a different transmission route from mosquito inoculation.
