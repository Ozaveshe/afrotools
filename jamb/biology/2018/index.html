Question 3

The cytoplasm of the cell is considered a very important component because it

  1. regulates the amount of energy in the cell
  2. suspends cytoplasmic organelles
  3. is the outermost part of the cell
  4. is solely responsible for cell division
Answer and explanation

B: suspends cytoplasmic organelles

Cytoplasmic organelles are suspended within the cytoplasm. The cell membrane forms the outer boundary, and cell division involves several coordinated cellular structures.

Question 7

Red blood cells burst after being placed in distilled water. This rupture is called

  1. plasmolysis
  2. diffusion
  3. haemolysis
  4. wilting
Answer and explanation

C: haemolysis

Red blood cells take up water in a strongly hypotonic solution such as distilled water. If they rupture, the breakdown of the red cells is called haemolysis.

Question 11

Which of the following structures is not a skeletal material?

  1. Chitin
  2. Cartilage
  3. Bone
  4. Muscle
Answer and explanation

D: Muscle

Bone and cartilage form supporting skeletal tissues in vertebrates, while chitin contributes to many arthropod exoskeletons. Muscle contracts to move supporting structures.

Question 12

The slow flow of blood through capillary beds is useful because it helps

  1. because the walls of capillaries are very thin
  2. to avoid high — blood pressure
  3. to ensure that the individual does not get dizzy
  4. to allow adequate time for exchange of materials
Answer and explanation

D: to allow adequate time for exchange of materials

Slow capillary flow gives more time for exchange of gases, nutrients and wastes across vessel walls. The large total cross-sectional area of capillary beds helps produce that low velocity.

Question 17

Compared with plain water, an appropriately formulated isotonic saline infusion helps

  1. because salt is a preservative
  2. to prevent contamination of the body
  3. to maintain the composition of body fluids
  4. to increase the number of blood cells
Answer and explanation

C: to maintain the composition of body fluids

An appropriately formulated isotonic saline solution helps preserve extracellular osmotic conditions. Plain water introduced into the circulation can cause harmful osmotic shifts in blood cells.
