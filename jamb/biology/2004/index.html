Question 2

Stomatal opening is promoted by

  1. presence of guard cells
  2. decrease in solute concentration in the guard cells
  3. increase in solute concentration in the guard cells
  4. presence of a pore
Answer and explanation

C: increase in solute concentration in the guard cells

An increase in guard-cell solute concentration lowers water potential. Water enters by osmosis, turgor rises and the guard cells bow apart, opening the pore.

Question 3

A hollowed yam tuber contains water and stands in a beaker of salt solution. The yam tissue is useful in this osmotic setup because it

  1. contains selectively permeable cell membranes
  2. acts as a storage organ
  3. is permeable to the salt solution
  4. is a plant material
Answer and explanation

A: contains selectively permeable cell membranes

Living yam tissue contains selectively permeable cell membranes. These permit osmotic water movement while restricting solute movement, which is the property used in this demonstration.

Question 6

A distinct broad central pith is typically found in

  1. dicot root and monocot stems
  2. dicot stems and monocot stems
  3. dicot stem and monocot roots
  4. dicot roots and monocot roots
Answer and explanation

C: dicot stem and monocot roots

A typical dicot stem has vascular bundles around a central pith, and a typical monocot root also has a broad central pith. Monocot stems generally have scattered bundles without the same distinct pith region.

  3. insulin

Question 10

Which is the most specific listed enzyme group that catalyses the breakdown of fats and oils into absorbable products?

  1. peptidases
  2. amylases
  3. lipases
  4. hydrolases
Answer and explanation

C: lipases

Lipases hydrolyse fats into smaller products, including fatty acids and monoglycerides during digestion. They are a specific type of hydrolase, the broader class of enzymes that break bonds using water.

Question 11

  2. Na⁺ and Fe²⁺
  3. Fe²⁺ and Mg²⁺
  4. Na⁺ and K⁺
Answer and explanation

Question 14

Alcoholic fermentation of one glucose molecule in yeast, including glycolysis, yields which listed products?

  1. pyruvic acid and alcohol
  2. 38 ATP molecules
  3. water and carbon (IV) oxide
  4. a net 2 ATP molecules and alcohol
Answer and explanation

D: a net 2 ATP molecules and alcohol

Alcoholic fermentation in yeast allows glycolysis to continue without oxygen. Glycolysis provides a net two ATP per glucose; fermentation produces ethanol and carbon dioxide while regenerating NAD+.

Question 18

During the emergence stage of epigeal germination, a seedling is characterised by

  1. lack of growth of the hypocotyl
  2. more rapid elongation of the hypocotyl than the epicotyl
  3. more rapid elongation of the epicotyl than the hypocotyl
  4. equal growth rates of the hypocotyl and epicotyl
Answer and explanation

B: more rapid elongation of the hypocotyl than the epicotyl

During epigeal emergence, elongation of the hypocotyl raises the cotyledons above the soil. In hypogeal emergence, the cotyledons remain below ground while growth above them becomes prominent.

Question 20

  1. capillaries
  2. arteries
  3. arterioles
  4. venules
Answer and explanation

Question 22

Identical twins inherit their genes from

  1. one egg and two sperms
  2. two eggs and a sperm
  3. the same egg and sperm
  4. different eggs and sperms
Answer and explanation

C: the same egg and sperm

Identical, or monozygotic, twins develop when a single fertilised egg gives rise to two embryos. They therefore originate from the same egg and sperm, although later mutations can create small genetic differences.

Question 23

Paternity disputes can most accurately be resolved through the use of

  1. DNA analysis
  2. physical fingerprint patterns
  3. tongue-rolling
  4. blood group typing
Answer and explanation

A: DNA analysis

DNA analysis compares inherited genetic markers and can assess whether a proposed parent is genetically compatible with a child. Ordinary fingerprints, tongue rolling and blood groups provide far less discriminatory information.

  2. cells

  4. tissues

  1. sandy
  2. loamy
  3. silty
  4. clayey
Answer and explanation

  1. mutualism
  2. saprophytism
  3. commensalism
  4. parasitism
