Question 1

Which organelle carries out major stages of aerobic cellular respiration?

  1. endoplasmic reticulum
  2. ribosome
  3. golgi body
  4. mitochondrion
Answer and explanation

D: mitochondrion

Mitochondria carry out major stages of aerobic cellular respiration and produce much of a cell’s ATP. Glycolysis occurs in the cytosol, so mitochondria are not the site of every reaction involved in respiration.

Question 3

Which of the following correctly summarizes the life cycle of a fern plant?

  1. Spore → prothallus → thallus → sporangium
  2. Male and female gametangia → zygospore → sporangium → spores
  3. Spore → thallus → spermatozoa + ovum → sporangium
  4. Prothallus → spermatozoid + egg cell → leafy plant → sporangium → spore
Answer and explanation

D: Prothallus → spermatozoid + egg cell → leafy plant → sporangium → spore

The prothallus is the fern gametophyte and produces sperm and eggs. Fertilisation gives a zygote that develops into the leafy sporophyte, whose sporangia produce spores. Each spore can grow into another prothallus.

Question 5

The essential structural difference between Hydra and tapeworm is that while Hydra,

  1. has tentacles, tapeworm is parasitic
  2. is diploblastic, tapeworm is triploblastic
  3. has a mouth, tapeworm feeds by suckers
  4. has mesoderm, tapeworm has mesogloea
Answer and explanation

B: is diploblastic, tapeworm is triploblastic

Hydra develops from two principal embryonic germ layers and is described as diploblastic. A tapeworm, a flatworm, has three germ layers including mesoderm and is triploblastic. This is a structural developmental difference.

Which of the following insects undergoes incomplete metamorphosis?

  1. mosquito
  2. Termite
  3. Housefly
  4. Moth
Answer and explanation

B: Termite

Termites develop through immature stages that resemble the adult body plan without a pupal stage. Mosquitoes, houseflies and moths pass through larva and pupa stages, which is complete metamorphosis.

Question 11

Which cells regulate the opening of stomata and thereby control gas exchange and water-vapour loss from a leaf?

  1. stomata
  2. lenticels
  3. air spaces
  4. guard cells
Answer and explanation

D: guard cells

Guard cells change shape as their turgor changes, opening or closing the stomatal pore. This regulates gas exchange and loss of water vapour from the leaf; the pore itself is the passage rather than the controlling cell pair.

Question 12

Fungi are heterotrophic because they

  1. have no leaves
  2. lack roots
  3. are filamentous
  4. lack chlorophyll
Answer and explanation

D: lack chlorophyll

Fungi do not possess chlorophyll for photosynthesis and obtain organic nutrients from other sources. Having no leaves or roots is not enough to establish heterotrophy, since some photosynthetic organisms also lack those organs.

Question 14

A food solution is mixed with 5 cm³ of dilute sodium hydroxide and 5 cm³ of 1% copper sulphate solution. A purple colour indicates the presence of

  1. glucose
  2. starch
  3. fat
  4. protein
Answer and explanation

D: protein

In the Biuret test, copper ions in alkaline solution form a violet complex with peptide bonds. A purple result therefore indicates protein or sufficiently long peptides, unlike the tests used for sugars, starch or fats.

Question 17

During alcoholic fermentation in yeast, pyruvate produced by glycolysis is converted to carbon dioxide and

  1. water
  2. glycerol
  3. ethanol
  4. citric acid
Answer and explanation

C: ethanol

During alcoholic fermentation in yeast, pyruvate is decarboxylated to acetaldehyde, releasing carbon dioxide. Acetaldehyde is then reduced to ethanol, regenerating NAD+ so glycolysis can continue.

  1. kidney
  2. Malpighian tubule
  3. flame cell
  4. nephridium
Answer and explanation

B: Malpighian tubule

Malpighian tubules remove nitrogenous wastes and other solutes from insect body fluid and deliver them to the gut. Subsequent water recovery helps conserve water, especially in terrestrial insects.

Question 31

Neurons that receive stimuli from the body or internal organs are called

  1. sensory neurons
  2. efferent neurons
  3. motor neurons
  4. relay neurons
Answer and explanation

A: sensory neurons

Sensory neurons carry information from receptors toward the central nervous system. They report conditions at the body surface and within organs. Motor or efferent neurons carry commands outward, while relay neurons connect other neurons.

Question 32

In the classical model of ecological succession, which statement describes a climax community?

  1. It persists until the environment or climate changes
  2. It changes drastically from one year to the next
  3. It is the first stage in succession
  4. It is made up of the tallest trees and the smallest animals
Answer and explanation

A: It persists until the environment or climate changes

In the classical succession model, a climax community is relatively stable while its environmental conditions remain similar. Disturbance or climatic change can alter it; stability does not mean every organism or population remains unchanged.

Question 40

Under the standard X-linked inheritance model, a man with haemophilia has children with a woman who neither has nor carries the condition. Which statement describes their children’s genotypes?

  1. All their sons will be haemophilic
  2. All their daughters will be haemophilic
  3. All their daughters will be carriers
  4. All their sons will be carriers
Answer and explanation

C: All their daughters will be carriers

For X-linked haemophilia, the father passes his affected X chromosome to every daughter and his Y chromosome to every son. A non-carrier mother supplies an unaffected X, so all daughters are heterozygous carriers and sons do not inherit the father’s affected X. Carrier daughters can still have bleeding symptoms.

Question 42

If R and r are alleles at one locus, which four-cell Punnett-square result represents the expected RR × Rr cross?

  1. RR, 2Rr,rr
  2. 2RR,2rr
  3. 2RR, 2Rr
  4. 4Rr
Answer and explanation

C: 2RR, 2Rr

The RR parent contributes only R gametes, while the Rr parent contributes R or r. The expected offspring genotypes are RR and Rr in equal proportions. A four-cell Punnett square therefore gives two RR and two Rr entries.

Question 44

Which explanation can account for papaya seedlings differing from a parent tree with desirable agronomic traits?

  1. seeds are not reliable for propagating plants
  2. uncontrolled out-crossing can introduce unwanted variability
  3. vegetative propagation is the best form of reproduction for all crops
  4. seeds were not physiologically mature at harvest
Answer and explanation

B: uncontrolled out-crossing can introduce unwanted variability

Seeds formed after cross-pollination can inherit alleles from an uncontrolled pollen parent. Recombination and segregation can therefore produce offspring that differ from a desirable parent instead of faithfully cloning its traits.

Question 50

Fossil records found in sedimentary rocks offer some explanation for the theory of evolution because

  1. the deposits have remains of organisms characteristic of when they were formed
  2. different strata have remains of organisms of the same kind
  3. only organisms with strong parts are fossilized
  4. most animals and plant fossils bear little resemblance to present-day living specimens
Answer and explanation

A: the deposits have remains of organisms characteristic of when they were formed
