Question 2

Which listed substance is a mixture?

  1. Sulfur powder
  2. Bronze
  3. Distilled water
  4. Ethanol
Answer and explanation

B: Bronze

Bronze is an alloy containing copper and tin, often with smaller amounts of other elements. Its components are mixed rather than combined in one fixed molecular ratio, so it is a mixture. Pure sulfur, distilled water and ethanol are single substances.

Question 3

How many moles of O₂ are formed by complete decomposition of 2.5 mol of KClO₃?

  1. 2.50 mol
  2. 3.50 mol
  3. 3.75 mol
  4. 7.50 mol
Answer and explanation

C: 3.75 mol

The balanced equation is 2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂. Two moles of chlorate produce three moles of oxygen, so 2.5 mol produces 2.5×3/2 = 3.75 mol of O₂.

Question 5

A gas occupies 1.50 dm³ at 25 °C and 1 atm. What volume does the same amount occupy at 100 °C and 1 atm?

  1. 1.88 dm³
  2. 6.00 dm³
  3. 18.80 dm³
  4. 60.00 dm³
Answer and explanation

A: 1.88 dm³

At constant pressure, volume is proportional to absolute temperature. Convert 25 °C and 100 °C to about 298 K and 373 K. Then V₂ = 1.50×373/298 ≈ 1.88 dm³. Celsius temperatures cannot be used directly in the ratio.
