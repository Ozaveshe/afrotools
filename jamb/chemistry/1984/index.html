Question 1

Sodium chloride may be obtained from brine by

  1. titration
  2. decantation
  3. distillation
  4. evaporation
  5. sublimation
Answer and explanation

D: evaporation

Evaporation removes water from brine. The dissolved sodium chloride remains and crystallises as enough water is lost.
