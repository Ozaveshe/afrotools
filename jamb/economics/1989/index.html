Question 1

In its simplified form, the circular flow of income concept refers to the

  1. Real flows in goods and services between the product and factor markets
  2. Monetary payments for goods and services produced by individual firms
  3. Transactions in goods and services and payments for such transactions between individual firms and households
  4. Money flows associated with transactions between individual firms and households
Answer and explanation

C: Transactions in goods and services and payments for such transactions between individual firms and households

The simple circular flow connects households and firms through real exchanges and money payments. Households supply factors and buy output, while firms pay factor incomes and receive spending on goods and services.

Question 6

If X represents the factors of production and Y represents the factor price, which of the following sets of association is correct?

  1. (Land, rent), (capital, wage), (labour, profit),
  2. (Land, interest), (capital, profits), (labour, wage),
  3. (Land, wage), (capital, interest), (labour, rent),
  4. (Land, rent), (capital, interest), (labour, wage).
Answer and explanation

D: (Land, rent), (capital, interest), (labour, wage).

The conventional factor rewards are rent for land, interest for capital and wages for labour. Profit is associated with entrepreneurship, rather than with the labour input.

Question 8

Given perfect competition in the capital market, the opportunity cost of capital is adequately reflected by the

  1. Interest rate
  2. Returns on capital
  3. Alternative capital forgone
  4. Shadow price of foreign exchange
Answer and explanation

A: Interest rate

In a competitive capital market, the interest rate measures the return forgone by committing funds to one use instead of lending them. It therefore represents the opportunity cost of using capital.

Question 10

If, as the price of a commodity rises, the quantity demanded of the commodity remains the same, then the demand for the commodity is

  1. Static
  2. Infinitely elastic
  3. Externally determined
  4. Perfectly inelastic
Answer and explanation

D: Perfectly inelastic

Perfectly inelastic demand means quantity demanded does not change when price changes. Its price elasticity is zero, and the demand curve is vertical.

  1. External economies of scale
  2. Vertical integration
  3. Horizontal integration
  4. Economic union

Question 20

One of the purposes of advertisement in marketing is to

  1. Change the quality of the product
  2. Raise the quantity of the product demanded as its price falls
  3. Shift the demand curve for the product to the right
  4. Raise production costs
Answer and explanation

C: Shift the demand curve for the product to the right

Advertising can increase consumers’ willingness to buy a product at each price, shifting demand to the right. A price fall causes movement along an existing demand curve, rather than this shift.

Question 23

The co-operatives as a form of business organization differs from partnership by having

  1. Several sources of capital for business financing
  2. Ability to issue preferred stocks to members
  3. Entrenched democratic control in the conduct of business
  4. Established rules and regulations governing the activities of its members.
Answer and explanation

C: Entrenched democratic control in the conduct of business

Democratic member control is a defining cooperative principle, commonly one member, one vote. Partnership control is instead governed by the partners’ agreed arrangements and need not follow that cooperative rule.

Question 39

  1. Terms of payments
  2. Balance on current account
  3. Balance of trade
  4. Terms of trade
Answer and explanation

B: Balance on current account

The goods balance is55.54−53.32=2.22 million naira. The services balance is30.56−28.70=1.86 million. Their sum is4.08 million; with no other current-account transactions in this schedule, it is the current-account balance.

Question 41

  2. In which its opportunity cost is lower than that of the trade partner
  3. For which foreign demand is greatest
  4. For which there is abundant supply of raw materials
Answer and explanation

B: In which its opportunity cost is lower than that of the trade partner

Comparative advantage is determined by relative opportunity cost. A country gains a specialisation advantage in a good when it sacrifices less alternative output than its trading partner.
