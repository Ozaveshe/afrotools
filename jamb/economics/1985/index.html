Question 1

One main quality of a good is that it

  1. Is sold in the market
  2. Satisfies wants
  3. Is made in the factory
  4. Is always tangible
  5. Is non-perishable
Answer and explanation

B: Satisfies wants

A good has utility: it can satisfy a human want. Goods need not be factory-made, sold for money or non-perishable.

Question 3

Bisi needs a book costing N10.00. If Bisi buys the book instead of a hat, the opportunity cost of this choice is the

  1. Cost of the book
  2. Cost of the hat
  3. Book
  4. Hat
  5. N10.00
Answer and explanation

D: Hat

Opportunity cost is the next-best alternative forgone. By choosing the book instead of the hat, Bisi gives up the hat; the opportunity cost is that alternative, rather than the money price itself.

  2. There are no buyers for our goods
  3. There are too many sellers
  4. Many people are out of work
  5. All raw materials are imported
Answer and explanation

An economic problem arises when resources are scarce relative to wants. Scarcity makes choice necessary, because using resources for one purpose means giving up another use.

Question 8

One theory of population contends that population tends to increase much faster than the supply of food, and that if the growth in population is not checked, it will be checked by natural forces like misery, disaster and food shortage. This theory was propounded by

  1. Prof. Paul Samuelson
  2. Adam Smith
  3. David Ricardo
  4. Rev. Thomas Malthus
  5. Prof. Ojetunji Aboyade
Answer and explanation

D: Rev. Thomas Malthus

Thomas Malthus proposed that unchecked population could grow geometrically while food supply grew arithmetically. He argued that preventive and positive checks would restrain population. This identifies his historical theory, rather than a universal forecast.

Question 9

For quantities consumed of 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 units, the total-utility entries are –, 10, 15, 17, 18 and 18. The corresponding marginal-utility entries are –, 10, 5, 2, 1 and 0. Which law does this schedule illustrate?

  1. Diminishing marginal utility
  2. Diminishing marginal productivity
  3. Diminishing returns
  4. Increasing returns
  5. Increase total utility
Answer and explanation

A: Diminishing marginal utility

Marginal utility is the extra satisfaction from one more unit. The listed marginal utilities fall from 10 to 5, 2, 1 and then 0. Total utility still rises until the final unit adds no further satisfaction.

Question 13

At every point on an indifference curve, the

  1. Total utility is decreasing
  2. Prices of all goods are constant
  3. Consumer is satiated
  4. Level of utility is constant
  5. Level of utility is increasing
Answer and explanation

D: Level of utility is constant

An indifference curve joins combinations of goods that give a consumer the same level of satisfaction. The quantities of the goods can change along the curve while utility remains constant.

Question 35

By terms of trade, we mean the

  1. Ratio of exports to imports
  2. Difference between exports and imports
  3. Difference between current account and capital account
  4. Index of export prices to import prices expressed as a percentage
  5. Ratio of short-term capital movements
Answer and explanation

D: Index of export prices to import prices expressed as a percentage

The terms-of-trade index equals the export-price index divided by the import-price index, multiplied by 100. It compares the prices a country receives for exports with the prices it pays for imports.

Question 43

Under normal circumstances a producer will bear the entire burden of taxation on his output if the

  1. Demand for his product is completely elastic
  2. Supply of his goods is more elastic than the demand
  3. Demand for his product is more elastic than the supply
  4. Production of his commodities is subject to diminishing returns
  5. Production fo his commodities is subject to increasing returns to scale
Answer and explanation

A: Demand for his product is completely elastic

With perfectly elastic demand, buyers will not pay a higher market price. A per-unit tax therefore lowers the price received by producers by the tax amount, placing the tax burden on them in the standard competitive model.

Question 49

The unemployment associated with a recession is called

  1. Voluntary
  2. Frictional
  3. Cyclical
  4. Structural
  5. Disguised
Answer and explanation

C: Cyclical

Cyclical unemployment rises when an economic downturn reduces aggregate demand and employers need fewer workers. It differs from frictional unemployment during job searches and structural unemployment caused by mismatched skills.

Question 50

At a smooth interior maximum of total revenue, marginal revenue is

  1. Constant
  2. Negative
  3. Zero
  4. Positive
  5. Maximum
Answer and explanation

C: Zero

Marginal revenue measures the change in total revenue from an additional unit. At a smooth interior maximum of total revenue, its slope is zero, so marginal revenue is zero.
