Question 2

The reward for capital is

  1. interest
  2. rent
  3. risk
  4. premium.
Answer and explanation

A: interest

In the standard classification of factor rewards, capital earns interest. Land earns rent and labour earns wages; risk is not itself a payment category.

Question 7

International trade is an application of the principle of

  1. industrial production
  2. mass production
  3. regional production
  4. comparative cost advantage
Answer and explanation

D: comparative cost advantage

Comparative advantage explains gains from specializing in goods with lower opportunity costs and trading. Countries need not be absolutely more productive in every good to benefit.

Question 13

  1. volume of trade between countries
  2. direction of international trade
  3. commodity terms of trade
  4. volume of imports
Answer and explanation

C: commodity terms of trade

Commodity terms of trade compare export prices with import prices. Dividing the export price index by the import price index and multiplying by 100 measures that relative price relationship.

Question 14

  1. Ghana should produce coffee and cocoa
  2. Ghana would benefit by producing cocoa only
  3. Sierra Leone should not produce any of the products
  4. Sierra Leone should produce cocoa and Ghana coffee
Answer and explanation

D: Sierra Leone should produce cocoa and Ghana coffee

Ghana gives up 7/8 tonne of cocoa per tonne of coffee, compared with 9/2 in Sierra Leone. Sierra Leone gives up less coffee per tonne of cocoa, so it specializes in cocoa and Ghana in coffee.

Question 17

Question 22

Value X | Frequency F 2 | 2 3 | 5 4 | 2 5 | 1 What is the mean?

  1. 2.2
  2. 2.5
  3. 3.2
  4. 4.5
Answer and explanation

C: 3.2

The weighted sum is 2x2 + 3x5 + 4x2 + 5x1 = 32. There are 10 observations, so the mean is 32/10 = 3.2.

  2. Money
