  3. At a price equal to the cost price in the home market
  4. In order to encourage indigenous producers

A: At a price below that received in the home market

Dumping involves an export price below the product’s normal value, commonly assessed using its comparable home-market price. It is not simply exporting at any low price.

  2. Cause overpopulation
  3. Increase its growth rate
  4. Reduce the proportion of children
  5. Reduce its size
Answer and explanation

E: Reduce its size

Emigration means people leave the country. Holding births, deaths and immigration unchanged, each emigrant reduces the resident population. It does not by itself establish a change in the proportion of children.

When demand is elastic, it means that consumer

  1. React more proportionately to price change
  2. Are not sensitive to price change
  3. Will stop buying when price increases
  4. React less proportionately to price change
  5. React equally to price change
Answer and explanation

A: React more proportionately to price change

Elastic demand means the percentage change in quantity demanded exceeds the percentage change in price in magnitude. Consumers therefore respond more than proportionately to the price change.
