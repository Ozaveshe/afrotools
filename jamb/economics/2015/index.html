Question 5

Structural unemployment is mainly caused by

  1. a change in the consumption pattern
  2. a change in the business cycle
  3. decrease in the output level
  4. Seasonal variation.
Answer and explanation

A: a change in the consumption pattern

A lasting change in the goods consumers want can reduce demand for some occupations and increase it for others. This mismatch of skills and vacancies causes structural unemployment.

Question 8

The production table is: Labour | TP | AP | MP 1 | 6 | 6 | -- 2 | 14 | 7 | 8 3 | K | 9 | 13 4 | 40 | 10 | F Find K and F respectively.

  1. 13 and 27
  2. 27 and 13
  3. 10 and 27
  4. 27 and 10.
Answer and explanation

B: 27 and 13

At three workers, total product is 3 × 9 = 27, which also equals 14 + 13. The fourth worker adds 40 - 27 = 13, so K = 27 and F = 13.

Question 9

In the theory of consumer behaviour, the sum of all marginal utilities is

  1. average marginal utility
  2. the initial marginal utility
  3. total utility
  4. diminishing marginal utility.
Answer and explanation

C: total utility

Marginal utility is the additional utility from each successive unit. Adding these increments from zero consumption gives total utility.

Question 13

The demand for money is referred to as the

  1. amount of money in fixed deposit
  2. desire to hold money in tangible assets
  3. need for money to invest
  4. desire to hold money in liquid form rather than investing it
Answer and explanation

D: desire to hold money in liquid form rather than investing it

Demand for money is the desired holding of liquid monetary balances rather than spending or committing the funds to other assets. It is a stock of desired balances.

Question 14

In the theory of production, a producer is at equilibrium at a point where

  1. isocost is above the isoquant curve
  2. isoquant is below the isocost curve
  3. isocost is tangent to the marginal revenue
  4. isoquant is tangent to the isocost curve.
Answer and explanation

D: isoquant is tangent to the isocost curve.

For an interior least-cost input combination, the isoquant is tangent to the isocost line. The technical substitution rate equals the ratio of input prices.

Question 15

Balance of trade shows the relationship between the

  1. internal and external trade
  2. total value of visible imports and exports
  3. deficit and surplus balance of payments
  4. total value of invisible imports and exports
Answer and explanation

B: total value of visible imports and exports

The balance of trade compares the monetary value of visible goods exports with visible goods imports. Services and other international flows are recorded elsewhere in the balance of payments.

Question 18

Payment of interests on loans and the repayment of capital sum at a future date is

  1. balance debt
  2. debt servicing
  3. debt relief
  4. debt retaking.
Answer and explanation

B: debt servicing

Debt servicing consists of paying the interest and principal obligations associated with a loan according to its terms. It differs from debt relief, which reduces or cancels obligations.

Question 23

Higher income taxes can be used to control

  1. demand-pull inflation
  2. high interest rates
  3. cost- push inflation
  4. hyperinflation.
Answer and explanation

A: demand-pull inflation

Higher income taxes reduce disposable income and household spending, other things equal. This contracts aggregate demand and can restrain demand-pull inflation.

Question 28

  2. provision of long-term loans for infrastructure
  3. development of agriculture in member countries
  4. maintenance of stable exchange rates
Answer and explanation

Question 30

The law of supply states that the

  1. higher the price the higher the quantity supplied
  2. quantity supplied is always equal to the quantity demanded
  3. lower the price the higher the quantity supplied
  4. higher the price the lower the quantity supplied.
Answer and explanation

A: higher the price the higher the quantity supplied

The law of supply states that, other things equal, a higher price induces a larger quantity supplied. It describes movement along a normal upward-sloping supply curve.

Question 34

In a free market economy, prices are determined by

  1. consumers
  2. producers
  3. the central government
  4. the forces of demand and supply.
Answer and explanation

In a free market, interactions between buyers' demand and sellers' supply establish prices. Neither side alone normally dictates the competitive market price.

Question 38

Government can protect consumers from exploitative prices by introducing

  1. minimum price
  2. price differentiation
  3. price floor
  4. maximum price.
Answer and explanation

D: maximum price.

A maximum price is a legal price ceiling. A binding ceiling can prevent sellers charging above that limit, although it may create shortages and does not guarantee sufficient supply.

Question 45

Determine the median of 6, 10, 5, 12, 20, 18, 20 and 4.

  1. 11
  2. 9.67
  3. 12.56
  4. 12.
Answer and explanation

A: 11

Ordering the eight observations gives 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 18, 20, 20. The median is the average of the fourth and fifth values: (10 + 12)/2 = 11.

Question 50

The supply of farm produce is mainly determined by

  1. consumers' income
  2. weather conditions
  3. population
  4. consumers' taste
Answer and explanation

B: weather conditions

Weather affects crop growth, yields and harvest availability. Of these choices it is the direct physical influence on farm supply; income, taste and population mainly affect demand.
