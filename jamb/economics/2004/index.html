Question 1

By advertising, a monopolistic competitive firm tries to shift its

  1. supply curve to the right
  2. demand curve to the right
  3. supply curve to the left
  4. demand curve to the left
Answer and explanation

B: demand curve to the right

Advertising aims to persuade more consumers to buy the firm's differentiated product at each price. Successful advertising therefore shifts the firm's demand curve to the right.

Question 2

Given that Y = C + I and C = bY, where b = 0.8, what is the multiplier?

  1. 5
  2. 10
  3. 4
  4. 2
Answer and explanation

A: 5

Substituting C = 0.8Y into Y = C + I gives 0.2Y = I, so Y = 5I. A one-unit increase in autonomous investment therefore produces a five-unit increase in equilibrium income.

Question 10

GDP differs from GNP because GDP

  1. is inclusive of depreciation
  2. emphasizes the final goods and services
  3. measures production within the country during a particular period
  4. emphasizes the market value of all final goods and services produced by nationals
Answer and explanation

C: measures production within the country during a particular period

GDP measures production within a country's territory during a period. GNP adds net primary income from abroad to GDP, so the domestic location of production is the relevant distinction.

Question 11

The distinction between perfect competition and monopolistic competition is that the latter is characterized by

  1. a single market price
  2. many buyers and sellers
  3. product differentiation
  4. homogeneity of product
Answer and explanation

C: product differentiation

Monopolistic competition involves differentiated products, giving each seller some influence over its own price. Perfect competition assumes homogeneous products, despite both models having many buyers and sellers.

Question 21

The difference between birth and death rates of a given population is referred to as the

  1. mortality rate
  2. fertility rate
  3. natural growth rate
  4. linear growth rate
Answer and explanation

C: natural growth rate

Natural population growth is births minus deaths, excluding migration. When birth and death rates use the same population base, their difference gives the rate of natural increase.

Question 25

  1. government expenditure
  2. savings
  3. interest rates
  4. exchange rates
Answer and explanation

B: savings

Government borrowing can compete with private borrowers for available funds. More saving expands the supply of loanable funds, reducing the upward pressure on interest rates and the resulting crowding out.

Question 27

The excess of aggregate expenditure over full emploment level of output is referred to as

  1. GNP gap
  2. Deflationary gap
  3. Inflationary gap
  4. recessionary gap
Answer and explanation

C: Inflationary gap

An inflationary gap exists when planned aggregate expenditure exceeds the value of output at full employment. With limited spare capacity, excess spending puts upward pressure on prices.

Question 36

In order to increase revenue, the seller of a commodity whose demand is fairly elastic is advised to

  1. increase price
  2. reduce output
  3. reduce price
  4. retain price
Answer and explanation

C: reduce price

With elastic demand, a percentage reduction in price causes a larger percentage increase in quantity demanded. Over the elastic portion of the demand curve, lowering price therefore increases total revenue.

Question 38

  1. 2 units of rice

  3. 17 units of rice

Answer and explanation

A: 2 units of rice

Moving from K to L increases beans from 15 to 20 units but reduces rice from 19 to 17 units. The sacrifice, and therefore the opportunity cost of the move, is 2 units of rice.

Question 39

One of the assumptions of ordinal utility in consumer behaviour is that

  1. consumers are irrational
  2. marginal utility rises continuously
  3. utility is measurable
  4. utility can only be ranked
Answer and explanation

D: utility can only be ranked

Ordinal utility describes preferences by ranking alternatives, without assigning measurable amounts of satisfaction. A consumer may prefer one bundle to another without saying how many units of utility it provides.

Question 42

Given the cost function C = 160 + 36Q, what is the average cost at 20 units of output?

  1. N720.00
  2. N216.00
  3. N44.00
  4. N880.00
Answer and explanation

C: N44.00

At Q = 20, total cost is 160 + 36 × 20 = N880. Average cost is total cost divided by output: 880/20 = N44 per unit.

  1. an average cost
  2. a variable cost
  3. a total cost
