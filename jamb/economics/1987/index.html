Question 5

The additional revenue obtained by using one more unit of a factor is called its

  1. Marginal product
  2. Additional product
  3. Marginal revenue product
  4. Average product
Answer and explanation

C: Marginal revenue product

Marginal revenue product is the extra revenue generated by employing one additional unit of an input. It combines the input’s extra physical output with the revenue earned from that output.

Question 6

Labour productivity is defined as

  1. Output per worker-hour
  2. Average output
  3. The maximum number of hours worked
  4. Total level of output
Answer and explanation

Labour productivity measures output per unit of labour input. Output per worker-hour relates production to the hours worked, enabling comparison across different amounts of labour.

Question 10

In the history of Economic Thought, the concept of Division of Labour is usually associated with

  1. David Ricardo
  2. J. M. Keynes
  3. Adam Smith
  4. Karl Marx
Answer and explanation

C: Adam Smith

Adam Smith’s discussion of a pin factory famously explained how dividing work into specialised tasks can raise productivity. Division of labour is therefore closely associated with his economic writing.

Question 11

The form of capital which is usually consumed or transformed into finished goods and services in the production process is called

  1. Industrial capital
  2. Social capital
  3. Fixed capital
  4. Circulating capital
Answer and explanation

D: Circulating capital

Circulating capital, such as raw materials, is used up or transformed during production. Fixed capital, such as machinery, normally provides services over multiple production cycles.

Question 13

In the long run a firm will leave an industry if price

  1. Does not cover at least Average Total Cost
  2. Is not equal to Marginal Cost
  3. Is higher than Average Variable Cost
  4. Is not at least equal to the minimum of the Marginal Cost curve
Answer and explanation

A: Does not cover at least Average Total Cost

In the long run a firm must cover all economic costs to remain viable. If the market price cannot cover average total cost at its best available operating scale, the firm has an incentive to leave the industry.

Question 19

For normal goods, the income elasticity of demand is

  1. Positive
  2. Negative
  3. Zero
  4. Infinite
Answer and explanation

A: Positive

For a normal good, demand increases as income rises, other influences unchanged. The percentage quantity change and percentage income change have the same sign, so income elasticity is positive.

Question 22

  1. 65 units
  2. 40 units
  3. 30 units
  4. 20 units
Answer and explanation

C: 30 units

Market demand is the sum of individual quantities demanded at the same price. AtN5, consumersX,Y andZ demand10,5 and15 units respectively, giving10+5+15=30 units.

Question 35

In the keynesian model,

  1. The demand for money is inversely related to the rate of interest
  2. Investment is directly related tot he rate of interest
  3. Investment is not related to the rate of interest
  4. The demand for money is directly related to the rate in interest
Answer and explanation

A: The demand for money is inversely related to the rate of interest

In the Keynesian liquidity-preference model, higher interest rates raise the opportunity cost of holding money instead of interest-bearing assets. Money demand therefore falls as the interest rate rises, with income and other influences unchanged.

  1. Elastic
  2. Inelastic
  3. Perfectly elastic
  4. Unitary elastic
