Question 2

An economy in which both the public and private sectors contribute to economic growth is known as a

  1. feudal economy
  2. capitalist economy
  3. socialist economy
  4. mixed economy
Answer and explanation

D: mixed economy

A mixed economy combines private enterprise with public-sector participation. Both sectors therefore contribute to production and economic activity.

Question 3

If the standard deviation of a given data is 53, what is the variance?

  1. 2,082
  2. 2,809
  3. 2,808
  4. 2,209
Answer and explanation

B: 2,809

Variance is the square of the standard deviation. Squaring 53 gives 53 × 53 = 2,809, expressed in squared units of the original variable.

Question 6

Find the median of 35, 10, 14, 38, 15, 18, 22, 30 and 28.

  1. 10
  2. 38
  3. 35
  4. 22
Answer and explanation

D: 22

Putting the nine observations in order gives 10, 14, 15, 18, 22, 28, 30, 35, 38. The fifth observation is the middle value, so the median is 22.

Question 7

An increase in demand without a corresponding change in supply will lead to

  1. a decrease in equilibrium price and increase in equilibrium quantity
  2. an increase in equilibrium price and quantity
  3. a decrease in equilibrium price and quantity
  4. an increase in equilibrium price and a decrease in equilibrium quantity
Answer and explanation

B: an increase in equilibrium price and quantity

With the usual upward-sloping supply curve unchanged, an outward demand shift moves the market intersection to a higher price and larger traded quantity.

Question 8

An increase in the price of a commodity will result in

  1. a decrease in the quantity demanded
  2. an increase in demand
  3. an increase in quantity demanded
  4. a decrease in demand
Answer and explanation

A: a decrease in the quantity demanded

For a normal downward-sloping demand curve, a higher own price reduces the quantity consumers are willing to buy. This is a change in quantity demanded, not a shift of demand.

Question 9

If the price of a bicycle falls from N120 to N80 and quantity bought rises from 300 to 500 units, calculate the magnitude of price elasticity using the initial values as percentage bases.

  1. 66.7
  2. 0.5
  3. 1.5
  4. 2.0
Answer and explanation

D: 2.0

Using initial values, quantity rises by 200/300 while price falls by 40/120. Dividing the percentage changes gives elasticity magnitude (200/300)/(40/120) = 2.

Question 10

A demand graph has price on the vertical axis and quantity on the horizontal axis. The demand curve D is a vertical line rising from the fixed quantity Q. What is its price elasticity of demand?

  1. unitarily elastic
  2. perfectly elastic
  3. perfectly inelastic
  4. fairly inelastic
Answer and explanation

C: perfectly inelastic

A vertical demand curve keeps quantity fixed at Q while price changes. The quantity response is zero, so price elasticity of demand is zero, or perfectly inelastic.

Question 16

If the supply of a product is elastic, a small reduction in price will

  1. reduce the cost of production
  2. reduce the quantity supplied
  3. increase the quantity supplied
  4. lead to no change in the quantity supplied
Answer and explanation

B: reduce the quantity supplied

Along an upward-sloping elastic supply curve, reducing price reduces quantity supplied. Elasticity means the percentage quantity response exceeds the percentage price change.

Question 18

If the price of a commodity is fixed below equilibrium, this will lead to

  1. excess demand
  2. a decrease in price
  3. an increase in price
  4. excess supply
Answer and explanation

A: excess demand

A binding price ceiling below equilibrium raises quantity demanded and lowers quantity supplied relative to equilibrium. The resulting shortage is excess demand.

Question 19

One of the criticisms of the price mechanism is that

  1. producers are sovereign
  2. it provides low degree of freedom
  3. it widens the inequitable gap
  4. consumers are sovereign
Answer and explanation

C: it widens the inequitable gap

Market demand reflects purchasing power as well as need. A price mechanism can therefore allocate more goods to those with higher incomes and preserve or widen unequal outcomes.

Question 20

In Nigeria, government can reduce the cost of accommodation by fixing rent

  1. at the prevailing rate
  2. at the equilibrium price
  3. above the equilibrium price
  4. below the equilibrium price
Answer and explanation

D: below the equilibrium price

A rent ceiling below the equilibrium rent lowers the permitted rent for covered accommodation. Such a binding ceiling may also create shortages, so lower legal rent does not guarantee availability.

Question 27

The speculative demand for money is inversely related to the

  1. interest rate
  2. level of income
  3. exchange rate
  4. inflation rate
Answer and explanation

A: interest rate

In the standard liquidity-preference model, a higher interest rate increases the return forgone by holding money instead of bonds. Speculative demand for money therefore falls as the interest rate rises.

Question 28

If Mr K obtains a N50,000 loan to provide for ordinary household needs, the demand for money is said to be

  1. transactionary
  2. speculative
  3. precautionary and speculative
  4. transactional and speculative

Money held to meet ordinary household purchases serves the transactions motive. Speculative balances are instead held in response to expected changes in financial-asset prices.

Question 30

If CBN reduces money supply, the interest rate will

  1. fluctuate
  2. rise
  3. fall
  4. remain unchanged
Answer and explanation

B: rise

In the standard money-market model with money demand unchanged, a reduction in money supply makes liquidity scarcer. The interest rate rises to bring money demand into line with the lower supply.

  1. decrease in the corporate profit tax rates

Question 48

The rate of interest charged on loans depends largely on

  1. the prevailing exchange rate
  2. marginal efficiency of capital
  3. the risk associated with the loan
  4. the prevailing tax rate
Answer and explanation

C: the risk associated with the loan

Lenders normally charge a risk premium when repayment is less certain. Greater default risk therefore tends to raise the rate charged, other loan terms and market conditions equal.

Question 50

If Mr. X lost his clerical job at a store and searched for a similar job for ten months before finding one this implies that Mr. X was

  1. structurally unemployed
  2. frictionally unemployed
  3. seasonally unemployed
  4. cyclically unemployed
Answer and explanation

B: frictionally unemployed

Frictional unemployment occurs while a worker searches and is matched with another job. Seeking a similar clerical position describes this job-search process without evidence of a skills mismatch.
