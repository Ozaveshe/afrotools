Question 1

If the arithmetic mean of 2, 3, 5, 8, Z, 10 and 12 is 7, what is the value of Z?

  1. 8
  2. 9
  3. 10
  4. 7
Answer and explanation

B: 9

Seven observations with mean 7 must sum to 49. The six known values sum to 40, leaving Z = 49 - 40 = 9.

Question 4

Scale of preference is referred to as the

  1. choices consumers make
  2. array of consumer's needs
  3. consumer preference for luxurious goods
  4. consumer wants in order of priority
Answer and explanation

D: consumer wants in order of priority

A scale of preference orders wants from most to least important. It helps a consumer choose which wants to satisfy first when resources are limited.

Question 5

One of the basic assumptions of monopoly is

  1. perfect information of the market condition
  2. the ability to either control price or output
  3. perfect mobility of factors of production
  4. large number of buyers and sellers
Answer and explanation

B: the ability to either control price or output

A monopolist faces the market demand curve and can choose a price or an output level. Demand determines the corresponding quantity or price, so both cannot be chosen independently.

Question 6

A major advantage of a public limited liability company over a private limited liability company is

  1. limited liability enjoyed by owner
  2. separate legal entity
  3. easy transferability of shares
  4. perpetual existence
Answer and explanation

C: easy transferability of shares

Public-company shares are generally more readily transferable than shares in a private company, which commonly restricts transfers. Limited liability and separate identity are shared features.

Question 15

The type of inflation that emanates from excess demand over supply is

  1. galloping inflation
  2. cost-push inflation
  3. demand-pull inflation
  4. imported inflation
Answer and explanation

C: demand-pull inflation

Demand-pull inflation arises when aggregate spending exceeds what the economy can supply at existing prices. Excess demand pushes the general price level upward.

Question 16

Which of the following problems is associated with national income measurement?

  1. High interest rate
  2. Inflation and deflation
  3. Overpopulation
  4. population growth
Answer and explanation

B: Inflation and deflation

Inflation and deflation change money values even if real production does not change. National-income comparisons must therefore adjust for price changes to measure real output consistently.

Question 28

  1. a decrease in quantity supplied

Question 36

Quantity consumed | Total utility | Marginal utility 0 | 0 | -- 1 | 10 | 10 2 | 15 | 5 3 | 17 | 2 4 | 18 | 1 5 | 18 | 0 What can be inferred?

  1. diminishing returns to scale
  2. excess demand
  3. diminishing marginal utility
  4. diminishing marginal productivity
Answer and explanation

C: diminishing marginal utility

The extra satisfaction from successive units falls from 10 to 5, 2, 1 and then 0. This pattern is diminishing marginal utility, even though total utility initially rises.

Question 38

The cost-saving advantages which a large firm can achieve on its own is called

  1. internal economies of scale
  2. diseconomies of large scale
  3. external diseconomies of scale
  4. external economies of scale
Answer and explanation

A: internal economies of scale

Internal economies of scale are cost advantages arising from expansion within the firm itself. External economies instead arise from developments outside an individual firm.
