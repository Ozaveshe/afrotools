Question 1

It is impossible to satisfy all human wants because

  1. Areas of fertile land are very limited
  2. Oceans and seas limit land space for farming
  3. Resources are not equitably distributed
  4. Available resources are limited
Answer and explanation

D: Available resources are limited

Available resources are limited relative to wants, so not every want can be satisfied. Scarcity persists even with fair distribution of existing resources.

Question 10

In the theory of production and cost, the average total cost of a firm is minimized when the marginal cost curve cuts the average total cost at its.

  1. Lowest point
  2. Middle point
  3. Maximum point
  4. Downward-sloping section
Answer and explanation

A: Lowest point

In the usual smooth cost model, marginal cost intersects average total cost at its minimum. Marginal cost below the average pulls it down; above it, it raises it.

Question 13

For a profit-maximising firm taking the market wage as given, the wage at an interior optimal employment level equals the

  1. Marginal revenue of the enterprise
  2. Marginal revenue product of labour in the enterprise
  3. Average revenue of the enterprise
  4. Marginal cost of production
Answer and explanation

B: Marginal revenue product of labour in the enterprise

A wage-taking firm hires to the point where another worker’s marginal revenue product equals the wage. This balances extra revenue with extra labour cost.

Question 18

  1. Price is always equal to marginal cost
  2. Supply is always equal to demand
  3. Price is always higher than marginal cost
  4. There are always many buyers and many sellers

C: Price is always higher than marginal cost

For uniform-price monopoly, marginal revenue is below price. At an interior profit maximumMC=MR, so price exceeds marginal cost.

Question 49

Tariffs on imports are meant to

  1. Prevent a country from becoming a dumping ground for foreign goods
  2. Discourage exports
  3. Encourage exports
  4. Increase the level of external dependency
Answer and explanation

A: Prevent a country from becoming a dumping ground for foreign goods

An import tariff raises the domestic price of affected foreign goods and can protect domestic producers against import competition. Protection is one purpose of tariffs; tariffs may also raise revenue. They do not ordinarily encourage dependence on foreign goods.
