Question 1

Which of the following defines the concept of government?

  1. the process of administering justice in a country
  2. the process of supervising the activities of the legislature
  3. the orderly management and control of the affairs of a country
  4. the orderly transfer of power to duly elected politicians
Answer and explanation

C: the orderly management and control of the affairs of a country

Government organizes and manages public affairs through institutions and decisions. Administering justice and supervising legislation are narrower activities within that broader role.

Question 2

The most distinguishing characteristic of the state is

  1. government
  2. population
  3. territory
  4. sovereignty
Answer and explanation

D: sovereignty

Sovereignty distinguishes a state as an authority that is supreme internally and independent externally. Other organizations can have populations, territories or governing bodies without being states.

Question 3

The judiciary assigns clear meaning to the laws by

  1. interpretation
  2. enforcement
  3. adjudication
  4. revision
Answer and explanation

A: interpretation

Interpretation explains the meaning and application of legal provisions. Enforcement puts rules into effect, while adjudication is the broader process of resolving a dispute.

Question 4

The structure of government implies the

  1. law making process of government
  2. law enforcement process of government
  3. organization of powers and functions of government
  4. method of revenue allocation by government
Answer and explanation

C: organization of powers and functions of government

Government structure describes how public institutions, powers and functions are organized. Making laws, enforcing them and allocating revenue are activities within that structure.

Question 5

If the central government has less powers than the component units, the constitution is said to be

  1. federal
  2. unitary
  3. confederal
  4. unwritten
Answer and explanation

C: confederal

A confederal arrangement leaves the member states with primary sovereignty and gives the common centre limited delegated functions. This explains a centre with less authority than its constituent units.

Question 9

The resignation of the cabinet after a defeat in parliament, is an expression of the principle of

  1. political accountability
  2. collective responsibility
  3. checks and balances
  4. rule of law
Answer and explanation

B: collective responsibility

The cabinet acts as a collective team responsible to parliament. When loss of parliamentary confidence leads the whole cabinet to resign, it demonstrates collective responsibility.

Question 10

Ceremonial and executive powers are fused in the

  1. parliamentary system of government
  2. presidential system of government
  3. federal system of government
  4. unitary system of government
Answer and explanation

B: presidential system of government

In a presidential system the president is both head of state and head of government. The office therefore combines ceremonial representation with executive leadership.

Question 12

the principle by which both the governors and the governed are bound by the laws is called

  1. constitutionalism
  2. constitutional supremacy
  3. rule by decree
  4. rule of law
Answer and explanation

The rule of law binds rulers and citizens alike. Public office does not grant a general exemption from legal duties and limits.

Question 13

The empowerment of the police service commission to make rules for the appointment, promotion and dismissal of its personnel, is an example of

  1. a decree
  2. a penal law
  3. delegated legislation
  4. an executive order
Answer and explanation

C: delegated legislation

Rules made by a body under powers granted to it are delegated legislation. The enabling authority sets the limits within which the body can regulate its affairs.

Question 14

In democracy, Franchise is given to all

  1. Adult citizens
  2. citizens
  3. loyal party members
  4. resident adults
Answer and explanation

A: Adult citizens

Democratic adult suffrage gives voting rights to adult citizens subject to lawful electoral qualifications. It is not restricted to supporters of the ruling party or automatically extended to every resident.

Question 29

Which of the following statements is true about the 1963 and 1979 constitutions?

  1. both had provisions for the office of the president
  2. both had provisions for the office of a constitutional president
  3. both provided for the offices of prime minister and president
  4. both had provisions for the office of an executive president
Answer and explanation

A: both had provisions for the office of the president

Both constitutions provided for a president, but the roles differed. The 1963 president was largely ceremonial, whereas the 1979 president headed the executive government.

Question 31

During the period 1960, Nigeria was governed under the

  1. presidential system of government
  2. Westminster system of government
  3. Confederal system of government
  4. unitary system of government
Answer and explanation

B: Westminster system of government

Nigeria used the Westminster parliamentary model at independence in 1960, with a prime minister leading government. The executive presidential model was introduced in 1979.

  1. educational imbalance
  2. national identity
  3. ethnic pluralism
  4. manpower and resources
Answer and explanation

Question 34

In a federal system, the power allocated to both the central and the state governments is said to be

  1. exclusive
  2. concurrent
  3. residual
  4. inherent
Answer and explanation

B: concurrent

Concurrent powers are shared by central and state governments within the constitutional allocation. Exclusive powers are reserved to one level, while residual powers are those left unlisted.

Question 36

Public corporations mainly differ from ministries in that they

  1. are not bureaucratic
  2. provide social services to the public
  3. require highly professional staff
  4. are organized as business enterprises
Answer and explanation

Public corporations have a separate enterprise structure for carrying out particular services or commercial activities. Ministries are departments of government; both may employ professionals and provide services.

Question 37

The privatization of public corporation is aimed at

  1. making their goods and services available
  2. making them reliable
  3. making them more efficient
  4. allowing the public to control them
Answer and explanation

C: making them more efficient

A policy aim of privatization is to improve enterprise efficiency through changes in ownership, incentives and management. This is an intended benefit, not a guarantee that every privatization succeeds.

Question 38

One major democratic innovation in local government administration introduced by the Babangida regime was the

  1. appointment of portfolio councillors
  2. appointment of retired military officers as sole administrators
  3. selection of heads of personnel management departments from the councillors
  4. separation of powers between the executive and legislative arms of the councils
Answer and explanation

D: separation of powers between the executive and legislative arms of the councils

The local presidential model separated executive responsibilities from the council's legislative functions. This institutional distinction differs from appointing unelected administrators or assigning individual portfolios.

Question 40

Which of the following factors was not responsible for the military intervention in Nigerian politics in 1966?

  1. Willingness of politicians to relinquish power
  2. the Western Regional election of 1965
  3. The federal election of 1964
  4. Ethnic politics and lawlessness
Answer and explanation

A: Willingness of politicians to relinquish power

The 1966 military intervention was not a planned voluntary transfer by politicians. Electoral crises and political disorder formed part of the background, whereas willingness to surrender civilian authority does not describe that process.
