Question 6

The fear of domination in the public service led to the

  1. adoption of population as a formula for revenue allocation
  2. establishment of the Civil Service Commission
  3. introduction of rotational presidency
  4. establishment of the Federal Character Commission
Answer and explanation

D: establishment of the Federal Character Commission

The Federal Character Commission promotes equitable representation of states and ethnic groups in federal institutions. Its purpose includes preventing domination of public positions by a few groups.

Question 9

The exclusive list is reserved for legislation by the

  1. regional government
  2. state government
  3. local government
  4. federal government
Answer and explanation

D: federal government

An exclusive legislative list assigns subjects to the federal legislature alone. This differs from a concurrent list, on which both federal and state legislatures may have law-making authority.

Question 23

The permanent membership of the UN Security Council is

  1. ten
  2. fifteen
  3. five
  4. eleven
Answer and explanation

C: five

The UN Security Council has five permanent members: China, France, Russia, the United Kingdom and the United States. Ten additional members are elected for fixed terms.

Question 24

An advantage of a flexible constitution is that it allows for

  1. political awareness
  2. constitutional stability
  3. reduction of ambiguities
  4. ease of amendment
Answer and explanation

D: ease of amendment

A flexible constitution can be amended through a comparatively simple legislative procedure. This makes adaptation easier, although it does not automatically ensure constitutional stability.

Question 26

Prorogation is the bringing of a session of a parliament to an end through

  1. royal proclamation
  2. final reading
  3. guillotine
  4. impeachment
Answer and explanation

A: royal proclamation

In the British parliamentary system, prorogation formally ends a parliamentary session under the Crown's authority. It differs from a final reading of a bill or the removal of an office-holder.

Question 27

The right of all eligible citizens to vote and be voted for is referred to as

  1. gender equality
  2. political mandate
  3. restricted franchise
  4. universal suffrage
Answer and explanation

D: universal suffrage

Universal suffrage extends electoral participation to all citizens who meet general eligibility requirements rather than restricting it to a privileged class. Eligibility rules still apply to voters and candidates.

Question 28

Values beliefs and orientation of a political system are internalized through

  1. political culture
  2. political socialization
  3. political development
  4. political power
Answer and explanation

B: political socialization

Political socialization is the process through which people learn political beliefs, values and expectations. Political culture describes the shared orientations that this learning helps transmit.

Question 30

In a cabinet system, the legislature comprises of the

  1. prime minister and members of the parliament
  2. prime minister and members of the jury
  3. prime minister and ministers
  4. president and ministers
Answer and explanation

A: prime minister and members of the parliament

In a parliamentary cabinet system, the prime minister is normally a member of parliament alongside other legislators. Juries belong to the judicial process and do not form the legislature.

Question 32

Democracy is a system of government that is

  1. constituted by the will of the majority of the citizens
  2. imposed on the majority of the citizens
  3. designed to favour the elite
  4. put in place to favour the party caucus
Answer and explanation

A: constituted by the will of the majority of the citizens

Democratic government derives authority from the people, commonly expressed through majority choice. Constitutional democracy also protects rights and limits what a majority may do.

Question 33

Collective ownership of property is associated with

  1. feudalism
  2. communism
  3. mercantilism
  4. capitalism
Answer and explanation

B: communism

Communism is associated with common ownership of productive property. Capitalism emphasizes private ownership, while mercantilism concerns policies for national commercial advantage.

  1. Federal Civil Service Commission

Question 36

The principle that advocates strict adherence to the fundamental laws of a country is

  1. constitutionalism
  2. individual responsibility
  3. absolutism
  4. collective responsibility
Answer and explanation

A: constitutionalism

Constitutionalism requires government to operate within constitutional rules and limits. Public authority must respect the fundamental law rather than relying on unrestricted personal power.
