Question 1

Which of the following is not a fundamental human right?

  1. Freedom of speech
  2. Freedom of religion
  3. Freedom of the press
  4. Freedom of association
  5. None of the above
Answer and explanation

E: None of the above

Speech, religion, expression through the press, and association are recognized human freedoms. Since each listed freedom is protected, none of the first four is an exception.

Question 3

Pressure groups are really

  1. Nuisances which most governments would like to ignore
  2. people with sinister motives who pretend to serve the public interest
  3. Greedy citizens who pursue purely selfish interests
  4. organized bodies which try to influence legislation or government decisions in their favour
  5. organized bodies that are vulnerable to giant companies who use them to promote their interests
Answer and explanation

D: organized bodies which try to influence legislation or government decisions in their favour

Pressure groups are organized bodies seeking to influence public policy or legislation in support of particular interests. They do not necessarily seek to form a government themselves.

Question 4

The major role of national legislature is to

  1. scrutinize and criticize the executive branch
  2. step into political vacuum created by weak executives
  3. Expose and supervise the government
  4. make laws for the general welfare of citizens
  5. find ways and means to get money for governments
Answer and explanation

D: make laws for the general welfare of citizens

The legislature's central role is making laws for the community. Oversight and financial scrutiny are also legislative activities, but they support rather than replace its lawmaking role.

Question 7

In general constitutions are designed to provide

  1. legal remedies for all political problems
  2. Clear alternative to military take-overs of elected governments
  3. general arrangement rules and national objectives within which political activities are conducted
  4. codes of ethic for politicians and punishment for those who break the laws of the land
  5. rigid ideological frameworks for nations that operate the constitutions
Answer and explanation

C: general arrangement rules and national objectives within which political activities are conducted

A constitution establishes the basic institutions, powers and rules through which political activity takes place. It provides the framework for government rather than a ready-made solution to every political dispute.

Question 8

The main function of the legislature is

  1. to implement law
  2. to enforce law
  3. to make law
  4. to interpret law
  5. to nullify law
Answer and explanation

C: to make law

The legislature primarily makes laws. Implementing and enforcing laws belong mainly to the executive, while interpreting and applying them in disputes belong to the judiciary.

Question 9

The franchise is

  1. the receipt given to taxpayer
  2. an order of the court
  3. the paper which a voter casts at elections
  4. the right to vote
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

D: the right to vote

The franchise is the right to vote. A ballot paper is the instrument used to cast a vote, while a tax receipt or court order is not itself the electoral right.

Question 14

Another name for delegated legislation is

  1. Administrative legislation
  2. Executive legislation
  3. Subordinate legislation
  4. all of the above
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

D: all of the above

Delegated legislation is also described as subordinate, administrative or executive legislation. Each label refers to rules made under authority granted by the primary legislature.
