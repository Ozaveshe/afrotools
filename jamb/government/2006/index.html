Question 2

The chairman of the UN Security Council emerges through

  1. consensus
  2. rotation
  3. election
  4. selection
Answer and explanation

B: rotation

The Security Council presidency rotates among its members in English alphabetical order. It is not filled through a separate election for each monthly term.

Question 4

Judges of the World Court are appointed to serve for

  1. five years
  2. ten years
  3. four years
  4. nine years
Answer and explanation

D: nine years

Judges of the International Court of Justice serve nine-year terms and may be re-elected. They are elected through the UN General Assembly and Security Council.

Question 5

The highest decision-making organ of OPEC is the

  1. Conference
  2. Special Fund
  3. Board of Governors
  4. Secretariat
Answer and explanation

A: Conference

The OPEC Statute identifies the Conference as the organization's supreme authority. It sets general policy and takes the principal decisions, while other organs perform assigned supporting functions.

Question 6

Diplomatic missions among the Commonwealth member states are referred to as

  1. legations
  2. high commissions
  3. embassies
  4. consulates
Answer and explanation

B: high commissions

Resident diplomatic missions between Commonwealth countries are conventionally called high commissions. They perform diplomatic functions comparable to embassies in other bilateral relationships.

Question 7

The New Partnership for Africa's Development was mainly promoted by

  1. Kenya, South Africa and Senegal
  2. Nigeria, Senegal and South Africa
  3. Ghana, Sierra Leone and Nigeria
  4. Tanzania, Burkina Faso and Egypt
Answer and explanation

B: Nigeria, Senegal and South Africa

Nigeria, Senegal and South Africa were among the founding countries promoting NEPAD. The founding group also included Algeria and Egypt; the option lists three members of that group.

Question 8

Nigeria became a front-line state in the South Africa liberation struggle because of her

  1. commitment to end colonialism
  2. influence on African proxy wars
  3. connection with South Africa
  4. anti-apartheid stance
Answer and explanation

D: anti-apartheid stance

Nigeria supported the struggle against apartheid through diplomatic and material action. Its anti-apartheid position explains its prominent role despite not sharing a border with South Africa.

Question 9

Nigeria's first participation in a United Nations peace-keeping operation was in

  1. Angola
  2. Lebanon
  3. Chad
  4. Congo
Answer and explanation

D: Congo

Nigeria first contributed to United Nations peacekeeping in the Congo in 1960. This was the year of Nigerian independence and preceded its contributions to the other operations listed.

Question 11

The first Nigerian leader to become chairman of the Organization of African Unity was

  1. Johnson Aguiyi Ironsi
  2. Murtala Muhammed
  3. Yakubu Gowon
  4. Tafewa Balewa
Answer and explanation

C: Yakubu Gowon

Yakubu Gowon chaired the Organization of African Unity in 1973–1974. He was the first Nigerian leader to occupy that chairmanship.
