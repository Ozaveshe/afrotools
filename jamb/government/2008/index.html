Question 2

The Organization of Petroleum Exporting Countries came into existence at a conference held in

  1. Tripoli
  2. Caracas
  3. Riyadh
  4. Baghdad
Answer and explanation

D: Baghdad

OPEC was founded at the Baghdad Conference in September 1960. Representatives of Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia and Venezuela participated in its creation.

Question 4

In which of the following countries has the UN carried out peace-keeping operations?

  1. Cyprus, Singapore and South Africa
  2. Lebanon, the Congo and Mexico
  3. Cyprus, Somalia and Yugoslavia
  4. Nigeria, Botswana and the Congo
Answer and explanation

C: Cyprus, Somalia and Yugoslavia

The UN has conducted peacekeeping operations in Cyprus, Somalia and the former Yugoslavia. All three places in that option appear in the UN's history of peacekeeping missions.

Question 5

Which of the following was the main organ of the defunct OAU?

  1. The Liberation Committee
  2. The Council of Ministers
  3. The Commission for Meditation, Conciliation and Arbitration
  4. The Assembly of Heads of State and Government
Answer and explanation

D: The Assembly of Heads of State and Government

The OAU Charter designated the Assembly of Heads of State and Government as the organization's supreme organ. It determined policy and reviewed the work of other organs.

Question 8

An example of a state with an unwritten constitution is

  1. Canada
  2. India
  3. Britain
  4. France
Answer and explanation

C: Britain

Britain is commonly described as having an unwritten constitution, meaning an uncodified one. Its rules include statutes, legal decisions and conventions rather than a single consolidated constitutional document.

Question 12

Equality before the law is a component of

  1. separation of powers
  2. checks and balances
  3. the rule of law
  4. constitutional law
Answer and explanation

C: the rule of law

Equality before the law is a central principle of the rule of law. It means legal accountability does not depend on holding political office or belonging to a privileged group.
