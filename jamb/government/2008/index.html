Question 2

The Organization of Petroleum Exporting Countries came into existence at a conference held in

  1. Tripoli
  2. Caracas
  3. Riyadh
  4. Baghdad
Answer and explanation

D: Baghdad

OPEC was founded at the Baghdad Conference in September 1960. Representatives of Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia and Venezuela participated in its creation.

Question 5

Which of the following was the main organ of the defunct OAU?

  1. The Liberation Committee
  2. The Council of Ministers
  3. The Commission for Meditation, Conciliation and Arbitration
  4. The Assembly of Heads of State and Government
Answer and explanation

D: The Assembly of Heads of State and Government

The OAU Charter designated the Assembly of Heads of State and Government as the organization's supreme organ. It determined policy and reviewed the work of other organs.

Question 8

An example of a state with an unwritten constitution is

  1. Canada
  2. India
  3. Britain
  4. France
Answer and explanation

C: Britain

Britain is commonly described as having an unwritten constitution, meaning an uncodified one. Its rules include statutes, legal decisions and conventions rather than a single consolidated constitutional document.

Question 12

Equality before the law is a component of

  1. separation of powers
  2. checks and balances
  3. the rule of law
  4. constitutional law
Answer and explanation

C: the rule of law

Equality before the law is a central principle of the rule of law. It means legal accountability does not depend on holding political office or belonging to a privileged group.

Question 16

Government exercises political power on behalf of the

  1. elite
  2. citizens
  3. party caucus
  4. vocal few
Answer and explanation

B: citizens

In the representative conception of government, public power is exercised on behalf of citizens. It is not supposed to serve only a party caucus or a vocal minority.

Question 18

The citizenship of a country can be acquired by an alien through

  1. habitation
  2. assimilation
  3. association
  4. naturalization
Answer and explanation

D: naturalization

Naturalization is a legal process through which a foreign national may acquire citizenship after meeting the country’s requirements. Residence alone does not automatically confer citizenship.

Question 21

In the presidential system of government, the leader is basically the head of state and

  1. government
  2. the police
  3. the military

Question 22

An economic system that encourages even distribution of wealth in the society is

  1. liberalism
  2. socialism
  3. mercantilism
  4. capitalism
Answer and explanation

B: socialism

Socialism emphasizes social ownership or control and reducing economic inequality. More even distribution is an aim of socialist policy, not an automatic guarantee of every government using the label.

Question 23

An attribute that makes a state to be recognized as independent is

  1. government
  2. population
  3. sovereignty
  4. territory
Answer and explanation

C: sovereignty

Sovereignty identifies a state’s supreme authority and independence from external control. Population and territory are necessary attributes but do not by themselves establish independence.

Question 24

The act of denying an eligible person the right to vote in an election is

  1. gerrymandering
  2. disenfranchisement
  3. filibustering
  4. restricted franchise
Answer and explanation

B: disenfranchisement

Disenfranchisement means depriving someone of the right to vote. Gerrymandering instead manipulates electoral boundaries and does not itself define removal of voting eligibility.

Question 26

One merit of a written constitution is its

  1. legibility
  2. total flexibility
  3. rigidity
  4. ease of reference
Answer and explanation

D: ease of reference

A written constitution places constitutional rules in an identifiable document, making them easier to locate and consult. Being written does not by itself guarantee flexibility or rigidity.

  1. revolutionary
  2. reformist
  3. reactionary
  4. radical

Question 42

Which of the following formalized colonial penetration into Africa?

  1. Paris Treaty
  2. Treaty of Westphalia
  3. Berlin Conference
  4. Treaty of Versailles
Answer and explanation

C: Berlin Conference

The Berlin Conference of 1884–1885 established rules relevant to European colonial claims and occupation in Africa. It helped formalize the expansion of colonial control, without itself drawing every later African boundary.

Question 43

The 1989 Nigerian Constitution provided for a

  1. multi-party system
  2. zero-party system
  3. two-party system
  4. one-party system
Answer and explanation

C: two-party system

The 1989 constitutional transition framework specified a two-party system. This was the planned Third Republic arrangement and should not be confused with the later multiparty system.

Question 45

The arm of government mainly responsible for the implementing of law in Nigeria is the

  1. judiciary
  2. police
  3. legislature
  4. executive
Answer and explanation

D: executive

The executive implements laws and administers government policy. The legislature makes laws and the judiciary interprets and applies them in adjudication; the police perform specific enforcement duties.

Question 48

Which of the following is saddled with the conduct of the general elections in Nigeria's Fourth Republic?

  1. National Electoral Commission of Nigeria
  2. Independent National Electoral Commission
  3. National Electoral Commission
  4. Federal Electoral Commission
Answer and explanation

B: Independent National Electoral Commission

The Independent National Electoral Commission conducts Nigeria's federal and state general elections under the Fourth Republic framework. Earlier commissions used different names in preceding political periods.
