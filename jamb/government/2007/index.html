Question 2

An essential principle that guides the activities of the United Nations is

  1. the regulation of international trade
  2. the protection of the United States of America against terrorism
  3. respect for the sovereign equality of member states
  4. the transfer of technology to developing countries
Answer and explanation

C: respect for the sovereign equality of member states

Article 2 of the UN Charter establishes sovereign equality as a principle of membership. Each member is recognized as a sovereign state despite differences in size or wealth.

Question 7

NEPAD was adopted during the OAU summit held in

  1. Tunis
  2. Lusaka
  3. Cairo
  4. Abuja
Answer and explanation

B: Lusaka

The African development initiative that became NEPAD was adopted at the OAU summit in Lusaka in 2001. It aimed to promote development, economic growth and poverty reduction.

Question 8

Nigeria's interest in teaming up with other developing countries in the world is predicated on

  1. her desire to consume more manufactured goods
  2. her desire to produce more raw materials
  3. preserving her independence and promoting development
  4. getting a permanent seat in the United Nations Security Council
Answer and explanation

C: preserving her independence and promoting development

Cooperation among developing countries can protect their autonomy and support development through shared positions and practical partnerships. Producing raw materials or consuming imports alone is not the broader strategic objective.

Question 11

The process of law-making by the legislature begins with

  1. initiating a bill
  2. commencing a debate
  3. a public hearing
  4. the first hearing
Answer and explanation

A: initiating a bill

A legislative proposal must first be initiated as a bill. Debate, hearings and other scrutiny occur after there is a proposal for the legislature to consider.

Question 13

The ancient Greeks practiced

  1. direct democracy
  2. representative democracy
  3. liberal democracy
  4. benevolent dictatorship
Answer and explanation

A: direct democracy

Ancient Athens provides the classic example of direct democracy, where eligible citizens participated personally in decisions. This did not include all residents and was not the system of every Greek city-state.

Question 15

The declaration of election results is the exclusive duty of

  1. political party chairman
  2. electoral commissions
  3. chief justices
  4. election petition tribunals
Answer and explanation

B: electoral commissions

Electoral commissions administer the official declaration of election results through authorized officers. Party chairmen do not independently make the official electoral return.

Question 16

The traditions of a people that shape their government and politics represent their

  1. political culture
  2. political ideology
  3. political acculturation
  4. political socialization
Answer and explanation

A: political culture

Political culture comprises shared traditions, values and beliefs concerning government and politics. Political socialization is the process by which people learn those orientations.

Question 17

According to Karl Marx, the mode of production that precedes capitalism is

  1. mercantilism
  2. feudalism
  3. socialism
  4. communalism
Answer and explanation

B: feudalism

In Marx’s historical account, capitalism develops after feudalism. Feudal relations centre on landed obligations, while capitalism centres on wage labour and privately owned capital.

Question 25

An exception to the mandate of the legislature is the

  1. enactment of laws
  2. oversight of the executive
  3. representation of citizenry
  4. conduct of external relations
Answer and explanation

D: conduct of external relations

Conducting external relations is primarily an executive responsibility. The legislature makes laws, represents citizens and scrutinizes government, including checks on some foreign-policy decisions.

Question 26

One of the obligations of a citizen is

  1. obedience to the laws of the land
  2. active participation in partisan politics
  3. sticking to his beliefs
  4. loyalty to the ruling party
Answer and explanation

A: obedience to the laws of the land

Citizens are expected to obey the law. Citizenship does not require joining a political party or remaining loyal to whichever party currently forms the government.

Question 27

One of the objectives of a multi-party system is to

  1. prevent rigging
  2. provide alternatives
  3. avoid ethnicity
  4. eliminate corruption
Answer and explanation

B: provide alternatives

A multi-party system gives voters a choice among different parties, candidates and programmes. Having several parties does not automatically eliminate corruption or electoral malpractice.
