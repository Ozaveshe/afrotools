Question 2

An essential principle that guides the activities of the United Nations is

  1. the regulation of international trade
  2. the protection of the United States of America against terrorism
  3. respect for the sovereign equality of member states
  4. the transfer of technology to developing countries
Answer and explanation

C: respect for the sovereign equality of member states

Article 2 of the UN Charter establishes sovereign equality as a principle of membership. Each member is recognized as a sovereign state despite differences in size or wealth.

Question 8

Nigeria's interest in teaming up with other developing countries in the world is predicated on

  1. her desire to consume more manufactured goods
  2. her desire to produce more raw materials
  3. preserving her independence and promoting development
  4. getting a permanent seat in the United Nations Security Council
Answer and explanation

C: preserving her independence and promoting development

Cooperation among developing countries can protect their autonomy and support development through shared positions and practical partnerships. Producing raw materials or consuming imports alone is not the broader strategic objective.

Question 13

The ancient Greeks practiced

  1. direct democracy
  2. representative democracy
  3. liberal democracy
  4. benevolent dictatorship
Answer and explanation

A: direct democracy

Ancient Athens provides the classic example of direct democracy, where eligible citizens participated personally in decisions. This did not include all residents and was not the system of every Greek city-state.

Question 17

According to Karl Marx, the mode of production that precedes capitalism is

  1. mercantilism
  2. feudalism
  3. socialism
  4. communalism
Answer and explanation

B: feudalism

In Marx’s historical account, capitalism develops after feudalism. Feudal relations centre on landed obligations, while capitalism centres on wage labour and privately owned capital.

Question 27

One of the objectives of a multi-party system is to

  1. prevent rigging
  2. provide alternatives
  3. avoid ethnicity
  4. eliminate corruption
Answer and explanation

B: provide alternatives

A multi-party system gives voters a choice among different parties, candidates and programmes. Having several parties does not automatically eliminate corruption or electoral malpractice.

Question 29

In a parliamentary system, the term shadow cabinet is often used to refer to

  1. back-benchers in the house
  2. deputy prime ministers and assistant ministers
  3. rebellious members of the ruling party
  4. portfolio designates of the party in opposition
Answer and explanation

D: portfolio designates of the party in opposition

A shadow cabinet consists of opposition politicians assigned to scrutinize particular government departments. Its members develop alternatives and may take the corresponding ministerial roles if their party forms government.

  1. promulgate edicts
  2. promulgate and enforce bye-laws
  3. appoint and dismiss chairmen
  4. appoint and dismiss directors of personnel
Answer and explanation

Question 36

The pre-colonial Igbo political system was largely

  1. republican
  2. dictatorial
  3. monarchical
  4. authoritarian
Answer and explanation

A: republican

Many pre-colonial Igbo communities governed through assemblies, councils and consensus rather than a centralized hereditary monarch. Largely republican recognizes this general pattern while allowing for local exceptions.

Question 49

Deregulation as an economic policy concerns

  1. the removal of subsidies on public utilities
  2. the removal of restrictions and extension of competition
  3. privatization
  4. commercialization
Answer and explanation

B: the removal of restrictions and extension of competition

Deregulation reduces or removes specified restrictions to allow greater competition and market activity. Privatization changes ownership, while commercialization changes how an enterprise operates.
