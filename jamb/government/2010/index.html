Question 6

In a parliamentary system, when the legislature passes a vote of no confidence on the executive.it means that the

  1. executive is expected to go on suspension
  2. executive is required to resign
  3. legislature ceases to trust the executive
  4. legislature commences legal proceeding against the executive
Answer and explanation

B: executive is required to resign

A government in a parliamentary system depends on the confidence of the elected chamber. Defeat on a formal confidence vote requires resignation or the constitutional process for a new election, rather than continued ordinary government.

Question 7

The legislative body of the United States of America is the

  1. Parliament
  2. Congress
  3. National Assembly
  4. Council
Answer and explanation

B: Congress

Congress is the national legislature of the United States. It consists of two chambers, the Senate and the House of Representatives, which participate in making federal laws.

Question 10

In which of the following systems is the power of the component units more than that of the central government?

  1. Monarchical
  2. Unitary
  3. Federal
  4. Confederal
Answer and explanation

D: Confederal

In a confederation the member states retain substantial sovereign authority and grant limited powers to the common body. The central authority is therefore weaker than in a typical federation.

Question 11

One of the general tenets of fascists doctrine is that a leader is

  1. supreme relative to the constitution
  2. subordinate to the laws of the state
  3. weak relative to the constitution
  4. subordinate to the norms of the society
Answer and explanation

A: supreme relative to the constitution

Fascist leadership claims place the leader above ordinary constitutional restraints. This authoritarian principle conflicts with government under law and institutional limits.

Question 12

In a cabinet system of government, executive power is exercised by the

  1. head of government
  2. president
  3. monarch
  4. dominant party
Answer and explanation

A: head of government

In the cabinet system, the head of government leads the executive with cabinet ministers. A ceremonial president or monarch is distinct from this day-to-day governing leadership.

Question 13

The principle of separation of powers is best practiced in the

  1. presidential system
  2. monarchical system
  3. parliamentary system
  4. feudal system
Answer and explanation

A: presidential system

The presidential model assigns separate executive, legislative and judicial responsibilities. Parliamentary government more closely connects the executive and legislature.

Question 16

An electoral process in which candidates are selected for elective offices by party members is

  1. primary election
  2. bye election
  3. electoral college
  4. general election
Answer and explanation

A: primary election

A primary election chooses party nominees for public office. A general election then determines who will hold the office among the nominated candidates.

Question 17

In theory, one major advantage of the one-party system is that it

  1. eliminates intra-party conflict
  2. promotes greater mass participation in government
  3. serves as an instrument of national integration
  4. guarantees social justice
Answer and explanation

C: serves as an instrument of national integration

Supporters of a one-party system claim that a single national party can promote integration across divisions. This is a theoretical justification, not a guarantee of unity or justice.

Question 19

Public opinion can be measured through

  1. negotiation
  2. strike action
  3. referendum
  4. rumour
Answer and explanation

C: referendum

A referendum records voters’ choices on a specified public question. Its result provides direct evidence of opinion on that issue, unlike rumour or informal negotiation.

Question 32

One of the strong points of the multiparty system in Nigeria's Fourth Republic is

  1. the provision for a bicameral legislature
  2. government interference
  3. wider political participation
  4. wider anti-democracy campaign
Answer and explanation

C: wider political participation

A multi-party system can broaden participation by allowing different parties to organize and compete. Bicameralism concerns legislative chambers and is separate from the number of political parties.

Question 33

In which of the following is the ceremonial and executive powers fused?

  1. Presidential system of government
  2. Federal system of government
  3. Parliamentary system of government
  4. Unitary system of government
Answer and explanation

A: Presidential system of government

A presidential system normally combines head-of-state and head-of-government functions in the president. Federal and unitary describe territorial organization rather than that executive arrangement.

Question 40

Nigeria broke diplomatic relations with France in 1961 because

  1. France's atomic test in the Sahara Desert
  2. Her poor relations with the francophone countries
  3. General de-Gaulle's negative attitude towards her
  4. France's diplomatic relations with Israel
Answer and explanation

A: France's atomic test in the Sahara Desert

Nigeria broke diplomatic relations with France in 1961 in protest against French nuclear tests in the Sahara. The dispute concerned those tests rather than France's relations with Israel.
