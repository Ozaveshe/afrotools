Question 6

In a parliamentary system, when the legislature passes a vote of no confidence on the executive.it means that the

  1. executive is expected to go on suspension
  2. executive is required to resign
  3. legislature ceases to trust the executive
  4. legislature commences legal proceeding against the executive
Answer and explanation

B: executive is required to resign

A government in a parliamentary system depends on the confidence of the elected chamber. Defeat on a formal confidence vote requires resignation or the constitutional process for a new election, rather than continued ordinary government.

Question 7

The legislative body of the United States of America is the

  1. Parliament
  2. Congress
  3. National Assembly
  4. Council
Answer and explanation

B: Congress

Congress is the national legislature of the United States. It consists of two chambers, the Senate and the House of Representatives, which participate in making federal laws.

Question 10

In which of the following systems is the power of the component units more than that of the central government?

  1. Monarchical
  2. Unitary
  3. Federal
  4. Confederal
Answer and explanation

D: Confederal

In a confederation the member states retain substantial sovereign authority and grant limited powers to the common body. The central authority is therefore weaker than in a typical federation.

Question 11

One of the general tenets of fascists doctrine is that a leader is

  1. supreme relative to the constitution
  2. subordinate to the laws of the state
  3. weak relative to the constitution
  4. subordinate to the norms of the society
Answer and explanation

A: supreme relative to the constitution

Fascist leadership claims place the leader above ordinary constitutional restraints. This authoritarian principle conflicts with government under law and institutional limits.

Question 12

In a cabinet system of government, executive power is exercised by the

  1. head of government
  2. president
  3. monarch
  4. dominant party
Answer and explanation

A: head of government

In the cabinet system, the head of government leads the executive with cabinet ministers. A ceremonial president or monarch is distinct from this day-to-day governing leadership.

Question 13

The principle of separation of powers is best practiced in the

  1. presidential system
  2. monarchical system
  3. parliamentary system
  4. feudal system
Answer and explanation

A: presidential system

The presidential model assigns separate executive, legislative and judicial responsibilities. Parliamentary government more closely connects the executive and legislature.
