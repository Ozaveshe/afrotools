Question 3

Citizenship refers to the

  1. indigenous member of a state
  2. social status of a person in a state
  3. highest position in a state
  4. legal status of a member of a state
Answer and explanation

D: legal status of a member of a state

Citizenship is a legal relationship between a person and a state, carrying rights and responsibilities. It does not necessarily depend on indigenous ancestry or social rank.

Question 4

The rule of law is a cardinal principle associated with

  1. democratic systems
  2. federal systems
  3. communist systems
  4. feudal systems
Answer and explanation

A: democratic systems

Democratic government is associated with the rule of law: public authority is constrained by law and citizens are entitled to legal protection. A federal arrangement alone does not guarantee that principle.

Question 5

The main function of public opinion is to

  1. change the policy of government
  2. provide direction for public policy
  3. support the policy of government
  4. indoctrinate the people
Answer and explanation

B: provide direction for public policy

Public opinion communicates preferences and concerns that can guide policy choices. It does not always support or reverse existing policy, and its purpose is not to indoctrinate people.

Question 8

One main weakness of a unitary system of government is that

  1. it facilitates the domination of minority groups
  2. the constitution can be amended easily
  3. it is run as a single entity
  4. carpet - crossing and opposition are made difficult
Answer and explanation

A: it facilitates the domination of minority groups

Concentrating authority at the centre can allow a dominant national majority to overlook minority interests. This is a possible risk of unitary government, rather than an inevitable feature of every such state.

Question 9

  1. mobilization
  2. aggregation
  3. manipulation
  4. articulation
Answer and explanation

B: aggregation

Interest aggregation combines different demands into a common position or programme. Interest articulation expresses demands, while aggregation reconciles and brings them together.

Question 10

The act of removing an elected official by the electorate is referred to as

  1. impeachment
  2. consensus
  3. plebiscite
  4. recall
Answer and explanation

D: recall

Recall is a procedure through which voters remove an elected representative before the normal end of the term. Impeachment instead involves a constitutional process conducted by designated public institutions.

Question 17

  2. elected separately to an unfixed term
  3. appointed by the judiciary to a fixed term
  4. appointed at the same time to an unfixed term
