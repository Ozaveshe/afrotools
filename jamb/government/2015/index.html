Question 1

The main attributes of a state are

  1. government, the police and the armed forces
  2. population, territory, government and sovereignty
  3. federal state and local governments
  4. the press, the legislature, the executive and the judiciary
Answer and explanation

B: population, territory, government and sovereignty

The conventional elements of a state are a population, defined territory, government and sovereignty. Particular institutions such as police or a press do not replace these elements.

Question 3

In the history of Nigeria, 1914 was the

  1. year the British established their rule over Nigeria
  2. beginning of first world war
  3. year Lugard effected amalgamation of Nigeria
  4. year the railway system was completed
Answer and explanation

C: year Lugard effected amalgamation of Nigeria

In 1914, Frederick Lugard brought the Northern and Southern protectorates together in the amalgamation that formed Nigeria. British rule in parts of the territory had begun earlier.

Question 4

Which of these rulers resisted colonial rule and was deported to Calabar?

  1. King Kosoko
  2. King Dosunmu
  3. Oba Ovonramwen
  4. King Jaja
Answer and explanation

C: Oba Ovonramwen

Oba Ovonramwen of Benin was deposed following the British expedition of 1897 and sent into exile in Calabar. He remained there until his death in 1914.

Question 5

How many states were created in Nigeria in 1967?

  1. 4
  2. 12
  3. 19
  4. 21
Answer and explanation

B: 12

The1967 state-creation arrangement divided Nigeria into twelve states, replacing the former regional structure. Later exercises increased the number of states.

Question 9

The main role of pressure groups is to

  1. influence the legislature to make decision favourable to them
  2. inject emotion and selfish debates
  3. influence the public
  4. use all means, including bribery, to achieve their objectives
Answer and explanation

A: influence the legislature to make decision favourable to them

Pressure groups seek to influence public decisions in favour of their interests or causes. Lobbying the legislature is one method; bribery is not a legitimate defining feature of their role.

Question 10

The OAU was formed in

  1. 1946
  2. 1956
  3. 1960
  4. 1963
Answer and explanation

D: 1963

The Organization of African Unity was established in Addis Ababa on 25 May 1963. It later developed into the African Union, launched in 2002.

Question 11

The first African Secretary-General of the United Nations is

  1. Boutros Boutros-Ghali
  2. Joe Garba
  3. Ibrahim Gambari
  4. Kofi Annan
Answer and explanation

A: Boutros Boutros-Ghali

Boutros Boutros-Ghali of Egypt became the first African Secretary-General of the United Nations in 1992. Kofi Annan of Ghana succeeded him in 1997.
