Question 3

During the 1964 Federal Election in Nigeria, the United Progressive Grand Alliance comprised the

  1. NNDP, NPC and NDC
  2. NPC, UMBC and BYM
  3. UMBC, AG, NEPU and NCNC
  4. UMBC, BYM, NEPU and NPC
Answer and explanation

C: UMBC, AG, NEPU and NCNC

The UPGA alliance included the Action Group, NCNC, NEPU and United Middle Belt Congress. The NPC belonged to the rival Nigerian National Alliance, so options containing it do not describe UPGA.

Question 7

Foreign Policy refers to

  1. a policy made by a country when it is at war
  2. The general principles of a states’ international behaviour
  3. a policy made by a foreign country
  4. a policy made by a country for foreigners
Answer and explanation

B: The general principles of a states’ international behaviour

Foreign policy consists of the principles and objectives guiding a state in its relations with other states and international actors. It covers peacetime diplomacy as well as responses to conflict.

Question 8

'' From each according to his ability to each according to his needs' is the principle of

  1. Communism
  2. Socialism
  3. Capitalism
  4. Communalism
Answer and explanation

A: Communism

Marx associated this principle with the higher phase of communist society: contributions follow ability and distribution follows need. It differs from distribution based on private profit or individual earnings.

Question 9

The maintenance of law and order is a function of the

  1. legislature
  2. military
  3. judiciary
  4. executive
Answer and explanation

D: executive

The executive administers and enforces laws through institutions such as the police. Courts adjudicate disputes and the legislature makes laws, while routine public order falls primarily to the executive.

Question 10

The development of attitudes and beliefs about a political system is known as

  1. political orientation
  2. political apathy
  3. political culture
  4. political socialization
Answer and explanation

D: political socialization

Political socialization is the process through which people develop political attitudes, beliefs and values. Political culture refers to the resulting shared patterns rather than the learning process itself.

Question 15

The appointment, promotion and discipline of civil servants is the responsibility of the

  1. Civil Service Commission
  2. Public Complaints Commission
  3. Judicial Service Commission
  4. Public Service Institute of Nigeria
Answer and explanation

A: Civil Service Commission

Civil Service Commissions manage appointments and exercise personnel authority, including promotion and discipline, within their jurisdiction. The Public Complaints Commission handles grievances rather than routine civil-service staffing.

  4. republicanism

A monarchy has a monarch, such as a king or queen, as head of state. The monarch’s powers can be limited by a constitution or be much broader.

Question 18

Citizens exercise their sovereignty by

  1. attending political rallies
  2. voting at elections
  3. registration to vote
  4. their right to sue the state
Answer and explanation

B: voting at elections

Voting allows citizens to choose representatives and authorize governments in a representative democracy. Registration prepares a citizen to vote but does not itself make an electoral choice.

Question 20

Nigerian Judiciary is headed by

  1. Attorney-General and Minister of Justice
  2. Solicitor-General of the Federation
  3. Chief Justice of Nigeria
  4. President, Court of Appeal
Answer and explanation

The Chief Justice of Nigeria heads the Supreme Court and chairs the National Judicial Council. The Attorney-General and Solicitor-General perform separate executive legal functions.

Question 25

Separation of powers as an ingredient of the presidential system implies the

  1. distinction of governmental powers
  2. demarcation of levels of government
  3. distinction between head of state and head of government
  4. sharing of governmental powers.
Answer and explanation

A: distinction of governmental powers

Separation of powers distinguishes legislative, executive and judicial functions among different institutions. Division between central and state governments is federalism, a different constitutional principle.

Question 26

The major objective of pressure group is to

  1. change government
  2. form government
  3. influence government
  4. fund government
Answer and explanation

C: influence government

Pressure groups primarily seek to influence government decisions on issues affecting their members or causes. Political parties, by contrast, normally compete to form or participate in government.

Question 27

The foreign policy principle that informed Nigeria's position in the liberation of African states is

  1. economic diplomacy
  2. Afrocentrism
  3. non-alignment
  4. non-interference
Answer and explanation

  4. nomination of judges

Question 36

Government by the best citizens is referred to as

  1. aristocracy
  2. democracy
  3. oligarchy
  4. plutocracy
Answer and explanation

A: aristocracy

Aristocracy originally denotes rule by those considered the best or most qualified citizens. Plutocracy refers specifically to the wealthy, while oligarchy describes rule by a small group without that ideal qualification.

Question 37

The Constitutive Act establishing the African Union was signed in

  1. Togo
  2. Libya
  3. South Africa
  4. Ethiopia.
Answer and explanation

A: Togo

The African Union's Constitutive Act was adopted at Lomé in Togo in July 2000. This differs from the OAU Charter, which was adopted in Addis Ababa in 1963.

Question 39

In Nigeria, items in the exclusive list are under the jurisdiction of

  1. regional government
  2. state government
  3. central government
  4. local government
Answer and explanation

C: central government

Subjects on Nigeria's Exclusive Legislative List are reserved for federal lawmaking. State legislatures cannot independently exercise the same exclusive legislative competence.
