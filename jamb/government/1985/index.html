Question 1

Public opinion is important because it

  1. tells government what action it must take
  2. let government know what the people want
  3. allows the police to determine troublemakers
  4. protects minorities
  5. guarantees a free press
Answer and explanation

B: let government know what the people want

Public opinion communicates people's views, preferences and concerns to government. It informs decisions without itself forcing a particular policy or guaranteeing press freedom.

Question 2

Which branch of government is responsible for implementing laws?

  1. Executive
  2. Legislature
  3. Judiciary
  4. Police
  5. Civil Service
Answer and explanation

A: Executive

The executive implements and administers laws and public policies. The legislature primarily makes laws, while the judiciary interprets them and decides disputes.

Question 3

Universal Adult Suffrage means all

  1. adult citizens can vote
  2. citizens can vote
  3. qualified citizens can vote
  4. literate citizens can vote
  5. males can vote
Answer and explanation

A: adult citizens can vote

Universal adult suffrage extends the vote to adult citizens without property, literacy or sex qualifications. Citizenship and legal adult eligibility distinguish it from voting by every person.

Question 4

In a democracy, sovereignty is vested in

  1. the community
  2. public officials
  3. judges
  4. the Head of Sate
  5. the legislature
Answer and explanation

A: the community

Popular sovereignty in a democracy rests with the people as a political community. Officials and institutions exercise authority on their behalf rather than owning that popular sovereignty.

Question 6

In federal state, powers are shared

  1. between the central government and local governments
  2. between the centre and other co- ordinate units of the state
  3. among the major regions of the country
  4. among the federal ministers
  5. equally among the ethnic groups in the country
Answer and explanation

B: between the centre and other co- ordinate units of the state

Federalism divides constitutional authority between the central government and coordinate constituent units. It is not a division of governmental powers among individual ministers or ethnic groups.

Question 7

The rule of law implies

  1. the rule by lawyers
  2. that only the Head of State is above the law
  3. the absence of a military government
  4. that no one is above the law
  5. that only the National Assembly can make laws
Answer and explanation

D: that no one is above the law

The rule of law subjects public officials as well as ordinary citizens to law. Holding political office does not make a person generally superior to the legal order.

Question 10

Delegated legislation is the power to make laws by

  1. local councils when parliament is in recess
  2. bodies other than parliament
  3. the international Law Commission
  4. military rulers
  5. Parliament
Answer and explanation

B: bodies other than parliament

Delegated legislation is made by bodies or officials acting under authority granted by the legislature. Parliament sets the enabling framework while the delegated body makes rules within that authority.

Question 11

In a unitary system of government

  1. political power is diffused
  2. there is a high degree of centralization
  3. there is no separation of powers
  4. parliament is very weak
  5. legislative powers cannot be delegated to local councils
Answer and explanation

B: there is a high degree of centralization

A unitary constitution places ultimate governmental authority at the national centre. Administrative tasks can still be delegated, so local councils and separation of powers are compatible with it.

Question 12

Case - laws are made by the

  1. Legislature
  2. Executive
  3. Judiciary
  4. Attorney General and Minister of Justice
  5. President
Answer and explanation

C: Judiciary

Case law develops through judicial decisions interpreting and applying legal rules. It differs from legislation enacted by a legislature.

Question 13

The theory of separation of powers was for the first time clearly formulated by

  1. Jean Bodin
  2. John Austin
  3. Baron de Montesquieu
  4. Lord Bryce
  5. A. V. Dicey
Answer and explanation

C: Baron de Montesquieu

Montesquieu clearly formulated the division of legislative, executive and judicial powers in his account of liberty. The doctrine aims to prevent tyranny through concentrated governmental authority.

Question 14

The deliberate tampering with the delimitation of constituencies in order to win more seats is called

  1. gerontocracy
  2. gerrymandering
  3. delimitation
  4. bureaucracy
  5. devolution
Answer and explanation

B: gerrymandering

Gerrymandering is manipulating electoral boundaries to give a political group an unfair advantage. Ordinary delimitation is the drawing of boundaries without that partisan meaning.
