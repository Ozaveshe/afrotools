Question 2

De Jure sovereignty is acquired through

  1. Law
  2. Grant
  3. Treaty
  4. Force
Answer and explanation

A: Law

De jure means recognized in law. De jure sovereignty is legal entitlement to supreme authority, distinct from de facto control obtained or maintained in practice.

Question 4

Political socialization is associated with

  1. Military takeover of civilian government
  2. The transmission of political values
  3. Political transition
  4. Free choice of party programmes
Answer and explanation

B: The transmission of political values

Political socialization transmits political values and attitudes through learning and experience. It is a continuing social process rather than a military takeover or a single change of government.

Question 5

According to Aristotle, a form of government in which the few rule for the benefit of all is

  1. Diarchy
  2. Aristocracy
  3. Autocracy
  4. polyarchy
Answer and explanation

B: Aristocracy

In Aristotle's classification, aristocracy is rule by a few for the common good. Rule by a few for their own interests is the contrasting form of oligarchy.

Question 6

Rule by the old people is known as

  1. Monarchy
  2. Gerontocracy
  3. Feudalism
  4. Theocracy
Answer and explanation

B: Gerontocracy

Gerontocracy means rule by elders or older people. Monarchy concerns a monarch, while theocracy identifies government based on religious authority.

Question 7

As an executive, the commissioner is charged with the responsibility of

  1. Implementing laws
  2. Writing laws
  3. Giving loans
  4. Making laws
Answer and explanation

A: Implementing laws

A commissioner directs an executive department and implements laws and policies within that portfolio. Primary law-making belongs to the legislature, rather than to an individual commissioner.

Question 8

Rules adjudication is a primary function of the

  1. Judiciary
  2. Executive
  3. Government
  4. Legislature
Answer and explanation

A: Judiciary

The judiciary adjudicates disputes by interpreting and applying legal rules to particular cases. Making general laws and carrying them out are primarily legislative and executive functions.

Question 9

The judiciary controls the executive in federal state through

  1. Delegated legislation
  2. Judicial overview
  3. Judicial review
  4. Motions
Answer and explanation

C: Judicial review

Judicial review allows courts to examine executive actions for conformity with the constitution and law. It is a legal check on executive authority rather than a form of delegated law-making.

Question 10

One major advantage of the unitary system is that it tends to make government

  1. Free of controversy
  2. Distant from the people
  3. Popular among the masses
  4. Strong and stable
Answer and explanation

D: Strong and stable

Concentrated central authority can give unitary government clearer direction and coordinated action. This can support strength and stability, though it does not eliminate disagreements or guarantee popularity.

Question 12

Which of the following advocates equitable distribution of wealth?

  1. Capitalism
  2. Aristocracy
  3. Socialism
  4. Plutocracy
Answer and explanation

C: Socialism

Socialism emphasizes a more equitable distribution of wealth and resources. Plutocracy instead describes political dominance by the wealthy.

Question 19

One of the functions of pressure groups is to

  1. Nominate the president
  2. Prepare the budget
  3. Articulate the opinion of their members
  4. Contest elections to serve the people
Answer and explanation

C: Articulate the opinion of their members

Pressure groups articulate their members’ interests by communicating demands to government and the public. Preparing the national budget and holding executive office are different functions.

Question 22

The Revenue Mobilization, Allocation and Fiscal Commission is statutorily empowered to determine the remuneration of

  1. Only elected representatives
  2. Political office holders
  3. Employees of public corporations
  4. All civil servants
Answer and explanation

B: Political office holders

RMAFC determines remuneration for specified political, public and judicial officeholders. Its remit is not limited to elected representatives and does not mean setting every civil servant’s salary.

Question 27

Nigeria became a federation of thirty-six states during the era of

  1. Abdulsalami Abubakar
  2. Yakubu Gowon
  3. Ibrahim Babangida
  4. Sani Abacha
Answer and explanation

D: Sani Abacha

Sani Abacha's government created six additional states in 1996, taking the federation from thirty to thirty-six states. The new states included Bayelsa, Ebonyi, Ekiti, Gombe, Nasarawa and Zamfara.
