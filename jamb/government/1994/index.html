Question 2

Political power is naked force when it is exercised without

  1. set objectives
  2. state apparatus
  3. governmental legitimacy
  4. sovereignty
Answer and explanation

C: governmental legitimacy

Legitimacy is the recognized justification for the exercise of political power. Without legitimacy, compliance obtained purely by coercion can be described as naked force.

Question 6

Constitutionalism means

  1. promotion of the constitution
  2. respect for the constitution
  3. electoral processes based on the constitution
  4. voting rule and regulations
Answer and explanation

B: respect for the constitution

Constitutionalism requires government to respect constitutional rules and limits. It concerns how power is exercised under those rules rather than merely promoting a constitutional document.

Question 7

Switzerland is often cited as a classic example of a country with

  1. unitary constitution
  2. quasi unitary constitution
  3. federal constitution
  4. confederal constitution
Answer and explanation

C: federal constitution

Switzerland is a federal state in which powers are divided between the Confederation and the cantons. Its historical name does not make its modern constitutional structure a confederation.

Question 8

What is the major distinguishing characteristic between flexible and rigid constitutions?

  1. manner of documentation
  2. one is unwritten
  3. amendment procedure
  4. degree of legality
Answer and explanation

C: amendment procedure

The distinction concerns amendment procedure. A rigid constitution requires a special process, while a flexible constitution can be changed through the ordinary law-making procedure.

Question 9

Which of the following best defines democracy?

  1. Government based on virtuous principles and laws
  2. government based on the spirit and letter of the constitution
  3. government based on the free consent of the governed
  4. government which recognizes and respects human rights as enshrined in the constitution
Answer and explanation

C: government based on the free consent of the governed

Democracy rests on the consent and participation of the governed. Constitutions and rights are important safeguards, but government based on freely expressed popular consent is the defining principle here.

Question 10

  1. socialism
  2. communism
  3. communalism
  4. capitalism

Capitalism allows private individuals and enterprises to own and control productive resources. The distinguishing feature is private ownership, rather than communal or state ownership.

Question 11

The essence of checks and balances is to

  1. enhance the functioning of government and prevent arbitrary use of power
  2. ensure that people have their own rights
  3. make sure that power is not delegated to other bodies or individuals
  4. prevent political parties from putting pressure on government
Answer and explanation

A: enhance the functioning of government and prevent arbitrary use of power

Checks and balances allow institutions to limit one another and reduce arbitrary power. They support accountable government without forbidding all delegation or political participation.

Question 12

The power of a head of state to dissolve the legislature and order a general election is usually associated with the

  1. presidential system of government
  2. monarchical system of government
  3. parliamentary system of government
  4. unitary system of government
Answer and explanation

C: parliamentary system of government

Parliamentary systems commonly allow dissolution so that voters can elect a new legislature. Presidential systems generally give the legislature an independent fixed term instead.

Question 18

An electoral process in which candidates for elective offices are selected by party members is known as

  1. primary election
  2. electoral college
  3. direct election
  4. preferential voting
Answer and explanation

A: primary election

A primary election allows members of a political party to select its candidates for a subsequent public election. It is distinct from the election that chooses the officeholder.

Question 19

The principle whereby a legislator's tenure is abruptly brought to an end by his constituency is known as

  1. rejection
  2. reference
  3. recall
  4. return
Answer and explanation

C: recall

Recall allows constituents to remove an elected representative before the normal end of the term. It is a mechanism of direct electoral accountability, distinct from an ordinary election at the end of a term.

Question 35

One of the basic differences between ministries and public corporations is that while ministries are

  1. run on rules, public corporations are not
  2. wholly owned by the government, public corporations are owned by both government and individuals
  3. not established with specific statutes, public corporations are
  4. not important in developmental process, public corporations are
Answer and explanation

C: not established with specific statutes, public corporations are

A statutory public corporation is created by a specific enabling law that sets out its powers and duties. Ministries form part of the executive administration and can be organized through the constitutional powers of government.

Question 49

The major strategy used by OPEC to influence oil price is by

  1. determining the quantity of oil to be produced at any given period
  2. influencing buyers at the international market to buy at high prices
  3. allowing member countries to produce at their discretion
  4. increasing the supply of the commodity
Answer and explanation

A: determining the quantity of oil to be produced at any given period

Coordinating production quantities is OPEC's principal means of influencing supply and hence prices. Quotas concern how much oil is produced, while market demand and other producers also affect prices.

Question 51

Political authority refers to the

  1. ability to effect political action
  2. capacity to produce desired political results
  3. capacity to reshape the political behaviour of citizens
  4. recognized right to exercise political power
Answer and explanation

D: recognized right to exercise political power

Political authority is the recognized right to exercise power. Mere ability to obtain results or influence behaviour is power, which may exist without legitimacy or recognized entitlement.
