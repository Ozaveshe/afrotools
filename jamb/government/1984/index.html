Question 3

One feature of a totalitarian State is the existence of

  1. a single recognized party
  2. pressure group
  3. opposition groups
  4. a colonial power
  5. fierce political rivalry
Answer and explanation

A: a single recognized party

A totalitarian party-state permits a single recognized ruling party and suppresses organized political opposition. The party is used to extend control across political and social life.

Question 5

The principle of checks and balances is necessary because it

  1. prevents governments from becoming dictatorial
  2. prevents the Executive from functioning
  3. makes the Executive stronger than the other organs
  4. makes the three organs hate each other
  5. leaves each organ of government independent of the Judiciary
Answer and explanation

A: prevents governments from becoming dictatorial

Checks and balances prevent any one branch from concentrating unchecked power. By allowing institutions to restrain one another, they help protect against dictatorial government.

Question 6

When a constitution is difficult to amend, we say it is

  1. federal
  2. unitary
  3. written
  4. fragile
  5. rigid
Answer and explanation

E: rigid

A rigid constitution requires a special, more demanding amendment process than ordinary legislation. Difficulty of amendment describes rigidity rather than whether the constitution is written.

Question 9

The most basic property of pressure groups which differentiates them from political parties is that they

  1. are not as interests in politics
  2. do not have permanent organizations
  3. do not support candidate in elections
  4. do not seek to influence public opinion
  5. do not nominate candidate as their own official representatives
Answer and explanation

E: do not nominate candidate as their own official representatives

Political parties nominate their own candidates to contest office. Pressure groups may support candidates or influence elections, but do not normally field candidates as their own official representatives.

Question 10

In a presidential system of government ministers are

  1. collectively responsible to the Senate
  2. collectively responsible to the President
  3. individually responsible to the President
  4. individually responsible to no one
  5. individually responsible to the electorate
Answer and explanation

C: individually responsible to the President

In a presidential system each minister is responsible to the president for the assigned department. This differs from the parliamentary cabinet convention of collective responsibility to the legislature.

Question 11

The three principal organs of government are the

  1. Legislature the Public Service and Judiciary
  2. Political Parties, the Executive and the Judiciary
  3. Executive, the Legislature and the Public Corporation
  4. Legislature, the Executive and the Judiciary
  5. Judiciary the Local Government and the Legislature
Answer and explanation

D: Legislature, the Executive and the Judiciary

The three principal organs are the legislature, executive and judiciary. They respectively make laws, administer government and adjudicate legal disputes.

Question 13

In a democracy, franchise is given to all

  1. Resident adults
  2. citizens
  3. citizens except members of the armed forces
  4. loyal party members
  5. qualified adult citizens
Answer and explanation

E: qualified adult citizens

Democratic franchise extends to qualified adult citizens under the electoral law. Residence alone or loyalty to a political party does not define the electorate.

Question 14

The citizenship of a country may be acquired by an individual through

  1. Decolonization
  2. Nomination
  3. Nationalization
  4. Neutralization
  5. Naturalization
Answer and explanation

E: Naturalization

Naturalization is a legal process through which a non-citizen acquires citizenship after meeting the countrys requirements. Nationalization concerns ownership of assets rather than citizenship.
