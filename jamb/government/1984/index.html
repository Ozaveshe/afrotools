Question 2

A Constitution is a legal document

  1. drawn up by lawyers
  2. enacted by military decree
  3. forming the basis upon which a government rules the country
  4. which must not be altered by any succeeding government
  5. only likely to succeed in a country where there is union government
Answer and explanation

C: forming the basis upon which a government rules the country

A constitution sets the fundamental rules through which government is organized and exercises authority. It defines institutions, powers and limits; it is not defined by who drafted it or by an absolute ban on amendment.

Question 3

One feature of a totalitarian State is the existence of

  1. a single recognized party
  2. pressure group
  3. opposition groups
  4. a colonial power
  5. fierce political rivalry
Answer and explanation

A: a single recognized party

A totalitarian party-state permits a single recognized ruling party and suppresses organized political opposition. The party is used to extend control across political and social life.

Question 5

  1. prevents governments from becoming dictatorial
  2. prevents the Executive from functioning
  3. makes the Executive stronger than the other organs
  4. makes the three organs hate each other
  5. leaves each organ of government independent of the Judiciary
Answer and explanation

Question 6

When a constitution is difficult to amend, we say it is

  1. federal
  2. unitary
  3. written
  4. fragile
  5. rigid
Answer and explanation

E: rigid

A rigid constitution requires a special, more demanding amendment process than ordinary legislation. Difficulty of amendment describes rigidity rather than whether the constitution is written.
