Question 1

Aristocracy is the system of government in which the few rule for

  1. their own benefit
  2. the benefit of all
  3. the benefit of their friends
  4. the benefit of a few
Answer and explanation

B: the benefit of all

In the classical classification, aristocracy is rule by a few for the common good. Rule by a few mainly for their own benefit is classified as oligarchy.

Question 4

Legislative supremacy exists in

  1. Britain
  2. France
  3. Soviet Union
  4. Nigeria
Answer and explanation

A: Britain

Britain follows the doctrine of parliamentary sovereignty, under which Parliament is the supreme legal lawmaking authority. This is the traditional example of legislative supremacy.

Question 5

Presidentialism is a system of government in which

  1. there is elected Head of State who exercises actual executive powers
  2. the Head of State is not the chief executive
  3. the executive functions are the responsibility of the entire members of a cabinet
  4. all members of cabinet must also be members of the legislature
Answer and explanation

A: there is elected Head of State who exercises actual executive powers

In presidential government, an elected president is both head of state and chief executive. This differs from a parliamentary model with a separate head of government.

Question 6

The main function of the upper chamber in a parliamentary system of government is to

  1. Initiate new legislation
  2. execute laws
  3. act as a check on the lower chamber
  4. direct the activities of the lower chamber
Answer and explanation

C: act as a check on the lower chamber

An upper chamber commonly reviews legislation from the lower chamber and provides an additional check. It does not carry out executive administration or simply command the lower house.

Question 9

An important advantage of creating more constituent units in a federal state is to

  1. enhance the people's participation in government
  2. enable ambitious politicians gain political power
  3. make the state gain more power
  4. curb the powers of the federal government
Answer and explanation

Additional constituent units can bring decision-making closer to communities and widen opportunities for participation. Giving individual politicians office is not the public-interest justification for creating units.

Question 10

Under the presidential system

  1. the party with the majority of seats forms the Executive
  2. there is the principle of collective responsibility
  3. the president may come from any of the parties
  4. the constitution must be unwritten
Answer and explanation

C: the president may come from any of the parties

The presidency is filled through a separate electoral process and need not belong to the party holding a legislative majority. A parliamentary majority therefore does not automatically determine the president.

Question 18

The political ideology which advocates the abolition of government and law is

  1. totalitarianism
  2. communism
  3. anarchism
  4. socialism
Answer and explanation

C: anarchism

Anarchism rejects coercive state authority and advocates social organization without a state. Communism and socialism concern economic and social organization and do not by themselves mean abolishing every government.

  2. independence of the judiciary
  3. freedom of the press
  4. right to own private property

  2. is simple to operate

Question 34

Before the 1963 Constitution, the highest court of appeal for Nigeria was the

  1. Supreme Court
  2. Federal Court of Appeal
  3. Privy Council
  4. Federal High Court
Answer and explanation

C: Privy Council

Before the republican constitution, final appeals could go to the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council. The 1963 changes made the Nigerian Supreme Court the final appellate court.

Question 35

Which of the following is NOT a function of the Police Force in Nigeria?

  1. Traffic control
  2. Arrest of criminals
  3. Making of laws
  4. Prosecution of criminals
Answer and explanation

The police enforce laws rather than enact legislation. Investigation, traffic control and legally authorized prosecution are distinct from the legislature's law-making function.
