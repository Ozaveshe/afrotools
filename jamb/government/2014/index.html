Question 1

The necessary attributes of a state are:

  1. Definite territory population, sovereignty, and government
  2. Police, army, sovereignty, and custom
  3. Resources, population, sovereignty, and government
  4. Sovereignty, police, army and immigration
Answer and explanation

A: Definite territory population, sovereignty, and government

A state conventionally has a defined territory, population, government and sovereignty. Specific agencies such as the police and army are institutions rather than the complete defining attributes.

Question 2

The process of taking part in political and public affairs can be termed political

  1. participation
  2. socialization
  3. recognition
  4. culture
Answer and explanation

A: participation

Political participation means involvement in political and public affairs, such as voting, campaigning or public deliberation. Socialization instead concerns learning political values and attitudes.

Question 4

In a democratic, political system, the political sovereign is usually the

  1. electorate
  2. legislature
  3. constitution
  4. political parties
Answer and explanation

A: electorate

The electorate is the body of eligible voters through which popular political authority is expressed. Legislatures and parties act within the political system but are not the ultimate popular sovereign.

Question 5

One basic feature of a monarchical form of government is that

  1. succession is through heredity
  2. the ruler has a fixed tenure
  3. separation of powers is absolute
  4. members of the executive are elected
Answer and explanation

A: succession is through heredity

Monarchical succession is commonly hereditary, passing within a ruling family. This is a typical feature of monarchy rather than a fixed electoral term for the ruler.

Question 6

One main advantage of bicameral legislature is that it

  1. is less cumbersome to pass bills
  2. is not easy to manipulate bills
  3. makes for quick deliberation during emergencies
  4. makes passage of bills easy
Answer and explanation

B: is not easy to manipulate bills

A second legislative chamber provides another stage of scrutiny. This additional review can make it harder for one group to manipulate a bill; it generally slows rather than accelerates passage.

Question 8

Unitary system of government, is more suitable to a country

  1. with a robust and dynamic economy
  2. with a relatively small area and a homogenous population
  3. that is sparsely populated
  4. that possesses a strong and modern army
Answer and explanation

B: with a relatively small area and a homogenous population

A relatively small territory with a broadly homogeneous population can make central administration easier. Population diversity and geographical size are relevant considerations when choosing how to distribute powers.

Question 9

An example of a country ruled by a constitutional monarch is

  1. Italy
  2. Libya
  3. Uganda
  4. Morocco
Answer and explanation

D: Morocco

Morocco is a constitutional monarchy, with the monarchs position and state institutions governed by a constitution. Italy, Libya and Uganda were not constitutional monarchies in the question period.

  3. promote the interest of political parties

Question 18

Which of the following is not a dimension of public opinion?

  1. intensity
  2. substance
  3. polling
  4. orientation
Answer and explanation

C: polling

Polling is a method for measuring public opinion. Intensity, substance and orientation describe characteristics of the opinions being measured rather than the measurement procedure.

Question 46

  1. SEATO
  2. League of Nations
  3. Warsaw pact
  4. NATO

The United Nations succeeded the League of Nations after the Second World War. SEATO, NATO and the Warsaw Pact were separate military alliances established later.

Question 47

The number of permanent members of the UN Security council is

  1. six
  2. seven
  3. eight
  4. five
Answer and explanation

D: five

The UN Security Council has five permanent members: China, France, Russia, the United Kingdom and the United States. These are distinct from its ten elected non-permanent members.
