Question 1

When did Nigeria gain her Independence?

  1. 1st October 1963
  2. 31st October 1690
  3. 1st October 2012
  4. 1st October 1960
  5. 12th October 1992
Answer and explanation

D: 1st October 1960

Nigeria became independent on 1 October 1960. It became a republic in 1963, which is a different constitutional milestone.

Question 2

Democracy means a system of government in which

  1. the majority rules
  2. the minority rules
  3. there is no party system
  4. the people rule
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

D: the people rule

Democracy means rule by the people, exercised directly or through elected representatives. Majority decision-making is a common method within democracy, but the defining idea is popular rule.

Question 4

The Executive is

  1. a committee of the legislature
  2. the body that makes laws
  3. the body that executes the policies of government
  4. the highest organ of government
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

C: the body that executes the policies of government

The executive carries out government policies and administers the law. Lawmaking is primarily the legislature's function, while interpreting the law is associated with the judiciary.

Question 5

The Judiciary is

  1. an arm of the Executive
  2. the body which makes the law
  3. a body of lawyers
  4. the body which interprets the law
  5. the body which enforces the law
Answer and explanation

D: the body which interprets the law

The judiciary interprets and applies the law in deciding disputes. It is a branch of government, not merely an association of lawyers or a committee of the executive.

Question 6

The separation of powers means the same as

  1. a presidential system of government
  2. checks and balances
  3. the rule of law
  4. supremacy of the judiciary
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

E: none of the above

Separation of powers divides governmental functions among branches. Checks and balances, the rule of law and presidentialism are related ideas, but none means exactly the same thing.

Question 8

Pressure groups are

  1. Organization which wants to overthrow the government
  2. organizations which seek to influence the policies of the government
  3. associations of people who share the same ideology
  4. political parties
  5. religious orders
Answer and explanation

B: organizations which seek to influence the policies of the government

Pressure groups organize to influence government policy in support of particular interests or causes. Their defining purpose is influence rather than winning office and forming a government.

Question 10

The citizen's obligations are

  1. what the government orders
  2. duties the individual imposes on himself
  3. what the law requires of the individual
  4. what the military decrees
  5. what political parties demand of their members
Answer and explanation

C: what the law requires of the individual

Citizens obligations are duties required by the legal and civic order. They do not arise simply from personal preference or the demands of a political party.
