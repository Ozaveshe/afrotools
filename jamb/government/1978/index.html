Question 1

When did Nigeria gain her Independence?

  1. 1st October 1963
  2. 31st October 1690
  3. 1st October 2012
  4. 1st October 1960
  5. 12th October 1992
Answer and explanation

D: 1st October 1960

Nigeria became independent on 1 October 1960. It became a republic in 1963, which is a different constitutional milestone.

Question 2

Democracy means a system of government in which

  1. the majority rules
  2. the minority rules
  3. there is no party system
  4. the people rule
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

D: the people rule

Democracy means rule by the people, exercised directly or through elected representatives. Majority decision-making is a common method within democracy, but the defining idea is popular rule.

Question 4

The Executive is

  1. a committee of the legislature
  2. the body that makes laws
  3. the body that executes the policies of government
  4. the highest organ of government
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

C: the body that executes the policies of government

The executive carries out government policies and administers the law. Lawmaking is primarily the legislature's function, while interpreting the law is associated with the judiciary.

Question 5

The Judiciary is

  1. an arm of the Executive
  2. the body which makes the law
  3. a body of lawyers
  4. the body which interprets the law
  5. the body which enforces the law
Answer and explanation

D: the body which interprets the law

The judiciary interprets and applies the law in deciding disputes. It is a branch of government, not merely an association of lawyers or a committee of the executive.

Question 6

The separation of powers means the same as

  1. a presidential system of government
  2. checks and balances
  3. the rule of law
  4. supremacy of the judiciary
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

E: none of the above

Separation of powers divides governmental functions among branches. Checks and balances, the rule of law and presidentialism are related ideas, but none means exactly the same thing.

  5. parliament must ratify executive decisions collectively reached

Collective cabinet responsibility requires ministers to support agreed government decisions publicly. A minister who cannot accept a collective decision is normally expected to resign before publicly opposing it.

Question 43

Indirect Rule as practised by the British in their West African colonies

  1. did not attempt to reform existing traditional institutions
  2. was over glorified and expedient nonsense
  3. satisfied neither the rulers nor the ruled
  4. had nothing in common with the reality of French rule in their colonies
  5. meant ruling through existing rulers and attempting to check excesses
Answer and explanation

E: meant ruling through existing rulers and attempting to check excesses

Indirect rule formally governed through existing traditional authorities under colonial supervision. Colonial officials claimed a supervisory role, though the system often altered those institutions and did not reliably prevent abuses.

Answer and explanation

The 1976 reforms sought a more uniform local government system with defined functions across Nigeria. Uniform rules did not by themselves guarantee efficiency or give traditional rulers unrestricted power.

Question 48

The principle of universal adult suffrage refers to

  1. the right of all adult people to vote
  2. the structure of political parties
  3. the legal nature of a constitution
  4. the right to free speech
  5. all of the above
Answer and explanation

A: the right of all adult people to vote

Universal adult suffrage is the principle of voting rights for adult citizens without exclusions based on wealth, sex or similar status. Electoral laws still define voting age and registration procedures.
