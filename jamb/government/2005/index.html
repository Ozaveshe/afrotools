Question 2

Nigeria supports the South-South cooperation as a foreign policy objective for mutual understanding among

  1. nations south of the equator
  2. sub-Saharan African countries
  3. developing countries
  4. Asian and sub-Saharan African countries
Answer and explanation

C: developing countries

South-South cooperation refers to collaboration among developing countries for shared development objectives. It is not limited to countries geographically south of the equator.

Question 24

The federal legislature in Nigeria under the 1999 Constitution is composed of

  1. 108 senators and 350 members of the House of Representatives
  2. 109 senators and 350 members of the House of Representatives
  3. 100 senators and 250 members of the House of Representatives
  4. 109 senators and 360 members of the House of Representatives
Answer and explanation

D: 109 senators and 360 members of the House of Representatives

The 1999 Constitution provides three senators for each of 36 states and one for the Federal Capital Territory: 109 in total. The House of Representatives has 360 members.

Question 27

The principle of checks and balances empowers the judiciary to

  1. invalidate the actions of the other arms
  2. administer the criminal justice system
  3. abrogate the law
  4. apply the law
Answer and explanation

A: invalidate the actions of the other arms

Judicial review allows courts to invalidate government action that exceeds lawful or constitutional authority. This is a check on other branches, not an unrestricted power to cancel any policy the judges dislike.

Question 28

In a first-past-the-post electoral system, a candidate is declared the winner when the candidate

  1. records the highest number of votes cast
  2. obtains a two-thirds majority of the votes cast
  3. obtains one-third of the votes cast
  4. scores the aggregates of the opponent
Answer and explanation

A: records the highest number of votes cast

First-past-the-post awards the seat to the candidate with more votes than any other candidate. An absolute majority or a two-thirds share is not normally required.

Question 34

The final stage in the process of enacting legislation is

  1. final reading
  2. assent
  3. notification
  4. guillotine
Answer and explanation

B: assent

Assent is the formal final step by which a bill completes the ordinary enactment process. A final reading is a legislative stage before assent, subject to any constitutional alternative procedure.

Question 35

How does the president relieve a minister of appointment in a presidential system?

  1. In consultation with the legislature
  2. After serving a full tenure
  3. In consultation with the judiciary
  4. By unilateral action
Answer and explanation

D: By unilateral action

Ministers normally serve at the president's pleasure in a presidential system. The president can dismiss a minister without a parliamentary vote of no confidence, subject to applicable constitutional rules.

  3. fascism
  4. feudalism
Answer and explanation

Serfs were dependent agricultural workers within feudal society, owing services or dues to a lord. Their restricted status was tied to the land rather than modern wage employment.

Question 39

The ultimate authority in a state is

  1. sovereignty
  2. power
  3. influence
  4. legitimacy
Answer and explanation

A: sovereignty

Sovereignty denotes the state's ultimate authority within its constitutional and territorial order. Power describes capacity more generally, while legitimacy concerns acceptance of the right to rule.
