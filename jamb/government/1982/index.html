Question 1

Universal Suffrage means

  1. Adult participation in politics
  2. giving voting right to women above the age of 21
  3. property ownership
  4. citizens who understand why they vote
  5. the right of qualified adults to vote
Answer and explanation

E: the right of qualified adults to vote

Universal suffrage is the right of eligible adults to vote without property, sex or similar exclusions. It concerns voting rights rather than all forms of political participation.

Question 2

In the Parliamentary System of government executive powers are vested in

  1. The President
  2. Parliament
  3. the Party in government
  4. the Cabinet
  5. a few powerful men in the country
Answer and explanation

D: the Cabinet

In the parliamentary model, the cabinet led by the prime minister exercises executive power. Parliament holds the cabinet accountable, but does not itself conduct day-to-day executive administration.

Question 3

In a federal system, powers that are allocated to both Central and State governments are called

  1. Exclusive
  2. Concurrent
  3. Arbitrary
  4. Copyrights
  5. Residual
Answer and explanation

B: Concurrent

Concurrent powers are assigned to both central and state governments within a federation. Exclusive powers belong to one level, while residual powers concern matters not expressly allocated.

Question 4

In a democracy, political power is exercised by

  1. the majority
  2. the literate public
  3. the few wealthy
  4. one person
  5. the monarchy
Answer and explanation

A: the majority

Democratic decisions commonly follow majority choice while preserving political participation and rights. Among these options, majority rule contrasts with control by wealth, literacy, one ruler or a monarchy.

Question 5

  2. know the names of all taxable adults
  3. know the number of people that support each political party
  4. Guard against fraud at elections
  5. identify citizens and non-citizens of a state

Voter registration establishes a list of eligible voters and helps prevent impersonation or multiple voting. It serves electoral integrity rather than counting taxpayers or party supporters.

Question 7

  2. a fixed term
  3. as long as he enjoys popular support
  4. two terms
  5. a period of six years

Question 9

  2. the constitution is foreign
  3. it has no chapter on human rights

  5. the process of changing it is too cumbersome
Answer and explanation
