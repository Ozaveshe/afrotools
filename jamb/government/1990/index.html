Question 2

The exercise of political power involves

  1. winning elections
  2. using the police
  3. deciding cases
  4. Allocating values
Answer and explanation

D: Allocating values

Political power involves making authoritative decisions about how values and resources are distributed in society. Allocating values captures that general function beyond particular acts such as an election.

Question 6

The second ballot is based on the principle that a successful candidate must obtain

  1. absolute majority
  2. simple majority
  3. forty percent of the votes
  4. fifty percent of the votes
Answer and explanation

A: absolute majority

A second ballot is used when the required absolute majority is not achieved in the first round. An absolute majority means more than half of the valid votes.

Question 7

Which of the following is the least democratic selection process?

  1. First past the post system
  2. Proportional representation
  3. Indirect Election
  4. Co-option
Answer and explanation

D: Co-option

Co-option fills a position through selection by existing members rather than by voters. It therefore provides less direct electoral accountability than the listed electoral methods.

Question 8

An indispensable aspect of the constitution of a democratic state is

  1. press censorship
  2. presidentialism
  3. fundamental human rights
  4. socialism ideology
Answer and explanation

C: fundamental human rights

Democratic constitutional government protects fundamental human rights, including freedoms needed for political participation. Democracy can operate without presidentialism or a socialist economic system.

Question 11

The principle of separation of powers is fundamental to the

  1. parliamentary system
  2. presidential system
  3. totalitarian system
  4. federal system
Answer and explanation

B: presidential system

The presidential model separates the executive from the legislature and assigns distinct institutional powers. Parliamentary government instead normally fuses executive leadership with membership of parliament.

Question 14

The rule of law implies that

  1. judges interpret the law
  2. lawyers interpret the law
  3. everyone is subject to the law
  4. the legislature makes the law
Answer and explanation

C: everyone is subject to the law

The rule of law subjects everyone to legal rules, including government officials. No class of people is generally above the law because of office or status.

Question 17

In a parliament, the most extensive amendment to a bill takes place at the

  1. first reading
  2. committee stage
  3. second reading
  4. final stage
Answer and explanation

B: committee stage

Committee stage examines a bill clause by clause and considers detailed amendments. Second reading concentrates principally on the overall purpose and principles of the bill.

Question 21

The most effective way of monitoring public opinion is

  1. by examining the questions asked by journalists
  2. by the frequency of the press releases of pressure groups
  3. by referendum
  4. through opinion poll.
Answer and explanation

D: through opinion poll.

Properly designed opinion polls can measure public attitudes repeatedly using representative samples. Journalists’ questions and the number of press releases do not reliably show how opinion is distributed.

Question 22

In a true democracy, franchise can be limited by

  1. sex
  2. education
  3. age

Democracies commonly set a minimum voting age while rejecting voting qualifications based on sex or wealth. The age threshold is specified by the relevant electoral law.

Question 35

Which of the following was the LEAST important rationale for Nigerian federalism?

  1. The vastness of the territory and the size of the population
  2. Diverse cultures
  3. Economic benefits
  4. Bicameral legislature
Answer and explanation

D: Bicameral legislature

A bicameral legislature is an institutional design rather than a fundamental social or geographical reason for adopting federalism. Territory, diversity and shared economic interests can motivate the arrangement.
