Question 2

The process by which political beliefs and values are transmitted from one generation to another is best known as political

  1. socialization
  2. culture
  3. training
  4. decentralization
Answer and explanation

A: socialization

Political socialization is the process of learning and passing on political beliefs, values and attitudes. Families, schools, peers and public institutions can all contribute to it.

Question 3

The fusion of the executive and legislative organs of government is associated with

  1. monarchy
  2. the presidential system
  3. fascism
  4. the parliamentary system
Answer and explanation

D: the parliamentary system

In a parliamentary system, the executive is drawn from and accountable to the legislature. This institutional connection is described as a fusion of executive and legislative powers.

Question 4

One criticism of delegated legislation is that it

  1. subjects’ citizens to executive decrees
  2. makes laws too flexible
  3. makes laws too technical
  4. is not useful in emergency situation
Answer and explanation

A: subjects’ citizens to executive decrees

Delegated lawmaking gives the executive authority to make detailed rules. A criticism is that citizens can become subject to executive-made rules with less direct legislative debate.

Question 6

Which of the following defines a constitution?

  1. Any written legal document
  2. Basic laws for the conduct of modern government
  3. Rules adopted in legislative proceedings
  4. The doctrine that limits the power of government
Answer and explanation

B: Basic laws for the conduct of modern government

A constitution sets out the basic rules for governing a political community. Ordinary legal documents or rules used only inside a legislature are narrower than a constitution.

Question 7

Which of the following ideologies advocates governmental control over all sources of power?

  1. totalitarianism
  2. feudalism
  3. liberation
  4. democracy
Answer and explanation

A: totalitarianism

Totalitarianism seeks comprehensive state control over political and social life, including competing centres of power. Democracy instead permits opposition and limits governmental authority.

Question 8

Which of the following is NOT an acceptable means of achieving democracy?

  1. referendum
  2. recall
  3. initiative
  4. riots
Answer and explanation

D: riots

Referendum, recall and initiative are formal mechanisms for public participation in political decisions. Riots are violent disorder and are not a constitutional democratic decision-making procedure.

Question 11

Law making under military regimes is done through

  1. delegated legislation
  2. administrative enactments
  3. the promulgation of decrees
  4. the ministry of justice
Answer and explanation

C: the promulgation of decrees

Military governments in the Nigerian historical context enacted laws through decrees at the national level. This bypassed the ordinary elected legislative process.

Question 14

Electoral colleges have been criticized because they

  1. are very expensive
  2. create more seats in the legislature
  3. make the legislature too responsive
  4. sometimes negate the wishes of the electorate
Answer and explanation

D: sometimes negate the wishes of the electorate

An electoral-college outcome can differ from the preference expressed by the largest number of individual voters. That possible divergence is a common criticism of indirect electoral arrangements.
