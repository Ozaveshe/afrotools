Question 6

The structure of the civil service is based on

  1. lateral organisation
  2. merit system
  3. patronage system
  4. hierarchical organisation
Answer and explanation

D: hierarchical organisation

Civil-service structure is hierarchical: offices are arranged in levels of authority and responsibility. Merit concerns recruitment or promotion, rather than the organizational chain itself.

Question 9

The final interpretation of the provisions of a federal constitution is vested in the

  1. head of state
  2. council of state
  3. highest legislative body
  4. highest court of the land.
Answer and explanation

D: highest court of the land.

The highest competent court gives the final judicial interpretation of constitutional provisions. This enables constitutional disputes between levels of government to be resolved through legal adjudication.

Question 11

Proportional representation is a system of allocating seats in the legislature based on

  1. gender participation in politics
  2. an area’s involvement in politics
  3. contribution to the national economy
  4. total votes in an election
Answer and explanation

D: total votes in an election

Proportional representation allocates legislative seats in relation to the votes won by competing parties or groups. Seat shares broadly follow electoral support rather than economic contributions or geographic involvement.

Question 14

Oligarchy is a form of government which

  1. enhances the interest of the ruling few
  2. enhances the electoral chances of the majority
  3. disregards the views of the minority
  4. protects the interest of the common people
Answer and explanation

A: enhances the interest of the ruling few

Oligarchy is government by a small group and commonly advances that ruling groups interests. It contrasts with broad popular control of government.

Question 16

Centralization of power is the basic feature of

  1. federalism
  2. a confederation
  3. a presidential system
  4. a unitary government
Answer and explanation

D: a unitary government

A unitary government concentrates ultimate constitutional authority at the national centre. Federalism divides powers constitutionally, while confederations leave substantial authority with member states.

Question 20

The principle of checks and balances reinforces separation of powers in order to

  1. protect the powers of the executive
  2. make the legislature more powerful
  3. prevent an unconstitutional change of government
  4. prevent the emergence of dictatorship
Answer and explanation

D: prevent the emergence of dictatorship

Checks and balances enable institutions to restrain one another and prevent excessive concentration of power. Combined with separation of powers, they reduce the risk of dictatorship.

Question 29

The constitution that introduced restricted franchise into Nigerian politics was the

  1. Independence Constitution
  2. Lyttelton Constitution
  3. Richards Constitution
  4. Clifford Constitution
Answer and explanation

D: Clifford Constitution

The Clifford Constitution introduced limited elections in Lagos and Calabar. Its restricted franchise is distinguished from universal voting rights extended to all eligible adults.

Question 33

  2. give the judiciary more powers to discipline erring judges
  3. protect public officers from the press
  4. give the police more powers to make arrests

Question 36

One major factor that differentiates the presidential from the parliamentary system is

  1. separation of powers
  2. judicial independence
  3. passage of bills
  4. party system
Answer and explanation

A: separation of powers

Presidential government institutionally separates the executive from the legislature. Parliamentary government normally draws the executive from parliament and makes it accountable to parliamentary confidence.

Question 48

Which of these international agencies is Nigeria a member of?

  1. London Club
  2. The Infrastructural Development Fund
  3. The Paris Club
  4. The International Monetary Fund.
Answer and explanation

D: The International Monetary Fund.

Nigeria is a member of the International Monetary Fund. The Paris and London Clubs are creditor arrangements; a debtor country negotiating with them is not thereby a member of those creditor groups.
