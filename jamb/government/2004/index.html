Question 2

The permanent members of the Security Council of the United Nations are

  1. Germany, France, Poland, Hungary and China
  2. Britain, Japan, Australia, Germany and the United States
  3. the United States, Russia, France, Britain and Japan
  4. the United States, Russia, France, Britain and China
Answer and explanation

D: the United States, Russia, France, Britain and China

The five permanent Security Council members are China, France, the Russian Federation, the United Kingdom and the United States. Japan and Germany are not permanent members.

Question 7

  1. African Economic Summit
  2. Lagos Plan of Action

  4. Economic Commission for Africa
Answer and explanation

The Treaty of Lagos signed on 28 May 1975 established ECOWAS. The organization was created to promote cooperation and economic integration among West African states.

Question 12

The responsibility for admitting new members to the OPEC rests with the

  1. Summit
  2. Secretariat
  3. Conference
  4. Board of Governors
Answer and explanation

C: Conference

The OPEC Conference takes membership decisions under the organizations statute. The Secretariat administers the organization but is not the body that admits new members.

Question 21

Under the 1999 Constitution, the power to declare war is vested in the

  1. National Security Council
  2. Legislature
  3. Executive
  4. National Council of States
Answer and explanation

C: Executive

Under section 5 of the 1999 Constitution, the President declares war subject to authorization by a resolution of both legislative houses in joint session. The executive power is therefore constitutionally checked.

Question 25

The principle of separation of powers was made popular by

  1. Thomas Hobbes
  2. John Locke
  3. Niccolo Machiavelli
  4. Baron de Montesquieu
Answer and explanation

D: Baron de Montesquieu

Montesquieu is especially associated with the separation of legislative, executive and judicial powers. Dividing these functions helps prevent excessive concentration of authority.

Question 26

An interest group that admits members and conducts its affairs according to stated rules is described as

  1. organizational
  2. associational
  3. non-associational
  4. institutional
Answer and explanation

B: associational

An associational interest group has an organized membership and formal rules for pursuing shared interests. Non-associational interests may arise from shared identity without that formal organization.

Question 27

Delegated legislation refers to laws made under legislative authority by

  1. non-legislative bodies
  2. the legislature
  3. military governments
  4. civilian governments
Answer and explanation

A: non-legislative bodies

Delegated legislation is made by an authority, such as a minister or local council, using powers granted by the legislature. The parent law defines the scope of that delegated power.

Question 29

The law of libel limits a citizen's right to freedom of

  1. movement
  2. association
  3. expression
  4. worship
Answer and explanation

C: expression

Libel concerns defamatory statements in a lasting form, so it limits some uses of expression. The law balances expression with protection of reputation.

Question 31

The fundamental assumption on which the idea of the rule of law is based is

  1. supremacy of the constitution
  2. rationality of human beings
  3. equality of human beings
  4. love for social justice
Answer and explanation

C: equality of human beings

Equality before the law is a fundamental premise of the rule of law. People holding public office should be subject to legal rules rather than exempt through status or privilege.

Question 34

In a confederation, the constituency that a member of the legislature represents is a

  1. nation-state
  2. region
  3. senatorial district
  4. parliamentary constituency
Answer and explanation

A: nation-state

A confederation's common assembly represents its member states. The members remain sovereign political units rather than ordinary districts within one centralized state.

Question 36

The economic basis of feudalism is

  1. trade
  2. capital
  3. agriculture
  4. slavery
Answer and explanation

C: agriculture

Feudal economies were primarily agrarian: control of land and agricultural production supported the obligations between lords and dependent cultivators. Agriculture therefore formed their economic base.
