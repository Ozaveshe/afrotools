Question 2

The permanent members of the Security Council of the United Nations are

  1. Germany, France, Poland, Hungary and China
  2. Britain, Japan, Australia, Germany and the United States
  3. the United States, Russia, France, Britain and Japan
  4. the United States, Russia, France, Britain and China
Answer and explanation

D: the United States, Russia, France, Britain and China

The five permanent Security Council members are China, France, the Russian Federation, the United Kingdom and the United States. Japan and Germany are not permanent members.

Question 3

The one-time president of the United Nations General Assembly was

  1. Joseph Garba
  2. Ibrahim Gambari
  3. Arthur Mbanefo
  4. Maitama Sule
Answer and explanation

A: Joseph Garba

Joseph Nanven Garba of Nigeria served as president of the forty-fourth session of the UN General Assembly. The other listed Nigerian diplomats did not hold that presidency.

Question 4

The countries in which Nigeria participated in the ECOMOG peace-keeping operations were

  1. Sierra Leone and Cote d'ivoire
  2. Liberia and Sierra Leone
  3. Liberia and Guinea
  4. Senegal and Cote d'ivoire
Answer and explanation

B: Liberia and Sierra Leone

Nigeria contributed to ECOMOG operations in Liberia and Sierra Leone. These were regional interventions connected with the conflicts in the two countries.

Question 7

The Lagos Treaty of May 28th, 1975 led to the formation of the

  1. African Economic Summit
  2. Lagos Plan of Action
  3. Economic Community of West African States
  4. Economic Commission for Africa
Answer and explanation

C: Economic Community of West African States

The Treaty of Lagos signed on 28 May 1975 established ECOWAS. The organization was created to promote cooperation and economic integration among West African states.

Question 12

The responsibility for admitting new members to the OPEC rests with the

  1. Summit
  2. Secretariat
  3. Conference
  4. Board of Governors
Answer and explanation

C: Conference

The OPEC Conference takes membership decisions under the organizations statute. The Secretariat administers the organization but is not the body that admits new members.

Question 14

The two-chambers of elected national representatives in Nigeria are called

  1. the National Assembly
  2. House of Assembly
  3. the Senate
  4. the parliament
Answer and explanation

A: the National Assembly

Nigerias federal legislature is formally called the National Assembly. Its two chambers are the Senate and the House of Representatives.
