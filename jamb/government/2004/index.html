Question 2

The permanent members of the Security Council of the United Nations are

  1. Germany, France, Poland, Hungary and China
  2. Britain, Japan, Australia, Germany and the United States
  3. the United States, Russia, France, Britain and Japan
  4. the United States, Russia, France, Britain and China
Answer and explanation

D: the United States, Russia, France, Britain and China

The five permanent Security Council members are China, France, the Russian Federation, the United Kingdom and the United States. Japan and Germany are not permanent members.

Question 21

Under the 1999 Constitution, the power to declare war is vested in the

  1. National Security Council
  2. Legislature
  3. Executive
  4. National Council of States
Answer and explanation

C: Executive

Under section 5 of the 1999 Constitution, the President declares war subject to authorization by a resolution of both legislative houses in joint session. The executive power is therefore constitutionally checked.

Question 26

An interest group that admits members and conducts its affairs according to stated rules is described as

  1. organizational
  2. associational
  3. non-associational
  4. institutional
Answer and explanation

B: associational

An associational interest group has an organized membership and formal rules for pursuing shared interests. Non-associational interests may arise from shared identity without that formal organization.

Question 27

Delegated legislation refers to laws made under legislative authority by

  1. non-legislative bodies
  2. the legislature
  3. military governments
  4. civilian governments
Answer and explanation

A: non-legislative bodies

Delegated legislation is made by an authority, such as a minister or local council, using powers granted by the legislature. The parent law defines the scope of that delegated power.

Question 40

In a presidential system of government, the president checks the legislature through

  1. executive review
  2. executive order
  3. legislative order
  4. exercise of veto
Answer and explanation

D: exercise of veto

A president may veto legislation by withholding assent under the applicable constitution. This checks the legislature, although a constitution may allow the legislature to override the veto.

Question 42

Independence of the judiciary is pertinent because it accords the judiciary the power to

  1. dismiss any judge who has breached the judicial code of conduct
  2. determine a fixed salary for judges
  3. determine a fixed term of office for the judges
  4. enable the judges to try and decide cases without bias
Answer and explanation

D: enable the judges to try and decide cases without bias

Judicial independence protects judges from improper pressure so they can decide cases impartially. It does not give individual judges unrestricted power to set salaries, terms of office or dismissal procedures.

Question 43

In a democracy, sovereign authority is exercised by the

  1. legislature
  2. people
  3. executive
  4. electorate
Answer and explanation

B: people

Democratic sovereignty ultimately belongs to the people, who authorize government through constitutional and electoral arrangements. The electorate is the eligible voting part of that wider population, not the whole sovereign community.

Question 48

The difference between commercialized and privatized companies is that in the former

  1. public ownership is dominant
  2. government subsidizes costs
  3. profit motive is recessive
  4. private ownership is dominant
Answer and explanation

A: public ownership is dominant

Commercialization changes how a public enterprise operates while retaining public ownership. Privatization transfers ownership or control to private investors, so the distinction concerns ownership rather than simply charging prices.
