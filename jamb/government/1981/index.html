  4. Black Americans enjoy preferential treatment to compensate for past injustices
  5. no branch of government can function without co-operating with the others

Question 8

  2. gives room for the development of conventions
  3. makes the parliamentary system unworkable
  4. allows unquestionable parliamentary sovereignty
  5. enables the queen to do no wrong
Answer and explanation

The British constitution includes conventions alongside statutes, common law and other sources. These accepted political practices can develop without being codified in a single constitutional document.
