Question 3

The main role of pressure groups is to

  1. Oppose oppressive legislation
  2. protect the interest of the country against foreign power
  3. resist temptation of gifts made by multi-national companies to influence their political parties
  4. to influence their judgement
  5. influence legislation in order to benefit their members
Answer and explanation

E: influence legislation in order to benefit their members

Pressure groups seek to influence public decisions in ways that advance their members or causes. Influencing legislation is one central means of pursuing those interests.

Question 7

Under the United States Constitution

  1. Congress is supreme
  2. the Supreme Court is dominant
  3. the presidency is clearly superior in practice to the other two branches of government
  4. Black Americans enjoy preferential treatment to compensate for past injustices
  5. no branch of government can function without co-operating with the others
Answer and explanation

E: no branch of government can function without co-operating with the others

Separation of powers is combined with checks and balances in the United States. Legislative, executive and judicial responsibilities interact, preventing any one branch from exercising all national governing powers alone.

Question 8

The British constitution, by being unwritten

  1. Encourages the dominance of the Prime Minister
  2. gives room for the development of conventions
  3. makes the parliamentary system unworkable
  4. allows unquestionable parliamentary sovereignty
  5. enables the queen to do no wrong
Answer and explanation

B: gives room for the development of conventions

The British constitution includes conventions alongside statutes, common law and other sources. These accepted political practices can develop without being codified in a single constitutional document.

Question 9

Constitutions are useful documents because they

  1. suppress individual rights and liberties
  2. provide ammunition to different sides in ideological disputes
  3. reward the patriotic and punish traitors
  4. lay down broad guidelines for national political interaction
  5. highlight the procedures for the creation of new states
Answer and explanation

D: lay down broad guidelines for national political interaction

A constitution sets the broad framework for national political interaction, including institutions and their powers. It is not primarily a system for rewarding personal loyalty or conducting ideological disputes.

Question 11

The Nigerian electoral system gives voting rights to

  1. aliens
  2. Africans above the age of twenty-one
  3. children over twelve years old
  4. Nigerian citizens who are 18 years old and above
  5. Friends of Nigerian
Answer and explanation

D: Nigerian citizens who are 18 years old and above

Nigerian voting eligibility includes citizenship and a minimum age of eighteen, subject to registration and other legal requirements. Age alone does not automatically complete the registration process.

Question 12

The inalienable rights of the citizens are best protected through

  1. mass participation in political processes
  2. an independent and fearless judiciary
  3. a cabinet system of government
  4. a good head of state
  5. a written constitution
Answer and explanation

B: an independent and fearless judiciary

An independent judiciary can enforce rights and provide remedies against unlawful interference. A written promise alone is insufficient if courts cannot apply it impartially.

Question 14

The main function of the National Assembly is to

  1. fix salaries for all the state governors
  2. make laws for the country
  3. pass motions on the welfare of the legislators
  4. direct public attention to the excesses of public officials
  5. condemn the south African government for its policy of apartheid
Answer and explanation

B: make laws for the country

The National Assembly is Nigerias federal legislature and makes laws within its constitutional competence. Motions and scrutiny are additional activities supporting that role.
