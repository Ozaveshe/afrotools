Question 1

Judiciary means

  1. a body of lawyers
  2. a committee of the legislature
  3. the highest organ of government
  4. none of the above
Answer and explanation

D: none of the above

The judiciary is the branch of government that interprets laws and resolves legal disputes. It is neither simply a body of lawyers nor a legislative committee, so none of the descriptions fits.

Question 2

An example of a country with an unwritten constitution is

  1. Nigeria
  2. United Kingdom
  3. U.S.A
  4. Sierra Leone
Answer and explanation

B: United Kingdom

The United Kingdom has an uncodified constitution: its constitutional rules come from several sources rather than one codified document. Many of those sources are written.

Question 3

The rule of law is only applicable in

  1. in developing countries
  2. in western type democracies
  3. in states where laws are supreme
  4. in socialist states
Answer and explanation

C: in states where laws are supreme

The rule of law requires public power to be exercised under law rather than arbitrary personal will. Supremacy of law, rather than a country's income or economic system, is the relevant condition.

Question 4

The legislative arm of government is

  1. the cabinet
  2. the body that interprets laws
  3. the body that executes laws
  4. the body that makes laws
Answer and explanation

D: the body that makes laws

The legislative branch makes laws. Interpreting laws belongs to the judiciary, while implementing policies and enforcing laws belongs primarily to the executive.

Question 5

The fundamental laws of a country are contained in its

  1. constitution
  2. party programmes
  3. manifestoes
  4. edicts
Answer and explanation

A: constitution

A constitution contains a country's fundamental governing rules and principles. Party programmes and manifestoes state political proposals, while individual edicts are not the whole constitutional framework.

Question 8

The crown colony in Lagos was created in

  1. 1914
  2. 1861
  3. 1960
  4. 1900
Answer and explanation

B: 1861

Britain annexed Lagos in 1861 following the treaty of cession. This preceded the later protectorate arrangements and the 1914 amalgamation of Nigeria.

Question 9

All these are civil obligations of every Nigerian citizen except

  1. obedience to law
  2. freedom of conscience
  3. respect for the national anthem and flag
  4. payment of tax.
Answer and explanation

B: freedom of conscience

Freedom of conscience is a right that protects beliefs and convictions. Obeying lawful rules, paying required taxes and respecting national symbols are framed as civic obligations.

Question 10

Why the Lyttleton Constitution is important is

  1. introduced franchise to Nigerian politics
  2. abolished the principle of indirect rule
  3. confirmed Nigeria as a federal structure
  4. made Nigeria a sovereign nation
Answer and explanation

C: confirmed Nigeria as a federal structure

The 1954 Lyttleton Constitution established a fully federal constitutional structure for Nigeria. It preceded independence and did not itself make Nigeria a sovereign country.

Question 11

An important advantage of public corporation is

  1. government competes with private enterprises
  2. eliminate private enterprises
  3. makes quick profit at the expense of the people
  4. render crucial services to the people in areas which the civils services cannot effectively handle
Answer and explanation

D: render crucial services to the people in areas which the civils services cannot effectively handle

A public corporation can deliver important services through a specialized operational body. Public-service provision, rather than eliminating competitors or maximizing profit at public expense, is the relevant advantage.
