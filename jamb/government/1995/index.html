Question 1

The concept of sovereignty can best be defined as the

  1. state of being supreme in authority
  2. power of the executive president
  3. supremacy of the legislature
  4. supremacy of the executive
Answer and explanation

A: state of being supreme in authority

Sovereignty describes supreme authority within a political community and independence in its external relations. It is broader than the powers of any one branch of government.

Question 2

Power is understood to mean the

  1. right to compel obedience
  2. capacity to compel obedience
  3. relationship between the government and the people
  4. desire to seize the state apparatus
Answer and explanation

B: capacity to compel obedience

Power is the capacity to influence or compel behaviour. The right to do so is authority, so capacity rather than a recognized right is the defining distinction.

Question 3

The institutions which constitute the main organs of the government of a nation are the

  1. legislature, the executive and the judiciary
  2. judiciary and the civil service
  3. trustee and the presidium
  4. civil service and the senate
Answer and explanation

A: legislature, the executive and the judiciary

The conventional three branches of government are the legislature, executive and judiciary. They respectively make laws, implement public policy and interpret laws or resolve disputes.

Question 4

The executive arm of government includes the

  1. police
  2. courts
  3. national assembly
  4. senate president
Answer and explanation

A: police

The police form part of the machinery that enforces laws and maintains public order. That places them within the executive rather than the legislative or judicial branch.

Question 5

Unicameral legislature is often criticized for

  1. restricting the scope of participation
  2. cheapening the opportunity for participation
  3. condoning the participation of traditional rulers
  4. Narrowing participation to the military
Answer and explanation

A: restricting the scope of participation

A unicameral legislature has only one chamber, which can limit the range of institutional representation compared with two chambers. This is a criticism of participation channels, not a claim that only military representatives may serve.

Question 7

According to Marxist theory, those who own and control the means of production in a capitalist society are the

  1. producers
  2. bourgeoisie
  3. colonialists
  4. proletariats
Answer and explanation

B: bourgeoisie

In Marxist analysis, the bourgeoisie own and control the means of production. The proletariat are wage workers who sell their labour power rather than owning those productive assets.

Question 9

The goal of the theory of separation of powers is to

  1. make the executive dependent on the legislature
  2. give more powers to the legislature
  3. protect individual liberty
  4. create efficiency in the judiciary
Answer and explanation

C: protect individual liberty

Dividing legislative, executive and judicial powers limits the concentration of authority. This helps protect individual liberty by making arbitrary or tyrannical rule harder.

Question 10

The power of the courts to declare legislative enactments as unconstitutional is known as judicial

  1. ruling
  2. review
  3. interpretation
  4. edict
Answer and explanation

B: review

Judicial review is the power to examine legislation for constitutional validity. When a court sets aside an unconstitutional enactment it is exercising this review function.

Question 14

The legal process by which an alien is accepted as a national of a country other than his own is known as

  1. naturalization
  2. citizenship
  3. expatriation
  4. indigenization
Answer and explanation

A: naturalization

Naturalization is the legal admission of a person to citizenship of a country other than their original one. Expatriation and indigenization describe different processes.

Question 15

Enfranchisement describes the process of

  1. allowing eligible persons the right to vote
  2. allowing special categories of persons to contest elections
  3. allowing all women the right to vote
  4. the actual act of voting
Answer and explanation

A: allowing eligible persons the right to vote

Enfranchisement grants or extends the right to vote to eligible people. Casting a ballot is exercising the franchise, not granting it.

Question 16

An electoral system that apportions legislative seats to a group relative to its actual voting strength is referred to as

  1. franchise
  2. simple plurality
  3. proportional representation
  4. discriminatory majority
Answer and explanation

C: proportional representation

Proportional representation allocates seats in relation to the votes received by parties or groups. It contrasts with winner-take-all plurality allocation.

Question 18

The major distinguishing factor between a political party and a pressure group is that while the former

  1. is effectively organized the latter is not
  2. engages in propaganda, the latter does not
  3. seeks power, the latter influences it
  4. does not form a government, the latter does
Answer and explanation

C: seeks power, the latter influences it

Political parties seek to win public office and exercise governmental power. Pressure groups chiefly seek to influence those who hold power on particular interests or causes.

Question 20

On what is charismatic authority based?

  1. Inherited wealth and power
  2. Tyrannical tendencies
  3. Institutional processes
  4. Personal ability and influence
Answer and explanation

D: Personal ability and influence

Charismatic authority rests on followers’ belief in an individual’s exceptional qualities and influence. It differs from authority derived from inherited office or established legal procedures.

Question 26

The first leader of the National Council of Nigeria and the Cameroons was

  1. Nnamdi Azikiwe
  2. Michael Okpara
  3. Samuel Ikoku
  4. Herbert Macaulay
Answer and explanation

D: Herbert Macaulay

Herbert Macaulay was the first president of the NCNC when it was formed in 1944. Nnamdi Azikiwe initially served as secretary and later led the organization.
