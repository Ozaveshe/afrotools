Question 2

Pressured groups' politics are usually directed to

  1. limited or sectoral goals
  2. national objectives
  3. the interest s of the Nigerian Medical Association
  4. the goals of the Nigeria Union of Teachers
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

A: limited or sectoral goals

Pressure groups usually pursue the particular interests or objectives of a sector or membership. This distinguishes their focused goals from a party seeking to govern across a full policy programme.

Question 3

The principle of separation of powers ensures

  1. the erosion of executive power
  2. that the legislature is supreme
  3. the preservation of the fundamental liberties of the citizens
  4. that all the branches of government have equal powers and functions
  5. that the rights of the ethnic minorities are safeguarded
Answer and explanation

C: the preservation of the fundamental liberties of the citizens

Separating governmental functions helps prevent concentrated power and protects individual liberty. Each branch has a distinct role rather than identical functions or unlimited supremacy.

Question 6

The concept of Indirect Rule in Nigeria is usually associated with

  1. Sir George T. Goldie
  2. Sir Donald Cameron
  3. Major Claude Macdonald
  4. Lord Lugard
  5. Sir Arthur Richards
Answer and explanation

D: Lord Lugard

Lord Lugard developed indirect rule in Northern Nigeria. British colonial authorities governed through existing emirs and other traditional institutions, placing them under colonial supervision.

Question 11

The legislative arm of a government is always

  1. the Parliament
  2. the Cabinet
  3. the highest organ of government
  4. the body that makes laws
  5. the body that interprets laws
Answer and explanation

D: the body that makes laws

The defining function of the legislative arm is making laws. Its name can differ between countries, so Parliament is not the universal institutional title.

Question 12

The Executive is the body that

  1. executes armed robbers
  2. interprets laws
  3. implements laws
  4. enacts laws
  5. none of the above
Answer and explanation

C: implements laws

The executive implements and administers laws. Interpreting laws in disputes is judicial work, while enacting primary legislation is principally legislative work.

Question 13

The principle of separation of powers implies that

  1. the Executive is supreme
  2. the Executive is powerless
  3. power is shared by different but co- ordinate organs in the state
  4. the rule of law obtains
  5. power is shared by competing departments in the state
Answer and explanation

C: power is shared by different but co- ordinate organs in the state

Separation of powers distributes state functions among distinct but coordinated organs. It does not make the executive either supreme or powerless.

Question 14

The term rule of law is applicable only

  1. in western-type democracies
  2. in socialist countries
  3. in developing countries
  4. in state where laws are supreme
  5. if the regime is not military
Answer and explanation

D: in state where laws are supreme

The rule of law applies where legal rules are supreme over arbitrary power. It is a principle of government under law, not a label restricted to one region or economic ideology.
