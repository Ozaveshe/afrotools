Senegal salary questions

How is Senegal salary income tax calculated?

The 30% salary allowance is capped at XOF 900,000 per year. Taxable salary is rounded down to XOF 1,000. The seven bands apply at 0%, 20%, 30%, 35%, 37%, 40% and 43%, with 43% above XOF 50 million taxable income. Family relief then reduces income tax. The allowance is not a cash deduction.

How do family relief and TRIMF differ?

Family shares depend on marital status, qualifying children and whether you are the only spouse with taxable income. TRIMF uses annual gross salary and the number of liable persons: normally you plus spouses with no income of any kind. Children are not counted for TRIMF. Confirmed payroll overrides are available for special cases.

Are IPRES and CSS the same deduction?

No. IPRES manages retirement pensions. The general employee rate used here is 5.6% on a capped salary base; the employer rate is 8.4%. CSS covers employer-funded family benefits and occupational risks. This calculator does not invent an employee CSS deduction or a universal employer CSS rate.

What does the employer amount include?

Only gross salary plus general employer IPRES. It is a partial employer-cost subtotal, excluding employer CSS, supplementary pension and other employment-specific charges.

Can I work backwards from a desired take-home amount?

Yes. Choose Net → Gross and enter the annual net target. The same family, IPRES and TRIMF settings apply. The main result is estimated monthly gross; the detailed result shows annual gross and net. Tax rounding can make more than one gross amount produce a similar net.

Can I use the calculator without an account or AI?

Yes. The calculation and printable estimate work locally without an account. Optional AI sends financial amounts to a provider only when requested. It is not needed for the calculation.

How can I check the example?

At XOF 7,500,000 annual gross, married with two qualifying children, the only spouse with taxable income, one spouse with no income, and general IPRES enabled: family shares 3, income tax 1,375,500, IPRES 290,304, TRIMF 36,000, annual net 5,798,196 and monthly net 483,183.
